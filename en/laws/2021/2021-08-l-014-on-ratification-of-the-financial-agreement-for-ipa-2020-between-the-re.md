# LAW NO. 08/L-014 ON RATIFICATION OF “THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2020 BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

### LAW NO. 08/L-014 ON RATIFICATION OF “THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2020 BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION”

### LAW NO. 08/L-014

### ON RATIFICATION OF “THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2020 BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2020 BETWEEN

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Financial Agreement for IPA 2020, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “The Financial Agreement for IPA 2020 between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 08/L-014 22 July 2021 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-114/2021 dated 02.08.2021 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu __________________________________ Annex of the Law No. 08/L-014, “The Financial Agreement for IPA 2020 between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union” attached to the law is published in the website of the Official Gazette (gzk.rks-gov.net)
