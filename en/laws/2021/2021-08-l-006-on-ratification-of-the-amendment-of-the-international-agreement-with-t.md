# LAW NO. 08/L-006 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT WITH THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK (EIB) ON THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR THE REHABILITATION OF RAILWAY LINE 10

### LAW NO. 08/L-006 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT WITH THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK

### (EIB) ON THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR THE REHABILITATION OF RAILWAY LINE 10

### LAW NO. 08/L-006

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT WITH

### THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK (EIB) ON THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR

### THE REHABILITATION OF RAILWAY LINE 10

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT

### WITH THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK (EIB) ON THE FINANCING OF THE

### PROJECT FOR THE REHABILITATION OF RAILWAY LINE 10

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank (EIB) regarding the amendment of the International Agreement with the European Investment Bank (EIB) for financing the project for the Rehabilitation of Railway Line 10, signed on December 8, 2015.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement This Law ratifies the agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank regarding the amendment of the International Agreement with the European Investment Bank (EIB) for financing the project for the Rehabilitation of Railway Line 10, signed on December 8, 2015.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the annex of this Law is attached the amendment of the International Agreement with the European Investment Bank (EIB) for financing the project for the Rehabilitation of the Railway Line 10.

#### Article 4

Entry into Force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-006 06 December 2021
