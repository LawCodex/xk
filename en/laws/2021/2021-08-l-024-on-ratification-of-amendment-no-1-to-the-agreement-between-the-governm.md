#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify Amendment No.1 to the Agreement Between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of Hungary about the establishment of a Framework Programme for Financial Cooperation, signed on 14 of December 2016.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies Amendment No.1 to the Agreement Between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of Hungary about the establishment of a Framework Programme for Financial Cooperation, signed on 14 of December 2016.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law there is attached the Amendment No. 1 of the Agreement Concerning the Draft Program for Financial Cooperation.
