# LAW NO. 08/L-036 ON OVERSEAS DEPLOYMENT OF THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L - 036 ON OVERSEAS DEPLOYMENT OF THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L - 036

### ON OVERSEAS DEPLOYMENT OF THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON OVERSEAS DEPLOYMENT OF THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law regulates the manner and procedures of overseas deployment and withdrawing of the Kosovo Security Force.

#### Article 2

Scope This Law applies in all cases of the KSF deployment in international operations and other military activities abroad.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Expressions, terms and abbreviations used in this Law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. KSF - Kosovo Security Force;

1.2. International Operations – are the international peace operations and international humanitarian operations;

1.3. Training and exercise – include individual or group KSF exercises abroad in order to enhance the pre-mission operational readiness;

1.4. KSF Personnel –military and civil personnel and their dependents who are deployed in overseas operations;

1.5. Host Country – a country in which the KSF personnel are deployed to conduct operation;

1.6. Inviting Country – a country which invites the KSF to participate in international operations, but which is not necessarily the host country;

1.7. Technical Agreements – agreements, arrangements, memoranda, technical protocols for the implementation of other military activities abroad, which are an integral part of the basic agreements.

\2. Expressions, terms and other abbreviations used in this Law have the same meaning as in the relevant legislation in this field.

#### Article 4

International Peace Operations

\1. International Peace Operations refer to operations aimed at establishing peace, maintaining peace, as well as supporting peace.

\2. The operations from paragraph 1 of this Article include:

2.1. Collective self-defense operations conducted in compatibility with International Agreements ratified by law and based on the rights recognized by Article 51 of the Charter of the United Nations or to fulfill other legal obligations;

2.2. The Military operations conducted to maintain and restore peace in accordance with VI and VII chapters of United Nations as well other military operations undertaken in compatibility with the International Agreements ratified by Law;

2.3. The International Military operations organized by military security organizations, by countries or group of countries.

#### Article 5

Procedures on participation to International Peace Operations

\1. The deployment of the KSF in international peace operations is done on the basis of agreements with the host country, the inviting country, at the invitation of any international organization, or at the invitation of any of the member states of international security organizations, as well as by Resolution of the Security Council of United Nations Security.

\2. After ratification of the International Agreements according to paragraph 1 of this Article, upon proposal of the Government of Kosovo, Assembly of Republic of Kosovo takes decision for the KSF overseas deployment in international peace operations.

\3. After the Assembly decision according to paragraph 2 of this Article, President of the Republic of Kosovo authorizes the KSF overseas deployment.

\4. Proposal of the Government according to paragraph 2 of this Article shall contain the following data:

4.1. mission task;

4.2. operation type;

4.3. place of mission performance;

4.4. basic Laws on mission performance;

4.5. maximal number of personnel who could be deployed in this mission;

4.6. duration of this mission;

4.7. delegation of commanding authority;

4.8. financial cost;

4.9. limitations in mission.

#### Article 6

International humanitarian operations International humanitarian operations refer to operations of providing assistance in cases of humanitarian disasters, technical, technological or ecological disasters which endanger the health and lives of the people, property and environment, as well as provision of assistance in cases of the destructions due to a war or terrorism or other disasters.

#### Article 7

Procedures on Participation to International humanitarian operations

\1. The deployment of the KSF in international humanitarian operations is done on the basis of the request for assistance by the host country, based on the offer by the Republic of Kosovo, based on international agreements, at the invitation of any international organization, or at the request of any of the member states of international security organizations, as well as by any Resolution of the Security Council of United Nations Security.

\2. Decision on the KSF personnel deployment for international humanitarian operations is taken by Government of the Republic of Kosovo with proposal of the Minister of Defense.

\3. Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo shall be informed immediately with the decision of the Government according to paragraph 2 of this Article.

\4. Following the decision of the Government, according to paragraph 2 of this Article, President of the Republic of Kosovo shall authorize the overseas deployment of KSF.

#### Article 8

Trainings and exercises prior to mission

\1. The KSF overseas deployment in training and exercises for preparation for the assigned mission is done on the basis of an invitation from the hosting country, based on bilateral agreements or within the invitation of any international organization.

\2. Decision on the KSF deployment according to paragraph 1 of this article is taken by the Minister of Defense.

#### Article 9

Other Military Activities

\1. Other military activities include, but are not limited to:

1.1. other military trainings and activities;

1.2. education;

1.3. races;

1.4. parades;

1.5. sports activities;

1.6. exchange of the personnel.

Procedure for the participation in other military activities

\2. For the participation of the KSF in other military activities according to Article 9 of this Law, the Minister of Defense issues a decision.

#### Article 11

Applicable Law KSF personnel, while participating in operations and other military activities overseas, shall be subject to the legislation of the Republic of Kosovo and the international law, unless otherwise provided by bilateral and multilateral International Agreements.

#### Article 12

Delegation and acceptance of the Commanding Authority

\1. When deploying the KSF overseas, the Republic of Kosovo may transfer part of its authority to the military commander of a foreign state or an international organization, in the same way, it may accept an authority given to it by a military commander of a foreign state or by an international organization, only within the mission duties and authorized limitations.

\2. Competent authorities for the KSF deployment in international overseas operations, in each case, in the act of the deployment shall define mission and mandate of these forces.

#### Article 13

Personnel replacement The replacement of the personnel within the mandate of deployment is done according to the procedures defined by a special sub-legal act issued by the Minister of Defense.

#### Article 14

Procedure on extension of the mission Procedure of the decision on extension of the overseas mission of KSF personnel shall be conducted according to the same procedure of its deployment.

#### Article 15

Withdrawal of the KSF Personnel

\1. In cases when it is necessary to protect the interests of the Republic of Kosovo, at any time the KSF personnel is withdrawn from the mission.

\2. Withdrawal of the KSF personnel from international operations and other international military activities is done according to the deployment procedure, set out in Articles 5, 7, 8 and 10 of this Law.

\3. In exceptional circumstances, the decision to withdraw the KSF personnel from the mission is taken by the President of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The KSF personnel will have provided transportation, necessary equipment for operation and training, health insurance, life insurance, salaries and wages, food and accommodation.

#### Article 17

Care provision for KSF personnel Caring for the KSF personnel before, during and after the return, as well as caring for their family during the mission, is an institutional responsibility, which is regulated by a special sublegal act issued by the Minister of Defense.

#### Article 18

Execution of Customs Procedure on Border Crossing

\1. According to existing custom legislation the Kosovo`s Customs applies Export-Import Standard Procedure for all goods and equipment in case of the border crossing by the Kosovo Security Force with intention their participation to the overseas operation and other military activities.

\2. Pursuant to paragraph 1 of this Article, the relevant Ministry of Finance in consultation with Kosovo Customs, issues a sub-legal act which determines the procedure to be followed when declaring goods/equipment of the KSF, as well as the manner of application of procedural facilities for crossing the customs control.

\3. Crossing border of other countries will be defined by an Operation Agreement.

#### Article 19

Responsibilities of Minister of Defense and Commander of the Kosovo Security Force for the KSF Overseas Deployment

\1. Minister of Defense is responsible:

1.1. to propose to the Kosovo`s Government the plan and necessary funding for the KSF activities in operations out of the Kosovo;

1.2. to assess and decide on starting of the planning process;

1.3. if required, to report on process of the deployment in front of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The KSF Commander is responsible for organization, training and equipment of the KSF in order to meet the requirements of the overseas deployment, as well as to provide care and monitor the members during the mission abroad.

\3. Procedures for the KSF deployment in international operations and other military activities include: planning, preparation, exercise, training and readiness of the KSF for overseas deployment. Deployment, withdrawal, replacement and post-return treatment procedures will be defined by special sub-legal act issued by the Minister of Defense.

#### Article 20

Reporting

\1. After completion of the mission in international peace and humanitarian operations and upon the request, KSF Commander shall prepare a report for the President and Minister of Defense.

\2. Minister of Defense shall, after the completion of the mission in international peace and humanitarian operations and upon the request, prepare a report for the Government and Assembly of Kosovo.

#### Article 21

Issuance of sub-legal acts

\1. The sub-legal acts stipulated by this Law are issued within a maximum of one (1) year from the entry into force of this law.

\2. The sub-legal acts that are in force continue to be implemented, until the issuance of the new sub-legal acts provided that they are not in conflict with this law.

#### Article 22

Abrogation Provision Upon the entry into force of this Law, Law No. 04/L-177 Law on Overseas Deployment of the Kosovo Security Force shall be abrogated.

#### Article 23

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication of Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
