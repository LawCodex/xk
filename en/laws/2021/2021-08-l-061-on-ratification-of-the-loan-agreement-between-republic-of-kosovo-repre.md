The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR FINANCING THE PROJECT “KOSOVO

### COVID19 RESPONSE FOR SMES”

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to Ratify the Loan Agreement for Financing the Project “Kosovo Covid19 Response for SMES” between Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the European Investment Bank.

#### Article 2

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law is attached the text of the Loan Agreement for Financing the Project “Kosovo Covid19 Response for SMES” between Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the European Investment Bank.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
