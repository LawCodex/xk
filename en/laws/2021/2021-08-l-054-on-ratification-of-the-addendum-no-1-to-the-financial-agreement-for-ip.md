# LAW NO. 08/L-054 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 1 TO THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

### LAW NO. 08/L -054 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM NO. 1 TO THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

### LAW NO. 08/L -054

ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 1 TO THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR

### IPA 2017, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN

### UNION”

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 1 TO THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT

### FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the “Addendum No. 1 to the Financial Agreement for IPA 2017, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Addendum No. 1 to the Financial Agreement for IPA 2017, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
