# LAW NO. 08/L-031 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT “KOSOVO EMERGENCY COVID-19 PROJECT– ADDITIONAL FINANCING”, BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

### LAW NO. 08/L -031 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT “KOSOVO EMERGENCY COVID-19 PROJECT– ADDITIONAL FINANCING”,

### BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

### LAW NO. 08/L -031

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT “KOSOVO EMERGENCY

### COVID-19 PROJECT– ADDITIONAL FINANCING”, BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT “KOSOVO EMERGENCY

### COVID-19 PROJECT– ADDITIONAL FINANCING”, BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify The Financing Agreement “Kosovo Emergency Covid-19 Project– Additional Financing”, between Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and Transfers and the International Development Association.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the agreement Through this Law there is ratified The Financing Agreement “Kosovo Emergency Covid-19 Project– Additional Financing”, between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and Transfers and the International Development Association.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law there is attached the Agreement for Additional Financing.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
