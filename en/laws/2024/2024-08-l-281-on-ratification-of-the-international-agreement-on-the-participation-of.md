# LAW NO. 08/L-281 ON RATIFICATION OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION PROGRAMME “DIGITAL EUROPE (2021-2027)” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

### UNION PROGRAMME “DIGITAL EUROPE (2021-2027)” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

### LAW NO. 08/L-281

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF

### KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION PROGRAMME “DIGITAL EUROPE (2021-2027)”

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF

### KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION PROGRAMME “DIGITAL EUROPE (2021-2027)”

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is ratification of the International Agreement on the participation of Kosovo in the European Union Programme “Digital Europe (2021-2027)” between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The International Agreement on the participation of Kosovo in the European Union Programme “Digital Europe (2021-2027)” between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union, shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law. The agreement and its annexes shall be integral parts of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-281 16 May 2024

Pursuant to the article 80, paragraph 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
