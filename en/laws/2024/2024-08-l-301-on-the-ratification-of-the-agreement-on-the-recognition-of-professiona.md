Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE RECOGNITION OF PROFESSIONAL

### QUALIFICATIONS OF NURSES, VETERINARY SURGEONS, PHARMACISTS AND MIDWIFES IN THE

### CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE AGREEMENT CONTEXT

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is the ratification of the agreement on the recognition of the professional qualifications of nurses, veterinary surgeons, pharmacists and midwives in the Central European Free Trade Association Agreement Context.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The agreement on the recognition of the professional qualifications of nurses, veterinary surgeons, pharmacists and midwives in the Central European Free Trade Association Agreement Context shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-301 23 May 2024

Pursuant to the article 80, paragraph 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo
