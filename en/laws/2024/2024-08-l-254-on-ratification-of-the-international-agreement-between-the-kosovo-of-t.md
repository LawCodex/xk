The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE KOSOVO OF

### THE ONE PART AND EUROPEAN UNION OF THE OTHER PART, ON THE PARTICIPATION OF

### KOSOVO IN THE UNION PROGRAMME SINGLE MARKET PROGRAMME

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the International Agreement between the Kosovo of the one part, and European Union, of the other part, on the participation of Kosovo in the Union Programme Single Market Programme.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement International Agreement between the Kosovo of the one part, and European Union, of the other part, on the participation of Kosovo in the Union programme Single Market Programme, concluded on date 11 May 2023, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to this Law is the International Agreement between the Kosovo of the one part, and European Union, of the other part, on the participation of Kosovo in the Union Programme Single Market Programme.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-254 16 May 2024

Pursuant to the article 80, paragraph 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
