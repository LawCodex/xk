### LAW NO. 08/L-182

### ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

### SUB-CHAPTER I

### SCOPE AND PURPOSE

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This Law is intended, to provide judicial protection of the subjective rights and legitimate interests of the subjects of the law against acts or omissions committed unlawfully during the exercise of public authority as well as the lawfulness in the exercise of such an authority.

#### Article 2

Scope

1.This law defines the principles, rules and procedures for adjudicating administrative disputes (hereinafter: “the administrative adjudication”).

\2. This law applies to the administrative adjudication regardless of which is the competent court that adjudicates the case.

\3. For all matters not regulated by this law, the provisions of the judicial procedural civil law shall apply, unless expressly provided otherwise by this Law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. In this law the following terms/expressions shall mean:

1.1 “Sublegal normative act” – is an act, issued based on the explicit authorisation of a law adopted by the Assembly, which establishes mandatory rules of conduct in a general and abstract manner.

1.2 “Other Administrative Action” – the real act and action for the fulfilment of the public service of general interest, in accordance with the law on general administrative procedure, taken together.

\2. The other terms and expressions used in this Law shall have the same meaning as defined or regulated in the Law on general administrative procedure or the civil procedural law in force, according to the context in which they are used.

#### Article 4

Administrative disputes Administrative judicial jurisdiction covers all disputes of public law except those of a constitutional nature in the jurisdiction of the Constitutional Court.

### SUB-CHAPTER II

### GENERAL PRINCIPLES OF ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES ADJUDICATION

#### Article 5

Principle of legality The competent court examines the dispute and decides based on the Constitution and the law.

#### Article 6

The principle of due process and adjudication within a reasonable time

\1. The court shall decide on the basis of a due judicial process, in accordance with the constitutional and legal provisions.

\2. The court shall conduct court proceedings promptly and without delay, avoiding unnecessary actions and costs, preventing abuse of the rights of parties and other participants in the proceedings, and shall render a decision within a reasonable time and according to legal deadlines.

#### Article 7

The principle of providing the possibility of expression The Court shall provide the parties an effective and equal opportunity to express and present their views on the claims of other participants as well as on any matter of fact and law necessary to resolve the administrative dispute.

#### Article 8

The principle of oral hearing

\1. The parties, during the administrative dispute trial, are entitled to a public oral hearing before at least one court instance.

\2. The court shall examine the administrative dispute without holding a direct and/or public hearing only in the cases expressly provided for by this Law.

#### Article 9

The principle of assisting, instruction of mediation and reconciliation

\1. The court shall ensure that the lack of knowledge of participants in the administrative dispute shall not violate their rights under the law.

\2. At every stage of the proceedings, the court shall provide the participants of proceedings sufficient and clear explanations to ensure that no claim or evidence necessary to protect their rights or interests remains unknown due to lack of experience in legal affairs and to ensure that any formal shortcomings in the submissions of the participants that would lead to their non-admission are normally completed or clarified.

\3. The court instructs the litigants that they can resolve the dispute in the mediation procedure in compliance with the Law.

\4. The court shall, during all procedure and when the nature of the matter allows, make all efforts, but without violating its impartiality, in order to resolve the administrative dispute, with the reconciliation of the litigants.

### CHAPTER II

### ORGANIZATION, KOMPETENCE AND COMPOSITION AT TRIAL

#### Article 10

Organisation and Competence

\1. The organization of the system of competent court or courts to adjudicate administrative disputes (hereinafter referred to as the “Court”) and the respective jurisdiction/competence shall be regulated by a separate law.

\2. Except when otherwise provided by law, all administrative disputes shall be initiated at the first instance.

\3. Except when otherwise provided by law, in the second instance shall be adjudicated the appeals against decisions of the first instance.

\4. The Supreme Court shall adjudicate the revision, and as first and last instance, the lawsuit against a sub-legal normative act.

#### Article 11

Court composition at trial

\1. Except as expressly provided otherwise by law, in the administrative trial of the first instance, the case is adjudicated by one (1) single professional judge.

\2. In the administrative trial in the second instance, the cases shall be, always, adjudicated in a panel with three (3) professional judges.

\3. The Supreme Court adjudicates the case in a panel of three (3) judges.

\4. In all cases when adjudicating in a panel, the panel, shall appoints one of the judges as a judge rapporteur on the case.

#### Article 12

Referral of cases to other courts and dispute of competences

\1. A Court that has received a lawsuit for which it finds that it is not within its jurisdiction under the law, with no delay, declares its incompetence with a ruling and will send the case to the court, which in accordance with the law, is competent for the case.

\2. If one of the parties raises an objection for the incompetence of the court that has accepted the case and claims that the case is in the jurisdiction of another court, the court decides, without delay, on the jurisdiction by a ruling. Against this ruling there may be filed a special appeal within seven

(7) days, to the court that is of immediate higher instance and common between the two courts in question.

\3. If the first court as well as the other court do not accept the jurisdiction over a case, the court to which the case was sent, according to paragraph 1 of this article, must refer the case for the resolution of the dispute of jurisdiction to the immediate higher instance court and common for the courts in dispute.

### CHAPTER III

### SUB CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 13

Types of lawsuits

\1. Through an administrative dispute the plaintiff may seek:

1.1. the annulment of an administrative act (hereinafter “action on annulment”);

1.2. the issuance of an administrative act (hereinafter “action on the issuance of an administrative act”);

1.3. performance or ceasing the performance of another administration action (hereinafter “action against another administrative action”);

1.4. declaration of the unlawfulness of an administrative act which no longer produces consequences, or the unlawfulness of a performed, non-performed or delayed real act, or the declaration of existence or non-existence of a public law relationship (hereinafter “declaratory action”);

1.5. reward of the unjust damage (hereinafter “action for damages”);

1.6. abrogation of a sublegal normative act (hereinafter “action against a sublegal normative act”);

1.7. clarification of the competence of a public organ (hereinafter “action on competence”);

1.8. resolution of a dispute related to the validity, extent of the effects, interpretation or execution of an administrative contract (hereinafter “actions on administrative contract”).

#### Article 14

Action on annulment

\1. An action on annulment may be filed to seek the total or partial annulment of an administrative act, which the plaintiff claims to affect his right or legal interests because of its unlawfulness.

\2. The partial annulment may include the partial amendment of an administrative act imposing/ consisting in a pecuniary obligation with the view of decreasing the pecuniary amount of such an obligation.

#### Article 15

Action for issuance of an administrative act

\1. An action for issuance of an administrative act may be filed to seek the obligation of the administration to issue a requested administrative act, which has been refused explicitly or omitted, to which the plaintiff claims to be entitled.

\2. An action for issuance of an administrative act in case of silence of the administration, may be filed only if the rule of silent consent does not apply in accordance with the respective law on General Administrative Procedure.

#### Article 16

Actions against another administrative action

\1. An action related to another administrative action may be filed to seek the obligation of the administration to:

1.1. stop/cease performing of another administrative action, which the plaintiff claims to affect his rights or legal interests, or

1.2. perform another administrative action, which the plaintiff claims that the administration was omitted despite the obligation of performance and to which he claims to be entitled.

\2. The action related to an action/non-action for the fulfilment of the public service of general interest, might be filed only if there is no available any other form of legal protection.

#### Article 17

Exhaustion of ordinary administrative legal remedies

\1. An action on annulment, an action for the issuance of an act as well as an action related to another administrative action, may be filed only after the exhaustion of the ordinary administrative legal remedies, according to the relevant Law on general administrative procedure.

\2. Notwithstanding paragraph 1 of this Article, the action may be filed directly to the court without the necessity of exhaustion of administrative remedies, in cases when:

2.1. there does not exist the superior body in administrative procedure;

2.2. the third party has been infringed of the rights or legal interests from the administrative act resolving the appeal; or

2.3. it is expressively foreseen by the Law.

#### Article 18

Deadline for filing an action of annulment and an action for issuance of an administrative act

\1. An action on annulment and an action for the issuance of a refused administrative act may be filed within the deadline of thirty (30) days, from the:

1.1. notification of the decision on the administrative appeal or from the expiration of the deadline for such a decision, or

1.2. notification of the administrative act of first instance, in case the exhaustion of the appeal is not mandatory for accepting the action.

\2. An action on annulment may be filed, within the deadline of two (2) months, from when the plaintiff has taken or should have taken, in any way, acting diligently, cognisance of the act, if:

2.1 the plaintiff is another person, different from the one the act is addressed to, and the exhaustion of the administrative appeal is not mandatory for accepting the action; or

2.2 the action is filed directly against the act resolving the administrative appeal, which is addressed to other subject of the law and that affects the plaintiff for the first time.

\3. An action for the issuance of an administrative act in case of administrative silence may be filed within the deadline of thirty (30) days, from the notification of the decision on the administrative appeal.

4.1. the expiration of the deadline for notification of the decision on the administrative appeal;

4.2. expiration of the deadline for the notification of the administrative act, in case the exhaustion of the administrative legal means is not mandatory for accepting the action.

\5. The deadlines foreseen by this article shall be doubled, if the administrative act that is challenged directly to the court or the decision that resolves the administrative appeal does not contain clear and precise information on the access to judicial remedies.

#### Article 19

Deadline for filing an action related to another administrative action

\1. The actions related to another administrative action may be filed within the deadline of thirty (30)

days, from the notification of the decision resolving the administrative complaint or the expiration of the deadline for the notification of such a decision.

\2. The action to cease performing another administrative action based on sub-paragraph 1.1 of Article 16 of this Law, if the exhaustion of the administrative complaint is not required, may be filed within thirty (30) days, from when the plaintiff got or had to get cognisance of the contested real act, but not later than within six (6) months from the performance of the action or beginning of its performance.

\3. The action to perform another administrative action based on sub-paragraph 1.2 of Article 16 of this Law, if the exhaustion of the administrative complaint is not required, may be filed within a deadline of four (4) months, from the submission of the request for performing another administrative action, in case of inaction by the administration.

\4. The deadline foreseen by paragraph 1 of this article shall be doubled, if the decisions that resolves the administrative complaint does not contain clear and precise information on the access to judicial remedies.

#### Article 20

Declaratory Action

\1. With the declaratory action, the plaintiff seeks:

1.1. the declaration of unlawfulness of an administrative act that has ceased producing its legal consequences;

1.2. the declaration of unlawfulness of a performed real act which has stopped or omission of such an act which was later performed; or

1.3. the declaration of the existence or inexistence of an administrative act or of a legal relationship of administrative law and clarification of the rights and obligations of the plaintiff deriving from such a relation.

\2. A declaratory action may be filed only if the plaintiff lawsuits to have a reasonable interest in such a declaration in accordance to paragraph 1 of this Article.

\3. A declaratory action may not be filed if the plaintiff can file another action foreseen by articles 14 to 16 of this Law.

\4. A declaratory action provided for in sub-paragraph 1.3 of this Article cannot be filed if the administrative authority is not exercised by the administration yet.

\5. If after filing an action foreseen by Articles 14 up to 16 of this Law, the administrative act has ceased producing legal effects, the objected real act has stopped or, respectively, the requested real act is carried out, the plaintiff may change the action into a declaratory action in accordance with sub-paragraph 1.1 or respectively 1.2 of this Article.

\6. The declaratory action can be filed at any time, as long as there is a reasonable interest of the plaintiff.

#### Article 21

Action for the compensation of the damage

\1. With the action for the compensation of the damage, the plaintiff seeks the compensation of an unfair damage caused by the compulsory exercise or non-exercise of the administrative activity.

\2. The action for the compensation of the damage may be filed as a subordinated request together with or during the adjudication of any of the actions foreseen in Articles 14, 15, 16 and 20 of this Law.

\3. The action for the compensation of the damage may be filed separately but after the adjudication of the actions foreseen in Articles 14, 15, 16 and 20 of this Law.

\4. In this cases foreseen by paragraph 3 of this Article, the action is statute-barred within the deadline foreseen in accordance with the provisions of the Law in force, and derives from the day when the damaged has taken cognisance of the existence, the extent of the damage and the administration that has caused such a damage.

#### Article 22

Standing to sue

\1. The right to file actions foreseen in Articles 14, 15, 16 and 20 of this Law shall have:

1.1. any natural or legal person that claims to be affected in his right or legal interest due to an action or non-action of the administration;

1.2. the holders of collective or broad interests of the public, if they fulfil the conditions to be part in the administrative proceeding, if they lawsuit that the collective interest or respectively broad interest of public is affected; or

1.3. a public organ authorised explicitly by a special law and for the protection of the public interest in cases foreseen by that Law.

\2. Except when otherwise explicitly provided by a special Law, a public organ cannot file an action against an act of a superior organ, issued to resolve an administrative legal remedy against an act or other administrative action of the public organ itself.

\3. The superior organ within the meaning of paragraph 2 of this Article shall be the organ that, in compliance with the Law, has the hierarchical control competences over the competent organ for issuing the act and that is charged to be involved in the review procedure of the administrative legal remedy.

\4. Any natural or legal person that lawsuits to has been infringed in an unjust manner by the exercise or non-exercise of the administrative activity shall have the right to file an action foreseen by Article 21 of this Law.

\5. The holder of collective interests may, according to this Article, exercise the right of action or intervene in a commenced adjudication even on behalf of one of its members with the consent of the latter.

#### Article 23

Passive legitimation

\1. The respondent in the action against the administration is, as a rule, the public body that has issued, refused, or failed to issue the requested administrative act or has or has not performed the other administrative act that is being challenged.

\2. Exceptionally, the respondent is the public body that has opted the administrative legal remedy, if the violation of the right or the legitimate interest of the plaintiff has occurred, for the first time, from the decision that resolves the administrative legal remedy.

#### Article 24

Other admissibility conditions A special law may foresee additional admissibility conditions for the admissibility of the actions.

#### Article 25

Action for abrogation of a sublegal normative act

\1. An action for abrogation of a sub-legal normative act may be filed to seek the declaration of the unlawfulness of a provision of a sub-legal normative act or of such an act in its entirety and their abrogation.

\2. An action for abrogation of a sublegal normative act, may be filed if a natural or legal person lawsuits that the act contravenes the law and that his right or legal interest has been or will be affected in the future.

\3. The holder of a collective or general interest of the public may file such an action, if lawsuits that the act contravenes the Law and that the collective or respectively a general interest of the public have been or will be affected in the future.

\4. The action can be filed as well by a public organ authorised explicitly by a special law and in cases foreseen by the latter.

\5. An action for abrogation of a sub-legal normative act may be filed against the organ that rendered

\6. An action for abrogation of a sub-legal normative act cannot be filed, in case there is a Constitutional Court decision on the constitutionality of such an act or of its provisions. \7. An action for abrogation of a sub-legal normative act may be filed at any time, as long as the act is in force, including after the approval but before its publication for the purpose of entering into force.

#### Article 26

Actions related to administrative contracts

\1. In relation to the eventual disputes, related to the validity, interpretation, execution or withdrawal from or resolution of an administrative contract, foreseen by the law on general administrative procedure, could be raised the following actions:

1.1. a third party (non-party of the contract), which lawsuits is affected in his right or legal interest, may seek the declaration of the inefficacity of a substitution contract or a cooperation contract, towards him. Such an action might be filed within the general limitation period of three

(3) years from when the third party has or should have become aware of the administrative contract.

1.2. each of the parties of an administrative contract may seek the declaration of the invalidity of such a contract in the cases foreseen by the law on general administrative procedure.

Such an action may be filed within sixty (60) days from the conclusion of the contract.

1.3. each of the parties of an administrative contract, could file an action under the relevant law on obligations in order to seek the interpretation, execution or file an action for the resolution of a contract from the non-public organ party or the party public organ in other cases than the foreseen by paragraph 4 of Article 66 of the relevant law on general administrative procedure.

\2. Exceptionally, if a non-public organ party in a substituting contract seeks the execution of the contract it should file an action in accordance with article 15 of this Law.

#### Article 27

Action of competencies

\1. With the action of competence, the plaintiff requires the determination of the competent administration between the two administrations in dispute.

\2. An action for competence is filed as an independent action in the case foreseen by the relevant law on general administrative procedure, when no common superior organ exists between the parties in dispute, by each of the bodies in dispute.

\3. Each administration in dispute shall have the right to file an action against another administration.

\4. An action for competence may be filed at any time.

### ELECTRONIC MEANS

#### Article 28

Use of electronic means in court proceedings

\1. All court proceedings are registered in the case management information system.

\2. Parties may deliver electronic documents to the court. The court may transmit electronic documents to a party, if the party has provided prior consent.

\3. Court documents and documents submitted to the court may be kept electronically.

\4. The court can decide to hold sessions on line or in hybrid format if the party consents with it.

\5. The Kosovo Judicial Council shall, by a sub-legal act, determine the specific requirements and technical specifications for registration of proceedings and data in the case management information system, for electronic communication and electronic handling of documents as well as on the conduct of online and hybrid sessions.

### SUB-CHAPTER II

### PARTICIPANTS AND PROCEDURAL CAPACITY

#### Article 29

Procedural capacity in administrative proceeding

\1. Procedural capacity in the administrative proceeding shall be regulated by the provision of the law on civil procedure in force.

\2. Exceptionally, holders of broad public interests shall enjoy procedural capacities even in the absence of legal personality, provided they possess a separate steady organization and a satisfactory level of representation of the public interest, sought to be protected under the lawsuit.

\3. A public body shall enjoy procedural capacity by virtue of the law-ex lege.

\4. Unless otherwise provided by a separate law, a public body shall be represented by a civil servant, discharging the duties in that body, as authorized by the head of such organization. A public body may also be represented by an official of another institution, so authorized for this purpose. The said authorization shall be extended by the law or in writing.

#### Article 30

Parties and calling a third person

\1. Participants to administrative court proceedings shall be:

1.1 main parties, respectively plaintiff and respondent; and

1.2. any natural, legal or other entity that has the right to file a lawsuit under this Law, affected by or called upon in the proceedings (hereafter: “third person”).

2 When deciding that a third party holds legitimate rights or interests in relation to the subject of court proceedings, the court may, either ex-officio or upon request, call such person. The request may be

\3. The court, when deciding that the legal effect of the ruling will directly affect the rights or legitimate interests of a third person, shall summon such person to the court.

\4. The third person, so summoned, may make any submissions on the grounds of lawsuit or procedure. Submissions on the grounds shall not exceed those of the main parties.

### SUB-CHAPTER I

### FILING A LAWSUIT

#### Article 31

Submission of the lawsuit

\1. The court proceeding shall start with the filing of the lawsuit.

\2. The lawsuit is filed directly with the competent court, in written form.

\3. Lawsuits may also be filed by mail or by electronic means.

\4. When a lawsuit is filed by registered mail, the date of delivery to the post office or authorized postal service provider is considered as the date of its filing.

\5. The lawsuit shall be deemed filed with proscribed timeframe even in cases when filed within the timeframe but in a non-competent court.

#### Article 32

Filing a lawsuit by the party outside the Republic of Kosovo

\1. A party without a place of address or residence in the Republic of Kosovo may also file a lawsuit in an embassy or a consulate of the Republic of Kosovo. The lawsuit shall be filed in writing.

\2. The lawsuit shall be deemed filed with proscribed timeframe even in cases when is filed within the timeframe, under paragraph 1 of this Article. The embassy or consulate shall dispatch the lawsuit to the court to which it is addressed without delay. Original lawsuits or its copies shall be sent through any appropriate means.

\3. A party without an address or place of residence in the Republic of Kosovo shall appoint a person who has such address or residence in the Republic of Kosovo as a person authorized to receive any court notifications. Any notice to the authorized person so made by the court, regardless of the form, shall be presumed to have been serviced to party itself by virtue of this law.

\4. Paragraph 3 of this Article shall not apply if the party has a representative, whose address or residence is in the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 33

Content of the lawsuit

\1. The lawsuit, regardless of the form of its filing, shall be signed and sufficiently clear to identify:

1.1. the court to which it is addressed;

1.2. name, address and contact information of the plaintiff;

1.3. name and address of respondent;

1.4. the aim that the plaintiff seeks to achieve when filing the action.

\2. The plaintiff shall attach to the lawsuit a certificate of payment of court fee or a copy of the fee exemption request, as appropriate.

\3. The lawsuit shall also include:

3.1. an explanation of claims and specific requests of the plaintiff, including the secondary lawsuits, if any;

3.2. the facts underlying the claims and requests as well as any supporting evidence corroborating the lawsuit;

3.3. the original or copy of the act or decision being challenged-first instance administrative act or decision resolving the complaint or administrative challenge, administrative contract;

3.4. a copy of the lawsuit and appendices attached to the lawsuit for each of the other parties.

3.5. if they give consent to review the case under a written procedure.

3.6. the name, address and contact information of the person authorized to receive notices, if applicable, in accordance with Article 32 of this Law.

#### Article 34

General provisions for the preparation of the main hearing

\1. Upon receiving a lawsuit, with no delay, the court shall begin preparations for the main hearing of the case.

\2. The preparation includes the preliminary examination of the action, the filing of the action, requesting of the file of the administrative procedure, any other necessary action for the preparation of the main hearing of the case, including the scheduling of the main hearing (hereinafter “main hearing session”) or setting the deadline for submitting procedural documents in the case of the main hearing of the case at the panel session (written procedure).

#### Article 35

Preliminary review of the lawsuit The court shall initially examine its jurisdiction over the matter, whether the lawsuit is complete and comprehensible, whether the lawsuit is admissible or not, respectively whether the procedural requirements for filing a lawsuit are met.

#### Article 36

Review of competence

\1. The court shall assess its jurisdiction on the basis of the information contained in the lawsuit.

\2. If the court decides that another court has the jurisdiction, it shall forward the lawsuit to such competent court under Article 12 of this Law and shall notify the complainant to that effect.

\3. If, during the hearing, the circumstances upon which the court’s jurisdiction is based change, the competent court, which held jurisdiction at the time the lawsuit was filed shall retain competence to adjudicate the case.

#### Article 37

Deficient or incomprehensible lawsuit

\1. If the lawsuit fails to include the elements set out in paragraph 1 of Article 33 of this Law or is incomprehensible, the court shall ask the plaintiff to supplement, clarify of correct the lawsuit.

\2. In the request for supplementing the lawsuit, the court shall provide instructions on deficiencies to be supplemented or corrected, sets a deadline of eight (8) days for the completion and warns the plaintiff of the consequences of failure to complete the lawsuit within the deadline, according to paragraph 3 of this Article.

\3. If the plaintiff fails to supplement or does not correct the lawsuit within the set deadline and if the nature of deficiencies prevents the court to continue the review procedure, the party will be deemed to have renounced the lawsuit.

\4. Regarding the deficient or incomprehensible lawsuit, the court shall decide with e ruling that the party has renounced the lawsuit.

#### Article 38

Inadmissible lawsuit

\1. The court shall dismiss the lawsuit as inadmissible for trial on merits, if in its adjudication, it becomes apparent that:

1.1. the lawsuit is premature or it is filed after the deadline – lawsuit out of deadline;

1.2. the plaintiff has no right to file a lawsuit under this law;

1.3. the matter has already been adjudicated or there exists the litispendence for the same matter;

1.4. the plaintiff does not have the procedural capacity to file the lawsuit and the legal representative has not approved the lawsuit;

1.5. one of the other general or special procedural requirements, provided by law, for filing a lawsuit has not been met.

\2. If the court has received a request on the reinstatement of the case regarding the filing of a lawsuit or it has received a request on a court fee exemption, it shall review such request before taking the decision according to paragraph 1 of this Article.

\3. For reasons set out in paragraph 1 of this Article, the court shall dismiss the lawsuit, even ex officio, at any stage of proceedings.

\4. Dismissal of lawsuit under this Article shall be done by a ruling.

#### Article 39

Reinstatement of the case

\1. If, for a justifiable reason, which could have not been predicted or avoided, the party misses the deadline for filing an action, or any other deadline for carrying out an action in a procedure and, as a result, the action can no longer be carried out, such party may propose the reinstatement of the case.

\2. The proposal must be submitted within seven (7) days, starting from the day the reason for which the party missed the deadline ceased to exist.

\3. The proposal for the reinstatement of the case may not be made more than sixty (60) days after the day the delay occurred.

\4. The circumstances for the justification of the proposal must be presented at the time when the proposal is submitted. The omitted procedural action must also be carried out during the period in which it is possible to submit the proposal.

\5. The reinstatement of the case is not permitted if the deadline for submitting the proposal for the reinstatement of the case has been missed or if the party did not come to the hearing scheduled to review the proposal for reinstatement of the case.

\6. The proposal for the reinstatement of the case shall be submitted to the court, where the omitted action must have been adjudicated.

\7. No special appeal may be lodged against the ruling for the reinstatement of the case, unless the reinstatement of the case has been approved in contradiction to paragraph 5 of this Article.

#### Article 40

Serving the lawsuit for response

\1. The court shall serve the lawsuit and all its annexes to the respondent and any summoned third party in order to respond to the lawsuit.

\2. When serving the lawsuit, the court shall inform the respondent or third party of their right to file a response to the lawsuit and the respondent of the obligation to present complete file of administrative proceedings.

\3. When serving the lawsuit, the court shall, based on the circumstances of the case, set a reasonable time for responding to the lawsuit and presenting the file, and shall warn the respondent and the third party of necessary content of the response to the lawsuit as well as the procedural consequences of any failure to present the response to the lawsuit or failure to present the complete file of administrative proceedings.

### SUB-CHAPTER IV

### RULES FOR JUDICIAL INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL

#### Article 50

Case adjudication principles

\1. The court shall investigate and decide within the limits of the subject of the statement of lawsuit but shall not be limited by either complainant’s reasoning or the wording of the lawsuit.

\2. In the trial of the case, the court shall determine the factual situation and identify and interpret the legislation applicable to the relevant case and determine whether the lawsuit should be approved or not. Should the lawsuit involve several claims, then it shall be done for each of the claims.

\3. In any case, the court shall decide on the matter only based on the confirmed facts during the judicial investigation and administrative procedures, and for which the parties have had the opportunity to preliminary get familiar with and comment upon.

#### Article 51

Responsibilities for clarification of the facts and burden of proof

\1. The court shall have the final responsibility to ex officio clarify all necessary facts for a correct and lawful decision. The court shall not be bound by the facts presented by the parties.

\2. The court may, without limitation, resort to any remedy provided by the judicial procedural civil law.

\3. The parties shall cooperate in submitting the facts and obtaining evidence available to them.

\4. If the court finds that a fact/an issue of a fact is significant/important/decisive in resolving the matter, but is unable to ascertain the fact, the party favoured by such fact shall prove its existence (the burden of proof).

\5. In the case provided under paragraph 4 of this Article, if the fact is in favour of the plaintiff and the plaintiff manages to present the beginning of the evidence, the court may decide to pass the burden of proof on the non-existence of such fact on the respondent administration. The court may order the respondent administration to submit the document or information that serves to obtain evidence, proving the existence of such fact.

\6. The court shall not be bound by a request of a party to obtain an evidence. However, the request may be dismissed by the court only if it finds that the fact has already been proven or that it does constitute meaningful or important evidence to adjudicating the matter.

#### Article 75

\1. A party may waive the right to appeal from the moment when the judgment is served.

\2. An appeal may be withdrawn until the ruling of the second instance court is rendered.

#### Article 76

Grounds for appealing a judgment

\1. A judgment may be appealed on the following grounds:

1.1 Substantial violation of provisions governing trial procedure;

1.2 Incorrect or incomplete establishment of the factual situation of the case, or

1.3. Erroneous application of substantive law.

\2. A substantial violation of procedural provisions shall have occurred if the first instance court failed to apply or improperly applied a provision of this law, which may have produced or could have produced an impact on issuance of a fair and lawful judgment.

\3. Erroneous or incomplete establishment of the factual situation in a case shall have occurred if the first instance court erroneously established a decisive fact, i.e., failed to establish a fact. Incomplete establishment of the factual situation shall have occurred if so established by new facts or new evidence, admitted under this Law.

\4. Erroneous application of the substantive law shall have occurred if the court failed to apply or improperly applied the provision of the substantive law.

1.2. an indication of whether or not a judgment is challenged in its entirety or for a specific part thereof;

1.3 grounds for appeal, along with justification, and

#### Article 81

\1. The first instance court shall deliver a copy of the admissible and complete appeal, lodged within the allowed period, to the opposing party, which may, submit to the court his response to the appeal within a period of seven (7) days upon receipt.

\2. The court of first instance shall send promptly a copy of the reply to the appellant, or within seven

(7) days of its receipt by court, at latest.

\3. The second instance court shall not take into account any responses to the appeal submitted after the deadline.

\4. Submissions received by the court after receipt of response to appeal, or after the expiry of deadline for appeal shall not be considered, unless the court expressly requests additional statements.

### LAW NO. 08/L-182 ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

#### Article 82

Sending the case to the second instance court

\1. Upon receipt of a response to an appeal or after the expiry of the deadline for a response to an appeal, the first instance court shall submit to the second instance court, in seven (7) days at latest, the appeal and any answers thereto, if filed, along with all case files to the second instance.

\2. If the appellant alleges that the procedural provisions have been violated in the first instance proceedings, the first instance court, within the set deadline envisaged by paragraph 1 of this article, may provide explanations regarding the allegations of the appeal pertaining to such violations and, if necessary, may conduct investigations in order to verify the veracity of such allegations raised by the appellant.

#### Article 83

Preparations for review by the judge rapporteur

\1. Upon receipt by the second instance court, the judge rapporteur shall prepare the report for the purpose of review by second instance court.

\2. The Judge Rapporteur may, if necessary, ask the first instance court to prepare a report on the violation of the procedural provisions and other omissions alleged in the appeal, and subsequently conduct the necessary investigations in order to ascertain such violations or conduct such investigations on its own.

#### Article 84

Review of the matter

\1. The second instance court shall decide on the appeal, as a rule, on the basis of review in a written procedure - in the panel of judges and without holding a main hearing session, except in cases when provided otherwise by the law.

\2. The second instance court, as a rule, shall decide on the appeal based on the review of the case in written procedure at the trial panel hearing, only if there are new facts or evidence or new legal issues that may affect the resolution of the case.

\3. New evidentiary evidence or facts, according to paragraph 2 of this Article, shall be admissible under Article 85 of this Law.

#### Article 85

New evidence and facts at second instance review

\1. The second instance court shall reject new facts and evidence, which were not submitted at the first instance proceedings pursuant to deadlines, prescribed under article 52 of this Law, if:

1.1. their admission would delay the ruling of the court;

1.2. the party fails to provide a reasonable justification for the delay; and

1.3. the party has been instructed on the consequences of failing to comply with the deadline.

\2. Refusal under paragraph 1 of this Article shall not apply if the facts of the case may be investigated by the court with a little effort and without the co-operation of the party.

\3. The second instance court shall also reject new facts and evidence, which were rejected by the first instance court on reasonable grounds.

### LAW NO. 08/L-182 ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

#### Article 86

Calling a court session

\1. Upon review of the case, the parties and their legal representatives or proxies, as well as witnesses and experts, whose hearing is deemed necessary by the appeal court, shall be summoned to the court session.

\2. The review in the court session shall be done and the court shall award the meritorious decision, even if one or both parties summoned properly fail to appear, without providing a reasonable explanation for the default. In this case, the decision shall be made on the basis of allegations in the appeal as well as responses to such allegations and court’s investigation.

\3. In the summons to a party, the party shall be duly notified of procedural consequences of the absence in the review.

#### Article 87

Rules for conducting court session

\1. The review of the case in a court session before the second instance court shall begin with concise explanations on the matter by the rapporteur, who shall, however, refrain from expressing any opinion on the merits of the appeal.

\2. Thereafter, appellant shall justify his/her appeal, while the opposing party shall respond to the appeal.

\3. If an evidence cannot be used directly, the appeal court shall decide to read the minutes in the detailing such evidence.

#### Article 88

Complementing procedural provisions for the first-instance trial Except as otherwise expressly provided in this chapter, other provisions of this Law, other than the provisions on the trial in written procedure, shall also apply mutatis mutandis to the proceedings before the second instance court.

#### Article 89

The principle of second instance review

\1. The second instance court shall, within the limits of the appeal, determine the facts, collect evidence, examine the lawfulness of the challenged judgment and decide on the matter, similar to the first instance court.

\2. New facts or evidence shall be admitted under the terms of Article 85 of this Law.

#### Article 90

Court decisions

1.The second instance court, as per case, may:

1.1. dismiss the appeal as late, incomplete or inadmissible;

1.2. annul the challenged judgment and dismiss the lawsuit;

1.3. approve the appeal, annul the challenged judgment and remand the case in retrial in the first instance court;

### LAW NO. 08/L-182 ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

1.4. approve the appeal, annul the challenged judgement and resolve the matter based on merits by changing the judgment of the first instance, or

1.5. dismiss the appeal as ungrounded and uphold the challenged judgment.

#### Article 91

Dismissing the appeal as late, incomplete or inadmissible The second instance court shall, by a ruling, dismiss the late, incomplete or inadmissible appeal, unless the first instance court has already done so under this Law.

#### Article 92

The annulment of the challenged judgment and dismissal of a lawsuit

\1. The second instance court shall annul a challenged judgment and dismiss a lawsuit if it finds that the first instance court has decided on a lawsuit:

1.1 which is not part of the judicial jurisdiction,

1.2 for which there is adjudication in another trial,

1.3 already subject to a final judgment or

1.4. waived by the plaintiff, or a lawsuit subject to a court settlement.

#### Article 93

Dismissal of the appealed judgment and remanding in retrial

\1. Second instance court shall, with a ruling, dismiss the judgment of the first instance and returns the matter in retrial to the same first instance court, if it ascertains that there exists one of the following reasons mentioned in the appeal:

1.1. if any of the parties, by an unlawful action, especially due to no invitation regularly, has not been given the opportunity to take part in the review of the matter, while such an action has had an effect in legal and fair issuance of the decision;

1.2. if the court has issued the judgment without main review of the legal matter;

1.3. if in the issuance of the judgment there has taken part the judge who, according to the law itself, must be excluded from the trial; or

1.4. if the decision of the court does not content any of the substantive elements required by the Law.

#### Article 94

Changing the judgment

\1. The second instance court shall annul the first instance judgment and shall correct any shortcomings thereunder, by adjudicating the case and amending the challenged judgment, if it finds that the first instance court has committed another substantive violation of the procedural rules, committed an erroneous or incomplete establishment of factual situation, or incorrectly applied the substantive law.

\2. The second instance court shall not change the first instance verdict at the detriment of the appellant, if the appellant was the only party to submit an appeal, without the opposing side.

#### Article 95

Dismissing the ungrounded appeal The second instance court shall, by a judgment, dismiss the appeal as ungrounded and uphold the first instance judgment if it finds that there are no grounds for the challenge or are otherwise not decisive to warrant a different judgment.

#### Article 96

The content of the second instance decision

\1. The provisions of this Law on the content of the judgment shall also apply mutatis mutandis to the content of the judgment, i.e. the ruling, of the second instance court.

\2. In the reasoning of the judgment, the second instance court shall evaluate the allegations in the appeal that are decisive for its decision and shall provide reasons it has taken in consideration when rendering such a decision.

#### Article 97

Remand of second instance decision

1.The second instance court shall remand all the case files to the first instance court with a sufficient number of copies of its decision to be sent to parties and interested persons, as well as a copy for the first instance court.

\2. The second instance court shall undertake actions from paragraph 1 of this Article within fifteen

(15) days upon reaching its decision.

Extraordinary remedies

### CHAPTER IX

### PROCEDURE EXPENSES

#### Article 101

Procedure expenses

\1. The party that loses the judicial process, entirely, shall be obliged to bear all judicial costs, except the case determined in Article 65 paragraph 6 of this Law.

\2. A third party shall bear the costs only if it filed a lawsuit or a legal remedy.

\3. Without prejudice to paragraph 1 of this Article, the respondent administration shall, in any case, bear its own costs, regardless of the outcome of the proceedings.

\4. Regardless of the outcome of the court proceedings, the court may decide to order the respondent administration to bear the costs of obtaining evidence, if done so by virtue of official position, because:

4.1 the respondent administration obviously failed to carry out the necessary review during the administrative procedure, or

4.2 failed to submit the file of administrative procedure.

### CHAPTER X

### ENFORCEMENT OF COURT DECISIONS

#### Article 102

General provisions on the applicable enforcement procedure

\1. Enforcement of an obligation in money against the debtor administration shall apply in compliance with the provisions of the law on enforcement procedure.

\2. Except when expressively foreseen otherwise by this Law, the enforcement of an obligation against an entity other than administration, shall even apply in compliance with the provisions of the law on enforcement procedure.

\3. Enforcement of another obligation, except the obligation in money, (hereinafter: another obligation), against the debtor administration, shall be done in compliance with the below provisions of this Chapter. In this case the provisions of the respective Law on enforcement procedure supplement and apply accordingly only if and to the extend they are not foreseen otherwise expressively.

#### Article 103

General rules of enforcement of another obligation

\1. The enforcement is decided and applied by the judge that has adjudicated the matter in the first instance (hereinafter “Competent Court”). When impossible, it shall be decided and applied by

### LAW NO. 08/L-182 ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

another judge of the same court.

\2. Decisions in the enforcement procedure are taken by ruling.

\3. Enforcement of another obligation against the debtor administration shall be done by means foreseen in Articles 107 and 108 of this Law.

#### Article 104

Enforceability

\1. A final judgment or ruling, the object of which is to award or order doing something, shall become enforceable upon expiry of the deadline for voluntary compliance.

\2. A final judgment/ruling, the object of which is to prohibit or allow an action, shall immediately become enforceable upon attaining its final form.

\3. In the case provided under paragraph 1 of this Article, the debtor is required to voluntarily enforce the judgment / ruling within a reasonable period of time, expressly set by the Court in the judgment/ ruling that is being enforced. If the Court does not expressly set a deadline for voluntary enforcement, the general deadline of fifteen (15) days from the date on which the decision has become final, shall apply.

\4. A ruling on imposing a security measure shall be executed immediately upon its delivery to the party, unless a longer term is prescribed by the Court in the ruling.

#### Article 105

Commencement of enforcement procedure

\1. The enforcement procedure shall commence at the request of the creditor, except where otherwise expressively provided in this Law.

\2. The request for enforcement shall be clear to identify the judgment or ruling that is being enforced and to clarify the full or partial of voluntary non-enforcement of the obligation.

\3. The application of enforcement shall only commence on the basis of an enforcement ruling, issued by the Competent Court, unless otherwise provided in this Law.

\4. The means of enforcement shall, in any case, be determined by the Competent Court ex officio, in accordance with the principle of effectiveness and proportionality and regardless of the request of the debtor. Enforcement means shall be stipulated in the Enforcement Decision.

\5. The enforcement ruling against the administration shall be delivered to the head of the respective administration according to the law. Enforcement shall, in such case, commence, without delay, immediately upon the servicing of the ruling.

\6. Exceptionally, the ruling on determining the security measure shall be executed, ex officio, without requiring the request of the debtor or issuance of enforcement ruling. In this case, enforcement means shall be directly determined with the ruling for setting the security measure.

#### Article 106

General obligations

\1. The debtor, creditor or third persons shall comply with obligations proscribed under enforcement ruling.

\2. Every public official or officer shall specifically cooperate for the enforcement of the ruling against

### LAW NO. 08/L-182 ON ADMINISTRATIVE DISPUTES

the administration.

\3. Any act or action of the debtor administration that is intended to or has the consequence of avoiding or failing to enforce the judgment / ruling is unlawful.

#### Article 107

Fines

\1. If the administration fails to enforce the court judgment/ruling, the Competent Court may, ex officio, cite a fine. The fine shall be cited:

1.1 against the official responsible for that administrative matter, according to the law on the general administrative procedure, or

1.2 the head of the institution, if the responsible officer is not assigned or cannot be identified.

\2. A fine shall be cited if such failure to comply is deliberate or for no lawful reason.

\3. The amount of the coercive fine shall range from fifty percent (50%) to one hundred percent (100%) of the monetary value of the salary coefficient, determined by the annual law on budget, for each day of delay in enforcement. In the event of further non-enforcement of the judgment or ruling within five (5) days from the imposition of the first fine, other higher fine shall be determined. The fine may be imposed several times until the full enforcement, if necessary.

#### Article 108

Issuance of the act directly by the court

\1. If the administration fails to enforce the judgment or ruling requiring it to issue the required or an administrative act, the Competent Court shall, if appropriate, and without the presence of the parties, issue the act itself, which shall supersede the act of the debtor administration.

\2. Such an action shall be deemed justified, in conformity with paragraph 1 of this Article, in the following cases:

2.1. the necessary administrative decision-making is not discretionary, or discretion is limited to a single choice,

2.2. further reviews (investigation) of facts that cannot be performed by the Court itself are not needed; or

2.3. the issuance of administrative act is not complex and does not require specific knowledge of certain technical conditions.

#### Article 109

Change of the factual or legal situation

\1. If a judgment or ruling cannot be enforced due to a change in the factual or legal situation that has occurred after it became enforceable, makes the enforcement objectively impossible or disproportionate, the debtor administration shall notify the Court without delay, but no later than ten

(10) days from the day of recognition of the consequence of the change.

\2. The Court shall assess the newly-emerged condition and shall adopt measures to ensure enforcement in the best possible way, appropriate to the new situation created, including the determination of cash compensation, for the part of the judgment, which cannot be enforced.

### CHAPTER XI

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 110

Transitional provisions

\1. Administrative disputes under adjudication before the first instance competent court, the second instance court or the Supreme Court, on the day of entry into force of this law, shall continue to be adjudicated in accordance with the provisions of the law in force at the time of filing the lawsuit.

\2. Administrative disputes submitted for adjudication to the second instance court or the Supreme Court after the entry into force of this Law shall be adjudicated in accordance with the legal provisions in force at the time of filing an appeal or revision.

\3. For disputes for which there is decided the annulment of the decision and remanding the case for retrial, after the entry into force of this law, the provisions of this Law shall be applied for retrial.
