# LAW NO. 08/L-297 ON RATIFICATION OF THE MILITARY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF TÜRKIYE

### LAW NO. 08/L-297 ON RATIFICATION OF THE MILITARY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF TÜRKIYE

### LAW NO. 08/L-297

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE MILITARY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT

### OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF TÜRKIYE

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE MILITARY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE

### GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF

### TÜRKIYE

#### Article 1

Aim This Law aims to ratify the Military Framework Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Republic of Türkiye.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Military Framework Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Republic of Turkey, signed on January 29, 2024 shall be ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Military Framework Agreement Between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Republic of Türkiye, signed on January 29, 2024 is attached to this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-297 16 May 2024
