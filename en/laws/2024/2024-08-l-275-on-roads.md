### ON ROADS

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON ROADS

#### Article 1

Purpose

1.This law aims to regulate the legal position, the way of using categorized and uncategorized public roads, planning, construction, financing, categorization, management, maintenance, supervision, measures for the protection of roads, traffic that takes place on them and the establishment of the Kosovo Roads Agency.

2.This law is partially in line with:

2.1. Directive 2008/96/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 November 2008 on Road Infrastructure Safety Management amended by Directive (EU) 2019/1936 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2019 amending Directive 2008/96/EC on Road Infrastructure Safety Management;

2.2. Directive 2010/40/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 July 2010 on the framework for the deployment of Intelligent Transport Systems in the field of road transport and for interfaces with other modes of transport, amended by Decision (EU) 2017/2380 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 December 2017 amending Decision (EU) 2023/2661 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 November 2023;

2.3. Directive 96/53/EC of 25 July 1996 laying down for certain road vehicles circulating within the Community the maximum authorized dimensions in national and international traffic and the maximum authorized weights in international traffic, amended by Directive 2002/7/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 February 2002, Directive (EU) 2015/719 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2015, Decision (EU) 2019/984 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 and Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019;

#### Article 2

Scope Provisions of this law shall be mandatory for all central and local institutions, natural and legal persons, as well as for all entities that deal with planning, construction, financing, categorization, management, maintenance, supervision, road protection measures as well as users of public roads.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The expressions used in this law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. RSA - Road Safety Audit;

1.2. Agency - the Kosovo Roads Agency (KRA);

1.3. Highway - designed and constructed public road especially for the traffic of motor vehicles which does not serve the properties around its borders and which is marked with a special traffic sign, it has two road lanes physically separated for movement in opposite directions. The road lane must have at least two traffic lanes with one emergency stop lane on both sides of the highway. Roads of highway junctions are part of the highway;

1.4. Embankment - element of the public road with filling which provides lateral safety, stability of the road construction and serves for the installation of signalling and other road equipment;

1.5. Berm - space between a rigola and an excavation escarpment which serves to protect the rigola from eroded material, place traffic signs and equipment and ensure road visibility;

1.6. Protection belt - the surface outside the land belt on both sides of the road in which the construction of buildings is not allowed, starting from the outer edge of the road belt;

1.7. Road belt - the land belt on both sides of the road which belongs to this road whose width outside the residence for highways, national and regional roads is at least two (2) meters, while for local roads it is at least one (1) meter, measuring it from the most distant points of the transverse profile of the road and from the outer edge of the drainage channel;

1.8. Necessary visibility at the intersection - is determined based on the road traffic conditions at the intersection and on the basis of the legal and technical rules that regulate the construction of the necessary visibility areas at the intersection and the removal of obstacles that may endanger road traffic;

1.9. Necessary visibility - the visibility for safe stopping of vehicles in front of immobile obstacles in the traffic lane which must be ensured at each point of the road and which is determined based on the value of the designed speed;

1.10. RSI - Road safety inspection;

1.11. The upper layer of the road construction is the circulation layer;

1.12. Road connection - part of the road whereby any vehicle traffic surface is connected to the public road except for highways, therefore according to this Law, the connection shall mean the surface that enables vehicles to connect to a public road;

#### Article 4

Legal status of Public Roads

\1. Public roads are under the ownership of the Republic of Kosovo:

1.1. Ministry is responsible for: Highways, National Roads, Regional Roads and Connecting Roads;

1.2. Municipality is responsible for Local Roads.

\2. A public road is declared for use after the issuance of the use permit.

\3. The rights for use and the rights for construction on a public road shall be registered in the cadastre.

#### Article 5

Constituent elements of a public road

\1. A public road consists of:

1.1. the body of the road which consists of the lower construction and the upper construction of the road:

1.1.1. lower construction of the road includes: subbase, filling, excavation, facilities and equipment for water removal and protection from surface water and groundwater from the road, and the like;

1.1.2. the upper construction of the road includes: the traffic lanes, edges, rigolas, embankments, berms, dividing strips and the like.

1.2. road structures: bridges, viaducts, aqueducts, underpasses, overpasses, culverts, tunnels, galleries, protective walls and the like;

1.3. connections on public roads built on the road land;

1.4. sidewalks, pedestrian and bicycle paths;

1.5. a road belt that extends on both sides of the road, serves for unobstructed maintenance of the road and has a width according to the project;

1.6. air space above roads at a height of seven point zero (7.0) meters, free profile on national and regional roads at a height of four point fifty (4.50) meters and for highways four point seventy-five (4.75) meters from the circulating surface;

1.7. facilities on the road land, for the needs of road maintenance and the provision of services to drivers and passengers, as well as road payments which are foreseen by the road project;

1.8. road land is the land surface which, according to the project, must be built or has been built. The surface of the road strip is also the surface of the land where, according to the project, there must be built facilities for maintenance needs and provision of services for drivers and passengers, as well as payments for roads foreseen by the road project such as: facilities for road maintenance, management and supervision of roads, road tolls, gas stations, various services, parking places, rest areas and the like;

1.9. fixed measuring equipment and other vehicle control equipment;

1.10. traffic signs, equipment for monitoring and development of road traffic and road equipment such as lighting bodies, stable telecommunication equipment, installations and lighting in function of road traffic, vehicle counters, road signs, various installations, equipment in tunnels, in parking lots and similar;

1.11. road and environment protection facilities and equipment such as snow guards, wind guards, guideposts, station signs, escarpment guards from collapse and erosion, traffic guards and pedestrian guardrails, noise guards and other impacts with negative effects on the environment and the like.

\2. Public road, in addition to the elements from paragraph 1. of this Article, shall also include facilities and equipment for regulating the traffic.

#### Article 6

Status change of a public road

\1. If there is a need to discontinue the use of a public road or a part of it, its status as public property for general use can be removed, the real estate from which this status is removed remains state property for general use, under the ownership of the Republic of Kosova.

\2. The proposal to remove the status of public property for general interest of a road or a part of it is initiated by the relevant Ministry or municipality, while the decision to remove the status of the road or any part of it is issued by the Government of the Republic of Kosova.

#### Article 7

Categorization of public roads

\1. Public roads, depending on their social, economic and traffic importance, are categorized into:

1.1. Highway;

1.2. National road;

1.3. Regional road;

1.4. Connecting road;

1.5. Local road;

1.6. Uncategorized road.

\2. For the roads from sub-paragraph 1.1., 1.2., 1.3. and 1.4. of paragraph 1. of this Article, the criteria, conditions, procedures, evidence, designation, marking and other activities related to the categorization of public roads shall be determined by the Ministry by a sublegal act, while for roads from sub-paragraph 1.5. and 1.6. of paragraph 1. of this Article shall be determined by the municipality.

#### Article 8

Road tax

\1. For the use of public roads or special structures, a special highway fee can be applied to them or the use of special structures such as bridges, tunnels, viaducts and similar.

\2. The manner, procedures, height and road segments or structures, whose use is paid according to paragraph 1. of this Article, is determined by the Government by means of a sub-legal act.

#### Article 9

Standards of public roads

\1. Public roads must be designed, constructed, reconstructed and maintained according to the standards in force.

\2. The standards of designing, construction, reconstruction and maintenance shall be determined by the Ministry.

#### Article 10

Procedure for issuance, suspension and revocation of the permit

\1. The construction permit and the necessary environmental consents shall be issued by the Ministry responsible for the construction/environment.

\2. The permit for reconstructing or widening of roads: highways, national, regional, connecting roads and other accompanying facilities, shall be granted by the Ministry;

\3. The permit for the construction or reconstruction of local roads and other accompanying facilities shall be granted by the Municipality.

#### Article 11

Strategy of public roads development

\1. The Government of the Republic of Kosova upon the proposal of the Ministry shall approve the strategy whereby it determines the orientations and the development plan of public roads in the Republic of Kosova.

2.3. the need for the maintenance of public roads, the principles of maintenance and the proposal of criteria for the priorities for the maintenance of highways, national, regional, connecting and local roads.

#### Article 12

Municipal infrastructure, energy and telecommunication lines

\1. If, during the construction or reconstruction of public roads, there is foreseen the construction or reconstruction of municipal infrastructure, energy and telecommunication lines and their connection with a public road, which entirely or in part are in the service of the public road, the project must also include the construction works that will be carried out on the surface, respectively above and below the road surface.

\2. Establishment of the right to use or the right to build the infrastructure and the investor’s obligations of energy and telecommunication lines and other transmission equipment from paragraph 1. of this Article, as well as the rights and obligations of the Agency, shall be determined by an agreement.

\3. The approval for the construction of the municipal infrastructure of power lines, telecommunications and other equipment on the public road that serve the needs of the public road shall be granted by the Agency.

\4. If during the construction or reconstruction of a public road, there is encountered the existing infrastructure of water, energy, telecommunication lines and other accompanying equipment, the costs of designing the project for the construction and reconstruction of this infrastructure belong to the investor of the public road if the person who, based on the separate law, is authorized to manage the construction of the public road for the existing building, has presented the documentation based on the rules governing the construction.

Otherwise, the costs are borne by the person who, based on the separate law, is authorized to manage that construction.

#### Article 18

Exceptional transport

\1. Natural person, natural person exercising individual business and legal person who exercises the exceptional transport activity are obliged to be equipped with a permit for exceptional transport, which shall be issued by the Ministry.

\2. All heavy vehicles with a gross weight greater than forty (40) tons must be equipped with a permit for exceptional transport, except for ISO standard containers up to forty-two (42)

tons with five (5) axles or forty-four (44) tons with six (6) axles.

\3. All transport vehicles, which exceed the permitted weight: length, width, height, total weight and axle weight, must be equipped with a permit for exceptional transport:

3.1. maximum length allowed eighteen point seventy-five (18.75) meters;

3.2. the largest width allowed two point sixty (2.60) meters;

3.3. maximum height allowed four (4.00) meters;

3.4. the maximum weight allowed is forty (40) tons.

\4. Authorized officials can use equipment for weight control, but if necessary they can also use equipment for measuring weights in licensed terminals:

4.1. if the transport vehicle has not exceeded the weight allowed according to this law, the transporter is released from payment;

4.2. if the weight is exceeded by more than ten (10%) percent of the allowed weight, the vehicle leaves the traffic, until the weight allowed by the law is adjusted;

4.3. the removal of the vehicle from traffic is made in the nearest parking lot from the place of measurement verification.

#### Article 19

Access on public road

\1. The natural person, natural person exercising an individual business and the legal person can construct and use a connection to a public road with the approval of the Agency.

\2. The conditions, criteria and procedures for granting consent, suspension and revocation of the permit for construction and use of a connection for roads under the responsibility of a Ministry shall be determined by the Ministry by a sub-legal act, while for roads under the responsibility of Municipalities shall be determined by the municipality by sub-legal act.

\3. Crossing public roads between themselves, as well as between public roads and noncategorized roads, is not considered as access to public roads.

\4. Access to the highway, including the junction roads of the highway, is prohibited.

\5. Persons who make a connection to the public road in violation of the provisions of this law, as well as persons who use a connection made in violation of the provisions of this law, have no right on any compensation for any damage that may be caused by the use of this connection, from the Agency.

\6. In case of construction of the junction without consent, the Agency, has the right to demolish any construction or structure in the road belt or public road belt protection, as well as to demand compensation for the costs of the demolition of the junction built without consent.

\7. The objects built in the protective belt road zone are treated according to the relevant Law on the treatment of illegal constructions.

#### Article 20

Adjustment of public road access

\1. If the access to public roads due to increased traffic, or use for other traffic, than that which was taken into account when granting consent for its construction is not relevant, then the Agency will ask for the adjustment of the road connection, by changing the reconstruction conditions.

\2. The costs of the suitability of the connection are borne by the authorized person of the right of construction, or the owner who is connected to the public road.

#### Article 22

Displacement of public road

\1. If due to the construction of other objects of public interest, railway infrastructure, mines, quarries, accumulative lakes, airports, and the like, the existing public road or part of it should be moved, the same or its part, which will be relocated, must be designed and built with at least the same technical elements before the relocation.

\2. The decision on allowing the relocation of a public road, defined in paragraph 1. of this Article shall be issued by the Ministry for the roads under its responsibility, respectively the municipality for the roads under its responsibility.

\3. The costs of the relocation of the public road, respectively of its part, shall be borne by the investor of the object, who requests the relocation of the public road, respectively of its part, unless otherwise provided by agreement.

#### Article 23

Protection belt of a public road

\1. In order to prevent the harmful effects of violation on the space along the road and the traffic on it, there will be a protective zone along these roads, in which the use of the space is limited.

\2. In the space defined for the road protection belt, no mine can be opened, no artificial lakes can be built, or industrial facilities, factories and similar construction structures cannot be built.

\3. The protective belt in the sense of paragraph 2. of this Article is measured from the outer edge of the road belt, so that on both sides it has the width for:

3.1. highway, one hundred (100) meters;

3.2. national road, sixty (60) meters;

3.3. regional and connecting roads, forty (40) meters;

3.4. local road thirty (30) meters.

\4. Buildings, structures, residences, offices, wells, septic tanks, electric poles and similar installations cannot be built in the space designated for road protection belt.

\5. The protective belt in the sense of paragraph 4. of this Article is measured from the outer edge of the road belt, so that on both sides it has the width for:
