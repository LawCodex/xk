# LAW NO. 08/L-261 ON PROTECTION FROM FIRE

### LAW NO. 08/L-261 ON PROTECTION FROM FIRE

### LAW NO. 08/L-261

### ON PROTECTION FROM FIRE

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON PROTECTION FROM FIRE

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law shall regulate the organisation, functioning and shall define the duties and responsibilities of the system and mechanisms of protection from fire, including explosions, with the purpose of protecting the lives of humans, animals, the property and the environment.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this law shall apply to all institutions of the Republic of Kosovo, as well as natural and legal entities, whose activity is related to protection from fire.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The expressions, terms and abbreviations used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Ministry – shall mean the Ministry responsible for Internal Affairs;

1.2. Minister – shall mean the Minister of the Ministry responsible for Internal Affairs;

1.3. EMA – shall mean the Emergency Management Agency;

1.4. Protection from fire – shall mean the entirety of measures and actions undertaken to prevent the cause of fire or mitigate the consequences of fire;

1.5. Inspector – shall mean the fire protection inspector within EMA, authorized to oversee the implementation of measures of protection from fire;

1.6. Property – shall mean immovable and movable property, owned by a natural or legal entity, as defined by the relevant Law on Property and Other Real Rights;

1.7. Owner – shall mean the natural or legal person owning the property;

1.8. User – shall mean the natural or legal entity that legally uses the property;

1.9. Categorisation – shall mean the classification of facilities and parts of facilities based on the scale of fire hazard;

1.10. National Fire Protection Plan – shall mean the determination of duties, action zones as well as areas of responsibility and activity for protection from fire at country level;

1.11. Municipal Fire Protection Plan – shall mean the determination of duties, action zones as well as areas of responsibility and activity for protection from fire at municipal plan;

1.12. Risk assessment – shall mean the determination of the scale of fire hazard in a specific location or site, as well as the appropriate protection measures;

1.13. Facilities – shall mean the buildings and parts of buildings as immoveable property;

1.14. Environment – shall mean the natural surroundings, air, earth, water, flora and fauna in the entirety of interaction and cultural heritage as part of the surroundings built by humans;

1.15. State institutions – shall mean the relevant competent institutions of the central and local level for the implementation of fire protection measures;

1.16. Fire – shall mean the self-supporting process of combustion that spreads uncontrollably in space;

1.17. Combustion – shall mean the rapid chemical reaction of a substance with an oxidant, most commonly oxygen from the air, in which combustion products are formed and heat, flame, and light are released;

1.18. Technological explosion – shall mean the sudden spread of gases due to combustion or other chemical reactions;

1.19. Fire resistance – shall mean the resistance of facilities to fire over time, meeting the required load capacities, entirety, thermal insulation and other expected properties, as defined by the standards of resistance of materials to fire;

1.20. Reaction to fire – shall mean the material which contributes to the spread of fire due to its decomposition, which occurs by exposing that material to certain test conditions;

1.21. Direct risk – shall mean the state of high fire risk, which at any time can cause an immediate fire;

1.22. Facility evacuation route – shall mean the special route designed and built in the facility that leads from any point inside the facility to the outer space or safe space in the facility, for the purpose of evacuating persons involved in the fire and their safe removal;

1.23. Fire protection system – shall mean the planning, determination and implementation of fire protection measures for facilities, the organization of fire protection entities, early detection, notification of the facility user about the explosion, prevention of the spread of fire and smoke in the facility, the effectiveness of fire extinguishing efforts, the funding of fire protection, training and licensing for the performance of fire protection activities of life and health of people, animals, material goods, the environment and nature with an acceptable fire risk

1.24. Occupancy certificate - shall mean a document issued in accordance with the relevant Law on construction in order to indicate that a construction is suitable for occupancy.

### CHAPTER II

### ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION FROM FIRE

#### Article 4

Responsibilities of the Ministry

\1. The Ministry shall be responsible for:

1.1. planning, organizing and implementing fire protection measures;

1.2. analysing the fire protection situation and undertaking measures for its improvement;

1.3. proposing the annual fire protection programme which is adopted by the Government of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.4. proposing the National Fire Protection Plan which shall be approved by the Government of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.5. drafting training and professional development programmes of the fire protection staff.

\2. In order to implement sub-paragraphs 1.4 and 1.5 of this Article, the Minister shall issue a sub-legal act.

#### Article 5

Responsibilities of municipalities

\1. Municipalities shall be responsible for:

1.1. issuing the Fire Protection Plan at municipal level based on the fire risk assessment, in line with the National Fire Protection Plan;

1.2. implementing and improving fire protection measures and their advancement;

1.3. at least once a year, reviewing the Fire Protection Plan.

#### Article 6

Responsibilities of the Emergency Management Agency

\1. The Emergency Management Agency shall be responsible for:

1.1. emergency management, according to its mandate;

1.2. drafting the Fire Risk Assessment Document;

1.3. drafting the National Fire Protection Plan;

1.4. drafting the Annual Fire Protection Programme;

1.5. overseeing all activities defined by the provisions of this law;

1.6. drafting and adopting instructions on all fire protection activities;

1.7. assessing compliance of the Municipal Fire Protection Plan with the provisions of this law and other acts in force.

### CHAPTER III

### CATEGORISATION OF FACILITIES AND FIRE PROTECTION MEASURES

#### Article 7

Categorisation of facilities

\1. Ministry shall categorise facilities and parts of facilities according to fire risk depending on the technological process taking place in them, types and quantities of materials produced, processed or stored, types of materials used for the construction of the facility, importance and size of the facility.

\2. Facilities and parts of facilities according to fire hazard shall be categorised as follows:

2.1. Category I, which includes facilities and parts of facilities depending on the technological process taking place in them;

2.2. Category II, which includes facilities and parts of facilities according to the type of material produced, processed or stored;

2.3. Category III, which includes facilities and parts of facilities according to the type of material composition; and

2.4. Category IV, which includes facilities and parts of facilities according to their relevance.

\3. Categorisation and classification of facilities and parts of facilities in the relevant fire hazard categories as referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article shall be defined by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

\4. Notwithstanding the definitions of this Article, the categorisation of military facilities shall be done by the Ministry of Defence.

#### Article 8

Fire protection measures by categories

\1. Owners or users of facilities, parts of facilities shall be obliged to undertake fire protection measures as defined by this law and the sub-legal acts issued based on this law and in accordance with the Fire Protection Plan and evacuation plan.

\2. Owners or users of facilities, parts of facilities shall be obliged to undertake the following measures:

2.1. For Category I of hazard, they shall be obliged to:

2.1.1. Draft the Fire Protection Plan and Evacuation Plan based on the assessment of hazard;

2.1.2. establish fire protection units with the required staff;

2.1.3. ensure the necessary tools and equipment for fire protection;

2.1.4. ensure the implementation of fire protection measures.

2.2. For Category II of fire hazard, they shall be obliged to:

2.2.1. draft the Fire Protection Plan and Evacuation Plan based on the assessment of hazard;

2.2.2. have a sufficient number of staffs that is trained on fire protection;

2.2.3. ensure the required tools and equipment for fire protection;

2.2.4. ensure employees will perform custody in fire extinguishing efforts;

2.2.5. ensure the implementation of fire protection measures.

2.3. For Category III of fire hazard, they shall be obliged to:

2.3.1. have at least one employee who is trained and directly organizes and ensures the implementation of fire protection measures;

2.3.2. have an evacuation plan;

2.3.3. ensure the implementation of fire protection measures.

2.4. For Category IV of fire hazard, they shall be obliged to:

2.4.1. have at least one employee who is trained and charged with fire protection works;

2.4.2. have an evacuation plan;

2.4.3. ensure the implementation of fire protection measures.

\3. Owners, respectively, users of facilities, parts of facilities categorised in the first and second category of hazard, shall develop the Fire Protection Plan in alignment with the Municipal Fire Protection Plan.

\4. Owners, respectively, users of facilities, parts of facilities who are responsible for fire protection activities shall hire persons who have received professional trainings on fire extinguishing or have completed technical education and have passed the professional fire protection examination.

\5. The Minister shall, by sub-legal act, define the criteria for the drafting and content of the Evacuation Plan according to this article.

#### Article 9

Protection measures

\1. The relevant institutions of the central and local level shall, when drafting spatial planning documents related to fire protection measures, comply with the provisions of this Law.

\2. Owners, respectively users of facilities, parts of facilities, depending on their conditions and needs, shall be obliged to align their internal acts on fire protection with the Fire Protection Plan and with the provisions of this law as well as to undertake measures and actions related to the implementation and advancement of fire protection.

#### Article 10

Special construction conditions for the purpose of fire protection

\1. For the purpose of meeting the special conditions for fire protection for facilities of category I and II according to the provisions of this law, EMA shall give:

1.1. Consent for fire protection in the location provided with the original project in order to obtain construction conditions;

1.2. Consent for fire protection in the main project for the determination of special fire protection conditions which are allowed in order to obtain a construction permit from the competent body.

#### Article 11

Measures provided for fire protection

\1. Legal entities registered for designs shall, for facilities in which special construction conditions are required, graphically show the calculated and explained fire protection measures in writing, that are provided for in the main project, in order to evaluate the fire protection measures.

\2. The consent for the provided fire protection measures shall be an integral part of the main project referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, for granting the construction permit.

\3. The legal entities registered for designs, which have drawn up the main project, shall carry out the verification of the same in the manner determined by their internal regulations.

\4. After the verification referred to in paragraph 3 of this Article, legal entities shall attach the statement where they confirm that the fire protection measures applied in the main projects are in compliance with this law, spatial regulation criteria and technical criteria and norms.

\5. When designing and building collective housing facilities, public facilities, shopping centres, hotels, cinemas, theatres, libraries, post offices, hospitals, sports halls, concert halls, as well as other public facilities, the conditions for the unobstructed evacuation of people in the event of a fire shall be ensured by building evacuation stairs, as well as with relevant fire alarm and extinguishing equipment.

\6. Legal entities registered for the designs referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall employ persons who have relevant professional training and have passed the professional exam in the field of fire protection.

#### Article 12

Condition for granting construction permit The body competent for granting construction or reconstruction permit may grant a construction permit only when it is ensured that the main construction project, on the basis of which the construction permit is granted, has provided for the fulfilment of special construction conditions for fire protection purposes as defined by Article 11 of this Law.

#### Article 13

Certificate of occupancy

\1. The certificate of occupancy for constructed or reconstructed facilities is granted when it is certified that the fire protection measures provided for in the main construction project have been implemented.

\2. The fire protection inspector, in cooperation with the relevant Construction Inspectorate, conducts the final inspection of the construction site, and the holder of the construction permit shall ensure unhindered access to the construction site.

\3. If, during the final inspection, it is confirmed that during the construction or reconstruction of the building, the fire protection measures provided for in the main construction project have not been implemented, the certificate of occupancy shall not be issued until the fire protection measures have been implemented.

#### Article 14

Fire resistant material

\1. During the design and construction of a facility and parts of a facility, protection from fire shall be ensured as one of the essential requirements for the facilities defined according to the categories regulating the field of spatial planning and construction, so that in the event of fire:

1.1. the capacity of the structure is preserved for a certain period of time;

1.2. the spread of fire and smoke within the facility and the part of the facility is prevented;

1.3. the spread of fire in the facility or parts of the neighbouring facilities is prevented;

1.4. the persons may leave the facility, or parts of the facility, without any harm; respectively their rescue is made possible;

1.5. protection of first responders is possible.

\2. The essential fire protection requirement is considered to be fulfilled if the facility meets the technical fire protection requirements and is in accordance with the environmental impact assessment. The technical fire protection requirements are determined by a sub-legal act approved by the Minister.

\3. The essential fire protection requirement shall also be verified according to calculation methods that are different from the verification method defined in paragraph 2 of this Article.

The calculation methods shall be defined by a sub-legal act approved by the Minister.

\4. The final works of the vertical and horizontal surfaces of the exits and exiting routes in the facilities shall be built of material with fire-resistant characteristics.

\5. For the construction material referred to in paragraph 4 of this Article, the performer of works is obliged to provide evidence for the characteristics of the fire-resistant material from the manufacturer, during construction, and provide the same for verification to the inspectors referred to in paragraph 2 of the Article 13 of this Law.

Owners and users of facilities, parts of facilities and the environment, shall be obliged to maintain in orderly condition the equipment, installations, gas ventilation, chimneys, as well as other equipment that pose a risk for the spread of fire, in line with technical norms and manufacturers’ instructions for which documentation should be available.

\2. In the event of the control referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, records shall be kept to note when the control was carried out, by whom and what was verified.

\3. Carrying and mobile apparatus for extinguishing fires shall be checked every six (6) months.

\4. Legal entities licensed to carry out controls shall have passed the relevant professional examination.

\5. EMA shall issue permit for the performance of activities referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 15 and paragraph 1 of this Article.

#### Article 17

Registration for production, circulation and import of equipment and appliances

\1. Legal entities should be registered with the EMA register to exercise the activity of production, circulation and import of equipment, appliances and tools dedicated to fire extinguishing, warning against and preventing the spread of fire and other protective equipment and installations, including protective and personal equipment of firefighters, which they use in extinguishing fires and other interventions.

\2. The equipment, tools and appliances referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, which are imported from abroad, may be put into circulation only if the importer has received the certificate of the manufacturer of the equipment, authorized on the regularity of those equipment, tools and appliances, as well as their adaptation for the intended purpose.

\3. For each imported equipment and for each imported quantity of parts of equipment or tools for fire extinguishing, alarming and preventing the spread of fire, the importer is obliged to provide the certificate provided for in paragraph 3 of this Article, unless otherwise regulated by international agreement.

\4. The conditions and criteria for production, circulation and import of equipment and appliances according to this article shall be defined by a sub-legal act issued by the minister.

#### Article 19

Firefighting custody

\1. In order to prevent and eliminate fire risk, firefighting custody should be ensured, i.e. alarm monitoring service as well as the relevant firefighting equipment and tools for the following cases:

1.1. in facilities, during public gatherings or other activities;

1.2. in open environments when there is an estimated or assumed risk for fire presence in such environments, during harvesting, in large construction sites, national parks, forests and the like.

\2. Firefighting custody, respectively the alarm monitoring service in cases referred to in paragraph 1 sub-paragraph 1.1 of this Article shall be the responsibility of the organizer.

\3. Firefighting custody, respectively the alarm monitoring service in cases referred to in paragraph 1 sub-paragraph 1.2 of this Article shall be ensured by the owner or user, if the owner of the above noted facilities cannot be verified.

#### Article 20

Firefighting custody in municipalities In municipalities where the firefighting custody is not organized, respectively the alarm monitoring service under Article 19 of this Law, if there is a possibility of occurrence of large scale fire, municipal authorities shall be required to organize such custody based on fire risk assessment.

#### Article 21

Technological processes in special facilities

\1. Technological processes in which fluids, flammable gases or explosive materials are produced may be performed only in the facilities or their parts which are separated from other production and storage parts, equipped with fire resistant barriers that prevent the spread of fire.

The technological processes referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article and the works with open fire near flammable fluids and gases, other explosive materials that can cause fire, should be organized in such a way that risk of fire is prevented, respectively reduced, depending on the working nature and conditions.

### CHAPTER V

#### Article 28

Competent bodies

\1. Supervision and inspection of the implementation of fire protection measures in facilities, parts of facilities, equipment and appliances in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo shall be performed by the fire protection inspectors within EMA.

\2. The relevant EMA inspectors, when necessary, shall perform inspection supervision for the implementation of fire protection measures in cooperation with other inspectors of relevant institutions in line with this law and the relevant Law on Inspections.

\3. Rules and procedures for inspection supervision of the implementation of fire protection measures referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be defined by a sub-legal act adopted by the Minister.

5.2. method and procedure of performing control works;

5.3. method of informing the competent body about emergencies over gaps found during the control;

5.4. conditions to be met by the persons for the performance of controls in terms of professional training.

#### Article 29

Fire protection inspectors at EMA

\1. Fire protection inspectors shall have the status of civil servants and their employment relationship shall be regulated by the relevant legislation on public officials and the relevant Law on Inspections.

\2. Fire protection inspectors, in addition to the general requirements defined by the relevant legislation, shall also have passed the professional examination.

#### Article 30

Rights and duties of the inspector

\1. During inspection supervision, inspectors shall check all facilities, part of the facilities, appliances, plants, and shall undertake other measures necessary for the verification and implementation of defined fire protection measures, as defined by this Law.

\2. During inspection supervision, inspectors shall have the right to enter residential buildings as requested by the residents in order to prevent a threat to the human lives and property.

\3. Owners, respectively users of facilities, should allow inspectors to perform inspection supervision, provide the required documentation and information.

\4. The inspector shall be obliged to provide their identity and authorization through the identification card during inspection supervision.

#### Article 31

Minutes kept by inspectors

\1. Inspectors shall keep minutes of the control performed on fire protection.

\2. A copy of the minutes shall be submitted to the owner, respectively the user of the facility or part of the facility.

\3. Fire protection inspectors shall be obliged to protect the classified information received during inspection in accordance with the relevant Law on Classified Information.

\4. If, during inspection supervision, the fire protection Inspector finds that the conditions from Article 16 of this Law have not been met, they shall terminate the use of such equipment and tools by a justified decision.

#### Article 33

Obligation of owners and users of facilities or parts of facilities

\1. In order to prevent the occurrence of fire, save people and property endangered by fire, owners, users of buildings or parts of buildings shall undertake certain fire protection measures, defined by this law, technical norms, fire protection plans and other legal acts as below:

1.1. maintain appliances, installations or equipment in an orderly condition;

1.2. reconstruct the facility or specific parts of the facility;

1.3. remove the waste from the facilities, which was left from the work process or is located in places prohibited by the relevant laws;

1.4. remove the substances from the facilities or the environment which present a risk of causing or spreading fire;

1.5. maintain all roads, including those in the forests, in order to enable the access of firefighters at any time;

1.6. install static, semi-static mobile installations, other relevant equipment and installations for fire alarm and extinguishing, as well as other protective equipment and installations;

1.7. provide necessary exits from facilities or parts of facilities to enable the unhindered evacuation of persons in case of fire;

1.8. prevent the approach of vehicles, which during work can throw sparks in places where there is a risk of fire;

1.9. keep the firefighting equipment and other firefighting tools in good order, or put those equipment and tools in a designated and visible place;

1.10. remove objects which during work can cause fire from appliances, installations and equipment;

1.11. remove flammable materials, which have been added to the structural elements of facilities or parts of facilities where horizontal and vertical road exists have been reconstructed, if those elements present a risk of causing and spreading fire;

1.12. prevent the throwing of objects from outside which could cause a fire in facilities with limited access;

1.13. ensure the specific quantity and type of technical equipment and fire alarming and extinguishing tools;

1.14. provide fire protection in facilities and parts of facilities based on the categorization of facilities and risk assessment.

\2. Due to the direct risk of fire, the fire protection inspector shall, by a decision, terminate:

2.1. storage of secondary materials, production material/raw material, final products and other materials;

2.2. use of open flame in closed and open objects;

2.3. use of facilities or parts of facilities, appliances, installations and other equipment if their adaptation/adjustment cannot eliminate the deficiencies that can cause fire;

2.4. use of equipment, installations and tools until measures are taken to avoid the risk;

2.5. carrying out certain works in objects or parts of objects, such as colouring, the flow of flammable fluids, gases, varnishes and the ignition of colours, until measures are taken to avoid the risk.

#### Article 35

### CHAPTER VII

### PUNITIVE MEASURES

#### Article 38

Fines

\1. Legal entity shall be fined from five hundred (500) to three thousand (3,000) Euros that:

1.1. does not possess the permit for the main project which also is not in compliance with the terms of spatial regulation under Article 12 of this Law;

1.2. performs the activities inconsistent with the provisions of Articles 13, 15 and 16 of this Law;

1.3. performs activities in controlling and servicing of fire protection apparatus contrary to the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 16 of this Law;

1.4. performs professional training and qualification of firefighters contrary to paragraph 1 of Article 36 of this Law.

\2. The responsible person of the legal entity shall be fined from three hundred (300) to five hundred (500) Euros for the offences referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article.

\3. The legal entity shall be fined from five hundred (500) to one thousand (1,000) Euros that:

3.1. fails to adopt and bring a regulation defining measures and works related to regulation and advancement of fire protection, or failure to implement measures of advancement of fire protection provided by Regulation on Protection from Fire under paragraph 2 of Article 9 of this Law;

3.2. performs works for processing the surfaces of horizontal and vertical exits and exiting routes in facilities and use of construction material that does not match the fire-resistant characteristics according to paragraph 4 of Article 14 of this Law;

3.3. fails to maintain appliances, electrical and gas equipment, ventilation and other installations, chimneys and other equipment in an orderly condition and lack of documentation for maintenance under paragraph 2 of Article 15 of this Law;

3.4. does not check the installations at least once a year by the authorized legal entity,

### CHAPTER VIII

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 40

Sub-legal acts The sub-legal acts defined by this Law shall be issued at latest one (1) year after the entry into force into force of this Law.

#### Article 41

Transitional provisions

\1. Legal entities shall align the fire protection regulations referred to in Article 9 of this law in a period of six (6) months after the day it enters into force.

\2. Persons referred to in Article 15, 16 and 18 of this law shall, within a period of six (6) months from the day when the relevant sub-legal acts are issued, align their activities with the provisions of these acts.

\3. The provisions of this law shall also apply to facilities, parts of facilities and the environment with special interest if they are not regulated by a special law in the field of fire protection.

\4. All permits issued until the day when this Law enters into force shall remain valid within the term for which they were issued.

\5. Legal entities that are authorized to carry out fire protection activities, after the entry into force of this law, within the term of one (1) year shall harmonize the permit document with the provisions of this law.

\6. New requests for permits after this law enters into force shall be reviewed in compliance with the provisions of this law.

#### Article 42

Abrogation Upon entry into force of this Law, the Law No. 04/L-012 on Fire Protection shall be abrogated.

#### Article 43

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
