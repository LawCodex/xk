# LAW NO. 08/L-284 ON THE ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF MUNICIPAL ACTS

### LAW NO. 08/L-284 ON THE ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF MUNICIPAL ACTS

### LAW NO. 08/L-284

### ON THE ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF MUNICIPAL ACTS

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo:

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF MUNICIPAL ACTS

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to create the legal basis for the administrative review of municipal acts to ensure that they are in accordance with the law and the Constitution.

#### Article 2

Scope This law is implemented by the municipalities, the ministries and any other body that has powers to review municipal acts.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The terms used in this Law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Ministry - means the ministry responsible for local self-government;

1.2. Municipal acts - means acts approved by the Municipal Assembly as well as acts issued by the Mayor;

1.3. Municipal bodies - means the Municipal Assembly and the Mayor;

1.4. UAD - means the Unit for Administration of the Documents in the Ministry.

\2. Other terms used in this law have the same meaning as in the relevant legislation for local self-government in Kosovo.

#### Article 4

Supervisory Bodies

\1. The supervisory body for reviewing the legality of municipal acts is:

1.1. Ministry responsible for local government;

1.2. Other line ministries; and

1.3. Other central level bodies when explicitly given such competence by law.

\2. The supervisory body for the review of suitability is the central level body which has delegated the powers to the municipalities.

#### Article 5

Request for Prior Consultation

\1. Municipal bodies, before issuing/approving an act, have the right to request preliminary consultation from the supervisory body to assess whether the act is in accordance with the legislation in force.

\2. Depending on the type of act, the request is made by the Chairman of the Assembly or the Mayor, to which is attached the proposal of the act that is intended to be issued and the specific issues for which prior consultation is necessary.

\3. The request for preliminary consultation is first sent to the Ministry responsible for local government, which, depending on the nature of the case, sends it to the ministry or the other body for the case, which prepares an opinion on the requested issue within thirty (30) days, giving an opinion containing the assessment of the act and instructions on how to proceed to ensure the legality.

\4. The process of preliminary consultation, coordination, form and content of the opinion and other details defined in this Article will be regulated by a sub-legal act proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government.

#### Article 6

Sending of the municipal acts

\1. Depending on the type of review, the Mayor is obliged to send to the Ministry the acts approved by the Municipal Assembly and the acts approved by the Mayor, together with the accompanying documents, within the deadlines set in this law.

\2. Acts with accompanying documents and the list of acts from the municipality are sent in official languages in accordance with the law on the use of official languages through the Municipality's Unit for the Administration of Documents.

\3. The rules defined in the relevant legislation for the administration of work in the office shall apply to the sending of acts from the municipalities to the ministry.

#### Article 7

Acceptance of municipal acts

\1. The acceptance of the municipalities` acts is done through the UAD in the ministry, which records and registers the acts and the list of acts of the municipality. After registering and recording, the acts and the list of acts shall be forwarded to the relevant unit for the assessment of legality.

\2. The acceptance of acts of the municipality is done in accordance with the rules defined in the relevant legislation for the administration of work in the office.

#### Article 8

Mandatory review of legality

\1. The mandatory review of legality means the review of acts of the Municipal Assembly in accordance with the relevant law on local self-government.

\2. Acts of the municipal assembly, which are subject to mandatory review of legality are:

2.1. Statutes;

2.2. Municipal regulations;

2.3. General decisions of the municipal assembly;

2.4. Agreements for inter-municipal and international municipal cooperation;

2.5. Documents of local spatial planning.

\3. Municipalities are obliged to send the acts for mandatory review of legality within seven

(7) days from the day of their approval.

\4. The acceptance of documents according to paragraph 2. of this Article is done physically, electronically and in any other suitable form by UAD of the Ministry responsible for Local Government, which issues a certificate of acceptance.

\5. Acts of the Municipal Assembly according to paragraph 1. of this Article, which are not sent by the Municipality within the deadline defined in paragraph 2. of this Article, shall be subject to a regular review of legality according to Article 10 of this law.

#### Article 9

The procedure for mandatory review of legality

\1. After the acceptance of the act that is the object of the mandatory review of legality, the Ministry conducts the review and, based on the nature and content of the act, defines which ministry or central level body is competent for the mandatory review of legality.

\2. If it cannot be defined which is the competent body or the competent body according to paragraph 1. of this Article refuses to review the act due to incompetence, the ministry creates an Ad Hoc commission of three (3) members to review the act for which it is carried out mandatory review of legality.

\3. The bodies according to paragraph 1. and 2. of this Article prepare the opinion for the mandatory review of legality within thirty (30) days from the date of acceptance, which can ascertain that the act:

3.1. is in accordance with the legislation in force;

3.2. is not in accordance with the legislation in force, thus giving instructions on which parts the act should be reviewed by the municipality in order to harmonize them with the law.

\4. If the municipality refuses to review the act within thirty (30) days according to the instructions given in sub-paragraph 3.1. of this Article, the supervisory body that conducts the mandatory review of legality shall file a lawsuit against the municipality in the competent Court.

\5. The details and form, content and correction of the opinion for the mandatory review of legality according to this Article are defined by a sub-legal act, proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government.

#### Article 10

Regular review of legality

\1. The regular review of legality means the review of the legality of the acts approved by the Municipal Assembly and the acts issued by the Mayor which have not been subject to the mandatory assessment of legality.

\2. In addition to the acts that are subject to mandatory review of legality, the Mayor sends to the Ministry the list of acts approved by the Assembly and the Mayor, from the previous month until the 10th of each month for the purpose of regular review of legality.

\3. The Ministry has the right to choose from the list which of the acts will be subject to regular review of legality based on the importance, nature and scope of the act subject to the review process.

\4. The Ministry has the right to ask the Mayor to send each act and accompanying documents of the acts selected from the list according to paragraph 2. of this Article for review of legality.

\5. For regular review of legality, the rules for mandatory review of legality defined in Article 8 and 9 of this law apply accordingly.

\6. The deadline for reviewing the legality of the act is thirty (30) days from the day of acceptance.

\7. The procedure for regular review of the legality of the acts will be regulated by a sub-legal act proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government.

#### Article 11

Review of Expediency

\1. Review of suitability means review of the legality and suitability of municipal acts adopted for the implementation of delegated powers in accordance with the law.

\2. The supervisory authority responsible for exercising review over the delegated competencies shall have the right to request, within thirty (30) days of reception of a copy of an act, that the municipal body modify or repeal the contested act on the basis of expediency considerations.

\3. If the municipal body fails to act accordingly, the supervisory body responsible for exercising review over the expediency may modify, replace the act or suspend the execution of the municipal acts.

\4. The supervisory authority shall inform the municipal body of such decision within five (5)

days from the date of adoption of the decision.

#### Article 12

Complaints against administrative acts

\1. An appeal is allowed against each administrative act issued by the municipality, which is exercised in accordance with the rules defined by the relevant law for the general administrative procedure.

\2. Unless otherwise specified by law, the superior body for complaints against administrative acts issued on the basis of the municipality's own and extended powers is the complaints commission established by the Mayor of the Municipality.

\3. Unless otherwise defined by the law, the superior body for complaints against administrative acts issued on the basis of delegated powers is the complaints commission established by the central public body that has delegated the power.

#### Article 13

Responsible for coordination and reporting

\1. The Ministry is responsible for the coordination and annual reporting related to the supervision of the legality of the acts of the municipal bodies.

\2. The line Ministries and other bodies responsible for reviewing municipal acts inform the Ministry, on annual basis, regarding the evaluation of the legality of acts from their field.

\3. The Ministry submits the annual report regarding the evaluation of municipal acts to the Government

\4. The report is accessible to the public through the website of the Ministry.

\5. The method of coordination between the Ministry, line ministries and other bodies responsible for reviewing municipal acts and the content of the report is defined by a by-law, proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government.

#### Article 14

By-laws The by-laws required by this law will be issued no later than six (6) months after the entry into force of this law.

#### Article 15

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the republic of Kosovo.
