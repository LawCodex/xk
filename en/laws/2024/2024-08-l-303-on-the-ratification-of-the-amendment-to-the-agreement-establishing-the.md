# LAW NO. 08/L -303 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT ESTABLISHING THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ("THE AGREEMENT") - ARTICLE 1

### LAW NO. 08/L-303 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT ESTABLISHING THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR

### RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ("THE AGREEMENT") - ARTICLE 1

### LAW NO. 08/L-303

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT ESTABLISHING THE

### EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ("THE AGREEMENT") ARTICLE 1

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT ESTABLISHING THE

### EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ("THE AGREEMENT") ARTICLE 1

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Amendment to the Agreement Establishing the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development ("Agreement") - Article 1, signed on December 13, 2012.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement Through this Law, the Amendment to the Agreement Establishing of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development ("Agreement") - Article 1 shall be ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Amendment to the Agreement on the Ratification of the Agreement on the Establishment of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development ("Agreement") - Article 1 is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-303 23 May 2024 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-69/2024 dated 06.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
