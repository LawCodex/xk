# LAW NO. 08/L-299 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE FERIZAJ WASTEWATER TREATMENT PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-299 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK

### FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE FERIZAJ WASTEWATER TREATMENT PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-299

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE FERIZAJ

### WASTEWATER TREATMENT PROJECT

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo,

Adopts:

### LAW RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE FERIZAJ

### WASTEWATER TREATMENT PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement for the Ferizaj Wastewater Treatment Project between Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. The purpose of the Project is to finance the construction of a new wastewater treatment plan, the expansion of the sewerage system in the Ferizaj region, and the purchase of equipment and supplies for the maintenance of the sewerage system.

\2. The project is located in the Ferizaj region of Kosovo and will serve to the Municipalities of Ferizaj and Kaçanik.

\3. The project consists of the following components:

3.1 Part A: Works, supplies and services for the construction of the new wastewater treatment plant and the expansion of the sewerage system in the Ferizaj region, serving a population of approximately 90,000 inhabitants with an expected average capacity of up to 17,500 m3/day; and

3.2 Part B: Consultancy services for the assistance and supervision of the Project Implementation Unit.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law there is attached the Loan Agreement for the Ferizaj Wastewater Treatment Project between Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
