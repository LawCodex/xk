### LAW NO. 08/L-305

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Adopts:

### LAW ON THE AIR NAVIGATION SERVICES PROVIDER

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. The purpose of this Law is to regulate the status, the way of organization, the competences of the Air Navigation Services Provider, as an independent state legal entity in the service of the public interest, to ensure the infrastructure and services of air navigation in the airspace of Republic of Kosovo.

\2. This Law is in compliance with the EU legislation related to the air navigation services.

#### Article 2

Scope This Law shall apply to every aircraft operating in the general air traffic in the territory and airspace of the Republic of Kosovo or other airspace the Republic of Kosovo is responsible for in providing the air navigation control services.

### CHAPTER II

### AIR NAVIGATION SERVICES PROVIDER

#### Article 4

Organizational status and structure

\1. Air Navigation Services Provider "KANS" is an independent legal entity serving the public interest, which will provide safe operations of air navigation services.

\2. KANS shall have a full operational and administrative autonomy and operates outside the legal framework of the state administration and public officials, and it shall not be considered a budget organization in the sense of the relevant Law on Public Financial Management and Accountability and the relevant Law on the Budget of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. KANS will act in accordance with policies, legislation, standards and international agreements applicable to civil aviation in the Republic of Kosovo, as well as in accordance with ICAO standards, practices and recommendations on air navigation services.

\4. KANS shall operate as a not-for-profit, cost-covering, performance-based service provider.

\5. KANS shall have its own bank account, in banks established in Kosovo as well as abroad based on the obligations derived from international institutions and organizations.

\6. The organizational structure of KANS shall be determined by the Regulation on internal organization, which shall be approved by the Supervisory Council.

### LAW NO. 08/L-305 ON THE AIR NAVIGATION SERVICES PROVIDER

#### Article 5

Separation of powers

\1. The CAA is the competent constitutional authority to regulate the activity of civil aviation in the Republic of Kosovo and the provisions of air navigation services, as well as it is independent from air navigation service providers as defined by the relevant Law on Civil Aviation.

\2. KANS shall operate and shall be organized separately from CAA and shall be authorized to provide air navigation services in the airspace of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. The CAA is the competent authority to represent the Republic of Kosovo in matters of air navigation related to the expression of sovereign interests and other commitments at the state level, while KANS is authorized to manage relations with international organizations and other service providers for all operational and technical matters.

\4. KANS shall be certified by CAA for the provision of air navigation services within the Republic of Kosovo and other airspace for which the Republic of Kosovo has the responsibility in providing air navigation services.

\5. KANS shall be responsible and accountable to CAA for the issues stipulated by the relevant Law on Civil Aviation.

\6. Unless otherwise specified by CAA, KANS shall act in accordance with ICAO SARPs, HPEA and EASA, regarding air navigation services.

#### Article 6

Rules and standards

\1. Civil aviation activities in the airspace of the Republic of Kosovo shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the relevant Law on Civil Aviation, the Chicago Convention and the Agreement on the Establishment of the European Common Aviation Area.

\2. Every legal provision arising from the Agreement on the Establishment of the European Common Aviation Area shall be directly applicable and shall prevail over any legal provision of the Republic of Kosovo, in cases when it is not in compliance with it.

#### Article 7

Duties and responsibilities of KANS

\1. Unless otherwise provided by contract or international agreement, KANS will be responsible for:

1.1. provision of air traffic control services;

1.2. air traffic and airspace flow management in cooperation with NATO;

1.3. communication, navigation and surveillance services;

1.4. aeronautical information services;

1.5. aeronautical meteorological service;

1.6. purchase, installation, maintenance, operation and modernization of communication, navigation, airspace surveillance systems as well as the required navigation aids;

1.7. KANS and airport operators or companies that manage and operate airports in the Republic of Kosovo will enter into a Memorandum of Understanding stating the ownership, responsibility, maintenance and operation of such equipment. The Memorandum of Understanding will be reviewed as necessary in order to ensure continued relevance and effectiveness;

1.8. providing training and advice related to air navigation;

1.9. carrying out research and developments related to air navigation;

1.10. developing methods to accelerate air traffic control modernization and improving aviation safety related to air traffic control;

1.11. acting in accordance with the standards set by the CAA and react quickly to findings during supervision by the CAA;

1.12. acting in accordance with the standards set by the Ministry of Internal Affairs for the field of civil aviation security and to react quickly to the findings during supervision by the Ministry of Internal Affairs;

1.13. consultation with users of services, such as airlines, cargo carriers, manufacturers, airports, general aviation and focus on achieving results that meet the needs of users of services provided by KANS;

1.14. consultation and cooperation with operators or companies that manage and operate airports in the Republic of Kosovo, with the CAA, the Ministry and NATO to determine and implement the best practices for meeting the various needs in the entire airspace of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.15. formulating and recommending to CAA any regulatory changes necessary for KANS, in order to achieve performance goals and provide security at an appropriate level;

1.16. creation of the security management system in KANS;

1.17. providing advice to the Government regarding the development of policies proposed by the Government for the use of the airspace of the Republic of Kosovo to meet the needs of users, taking into account national security, economic factors, the environment and the need for a high standard of safety;

1.18. performs other functions related to civil aviation in the Republic of Kosovo which are assigned to it by this Law or by any other normative act.

\2. KANS coordinates search and rescue efforts with the Ministry, the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Minister of Defense and closely cooperates with the Commission for the Investigation of Aeronautical Accidents and Incidents.

\3. KANS will provide safe, continuous and effective services through the performance of tasks as an air navigation service provider, airspace management and air traffic flow management, in accordance with the relevant Law on Civil Aviation, SPN and with the Regulations issued pursuant to the Agreement on the Establishment of the European Common Aviation Area.

#### Article 8

Relations with other service providers

\1. KANS shall be authorized to negotiate agreements with other providers of air navigation services certified under European Union legislation, to enable the implementation of the Single European Sky Agreement. Such agreements will be subject to approval by the CAA, as determined by the relevant legislation.

\2. Based on the contracts and agreements for air navigation services provided by KANS, the competences or activity can be provided in the airspace beyond the borders of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 9

Geographical definition and scope

\1. KANS shall coordinate and cooperate with the appropriate NATO services and units, services, other national units and take the necessary steps to ensure the implementation of written agreements between KANS and NATO authorities or equivalent legal agreements regarding the management of specific airspace blocks.

\2. Nothing in this Law shall prevent the development and implementation, in the future, of flexible airspace utilization and airspace management procedures together with the relevant NATO authority dealing with the air navigation service.

### CHAPTER III

### SUPERVISORY COUNCIL

#### Article 10

Composition and resnponsibility of the Supervisory Council

\1. The Supervisory Council of KANS shall be responsible for the general supervision of KANS in accordance with its mandate provided by this Law.

\2. The Supervisory Council consists of three (3) members as follows:

2.1. Minister of the relevant ministry for transport, chairman;

2.2. Minister of the relevant ministry for finance, member; and

2.3. Director General of KANS, member.

\3. The chairman shall convene, chair and ensure the regular progress of the meetings of the Supervisory Council.

#### Article 11

Powers of the Supervisory Council

\1. Supervisory Council:

1.1. appoints and dismisses the Director and Deputy Directors;

1.2. approves the internal Regulations of KANS;

1.3. approves the one (1) and five (5) year business plan of KANS;

1.4. approves the Annual Work Plan;

1.5. approves the annual budget, annual report and annual financial statements.

\2. The Supervisory Council for the activity of KANS shall be responsible to the Government.

#### Article 12

Meetings of the Supervisory Council

\1. Meetings of the Supervisory Council are chaired by the Chairman, or in his absence by one member delegated by the Chairman.

\2. The Supervisory Council meets as many times as necessary for the activity of KANS, but not less than twice per year.

\3. Meetings of the Supervisory Council are convened by the Chairman, who sets the agenda according to the proposals of the Council.

\4. Meetings can also be convened by a written request of any member of the Supervisory Council.

\5. Each member of the KANS Supervisory Council has one (1) vote. In case of equal votes, the vote of the Chairman will be decisive. In case of appointment and dismissal of the Director, the Director is excluded from voting.

\6. Except for the cases defined by this Law, the decisions of the Supervisory Council are taken by a simple majority of the members present. Meetings and voting by teleconference and other forms are allowed.

#### Article 22

Ownership of KANS infrastructure

\1. KANS infrastructure will be owned by KANS.

#### Article 23

Usage fees

\1. The fees for the services provided by KANS will include:

1.1. approach fees for the provision of air navigation service within the airspace of Kosovo; and

1.2. overflight fees for aircrafts that are authorized to use the airspace of Kosovo.

\2. The CAA shall adjust the fees in accordance with the relevant Law on Civil Aviation.

\3. KANS shall propose the usage fees for the air navigation services provided, in order that the fees to be as competitive as possible with similar fees imposed by other countries in the European Common Aviation Area and calculated on the basis of reasonable returns of expenses taking into account all other sources of income.

\4. CAA shall examine and approve the fees proposed by KANS in accordance with the relevant Law on Civil Aviation and shall inform the Ministry about the fees setting.

\5. The usage fees are mandatory for the aircraft operator. If the operator of the aircraft is unknown, the obligation to pay the usage fee falls on the owner of the aircraft.

\6. The usage fee regime will be determined by legislation for the creation of a common fee scheme for air navigation services and with its amendments and supplementations. The norms defined in this way ensure that there is no discrimination between different users.

\7. KANS may refuse to provide air navigation services to an airspace user that violates the terms of payment except for emergency landing cases.

#### Article 24

Flights exempt from usage fees

\1. Flights with visual flight rules and aircrafts used by the state such as military, customs and police service should be exempted from operating fees.

#### Article 25

\4. The provisions of the relevant Law on Public Procurement will be applied against decisions related to a suspected violation of the relevant Law on Public Procurement.

\3. In the transitional phase, after the entry into force of this Law, KANS will receive financial support, for capital projects, from the Government of the Republic of Kosovo for a period of three (3) years.

\4. In the event that this Law is interpreted inconsistently with the relevant Law on Civil Aviation, the relevant Law on Civil Aviation shall prevail.

\5. The entry into force of this Law does not change the authority given to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization NATO regarding the airspace of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 30

Sub-legal acts

\1. Sub-legal acts for the implementation of this Law shall be issued within one (1) year from the entry into force of this Law.

\2. Sub-legal acts that are into force shall continue to be applied until the issuance of new acts, provided that they are not in contradiction with this Law.

#### Article 31

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
