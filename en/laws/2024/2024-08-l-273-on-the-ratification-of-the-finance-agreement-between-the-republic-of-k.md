# LAW NO. 08/L-273 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOR AND TRANSFERS, KOSOVO ENERGY CORPORATION (KEK), AND KFW, FRANKFURT AM MAIN, FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR ENERGY PROGRAM I (SOLAR4KOSOVO I)

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOR AND TRANSFERS,

### KOSOVO ENERGY CORPORATION (KEK), AND KFW, FRANKFURT AM MAIN, FOR THE

### PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR ENERGY PROGRAM I (SOLAR4KOSOVO I)

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the Finance Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and KfW, Frankfurt am Main (“KfW”), for the Project “Solar Energy Program I (Solar4Kosovo I)" – Photovoltaic”.

#### Article 2

Attached documents Attached to the Annex of this Law is the text of the Finance Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and KfW, Frankfurt am Main (“KfW”), for the Project “Solar Energy Program I (Solar4Kosovo I) – Photovoltaic”.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-273 16 May 2024

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-68/2024 dated 06.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
