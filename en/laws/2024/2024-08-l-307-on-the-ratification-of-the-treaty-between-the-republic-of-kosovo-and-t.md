# LAW NO. 08/L-307 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF DENMARK ON THE USE OF THE CORRECTIONAL FACILITY IN GJILAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF THE EXECUTION OF DANISH SENTENCES

### LAW NO. 08/L-307 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF DENMARK ON

### THE USE OF THE CORRECTIONAL FACILITY IN GJILAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF THE EXECUTION OF DANISH SENTENCES

### LAW NO. 08/L-307

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### KINGDOM OF DENMARK ON THE USE OF THE CORRECTIONAL FACILITY IN GJILAN FOR THE

### PURPOSE OF THE EXECUTION OF DANISH SENTENCES

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### KINGDOM OF DENMARK ON THE USE OF THE CORRECTIONAL FACILITY IN GJILAN FOR THE

### PURPOSE OF THE EXECUTION OF DANISH SENTENCES

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Treaty between the Republic of Kosovo and the Kingdom of Denmark for the use of the Correctional Facility in Gjilan for the purpose of the execution of Danish sentences, signed by th th the Ministers of Justice of both countries, on April 26 , 2022, in Prishtina and May 16 in Copenhagen.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Treaty between the Republic of Kosovo and the Kingdom of Denmark on the use of the Correctional Facility in Gjilan for the purpose of the execution of Danish sentences is attached as Annex to this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-307 23 May 2024

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-64/2024 dated 06.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
