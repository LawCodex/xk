# LAW NO. 08/L-308 FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PUBLIC FINANCE AND ECONOMIC GROWTH PROGRAM BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE OPEC FUND FOR INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

### LAW NO. 08/L-308 FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PUBLIC FINANCE AND ECONOMIC GROWTH

### PROGRAM BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE OPEC FUND FOR INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

### LAW NO. 08/L-308

### FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PUBLIC FINANCE AND

### ECONOMIC GROWTH PROGRAM BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE OPEC

### FUND FOR INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PUBLIC FINANCE AND

### ECONOMIC GROWTH PROGRAM BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE OPEC

### FUND FOR INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement for the Public Finances and Economic Growth Program between the Republic of Kosovo and the OPEC International Development Fund.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Loan Agreement for the Public Finance and Economic Growth Program between the Republic of Kosovo and the OPEC Fund for International Development is attached to the Appendix of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-308 23 May 2024

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-76/2024 dated 11.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
