# LAW NO. 08/L-253 ON THE ACCESSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO TO THE CONVENTION ON TEMPORARY ADMISSION

### LAW NO. 08/L-253 ON THE ACCESSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO TO THE CONVENTION ON TEMPORARY ADMISSION

### LAW NO. 08/L-253

### ON THE ACCESSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO TO THE CONVENTION ON TEMPORARY

### ADMISSION

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) and (4) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON THE ACCESSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO TO THE CONVENTION ON

### TEMPORARY ADMISSION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is the accession of the Republic of Kosovo to the Convention on Temporary Admission done in Istanbul on June 26, 1990, amended in Brussels on July 26, 2010, May 15, 2014 and November 29, 2016.

#### Article 2

Scope The scope of this Law is the ratification of the Istanbul Convention on Temporary Admission done in Istanbul on June 26, 1990, through which uniform provisions are provided, ensuring at a high level the simplification and harmonization of customs procedures for goods and means of transport that use the temporary importation procedure.

#### Article 3

Scope of application This Convention applies to all state administration bodies that undertake to allow temporary importation, in accordance with the provisions of this Convention, for goods, including means of transport, specified in the appendices of this Convention.

#### Article 4

Attached documents The text of the Convention on Temporary Admission and its appendices translated from English to Albanian and Serbian are attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 5

Law enforcement For the implementation of this Law according to the proposal of the Customs, the relevant Minister for customs affairs may approve sub-legal acts necessary for the purpose of implementing the provisions of the Convention.

### LAW NO. 08/L-253 ON THE ACCESSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO TO THE CONVENTION ON TEMPORARY ADMISSION

#### Article 6

Transitional Provisions Sub-legal acts that were approved before the entry into force of this Law, which affect the scope of this Law, are valid to the extent that they are not in conflict with this Law.

#### Article 7

Entry into Force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-253 16 May 2024
