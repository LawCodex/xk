# LAW NO. 08/L-259 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, ESTABLISHED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS, AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE “STRENGTHENING DIGITAL GOVERNANCE FOR SERVICE DELIVERY PROJECT”

### LAW NO. 08/L-259 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO,, ESTABLISHED BYY

### THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE,, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS, AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE

### “STRENGTHENING DIGITAL GOVERNANCE FOR SERVICE DELIVERY PROJECT”

### LAW NO. 08/L-259

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO,

### ESTABLISHED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS, AND

### INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE “STRENGTHENING DIGITAL

### GOVERNANCE FOR SERVICE DELIVERY PROJECT”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO, ESTABLISHED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS, AND

### INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE “STRENGTHENING DIGITAL

### GOVERNANCE FOR SERVICE DELIVERY PROJECT”

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, established by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and Transfers, and the International Development Association for the “Strengthening Digital Governance for Service Delivery Project”.

#### Article 2

Attached documents Attached to the Appendix of this Law is the text of the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, established by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and Transfers, and the International Development Association for the “Strengthening Digital Governance for Service Delivery Project”.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-259 16 May 2024

Pursuant to the article 80, paragraph 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
