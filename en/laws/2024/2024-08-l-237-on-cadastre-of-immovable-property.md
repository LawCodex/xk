### LAW ON CADASTRE OF IMMOVABLE PROPERTY

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims at regulating the public service of registration of immovable properties in the cadastre, the administration of the cadastre and the organization of the responsible institution.

#### Article 2

Scope Provisions of this Law shall be implemented by central and local level institutions, natural and legal persons dealing with the cadastre of immovable properties and the registration of real rights.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. KCA - Kosovo Cadastral Agency;

1.2. MCO - Municipal Cadastral Office;

1.3. Surveyor - a natural person licensed to perform cadastral and surveying works;

1.4. Surveying Company - a legal entity licensed to perform cadastral and surveying works;

1.5. Immovable Property Rights Register (IPRR) - is a register of cadastral data on immovable properties throughout the territory of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.6. Cadastral zone - a bounded territorial unit, with a unique name and identification number in the territory of the municipality;

1.7. Cadastral unit - graphic and textual description of a parcel, building, part of a building and utility determined in the field. Each cadastral unit has a unique identification number as well as the attributes specified in this law;

1.8. Cadastral parcel - a marked out surface of land, with unique number, registered in the cadastre of immovable properties;

1.11. Utility - surface and subsurface network of public infrastructure with a unique number;

1.12. Division of a cadastral unit - the process of creating a cadastral unit, with division into two (2) or more units;

### CHAPTER II

### BASIC PRINCIPLES

#### Article 4

Principle of legality Cadastral activity and registration of immovable properties shall be exercised in compliance with the Constitution, legislation in force, as well as general administrative rules.

#### Article 5

Principle of registration

\1. The rights acquired by legal acts defined by the legislation in force on immovable property shall be registered in the Immovable Property Rights Register, hereinafter IPRR.

\2. The right acquired by a legal act shall be considered valid only after its registration in the IPRR.

#### Article 6

Principle of publicity

\1. Natural and legal persons shall have the right to access the data of the cadastre of immovable properties available at the Kosovo Cadastral Agency in the following text KCA.

\2. The data of the cadastre of immovable properties shall be public and shall be used under the conditions defined by the legislation in force.

#### Article 7

Principle of reliability The data on immovable properties and rights over them, registered in the IPRR shall be considered reliable.

#### Article 8

Principle of priority

\1. The registration of rights in the IPRR shall be performed according to the time order of submitting the application for registration.

\2. The legal effect of the registration against third parties shall commence from the moment of submitting the application for registration to the competent body for cadastre.

### ORGANIZATION OF THE CADASTRE

#### Article 9

Organizational structure

\1. Responsible institutions in the field of cadastre shall be organized in the following organizational structure:

1.1. Kosovo Cadastral Agency; and

1.2. Municipal Cadastral Offices as integral parts of KCA.

\2. The internal organization and systematization of jobs in the KCA and MCO shall be defined by a regulation approved by the Prime Minister.

3.5. creating and updating maps;

3.9. providing recommendations, orders for the elimination of irregularities found during the supervision;

3.10. prohibition of activities in the use of IPRR by officials who do not fulfil their obligations under this law, upon the recommendation of the immediate supervisor;

Creation of cadastral units

2.3. development and maintenance of the reference system of heights and other geodetic activities; as well as

2.4. maintenance of the state border line.

#### Article 27

Cadastral surveys

\1. Cadastral surveys are made for the purpose of collecting and processing cadastral data from the field for registration of a unit in the IPRR.

\2. There is no need for cadastral surveys if there are no changes for that unit, but only the registration of the property right in the IPRR.

\3. In cases when the registration of the property right is done through sale and purchase and it is ascertained that there is a difference in the surface of the cadastral unit between the ownership certificate and the copy of the plan, a cadastral survey should be made.

\4. Cadastral surveys from paragraph 3 of this Article are mandatory when the surface difference between the ownership certificate and the plan copy is beyond the technical tolerance.

\5. The new boundaries shall be marked with boundary signs.

\6. Boundary signs are common property of the owners of neighboring properties.

\7. Removal, restoration, concealment or damage of boundary marks and geodetic points in any way is prohibited.

\8. Ministry shall, by a sub-legal act, define the standard for the development, maintenance of networks, state surveys, cadastral surveys, the shape and the size of boundary signs and geodetic points.

#### Article 28

Geodetic surveys

\1. Geodetic surveys are conducted for the purpose of collecting and processing data spatial information from the field.

2.Geodetic surveys from paragraph 1 of this Article are used for engineering purposes.

#### Article 33

Review of MCOs decisions

\1. Within thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of the written decision of the MCO on the rejection of the application, the applicant may request from KCA to review the MCO decision.

\2. The appeal submitted from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be made through the MCO.

\3. KCA shall review the appeal for review of the MCO decision within thirty (30) days from the day of receipt of the claim.

\4. The review of appeals of MCO decisions shall be done by the Appeals Review Commission within the KCA.

\2. When the official ascertains that there is an error, he is obliged to correct it in the cadastre.

\3. Pertaining to errors which can not be corrected in the cadastre, the dissatisfied party may forward the case by a lawsuit to the competent court and request from the MCO to insert in that cadastral unit a note that the data on that property may be erroneous.

4.MCO shall decide on the correction of the error within fifteen (15) days.

#### Article 35

Re-registration of real rights in the immovable property

\1. When MCO finds that the applicant for registration proves with a valid document that he was registered in the cadastre, attaching the final legal act, the same application shall be considered as re-registration.

\2. Re-registration from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be done in the IPRR.

#### Article 36

Registration of a remark in the IPRR

\1. Any person who claims that the registration of the immovable property right in the IPRR is not accurate and that it violates his immovable property rights, may request from the MCO to register a remark in the IPRR based on his application.

\3. Ministry, by a sub-legal act, shall define the procedures for registration, modification and deletion of burdens and prohibitions.

#### Article 38

Reconstruction of cadastre

\1. Reconstruction of the Cadastre shall be done when:

1.2. cadastral data are not complete or are erroneous in the IPRR.

\3. Decision on commencement of RC pursuant to sub-paragraph 1.1. of this Article shall be based on the Reconstruction Plan which consists of the following information:

3.1. cadastral zone which will be reconstructed;

3.2. assessment of the current state of cadastral data and inconsistencies created by failure to update the cadastral information;

3.4. budget assessment.

\4. Decision on commencement of RC pursuant to sub-paragraph 1.2. of this Article shall be based on the Reconstruction Plan, which consists of the following information:

4.1. the cadastral zone in which the data in the cadastre will be enriched or corrected;
