# LAW NO. 08/L-292 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION PROGRAMME “JUSTICE”

### LAW NO. 08/L-292 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON THE

### PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION PROGRAMME “JUSTICE”

### LAW NO. 08/L-292

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION

### PROGRAMME “JUSTICE”

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo,

Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE EUROPEAN UNION

### PROGRAMME “JUSTICE”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in the EU Programme “Justice,” signed by the Justice Commissioner on behalf of the European Commission and the Minister of Justice on behalf of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, on 27.11.2023, in Brussels and on 14.12.2023 in Prishtina.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in the European Union Programme “Justice” and its annexes are attached to this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-292 16 May 2024

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-73/2024 dated 06.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
