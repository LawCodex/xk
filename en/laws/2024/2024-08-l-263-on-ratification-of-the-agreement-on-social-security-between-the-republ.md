# LAW NO. 08/L-263 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF NETHERLANDS

### LAW NO. 08/L-263 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### KINGDOM OF NETHERLANDS

### LAW NO. 08/L-263

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF NETHERLANDS

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF NETHERLANDS

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims at the ratification of the Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the former Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Kingdom of the Netherlands, represented by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, signed on 17.09.2020 in The Hague.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the former Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Kingdom of the Netherlands, represented by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, signed on 17.09.2020 in The Hague is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the former Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Kingdom of the Netherlands, represented by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, signed on 17.09.2020 in the Hague, is attached to this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
