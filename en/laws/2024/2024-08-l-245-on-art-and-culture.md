# LAW NO. 08/L-245 ON ART AND CULTURE

### ON ART AND CULTURE

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON ART AND CULTURE

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law shall define the general principles of cultural policies in the Republic of Kosovo and regulates the conditions for their implementation through the exercise and production of cultural activities, arts consumption, the rights, obligations and responsibilities in force within the territory of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. This Law shall also regulate the scope of art and culture and defines the basic rules for the establishment, organization, financing, administration and functioning of public institutions of art and culture, at the central and local level, the cooperation and financial support of independent art and culture institutions and independent artists.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this law are mandatory for all public institutions at the central and local level, in the field of art and culture, other relevant institutions, as well as other legal and natural persons, who exercise public authorizations according to the laws of the Republic of Kosovo as well as have legal obligations in the implementation of this law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The expressions used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Art - the expression of human creative skills and imagination through various forms appreciated for their idea, beauty and emotional power;

1.2. Culture - way of life, creativity and totality of spiritual, material, intellectual and emotional features of a society or of a social group;

1.3. Independent artist - is an artist without a permanent place of work, whose sole and main occupation is artistic creation and activity;

1.4. Public cultural goods - properties, goods and common services which are put in service of cultural and social development;

1.5. Meritorious artist - artist who have made valuable and proven contributions in the field of art and culture;

1.6. Ministry - Ministry responsible for culture;

1.7. Minister - Minister responsible for culture;

1.8. Independent Culture Scene - individuals and independent organizations of civil society whose activities are related to cultural production and management.

#### Article 4

The exercise of cultural activity Cultural activity in the Republic of Kosovo may be exercised by any local or foreign natural or legal person, in accordance with the legislation in force.

#### Article 5

General principles

\1. The Republic of Kosovo ensures the development of culture through the implementation of general principles, as follows:

1.1. freedom of expression in artistic creativity, balanced with public safety and protection of minors;

1.2. cultural development through support, production, research, evidence, preservation, education and promotion of creativity and public cultural goods;

1.3. promotion of cultural and artistic creativity through the development of a diverse and sustainable cultural environment;

1.4. coherence in the development of all cultural fields uniformly to achieve a sustainable cultural environment;

1.5. financial support for art and culture of public institutions and the independent cultural scene, inside and outside the country;

1.6. equal access and participation in resources, processes and artistic and cultural production for everyone, regardless of gender, nationality, religion, language, beliefs, social status or physical abilities;

1.7. promotion of international exchange and cooperation with the aim of networking cultural institutions and artistic representation of the Republic of Kosovo with cultural diplomacy;

1.8. socio-economic development through culture, creative industries and cultural tourism;

1.9. encouragement of innovation, digitization and experimentation in new artistic forms and techniques;

1.10. artistic education through programs that recognize, appreciate and promote creative expression and artistic production, ensuring continuity through the transmission of artistic creativity to younger generations;

1.11. exploration and professional and scientific research for the advancement of cultural education;

#### Article 6

Fields of artistic and cultural activity

\1. In accordance with this law, the field of artistic and cultural activity are considered:

1.1. visual arts;

1.2. performing arts,

1.3. music;

1.4. film and audio-visual works;

1.5. literature;

1.6. library activities;

1.7. digital and multimedia creativity;

1.8. research-scientific and educational activities in culture;

1.9. museum institutions;

1.10. other alternative forms of artistic expression.

### CHAPTER II

### ART AND CULTURE INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 7

Institutions

\1. Institutions of art and culture are institutions established to carry out cultural activities and to ensure the realization of public interests in specific fields of culture.

\2. Art and culture institutions may be public and independent institutions.

\3. Public institutions of art and culture are divided into central institutions and local institutions.

\4. Central public institutions of art and culture consist of:

4.1. National Theatre of Kosovo,

4.2. Opera, Ballet and National Ensemble,

4.3. Kosovo Philharmonic,

4.4. National Gallery of Kosovo,

4.5. Museum of Contemporary Art,

4.6. Kosovo Cinematography Centre,

4.7. National Library of Kosovo.

\5. The functioning of the Kosovo Cinematography Centre is regulated by the Law on Cinematography, while the functioning of the National Library of Kosovo is regulated by the Law on Libraries.

\6. Local public institutions of art and culture regulated by this law are: Theatres, Galleries, Orchestras, Choirs, Houses of Culture, Ensembles, and other local public institutions of art and culture.

#### Article 8

Establishment of public institutions of art and culture

\1. At the proposal of the Government, the Assembly of Kosovo may establish central public institutions in the field of art and culture.

\2. Municipalities may establish local public institutions in the field of art and culture.

#### Article 9

Criteria for establishing the public institution of art and culture

\1. The public institution of art and culture shall be established by the act of establishment.

\2. The criteria for the establishing public institution of art and cultural at the central and local level are:

2.1. name of the institution;

2.2. headquarters and place of activity;

2.3. definition of the activity;

2.4. steering bodies and organizational structure;

2.5. method of financing;

\3. Upon approval of the act of establishment for the central institutions of art and culture, the statute is issued by the Ministry of Culture, while for local institutions the statute is issued by the Municipal Assembly.

\4. The statute of the institution shall regulate: the activity of the institution, the internal organization of the institution, including number of artistic and technical-artistic staff, the institution’s bodies and their composition, as well as other important issues for the functioning of the institution.

#### Article 10

Status and activity of public institutions of art and culture

\1. The central public institutions of art and culture are public service institutions, under the relevant Ministry of Culture.

\2. Local public institutions of art and culture are public service institutions within the Municipalities.

\3. The central and local public institutions of art and culture have as main goals of their activity the following:

3.1. production and development of art and culture by encouraging new forms of artistic

Governing bodies of public institutions of art and culture

\1. The governing bodies of public institutions of art and culture are:

\3. The artistic leader, as in paragraph 2. of this Article, shall be elected and dismissed in the same procedure as the director of the central public institution of art and culture.

#### Article 12

Election of the Director

\1. The central and local public institutions of art and culture shall be managed by the Director.

\2. The director of the central and local public institution of art and culture shall be elected through the public competition process.

\3. The procedure for the election of the director shall be organized by the Ministry through the National Arts Council for the central institutions of art and culture, while by the relevant municipality for local art and culture institutions, in accordance with this law and relevant legislation in force.

\4. The election of the director of the central public institutions is made by the relevant Minister of Culture following the recruitment procedure through the public competition and the recommendation

\9. The director of the local public institution of art and culture may be dismissed in accordance with the relevant legislation in force.

#### Article 13

Criteria for the election of the director

\1. The candidate for director of the central and local public institution of art and culture must meet the minimum criteria as in the following:

1.1. to have university degree in the relevant field for the institution to which they apply;

1.2. to have professional work experience in the relevant field of the cultural institution where they apply and work experience in managerial positions;

1.3. to have general knowledge in the field of art and culture, especially in the relevant field;

1.4. to have a written ten-year vision and program for the institution to which they apply;

\2. Procedures and detailed criteria for the election of the director shall be defined by a sub-legal act approved by the Ministry.

#### Article 14

Duties and responsibilities of the director

\1. The director of the central and local public institution of art and culture is the main executive body and shall exercise the following duties and responsibilities:

1.1. he/she is the representative of the institution;

1.2. directs the artistic, cultural, administrative and organizational activity, based on the program for the institution management;

1.3. administers the institution’s financial and human resources;

1.4. performs all other duties assigned by this law and legislation in force.

#### Article 15

The composition, appointment, dismissal, duties and responsibilities of the artistic council

\1. The artistic council of the central and local public institution has an advisory role for the director and has the following duties and responsibilities:

1.1. advises the director in designing the artistic program;

1.2. supports the director in realization of the artistic program.

\2. The artistic council shall consist of three (3) members.

\3. Members of the artistic council must be well-known personalities of art and culture with personal and professional integrity.

\4. The right to propose one (1) member of the artistic council of the central public institution shall have:

4.1. Minister;

4.2. National Arts Council;

4.3. The director of the institution.

\5. The right to propose one (1) member of the artistic council of the local public institution shall have:

5.1. Mayor of the municipality;

5.2. Director of the Institution;

5.3. Local artistic community.

\6. The mandate of the artistic council is five (5) years.

\7. The artistic council is appointed by decision of the Minister of Culture, respectively Mayor of the Municipality.

\8. Members of the artistic council may be dismissed at the reasoned request of the proposer.

#### Article 16

Financing of public institutions of art and culture

\1. The means for financing the central and local public institutions of art and culture are provided by the budget of the Republic of Kosovo, own-source revenues, sponsorships and donations and other alternative financing in accordance with the legislation in force.

\2. Own-source revenues generated by public institutions of art and culture shall be transferred to the state budget and allocated according to the legislation in force.

\3. Public institutions of art and culture shall have their own budget codes.

#### Article 17

Early retirement The early retirement of specific performers is regulated by the relevant law in force that defines state-funded pension schemes.

#### Article 18

Supervision

\1. The Ministry shall supervise the work of the central cultural institutions.

\2. The municipality shall supervise the work of local cultural institutions.

### CHAPTER III

### NATIONAL ARTS COUNCIL

#### Article 19

Organization and functioning of the National Arts Council

\1. The National Arts Council is an advisory, election and supervisory body, appointed and dismissed by the Kosovo Government on the proposal of the relevant Minister of Culture.

\2. The mandate of the National Arts Council members shall be five (5) years.

\3. The Council shall consist of eleven (11) members, who are elected upon public invitation of the Ministry of Culture for the nomination of representatives from all fields of art and culture and related fields.

\4. Nominations for the members of the Council are made by central institutions of art and culture, academic, educational and scientific institutions, non-governmental organizations in the field of art and culture and other relevant bodies in the field of art and culture and related fields.

\5. The method of electing the members of the Council, the duties and responsibilities as well as the method of compensation shall be determined by a bylaw approved by the Government at the proposal of the Ministry of Culture.

#### Article 20

Responsibilities of the National Arts Council

\1. The National Arts Council has the following responsibilities:

1.1. analyses, recommends and gives suggestions in the creation of cultural policy;

1.2. makes proposals for the conduct and improvement of cultural activities;

1.3. reviews and gives recommendations for the cultural development strategy;

1.4. gives suggestions and proposals on regulation of other issues from the field of culture as well as inter-institutional and international cooperation;

1.5. leads the recruitment procedure of directors of public institutions of art and culture at the central level;

1.6. proposes to the Minister the names of qualified candidates for the position of director of central public institutions of art and culture;

1.7. monitors the work of central public institutions of art and culture and drafts reports on a quarterly and annual basis;

1.8. based on the performance evaluation of the directors, it may initiate the procedure for their dismissal;

### CHAPTER IV

### THE INDEPENDENT CULTURAL SCENE

#### Article 21

Independent culture scene The independent cultural scene in Kosovo is an inseparable part of the development of public interest and is one of the main contributors to the development of cultural policies, democratization of society, cultural promotion and state identity of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 22

Funding of the independent scene

\1. The Government and the Ministry of Culture shall financially support the artistic and cultural activity of the independent cultural scene in accordance with the development priorities of the state and with the aim of promoting cultural values inside and outside the country.

\2. Financing or co-financing of cultural programs and projects is done on the basis of a public call, with the exception of programs or projects of special importance.

\3. The method and procedure of financing the activity of the independent cultural scene and the criteria for classification of cultural events of special importance are determined by a bylaw approved by the Government.

#### Article 23

Council of the Independent Cultural Scene

\1. The Council of the Independent Cultural Scene is an advisory body appointed by the Minister of Culture, with a five (5) year mandate.

2.The Council consists of five (5) members:

2.1 two (2) representatives from the relevant Ministry of Culture;

2.2. two (2) selected from the independent cultural scene through public call;

2.3. one (1) external member, from the field of culture, appointed by the minister.

#### Article 24

Responsibilities of the Council of the Independent Cultural Scene

\1. Draws up the long-term policy and strategy for the development of the independent cultural scene through public financial support.

\2. Based on the analyses of the art and culture sectors, it recommends to the Ministry the direction of public financial support for the independent cultural scene.

\3. On a regular basis, it proposes the priorities, criteria and modalities of the Ministry’s supporting schemes for the independent culture scene.

\4. The Council shall approve the amounts of financial support for organizations and individuals of the independent culture scene, based on public calls that are opened within a year.

\5. Monitors the results of public financial support and based on them issues recommendations for future funding.

\6. The functioning of the Council of Independent Cultural Scene, the appointment and dismissal of members as well as their compensation shall be determined by a by-law, approved by the Minister.

### CHAPTER V

### INDEPENDENT ARTIST, MERITORIOUS ARTIST AND CULTURAL PROPERTY

#### Article 25

Status of the independent artist and other independent cultural workers

\1. Independent artists in the sense of this law are artists without a permanent place of work, whose sole and main profession is creation and artistic activity.

\2. According to the fields of creation and artistic activity, the artists may join professional artistic associations in accordance with the regulations of the associations, for the realization of common interests.

\3. Fields of artistic creation are considered: literature and literary translations, theatre, film, music, stage musical art, ballet, dance, including the artistic display of the author’s works in the fields of visual arts and applied fine arts, artistic design, artistic photography, multimedia creation and others.

\4. Independent cultural workers in the sense of this law are individuals without a permanent job, whose sole and main profession is service to creativity and artistic activity.

\5. The fields of service to creativity and artistic activity are considered: research and documentation in art and culture, cultural management, literary translation, performers, curators, technical services in art and culture, professional services of museology as well as other professional services in the field of art and culture.

#### Article 26

Associations of artists, performers and professional workers of art and culture

\1. Associations of artists are entities that represent the interests of creators, performers and professional workers of culture.

\2. The relevant ministry of culture is the competent authority that issues licences to these associations.

\3. The relevant ministry of culture shall create special programs that assist in protecting the status

#### Article 27

Status of the Meritorious Artist

\1. The status of meritorious artist is enjoyed by local artists who have made a valuable and proven contribution in the field of art and culture.

\2. The criteria and procedures for the status of the meritorious artist as well as the modalities of the benefits shall be determined by a bylaw approved by the Minister.

#### Article 28

Properties and public goods in the service of culture

\1. The Ministry of Culture and the municipalities of Kosovo shall coordinate their actions regarding the use of properties and public goods with a view to the development of art and culture.

\2. The method of using these properties and public goods shall be defined by a cooperation agreement between the ministry and the relevant municipality, where third parties may also be involved, in accordance with the legislation in force.

#### Article 29

Annual awards for art and culture

\1. The Ministry shall hand out annual awards in all fields of art and culture, in cooperation with the National Arts Council.

\2. The types of awards, categories, procedures, criteria and financial value of the awards are regulated by a bylaw issued by the Minister.

### CHAPTER VI

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

Transitional provisions

\1. Upon functionalization of the National Arts Council, the mandate of all governing councils of central public institutions of art and culture shall end.

\2. The existing governing councils of local public institutions of art and culture shall continue to function until the end of the mandate according to the act of appointment.

\3. General directors and artistic leaders of public institutions of art and culture shall have their mandate extended until the end of the existing contract.

\4. All by-laws in force that regulate the issues of cultural institutions and other issues related to the functioning of the cultural sector shall remain in force until the signing of the new by-laws provided for by this law.

#### Article 31

Final provisions

\1. The Opera, Ballet and National Ensemble, established according to Article 7, paragraph 4., subparagraph 4.2. of this Law, shall be the inheritor with all the rights and obligations of the institutions that have functioned as separate until entry into force of this law.

\2. The institution as in paragraph 1. of this Article shall be obliged to preserve, cultivate and promote the historical heritage of the Kosovo Opera, the National Ballet of Kosovo and the National Ensemble of Songs and Dances “Shota”.

\3. The establishment and full functioning of the new institution as in paragraph 1. of this Article shall take place in the period up to twenty-four (24) months from the entry into force of this law.

#### Article 32

Bylaws The by-laws provided for by this Law shall be issued within one (1) year from the date of entry into force of this law.

#### Article 33

Repeal

\1. By this law shall be repealed:

1.1. Law No. 02/L-59 on Philharmonic, Opera and Ballet of Kosovo;

1.2. Law no. 04/L-164 on National Ensemble of Songs and Dances “Shota” and other Ensembles;

1.3. Law no. 04/L-106 on Theatres.

\2. The provisions of the Law no. 02/L-57 for Cultural Institutions, which regulate the field of art and culture shall be repealed, while the provisions regulating the field of cultural heritage shall remain in force.

#### Article 34

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
