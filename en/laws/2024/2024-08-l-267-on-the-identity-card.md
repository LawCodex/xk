# LAW NO. 08/L-267 ON THE IDENTITY CARD

### LAW NO. 08/L-267 ON THE IDENTITY CARD

### LAW NO. 08/L-267

### ON THE IDENTITY CARD

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE IDENTITY CARD

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law shall define the conditions, procedures, the competent body for issuing the identity card as well as the right and obligations of the citizen of the Republic of Kosovo to acquire and use the identity card.

\2. This law aims to partially transpose:

2.1. Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing the Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation); and

2.2. Regulation (EU) 2019/1157 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 on strengthening the security of identity cards of Union citizens and of residence documents issued to Union citizens and their family members exercising their right of free movement.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this law shall be applied by the bodies responsible for issuing the identity cards and for other natural and legal persons whose activity is related to the use of identity cards.

#### Article3

Definitions

\1. For the purpose of this Law, terms used shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Identity card - the official document through which the citizen of the Republic of Kosovo proves his identity and citizenship;

1.2. Citizen - the person who has the citizenship of the Republic of Kosovo, in accordance with the relevant Law on Citizenship;

1.3. Competent body - Ministry responsible for Internal Affairs;

1.4. Minister - Minister of the Ministry responsible for Internal Affairs;

1.5. CRA – Civil Registration Agency;

1.6. Biometric data - all personal data resulting from specific processing related to the physical, psychological or behavioral features of a natural person that allows or confirms

the unique identification of that natural person as well as visual images or dactyloscopic, psychological data and behavior, which all individuals have, but which are specific and permanent for each individual, if in particular they can be used to identify an individual, such as: fingerprints, papillary finger lines, iris, retina, facial features and DNA;

1.7. Place of residence - according to the relevant law on place of residence;

1.8. Chip - the electronic device placed in the identity card, in which the data of the identity card holder are electronically set;

1.9. Electronic certificate - electronic authentication, which links the signature control data to a person and confirms the identity of that person;

1.10. Electronic signature - according to the relevant law on electronic identification;

1.11. Certifying authority - the authority within the Ministry responsible for Internal Affairs that issues the electronic certificates placed on the identity card chip;

1.12. Provider of electronic services - the public institution or private enterprise providing electronic services by using the identity card;

1.13. Authorization certificate - an electronic certificate issued to the service provider in order to enable electronic access to the electronic data in the identity card in a safe manner;

1.14. Electronic service – the service provided electronically through governmental platforms.

#### Article 4

Acquiring the identity card

\1. The citizen of the Republic of Kosovo, has the right to acquire the identity card.

\2. The citizen, from birth to the age of sixteen (16) may but he/she is not obliged to acquire an identity card.

\3. The citizen from the age of sixteen (16) shall be obliged to acquire an identity card.

\4. With the exception of paragraph 2. of this Article, citizens from the age of sixteen (16), who do not have permanent residence in the Republic of Kosovo, may but are not obliged to acquire an identity card.

\5. The identity card is the property of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 5

The use of identity card and citizens’ obligations

\1. The citizen is obliged to keep the identity card with him in physical or electronic form.

\2. At the request of the official person authorized by law, the citizen is obliged to provide the identity card to him/her for identification.

\3. The citizen cannot use the identity card of another citizen.

\4. The citizen cannot possess more than one valid identity card issued in the Republic of Kosovo.

\5. The identity card of another citizen is not allowed to be used for the purpose of securing any benefit or right, for oneself or for another natural or legal person.

\6. Changing, writing or deleting any information on the identity card is prohibited.

\7. The identity card may serve as a travel document for crossing the state border, if this is allowed between states.

\8. The identity card may also be used by the citizen to obtain electronic services.

#### Article 6

Competent body for issuing the identity card

\1. Personalization, placement of the electronic certificate on the chip, issuance and distribution of the identity card shall be done by the competent body.

\2. Authorizing certificates for electronic service providers shall be issued by the relevant certifying authority of the competent body.

\3. Issues related to the certifying authority and authorizations of service providers shall be regulated by a by-law approved by the Minister.

#### Article 7

Use of electronic data from the identity card

\1. The identity card enables the citizen to use electronic identification and authentication for access to electronic services and electronic signature in accordance with the relevant law on electronic identification.

\2. Electronic data in the identity card, in addition to the ID holder, shall be also used by:

2.1. the designated authority for identity verification, and

2.2. the electronic service providers who are furnished with an authorization certificate.

\3. The entities defined under paragraph 2. of this Article shall use the electronic data only for identity verification.

\4. The data under paragraphs 2. and 3. of this Article shall be used in accordance with the relevant Law on Protection of Personal Data.

#### Article 8

The form and content of the identity card

\1. The identity card shall contain the following data:

1.1. emblem of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. the constitutional name of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.3. the name of the document;

1.4. electronic document symbol (biometric);

1.5. personal name (name and surname);

1.6. date of birth;

1.7. sex;

1.8. citizenship;

1.9. expiry date;

1.10. place of birth;

1.11. picture;

1.12. signature;

1.13. card access number (CAN);

1.14. date of issuance;

1.15. the issuing body;

1.16. personal number;

1.17. identity card number;

1.18. place of residence;

1.19. machine readable zone (MRZ).

\2. The identity card shall also contain the electronic data placed in the chip, in which, in addition to the data under paragraph 1. of this Article, are also stored:

2.1. fingerprints, taken from persons from the age of twelve (12);

2.2. address of residence, and

2.3. electronic certificates.

\3. The Minister may, by a by-law, define other features regarding the form and content of the identity card, including the security features of the identity card.

\4. The Minister shall, by a by-law, define the form and manner of processing the identity card.

#### Article 9

Printing of forms and identity cards

\1. Forms and identity cards shall be printed in official languages, in accordance with the relevant Law on the use of languages.

\2. Forms and identity cards for members of non-majority communities shall be printed in their languages which are officially used at the local level.

#### Article 10

The validity period of the identity card

\1. For the citizen from birth to three (3) years of age, the identity card shall be issued with a validity period of three (3) years.

\2. For the citizen from the age of three (3) to the age of eighteen (18), the identity card shall be issued with a validity period of five (5) years.

\3. For the citizen from the age of eighteen (18), the identity card shall be issued with a validity period of ten (10) years.

\4. The citizen whose fingerprints cannot be taken during the submission of application for the issuance of the identity card, due to temporary medical issues, will be issued the identity card with a validity of one (1) year.

\5. With the exception of the validity periods determined under this article, in cases of force majeure and extraordinary circumstances, the validity of the identity card may be extended by a decision issued by the Minister.

#### Article 11

Application for acquiring an identity card

\1. The citizen shall personally submit the application for acquiring an identity card to the competent body.

\2. Citizens may submit an application for acquiring an identity card at the diplomatic and consular missions of the Republic of Kosovo offering these services.

\3. The citizen, within fifteen (15) days after reaching the age of sixteen (16) shall submit an application for acquiring the identity card.

\4. The citizen shall submit the application for acquiring an identity card at least fifteen (15) days before the expiration of validity of the identity card.

\5. Citizens under the age of eighteen (18), and citizens who do not have the capacity to act, at the time of submitting the application shall be accompanied by both parents or the custodian. The application may be submitted by one of the parents, when the other parent does not exercise parental rights or has passed away, and based on the authorization of one parent.

\6. When it is in the interest of the child, the application may be submitted by one parent, without the need for authorization from the other parent, but written consent must be submitted by the competent body for social work.

\7. With the exception of paragraph 5. of this Article, citizens younger than eighteen (18) years who have acquired the legal capacity to act, may personally submit the application for identity card to the competent body.

\8. The competent body shall issue to the applicant a proof on receiving the application.

\9. The competent body may determine other forms of application for identity card, including electronic application.

\10. Procedures for acquiring identity cards shall be determined by a by-law issued by the Minister.

#### Article 12

Procedure for issuance of identity card

\1. The competent body shall be obliged to issue the identity card within fifteen (15) calendar days from the date of submission of the application for identity card, in cases where the application for acquiring the identity card is submitted at the relevant Centers for Equipment with documents in the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The deadline for issuing an identity card is thirty (30) calendar days, in cases where the application for acquiring an identity card is made at the diplomatic or consular missions of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. In exceptional circumstances, the deadline defined in paragraph 1. and 2. of this Article, may be postponed with the decision of the competent body.

\4. The identity card shall be issued in a shorter period of time, in cases of accelerated procedure.

\5. The identity card shall be handed over to the citizen by the competent body together with the confidential data for the use of the electronic form of the identity card.

\6. The identity card containing the note “ID” in the Braille alphabet shall be issued at the request of the person to whom the ID is issued.

\7. The identity card may be taken at the competent body or by mail.

\8. The detailed procedure for the implementation of this Article shall be determined by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 13

Expiry of validity of identity card

\1. An identity card shall cease to be valid in the following cases:

1.1. upon death of the identity card holder;

1.2. by the loss of citizenship of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.3. by registration of the change of personal data;

1.4. in case of notification of loss, theft or damage;

1.5. expiry of the validity period;

1.6. in case it contains incorrect data.

#### Article 14

Damage, loss or theft of the identity card

\1. The citizen shall report the damage of the identity card to the competent body and at the same time apply for a new identity card.

\2. Damaged identity card shall be considered when it is damaged to the extent that the person cannot be identified through the data and/or picture.

\3. The citizen shall report the loss or theft of the identity card to the nearest police station within eight

(8) days, describing the circumstances of the loss or theft of the identity card.

\4. The citizen shall report the loss or theft of the identity card abroad to the nearest police station within eight (8) days after entering the territory of the Republic of Kosovo, in the manner defined under paragraph 3. of this Article.

#### Article 15

Changing the identity card

\1. The citizen shall, within fifteen (15) calendar days, submit the application to change the identity card if:

1.1. it changes any of the personal data from paragraph 1. of Article 8 of this law;

1.2. the picture no longer shows his/her true appearance;

1.3. the identity card is damaged;

1.4. the identity card contains incorrect data.

#### Article 16

Records of received applications and issued identity cards

\1. The records on received applications and issued identity cards shall be stored by the competent body in physical or electronic form.

\2. The records under paragraph 1. of this Article shall contain:

2.1. the data from paragraph 1. of Article 8 of this law;

2.2. identity card registration serial number;

2.3. the data on damaged, lost, stolen and invalidated identity cards;

2.4. the data from paragraph 1. of Article 18 of this law.

\3. The data from the records shall be stored by the competent body in a period of five (5) years after the expiry of the validity of the identity card and thereafter handled according to the relevant Law on State Archives.

\4. The data on banning the use of the identity card for crossing the state border shall be destroyed by the competent body after five (5) years, when the reason for this ban is no longer in force.

#### Article 17

Use of data from records State and private institutions, depending on the authorization defined by law, may use the data from the records for the identity cards issued in accordance with this law and the relevant law on protection of personal data.

#### Article 18

Ban on using the identity card according to the decision of the court

\1. A person who, according to the decision of the competent court, is not allowed to leave the place of residence, cannot use the identity card for leaving the territory during this period.

\2. The court shall notify the competent body of the decision according to paragraph 1. of this Article and after the cease of the reasons for taking the decision.

#### Article 19

Fines

\1. A fine of twenty (20) Euros shall be imposed to the citizen:

1.1. who does not submit the application for acquiring an identity card according to the deadline defined under paragraph 3. of Article 11 of this law;

1.2. who does not submit an application to the competent body for replacing the identity card within the deadline defined under paragraph 1. of Article 15 of this law;

1.3. who, does not have the identity card with him, according to paragraph 1. of Article 5 of this law.

\2. According to paragraph 2. of Article 5 of this law, a citizen who refuses to provide the identity card to an official person upon request for identification is subject to a fine of fifty (50) Euros.

\7. The relevant Law on minor offenses shall be applied for the imposition of fines under this law.

#### Article 20

The revenues collected from this law All revenues collected from the implementation of this law shall be deposited in the budget of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 21

Repeal Entry into force of this Law shall repeal the Law No. 05/L-015 on Identity Cards.

#### Article 22

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
