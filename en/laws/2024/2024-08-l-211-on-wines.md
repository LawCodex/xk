### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. The purpose of this law is to regulate the production, circulation, processing of grapes, must, wine, production and conditions for putting wine and other grape and wine processing products into circulation, distillation, protection of wine produced in the specified geographical region which it is distinguished by the special climatic, land and other conditions, the production and circulation of fruit wines, aromatic wines, other products based on fruit wines and aromatic wines.

\2. This law is partially in compliance with:

2.1. Regulation (EU) No. 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organization of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No. 1037/2001 and (EC) No. 1234/2007;

2.2. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/273 of 11 December 2017 supplementing Regulation (EU) No. 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council regards the scheme of authorizations for vine plantings, the vineyard register, accompanying documents and certification, the inward and outward register, compulsory declarations, notifications and publication of notified information,

2.3. #### Article 2

Scope

\1. The provisions of this law are mandatory for all relevant institutions and units as well as all persons participating in the production, processing, marketing, description, supervision, inspection, labelling of grapes, must, wine, fruit wines and aromatic wines during the presentation and food labelling in the use of ethyl alcohol and distillate originating from agricultural products.

\2. This law will be applied to the products defined in paragraph 1 of this article that are located in the market of the Republic of Kosovo or are produced in or outside of it and that are intended for the domestic market, export and import.

#### Article 4

Competent authorities

\1. The ministry through the responsible Department of Vineyards and Wine, performs the following duties:

1.1. maintains vineyard cadastre and wine quality control;

1.2. lead the system of production and circulation of grapes for consumption, grapes for wine, aromatic wines and other fruit wines;

1.3. maintains and updates the register of vineyards and growers of Grapes and Wine;

1.4. determines the recommended and permitted varieties of the vine and its rootstocks;

1.5. limits the maximum yields of grapes per hectare, depending on the variety and destination;

1.6. evaluates and defines wine and other products from grapes, fruit trees and wine.

\2. The physical-chemical analyses of grapes, must and wine are carried out in authorized laboratories within the Ministry.

\3. The department from paragraph 1 of this Article in accordance with this Law will do:

3.1. following the established technologies and methods for the production of grapes and wine;

3.2. evaluation of grapes and wine and classification of wine and other products according to their qualities - laboratory analyses and organoleptic evaluations;

3.3. issuance of certificates / permits for placing on the market for the assessment of wine and other products.

### CHAPTER II

### GRAPE AND WINE PRODUCTION

#### Article 5

Production

\1. Wine production defines all activities undertaken in the vineyard including harvesting, processing, reprocessing of grapes and all activities in oenological processes.

\2. Wine and other products can be produced only by persons who are registered in the register of grape growers and wine producers.

\3. Grape growers are local and foreign legal or natural persons who cultivate grapes for wine production or other products and who:

3.1. own, rent or keep - use in any other way a vineyard of at least 0.05 ha in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo;

3.2. have permanent residence in Kosovo and own or rent vineyards in border regions around Kosovo, ensuring that they transfer the grapes, wine and other products to the Republic of Kosovo.

\4. Producers of wine and other products are persons who produce wine or other products from grapes that they cultivate themselves or from purchased grapes.

\5. Processors of wine and other products are persons who process wine or other own or purchased products into new products.

\6. Family producers can produce wine in the amount of five hundred (500) litres per year for their own consumption, without trading rights. Producers are obliged to keep internal books of wine consumption.

\7. Producers of must, wine, aromatic wines, fruit wines and other products from grapes and wines must be licensed and are obliged to have an expert.

#### Article 6

Register of grape growers and wine producers

\1. The Ministry keeps records of people who cultivate grapes, produce and process wine or other products from grapes, wine and fruit trees. Data in the register are stored for at least five (5) trading years from the time of registration in the Register.

\2. Producers or processors of grapes, wine, fruit wines, aromatic wines and other products must be registered at the Ministry.

\3. The register must contain the following data:

3.1. for producers or processors: name, surname and address, personal number of the natural person, name of the headquarters and number of the business register of the legal person or individual business;

3.2. for vineyards: cadastral unit, cadastral municipality, cadastral plot number, cadastral order, net and gross area of the plot;

3.3. for vines: number, density of vines, varieties, rootstocks and year of planting;

3.4. for yields: quantities of grapes per variety, quantities of wine and other local products;

3.5. for supply: the quantity of grapes, must or wine of any type purchased.

\4. The producer or processor of grapes, wine or other products who wants to start with this activity, must apply in advance to be registered in the register.

#### Article 7

Register of vineyards

\1. For the maintenance, completion and updating of the register of vineyards, the Ministry, in addition to the central register, also uses other sources of data, which provide notes on existing and potential vineyards. The vineyard register is kept together with the cadastral register.

\2. Only areas suitable for the cultivation of vineyards may be registered as potential vineyards.

Vineyard area is an area of land on which grapes cultivated for the purpose of consumption, production of wine and other products will result in optimal qualitative and quantitative yields.

#### Article 8

Time for submission of declarations

\1. The declaration for the grape harvest is made from 1 September to 10 December of the current year.

\2. The declaration for wine production must be made from 11 December to 15 January of the following year.

\3. The declaration for annual reserves / stocks is submitted by 10 September of the fiscal year of trading.

\4. The Ministry, with sub – legal act, determines the method of submission and the content of the annual declarations.

#### Article 9

Grape

\1. Grapes for wine production can only be cultivated in registered viticulture sites, using only the recommended and authorized grape varieties as defined for the respective viticultural territorial unit.

\2. Grapes for the purpose of wine production must be cultivated with appropriate technology for the defined wine quality category.

\3. In the production of grapes and wine, the processing of grapes, grape juice, must and wine must preserve and develop all the natural quality characteristics of the grape, resulting in wine suitable for consumption.

\4. The production of wine from grapes, grape juice or imported must is prohibited. This provision does not apply to the grapes produced by the grape growers in the plots which they own and which are in border regions with Kosovo, ensuring that they transfer them to Kosovo in compliance with this law and sub – legal acts issued pursuant to it.

\5. The harvesting of grapes for the purpose of producing high quality wine must be registered in advance at the Department responsible for Vineyards and Wines.

\6. The importation of concentrated grape must be exceptionally allowed, intended only for the refinement of quality wine according to the conditions and procedures established by this law.

#### Article 11

Wine

\1. Despite the provisions set forth in Article 31 to Article 37 of this Law the production of grapes, wine and other grape or wine products must be carried out using only the defined technological processes and permitted oenological substances.

\2. If the time in the respective year is extremely unfavourable for the growth and development of the vine, taking into account the territorial units of viticulture and the expected quality of the wine as well as the type of grape, improvement of must, wine, sweetening of wine, grape juice or must will be made according to the recommendations of the National Council of Viticulture and Wines.

\3. The Ministry shall, by sub-legal act, determine the composition, duties and mandate of the National Council of Viticulture and Wines.

#### Article 12

Mixing grapes, grape juice, must and wine

\1. Mixing grapes, grape juice, must or wine of special quality and category for years and different colour of grapes is allowed within the limits set by this Law and sub-legal acts.

\2. The mixing of Kosovar and foreign wines is prohibited.

#### Article 15

Geographical zones of vine cultivation - territorial units

\1. Taking into account the ecological circumstances such as: relief, climate, soil, agrobiological and scientific factors, the principle organoleptic components of wines and the traditional historical aspects of viticulture, the viticulture territory of Kosovo will be divided into territorial units as follows: regions, subregions, vineyard zones and vineyard localities.

\2. The territory of the Republic of Kosovo is defined as a territorial unit of viticulture divided into two viticulture regions:

2.1. Dukagjin region and

2.2. The region of the Kosovo Plain.

\3. The vineyard region is the largest administrative and organizational unit that represents a geographical zone with similar climatic and pedological conditions, which together with agrobiological factors affect the main qualitative characteristics of grapes, must and wines produced in the Region.

\4. The vineyard sub-region is a smaller administrative unit within the wine region and represents a geographically limited zone with similar climatic and pedological conditions, similar assortment and other agrobiological conditions, which enable the production of grapes, must and wines with specific characteristics of the sub-region.

\5. The vineyard zone is the basic unit of viticulture within the wine sub-region, which represents a smaller geographical space with very uniform climatic and pedological conditions, one or several varieties and other agrobiological conditions that enable the production of grapes, and wine, with the specific characteristics of those vineyards.

\6. Vineyard localities are geographically limited parts of vineyards with suitable environmental conditions, in terms of climate, soil, altitude, exposure and assortment, which enable the production of grapes, must and wines that differ in quality and organoleptic characteristics from the same products from other production zones within the same sub- region.

\7. The Ministry by sub-legal act defines the conditions and the way of production of quality wines, the maximum yields per hectare, the definition of the borders for the viticulture regions, sub-regions of vineyards, viticulture zones and viticulture localities.

#### Article 17

\1. Aromatic wine products are products obtained from the wine sector which are aromatic. They are classified in the following categories:

1.1. aromatic wines;

1.2. aromatic wine-based drinks;

1.3. aromatic wine-based cocktails;

\2. Aromatic wine is a drink:

2.1. obtained from one or more grape vine products with the exception of table wine;

2.2. in which the grape vine products referred to in sub-paragraph 2.1 of this Article represent at least 75% of the total volume;

2.3. with the possibility of adding alcohol;

### LAW NO. 08/L-211 ON WINES

2.4. with the possibility of adding colour;

2.5. with the possibility of adding grape must, partially fermented grape must or both types of must together;

2.6. with the possibility of adding sugar;

2.7. which has a true alcoholic strength by volume of at least 14,5% vol. and a maximum of 22% vol., and a total alcoholic strength by volume of at least 17,5% vol.

\3. Aromatic wine-based drink is a drink:

3.1. obtained from one or more products of the grape vine with the exception of wine produced with the addition of alcohol and table wine;

3.2. in which the grape vine products defined in sub-paragraph 3.1 of this Article represent at least 50% of the total volume;

3.3. to which no alcohol has been added, unless otherwise specified;

3.4. with the possibility of adding colour;

3.5. with the possibility of adding grape must, partially fermented grape must or both types of must together;

3.6. with the possibility of adding sugar;

3.7. which has a real alcoholic strength by volume of not less than 4,5% vol. and a maximum of 14,5 % vol.

\4. Wine-based aromatic cocktail is a drink:

4.1. obtained from one or more specified grape vine products, excluding wines produced with the addition of alcohol and table wine;

4.2. in which the grape vine products referred to in sub-paragraph 4.1 of this Article represent at least 50% of the total volume;

4.3. to which no alcohol has been added;

4.4. with the possibility of adding colour;

4.5. with the possibility of adding sugar;

4.6. which has a real alcoholic strength expressed in volume of more than 1,2 % vol. and less than 10 % vol.

#### Article 34

Reinforcement limits

\1. When climatic conditions make it necessary, the National Council of Viticulture and Wines may allow the increase of the natural alcoholic strength of fresh grapes, grape must in fermentation and new wine in fermentation.

\5. Acidification and deacidification of wine can only be carried out by entities that produce wine and where the grapes used for the production of the wine in question were harvested.

2.9. prohibit the import and export of wine or products that do not comply with the criteria defined by this Law and the sub- legal acts issued pursuant to it.

\3. Producers, processors, fillers, packers, traders and hoteliers, regarding the provisions of this Law, are obliged to provide free access, to facilitate the work of the inspector and provide all the necessary data and information regarding their work.

#### Article 53

Right of appeal and initiation of administrative dispute

\1. An appeal can be filed to the Ministry against the inspector's decision.

\2. The review of the appeals is carried out by the Commission composed of three (3) members appointed by the Minister.

\3. The commission formed by paragraph 2 of this Article within a period of thirty (30) days from the day of receiving the complaint, issue a decision with full reasoning in accordance with the provisions of the relevant Law on General Administrative Procedure.

\4. Against the decision from paragraph 3 of this Article, the dissatisfied party who claims that the decision of the Commission is not legal can initiate an administrative dispute in the competent court with a lawsuit.

### CHAPTER XII

### PUNITIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 54

Minor offense sanction with fine

\1. With a fine of five hundred (500) euros to twenty thousand (20,000) euros, the legal person shall be punished for minor offence, if:

1.1. the production of grape or wine, the processing of wine and other products, storage or bottling by persons that are not registered or that do not provide data for registration in a defined manner and within the time limit set according to Article 5 of this Law;

1.2. the producers or processors of grapes, wine and their products are not registered with the Ministry according to Article 6 of this Law;

1.3. there has been carried out the cultivation of vineyards which are not classified in the special zone for viticulture according to Article 7 of this Law;

1.4. there has not been carried out the grape processing with adequate technology according to Article 9 of this Law;

1.5. the production of wine from imported grapes, juice or must has been carried out in contradiction to Article 9 of this Law;

1.6. the production of grapes, wine or other products is carried out using illegal oenological methods or substances in contradiction to Article 11 of this Law;

1.7. carries out the sale of wine produced from grapes which, at the time of harvest, has not reached the required amount of sugar according to Article 37 of this Law;

1.8. the mixing of grapes, juice, must or wine is carried out in contradiction to Article 12 of this Law;

1.9. carries out the selling, transporting or storing of the wine in a way that does not preserve the physical- chemical and organoleptic characteristics of wine according to Article 52 of this Law;

1.10. obstructs the inspector in taking samples and performing the control according to Article 52 of this Law;

1.11. labelling or selling wine or other products is carried out in contradiction to Article 38 of this Law;

1.12. sales the imported wine in contradiction to Article 49 of this Law;

1.13. does the marketing or sale of wine in contradiction to Article 46 of this Law;

1.14. treats the wines with prohibited oenological means and procedures according to Article 46 of this Law;

1.15. does not store the spoiled wine in separate containers, marked distinguishably or does not report to the relevant inspector according to Article 46 of this Law;

1.16. carries out the transportation of more than sixty (60) litres of wine illegally bottled and labelled or not accompanied by the documents defined according to Article 47 of this Law;

1.17. sends or receives the wine without documents required according to Article 47 of this Law;

1.18. does not keep the specified data of wine cellars according to Article 47 of this Law;

1.19. does not comply with the orders and decisions of the inspector according to Article 52 of this Law.

\4.
