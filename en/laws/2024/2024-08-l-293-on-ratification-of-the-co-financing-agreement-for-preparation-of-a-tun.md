# LAW NO. 08/L-293 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR PREPARATION OF A TUNNEL CONSTRUCTION PROJECT ON THE ROAD TETOVO – PRIZREN

### LAW NO. 08/L-293 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR PREPARATION OF A TUNNEL

### CONSTRUCTION PROJECT ON THE ROAD TETOVO – PRIZREN”

### LAW NO. 08/L-293

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE “CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR PREPARATION OF A TUNNEL

### CONSTRUCTION PROJECT ON THE ROAD TETOVO – PRIZREN”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR PREPARATION OF A

### TUNNEL CONSTRUCTION PROJECT ON THE ROAD TETOVO – PRIZREN”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Co-Financing Agreement for Preparation of a Tunnel Construction Project on the Road Tetovo – Prizren”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. The Co-Financing Agreement for Preparation of a Tunnel Construction Project on the Road Tetovo

– Prizren, shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law.

\2. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Offical Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-293 23 May 2024

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-77/2024 dated 11.06.2024 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
