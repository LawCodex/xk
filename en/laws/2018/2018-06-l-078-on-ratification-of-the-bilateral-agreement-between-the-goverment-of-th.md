Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE BILATERAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERMENT

### OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE GRAND DUCHY OF

### LUXEMBOURG RELATIVE TO THE DEVELOPMENT COOPERATION PROJECT, KSV/019:

### TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE IN THE CONTEXT OF EUROPEAN INTEGRATION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Bilateral Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg relative to the Development Cooperation Project, KSV/019: Technical Assistance in the context of European Integration.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Bilateral Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg relative to the Development Cooperation Project, KSV/019:

Technical Assistance in the context of European Integration shall be ratified. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No.06/L - 078 18 October 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-052-2018, dated 06.11.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
