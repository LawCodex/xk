# LAW NO. 06/L-059 ON THE MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

LAW No. 06/L-059 ON THE MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ LAW No. 06/L-059

### ON THE MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This Law defines the status of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz and obligations of the Kosovo institutions for planning, construction, protection, conservation, restoration, administration, care and its presentation.

\2. Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz has an ontological, anthropological, historical, cultural and civil importance for Skenderaj, Drenica, Kosovo and the entire Albanian nation.

#### Article 2

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. Special Zone of state and national interest - the area defined as the zone of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, for the purpose of this Law;

1.2. Special zone - zone identified in the Kosovo Spatial Plan and Zoning Map of Kosovo that has specific features that requires a special organizational regime, of use and protection developed by the Ministry through the Spatial Plan for special zones;

1.3. Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz - the space of a special area with a state and national interest, as it is defined by the provisions of this Law;

1.4. Expropriation – reimbursement of real properties and private buildings which are located in the space of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz and their transfer to the state property in accordance with the provisions of applicable Law on Expropriation in Kosovo;

1.5. Administration - management, protection, preservation and promotion of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz in accordance with relevant laws and sublegal acts into force;

1.6. Agency - Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo, established by the Law;

1.7. Management - activities coordinated to lead, administer and maintain the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz;

1.8. Financing - funds intended for financing the project of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, the expropriation of properties and the maintenance of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, in accordance with relevant Laws, appropriated from the budget of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.9. Manifestations - organization on the occasion of marking the important national and state dates, which in accordance with this law, shall be held in the premises of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz;

### CHAPTER II

### ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

### SPECIAL ZONE OF A STATE AND NATIONAL INTEREST

#### Article 3

Announcement of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz special zone of a state and national interest

\1. Taking into account the special role and importance of the war of the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA), the contribution of the legendary commander Adem Jashari and his family for freedom and independence of Kosovo, Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz village, municipality of Skenderaj, shall be announced “Special zone of a state and national interest”.

\2. Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz and its zone, defined by this Law and the Law on Cultural Heritage, has the permanent protection status.

#### Article 4

The area of the special zone

\1. Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz includes the space of martyrs cemetery of Jashari neighborhood, facilities from which there has been conducted the armed resistance, homes and accompanying facilities of this family, Jashari Neighbourhood, neighbourhood No. 3 of the city of Skenderaj, the zone of the former ammunition factory, a part of the Upper Prekaz;

a total of four hundred and fifty hectares (450 ha) under the conceptual plan for memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz designed by the group of experts and in accordance with the area defined by the applicable Laws.

### CHAPTER III

### PROJECT FOR MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

#### Article 5

Project for the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz

\1. Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, will be completed according to the Project, which is selected based on an open and public announcement. The project should express the spirit determined by paragraph 1. of Article 3 of this Law and contains: cemetery area, conservation of resistance Towers and bunker, information facility, summer amphitheatre with its accompanying facilities, museum, statue of Adem Jashari, obelisk, facility for KSF ceremonial guard, parking area, park of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, displacement of some of Jashari neighborhood houses including Jashari family houses and other necessary facilities.

\2. The selection of the project for implementation in conformity with this Law, is made by the Inter-ministerial Committee established by a decision of the Government.

\3. Committee from paragraph 2. of this Article shall consist of: the Prime Minister`s office head of the Committee, Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sport, Ministry of Environment and Spatial Planning, Ministry of Infrastructure, Ministry of Trade and Industry, Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare, Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo, Museum of Kosova, Institute of History, Institute of Albanology, Municipality of Skenderaj, representative of Jashari family, representative of the Association of Architects and the representative of the KLA WVO.

\4. In order to fulfill its role, as defined in paragraph 2. of this Article, the Inter-ministerial Committee may engage local or international experts of other fields as external advisers.

\5. Upon the proposal of the Inter-ministerial Committee, the Government decides on the criteria, conditions for announcing the competition and the application procedures for project selection.

After their approval by the Government, the Inter-ministerial Committee is obliged for their implementation.

\6. Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo is the authority to enter into a Contract with the Company/Winning Office and in cooperation with institutions of the Republic of Kosovo, specifies in particular its terms and conditions, as well as deadlines for its finalization, in compliance with criteria, conditions for announcing the competition and the application procedures for project selection proposed by the Inter-ministerial Committee and approved by the Government.

\7. The Inter-ministerial Committee established in accordance with this Article, shall complete its mandate upon the completion of the project implementation for Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz. The same shall be submitted to the Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo in compliance with it legal mandate.

#### Article 6

Accomplishment of the project for construction of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz

\1. The Inter-ministerial Committee under Article 5 of this Law is competent for the selection of the Committee of experts which is composed of at least three (3) experts and which will select the operator for the project accomplishment as well as the specialized company for monitoring performance of works by the selected operator, in compliance with criteria, conditions for announcing the competition and the application procedures for project selection proposed by the Inter-ministerial Committee and approved by the Government.

\2. The Committee referred to in paragraph 1. of this Article shall make the final technical acceptance of the accomplishment of works.

#### Article 7

Expropriation of immovable property for the purpose of building the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz

\1. For the purpose of construction and full functionality of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari”

in Prekaz will be carried out the expropriation of all properties and construction facilities, which are located in the area defined in paragraph 1. of Article 4 of this Law and that are needed for the accomplishment of this project.

\2. The expropriation will be made according to the applicable provisions of the Law on expropriation.

\3. The competent authorities, in cooperation with representatives of Jashari family in Prekaz, will determine in particular, the manner and conditions for the relocation of houses and other buildings providing that the same will be carried out according to the applicable legislation and in accordance with the Spatial Plan for Special Zone.

### CHAPTER IV

### EVENTS WITHIN THE SPACES OF MEMORIAL COMPLEX “ADEM JASHARI” IN PREKAZ

#### Article 8

Organization of state events

\1. In the space of Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, by the institutions of the Republic of Kosovo may be organized events and commemorative activities, marking the state dates, historical, and other with national importance.

\2. All manifestations, which are related to the dates listed as national and historic of the Republic of Kosovo and its citizens, at the spaces of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, shall be organized under the patronage of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 9

Manifestation “KLA Epopee”

\1. Manifestation “Epopee of the Kosovo Liberation Army” will be organized every year on dates:

5, 6 and 7 March.

\2. The Government of the Republic of Kosovo appoints the Coordination Council, in the composition of which will be the Commander of the KSF and the Director of the Agency for the Management of Kosovo Memorial Complexes.

\3. The Council will be responsible for the manner and forms of organization of this event.

#### Article 10

Visits in the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz With the proposal of the Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo, the Government shall approve the sub-legal act on the rules and manners of the visitors’ behavior in the spaces of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz.

#### Article 11

Prohibited activities in the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz

\1. In Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz it is prohibited any activity of a political character or other activity that is inconsistent with the purpose of this Law.

\2. Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo is responsible for security and protection of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, from organizations or events, contrary to paragraph 1. of this Article.

### CHAPTER V

### CEREMONIAL GUARD

#### Article 12

Ceremonial Guard

\1. Members of the Ceremonial Guard of the KSF will be placed permanently in a separate building of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz and will serve as a “Guard of Honour”.

\2. Ceremonial engagement of the members of this unit will be made in cases of state manifestations and other manifestations determined by the state protocol rules.

### CHAPTER VI

### SURVEILLANCE, CARE AND MAINTENANCE

#### Article 13

Competent authority

\1. Supervision, care and maintenance of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz is carried out by the Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo, in accordance with the Law.

\2. Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo in accordance with the law shall issue plan for management of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz.

\3. Preservation, protection and promotion of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz shall be carried out in accordance with the Law on Cultural Heritage.

### CHAPTER VII

### FINANCING AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT

#### Article 14

Financing

\1. The funds needed for the implementation of this Law will be allocated from the budget of the Republic of Kosovo and different donations.

\2. The funds collected in the form of donations or other ways for Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz, shall be deposited in the budget of the Republic of Kosovo, which then will be treated according to the legislation in force.

### CHAPTER VIII

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 15

Sub-legal acts The sub-legal acts stipulated by this law, shall be proposed by the Agency for the Management of Memorial Complexes of Kosovo and adopted by the Government, within three (3) months from the day of entry into force of this law.

#### Article 16

Repealing provisions With the entry into force of this law, the Law no. 2004/39 on Promulgation of the Memorial Complex “Adem Jashari” in Prekaz with particular national interest will be repealed.

#### Article 17

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
