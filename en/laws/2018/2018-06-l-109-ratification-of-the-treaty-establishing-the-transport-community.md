# LAW NO. 06/L-109 RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY ESTABLISHING THE TRANSPORT COMMUNITY

LAW No. 06/L –109 RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY ESTABLISHING THE TRANSPORT COMMUNITY LAW Nо. 06/L –109

### RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY ESTABLISHING THE TRANSPORT COMMUNITY

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18.1. (4) and 65 (4) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY ESTABLISHING THE TRANSPORT COMMUNITY

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is the ratification of the “Treaty Establishing the Transport Community“

between the EU and the countries of South East Europe: Kosovo, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia, Montenegro and Serbia.

#### Article 2

Ratification

\1. Ratification of the Treaty Establishing the Transport Community between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union and South East Europe parties shall be considered ratified upon entry into force of this Law.

\2. The Treaty Establishing the Transport Community, its Annexes and Protocols shall be an integral part of this Law.

\3. Notwithstanding paragraph 2 of this Article, the Republic of Kosovo does not recognize the way its state borders are represented in the maps of Annex 1 of the Treaty.

#### Article 3

Implementation The Ministry of Infrastructure shall, for the purposes of implementation of this Law, establish a body which will be funded by the Kosovo Budget as well as other sources.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 109 25 October 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-053-2018, dated 13.11.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
