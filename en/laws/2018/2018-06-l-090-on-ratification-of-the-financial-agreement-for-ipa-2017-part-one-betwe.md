# LAW NO. 06/L-090 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

LAW No. 06/L-090 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017,

### PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

LAW No. 06/L-090

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE,

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017, PART ONE,

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Financial Agreement for IPA 2017, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financial Agreement for IPA 2017, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 06/L-090 20 September 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-041-2018, dated 09.10.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
