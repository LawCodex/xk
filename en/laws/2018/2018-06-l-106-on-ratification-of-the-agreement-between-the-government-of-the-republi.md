# LAW NO. 06/L-106 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF SWITZERLAND RELATING TO THE SUPPORT PROGRAM FOR WATER SUPPLY AND SEWERAGE IN RURAL AREAS - PHASE VI

LAW No. 06/L-106

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF SWITZERLAND RELATING TO

### THE SUPPORT PROGRAM FOR WATER SUPPLY AND SEWERAGE IN RURAL AREAS -

### PHASE VI

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF SWITZERLAND RELATING TO

### THE SUPPORT PROGRAM FOR WATER SUPPLY AND SEWERAGE IN RURAL AREAS -

### PHASE VI

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of Switzerland, signed on 13th of July 2018, related to the Support Program for Water Supply and Sewerage in Rural Areas - Phase VI.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement This Law ratifies the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of Switzerland, signed on 13th of July 2018, related to Support Program for Water Supply and Sewerage in Rural Areas - Phase VI.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Annex of this Law is attached to the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of Switzerland, signed on 13th of July 2018, related to the Support Program for Water Supply and Sewerage in Rural Areas - Phase VI.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after the publication in Official Gazette of Republic of Kosovo.
