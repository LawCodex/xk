# LAW NO. 06/L –053 ON RATIFICATION OF THE COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE AUSTRIAN DEVELOPMENT AGENCY ADA REF. No. 8116-00/2016/LR 2016

LAW No. 06/L –053 ON RATIFICATION OF THE COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE AUSTRIAN DEVELOPMENT AGENCY ADA REF. No. 8116-00/2016/LR 2016 LAW No. 06/L –053

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN MINISTRY OF

### EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

AUSTRIAN DEVELOPMENT AGENCY ADA REF. No. 8116-00/2016/LR 2016 Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN MINISTRY OF

### EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

AUSTRIAN DEVELOPMENT AGENCY ADA REF. No. 8116-00/2016/LR 2016

#### Article 1

The purpose of the Law Through this Law, there is ratified the Cooperation Agreement between Ministry of Education, Science and Technology of the Republic of Kosovo and the Austrian Development Agency ADA Ref. No.8116-00/2016/Lr 2016 Project: “Heras – Higher Education, Research and Applied Sciences (Kosovo)” (Adc reference number 8116-00/2016).

#### Article 2

Scope of the Law The scope of this Law is “Heras - Higher Education, Research and Applied Sciences Kosovo”.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 053 15 March 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-004-2018, dated 04.04.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
