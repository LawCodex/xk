# LAW No. 06/L-062 ON RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND HUNGARY ON EXTRADITION

LAW No. 06/L –062 ON RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND HUNGARY ON EXTRADITION LAW No. 06/L –062

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### HUNGARY ON EXTRADITION

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1 and 4) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE TREATY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### HUNGARY ON EXTRADITION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratifiy the Treaty between the Republic of Kosovo and Hungary on Extradition, signed by the Ministers of Justice of both states, on 24 November 2017, in Prishtina.

#### Article 2

Scope This Law shall apply to all state administration bodies and judicial bodies in Kosovo that are responsible in the field of international legal co-operation.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
