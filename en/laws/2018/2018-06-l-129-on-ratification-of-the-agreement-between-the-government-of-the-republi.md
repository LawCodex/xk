### LAW NO. 06/L -129

### AGENCY FOR INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION) ON CO-FUNDING OF THE CETEP

### PROJECT

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO THE MINISTRY OF INNOVATION AND ENTERPRENEURSHIP

### AND GIZ (GIZ GESELLSCHAFT FÜR INTERNATIONALE ZUSAMMENARBEIT GERMAN

### AGENCY FOR INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION) ON CO-FUNDING OF THE CETEP

### PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo - Ministry of Innovation and Entrepreneurship and GIZ (Gesellschaft Für Internationale Zusammenarbeit - German Agency for International Cooperation) for Co-funding the Cetep Project.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo - Ministry of Innovation and Entrepreneurship and GIZ (Gesellschaft Fur Internationale Zusammenarbeit - German Agency for International Cooperation) for Co-funding the Cetep Project (Creation of Employment through Promotion of Export), signed on 11 July 2018, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo Ministry of Innovation and Entrepreneurship and GIZ (Gesellschaft Für Internationale Zusammenarbeit - German Agency for International Cooperation) for Co-funding the Cetep Project (Creation of Employment through Promotion of Exports), concluded on 11 July 2018, is attached to this Law.
