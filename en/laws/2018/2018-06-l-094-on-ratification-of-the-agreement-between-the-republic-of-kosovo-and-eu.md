# LAW NO. 06/L-094 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN THE “EUROPE FOR CITIZENS” PROGRAMME

LAW No. 06/L –094 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EUROPEAN

### UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN THE “EUROPE FOR CITIZENS” PROGRAMME

LAW No. 06/L –094

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN THE

### “EUROPE FOR CITIZENS” PROGRAMME

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN

### THE “EUROPE FOR CITIZENS” PROGRAMME

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on the Participation of the Republic of Kosovo in the “Europe for Citizens”

Programme.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on the Participation of the Republic of Kosovo in the “Europe for Citizens” Programme shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No.06/L - 094 20 September 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-043-2018, dated 09.10.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
