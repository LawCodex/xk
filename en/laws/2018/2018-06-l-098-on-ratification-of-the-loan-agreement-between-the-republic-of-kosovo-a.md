# LAW NO. 06/L-098 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE HIGHWAY PROJECT PRISHTINA - PEJË, THE SEGMENT KIJEVË – ZAHAQ

### EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE HIGHWAY PROJECT PRISHTINA - PEJË, THE SEGMENT KIJEVË – ZAHAQ

LAW No. 06/L –098

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE HIGHWAY PROJECT PRISHTINA -

### PEJË, THE SEGMENT KIJEVË – ZAHAQ

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE HIGHWAY PROJECT

### PRISHTINA - PEJË, THE SEGMENT KIJEVË – ZAHAQ

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank, signed on 29 May 2018, a loan for financing the Prishtina-Peja highway project, the segment Kijev-Zahaq.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement Through this Law, there is ratified the loan agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank, signed on 29 May 2018, for the highway project Pristina - Peja, the segment Kijevë - Zahaq.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex to this Law there is attached the Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, signed on 29 May 2018, for the Highway Project Segment Kijevë - Zahaq.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
