# LAW NO. 06/L-060 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE STATE BORDER BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND MONTENEGRO

LAW No. 06/L –060 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE STATE BORDER BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND MONTENEGRO LAW No. 06/L –060

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE STATE BORDER BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND MONTENEGRO

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, pursuant to Joint Statement of the President of the Republic of Kosovo H.E. Hashim Thaçi and the President of Montenegro H.E. Filip Vujanovic dated 16.02.2018 Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE STATE BORDER BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND MONTENEGRO

#### Article 1

The Agreement on the state border between the Republic of Kosovo and Montenegro, signed in Vienna, on 26 August 2015, is ratified.

#### Article 2

Provisions of the Agreement on the State Border between the Republic of Kosovo and Montenegro, signed in Vienna, on 26 August 2015, if new legal arguments are provided, can be amended and supplemented with the consent of the parties. Provisions of this Agreement can be changed following the same procedure envisaged for its adoption. With the consent of parties, international Arbitration can be sought for changing provisions of the agreement.

#### Article 3

\1. Integral parts of this Law are:

1.1. The Agreement on the State Border between the Republic of Kosovo and Montenegro, signed in Vienna, on 26 August 2015;

1.2. Joint statement of the President of the Republic of Kosovo H.E. Hashim Thaçi and the President of Montenegro H.E. Filip Vujanovic dated 16.02.2018.

1.3. Official records of the State Commission for demarcation of the State Border-for orientation.

LAW No. 06/L –060 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE STATE BORDER BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND MONTENEGRO

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
