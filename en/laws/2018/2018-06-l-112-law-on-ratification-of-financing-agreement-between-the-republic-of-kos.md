# LAW NO. 06/L-112 LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR KOSOVO DIGITAL ECONOMY PROJECT

LAW No. 06/L-112 LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR KOSOVO DIGITAL ECONOMY PROJECT

LAW No. 06/L-112

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR KOSOVO

### DIGITAL ECONOMY PROJECT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Pursuant to Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR KOSOVO

### DIGITAL ECONOMY PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for the Digital Economy Project of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement This Law ratifies the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for the Digital Economy Project of Kosovo, signed on 20.07.2018.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Annex of this Law is attached to the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for the Digital Economy Project of Kosovo, signed on 20.07.2018.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 06/L-112 20 September 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-042-2018, dated 09.10.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
