# LAW NO. 06/L-107 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

LAW No. 06/L –107 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

LAW No. 06/L –107

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare and the Kingdom of Belgium represented by the Minister of Social Affairs and Public Health.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Social Security Agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare and the Kingdom of Belgium, represented by the Minister of Social Affairs and Public Health signed on 20.02.2018 in Brussels, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare and the Kingdom of Belgium represented by the Minister of Social Affairs and Public Health, is attached to this Law.

LAW No. 06/L –107 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosova.
