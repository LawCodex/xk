# LAW NO. 06/L-012 ON THE CAPITAL CITY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, PRISTINA

### ON THE CAPITAL CITY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, PRISTINA

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) and Article 13 (2) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE CAPITAL CITY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, PRISTINA

#### Article 1

Purpose This law regulates the organization and functioning of the Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo, which has a special legal status under the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Scope

\1. This law applies to the Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The territory of the Capital City is the same with the territory of the municipality of Pristina set forth in the respective Law on Administrative Municipal Boundaries.

\3. The Law on Local Self-Government shall also apply to the Capital City, unless otherwise provided by this Law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Capital City - the Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo- Pristina;

1.2. Bodies of the Capital City - the Assembly and the Mayor of the Capital City;

1.3. Chairperson of the Assembly - the Chairperson of the Assembly as set forth in the Law on Local Self-Government;

1.4. Mayor of the Capital City - the Mayor of Municipality as set forth in the respective Law on Local Self Government;

1.5. Assembly of the Capital City - the Municipal Assembly as set forth in the respective Law on Local Self-Government;

1.6. Council of the Capital City for Community Safety- the Municipal Community Safety Council;

1.7. Deconcentration - the transfer of delivery of administrative services to other localities within the territory of the Capital City.

#### Article 4

Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo

\1. The Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo is Pristina.

\2. The historical capital of the Republic of Kosovo is Prizren.

#### Article 5

Status

\1. The Capital City has the status of a legal person.

\2. The Capital City is a unit of local self-government with a special status and organization, which exercises competences within its territory.

#### Article 6

Symbols The Capital City has its own coat of arms, seal, emblem and flag in compliance with the legislation on local self-government and other relevant laws.

#### Article 7

Competences of the Capital City

\1. In addition to the competences set forth in the Law on Local Self-Government, the Capital City exercises the following competences also:

1.1. Public safety:

1.1.1. the police establishes the Police Directorate of the Capital City, whose organisation and functioning is regulated by the Law on Police, which exercises its competences only in the territory of the Capital City;

1.1.2. the Mayor of the Capital City, after adoption by the Assembly of the Capital City, shall propose to the General Police Director at least two (2) candidates for the Director of the Police Directorate of the Capital City;

1.1.3. the Capital City Assembly exercises supervision of local safety policies falling within the competence of the Capital City;

1.1.4. the Assembly of the Capital City has the right to ask the Police Director of the Capital City to provide information on the safety situation;

1.1.5. upon request of the Mayor of the Capital City, the Kosovo’s Police, in compliance with the Law on Police, establishes special units based on the needs of the Capital City;

1.1.6. Safety Community Council of the Capital City is an advisory body responsible for inter-institutional coordination of matters related to safety in the Capital City, whose duties and responsibilities are stipulated by sub-legal acts.

1.2. Health protection:

1.2.1. the Capital City has competence to provide secondary health care;

1.2.2. the Capital City establishes the general hospital of the Capital City, whose functioning and organisation is regulated by the applicable respective legislation;

1.3. Public services:

1.3.1. the Capital City establishes and manages, as needed, publicly owned enterprises serving only in the territory of Capital City;

1.3.2. the establishment of a new publicly owned enterprise in the Capital City is done upon proposal of the Mayor of the Capital City, accompanied by a business plan and financial analysis and is adopted by the Capital City Assembly;

1.3.3. the procedures for the establishment of a local publicly owned enterprise of the Capital City do not require consent of the Government;

1.3.4. the decision on establishment of publicly owned enterprises is taken by two thirds (2/3) of votes of the Capital City Assembly’s members.

#### Article 8

Supervision from the central authorities The Capital City shall be subjected to the supervision from central authorities of the Republic of Kosovo in compliance with the legislation on local self-government.

#### Article 9

Bodies of the Capital City

\1. The bodies of the Capital City are:

1.1. Capital City Assembly, and

1.2. Mayor of the Capital City.

#### Article 10

Capital City Assembly The Capital City Assembly is the highest body of the Capital City. The members of the Capital City Assembly are elected directly by the citizens in accordance with the respective applicable legislation.

#### Article 11

Organization of the Assembly

\1. The number of members in the Capital City Assembly is determined according to the respective Law on Local Self-Government.

\2. The Capital City Assembly elects the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson from among its members.

\3. The mandate of the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson is the same with the mandate of Assembly’s members.

\4. The procedure for election and dismissal of Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson is carried out in accordance with the provisions of the applicable legislation.

\5. In the absence of the Chairperson of the Assembly, the Deputy Chairperson of the Assembly chairs the meetings of the Assembly and the Policy and Finance Committee.

\6. The provisions of the respective Law on Local Self-Government shall apply to other matters related to the competences, organization and functioning of the Capital City Assembly.

#### Article 12

Committees of the Assembly

\1. The Capital City Assembly establishes the standing committees, respectively the Policy and Finance Committee, the Communities’ Committee as well as other committees, as needed.

\2. The name, number of members and responsibilities of the standing committees are stipulated by the Statute of the Capital City.

\3. The Assembly establishes other temporary committees based on the needs of the Capital City.

\4. The Assembly establishes consultative committees for the purpose of involving citizens in the decision-making process.

#### Article 13

#### Article 14

Mayor of the Capital City

\1. Mayor of the Capital City is elected through direct elections. The provisions of the Law on Local Elections are applied accordingly to the election of the Mayor of the Capital City.

\2. The provisions of the Law on Local Self-Government on the competences of the Mayor of Municipality are applied also to the Mayor of the Capital City, unless otherwise provided by this law.

#### Article 15

Deputy Mayors of the Capital City and supporting staff

\1. The Mayor of the Capital City has two (2) Deputy Mayors, who assist him in the exercise of his competences, who are appointed and dismissed by the Mayor.

\2. The Mayor determines the duties and responsibilities of the Deputy Mayors by means of a decision.

\3. The mayor may appoint up to five (5) advisors, who advise him/her in the exercise of his/her duties and responsibilities, who are appointed and dismissed by the Mayor.

\4. The administrative staff of the Mayor includes: Chief of the Cabinet, the Administrative Assistant and the Logistics Officer.

\5. The employees in the cabinet of the Mayor are not civil servants. Their mandate expires with the termination of the mandate of the Mayor, unless their employment relationship is terminated earlier.

#### Article 16

Administration of the Capital City

\1. The administration of the Capital City is organized into directorates. Directories are led by directors who are appointed and dismissed by the Mayor.

\2. Directors perform their duties and manage with the directorates of the Capital City according to the provisions of the relevant Law on Local Self-Government.

\3. The Capital City ensures, as needed, the deconcentration in delivery of administrative services in the entire territory of the Capital City.

#### Article 17

Cooperation between the Capital City and central authorities

\1. The Capital City is the headquarters where the central authorities of the Republic of Kosovo and diplomatic missions exercise their state and diplomatic activity.

\2. The Capital City provides support to the central authorities of the Republic of Kosovo and diplomatic representations in exercising their functions and responsibilities.

\3. Capital City, central and diplomatic authorities are obliged to inform each other about the public events that are organized in the Capital City.

\4. Central authorities of the Republic of Kosovo support the Capital City in exercising its functions and activities.

\5. The Capital City provides conditions for organizing various public activities in accordance with applicable laws, interests of the citizens and interests of the state authorities of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 18

Property of the Capital City

\1. Movable and immovable property that are in the ownership of the Municipality of Pristina shall be registered in the ownership of the Capital City upon entry into force of this law.

\2. Competent cadastral authorities correct the cadastral and property data, according to official duty in order to transfer the ownership from Municipality of Pristina to the Capital City.

\3. The Capital City has the right to enter into agreements with the Government regarding ownership and/or management of public property in cases of mutual interest within the competences of the Capital City or central Government, and under the laws of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 19

Financing of the Capital City

\1. Each fiscal year, the Capital City of the Republic of Kosovo receives an additional grant from the Kosovo’s budget in the amount not less than six percent (6 %) of the general grant of municipalities, as defined in the Law on Local Government Finance. This allocation refers only to amount of the general grant of municipalities and it is not taken from this grant, but from other central budget allocations.

\2. Expenditures for carrying out additional competences of the Capital City provided by this law are foreseen in the regular budget allocations set forth by the central budget.

\3. Large public investments of the Capital City, which are of national character receive special support from the central level during the budgetary allocations for capital investments.

\4. The Grants Commission should examine in a special manner the grant for the Capital City in LAW No. 06/L-012 ON THE CAPITAL CITY OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, PRISTINA accordance with paragraph 1. of this Article.

\5. Secondary health grant treated within the health care is not covered by additional grant for capital city.

\6. The Capital City is obliged to deliver each fiscal year the grants’ planning for the Capital City regarding additional competences according to deadlines set forth from the Ministry of Finance.

\7. The sources of financing the Capital City remain the same with those set forth in Law on Local Government Finances, including the additional grant for the Capital City in accordance with the paragraph 1. of this Law.

\8. The Capital City sets additional taxes for the Capital City.

#### Article 20

Transitional provisions With the entry into force of this law, all normative acts of the Municipality of Pristina shall be harmonized with this law within one (1) year.

#### Article 21

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
