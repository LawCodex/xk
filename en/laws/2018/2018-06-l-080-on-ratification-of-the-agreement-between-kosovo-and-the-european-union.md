### UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION PROGRAMME

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION PROGRAMME

### “COMPETITIVENESS OF ENTERPRISES AND SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED

### ENTERPRISES (COSME) (2014-2020)”

#### Article 1

Purpose Purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement between the Kosovo and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in the Union Programme “Competitiveness of Enterprises and Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (COSME) (2014-2020).

#### Article 2

Agreement Ratification The Agreement between the Kosovo and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in the Union Programme “Competitiveness of Enterprises and Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (COSME) (2014-2020), concluded on date 19 December 2017, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Agreement between the Kosovo and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in the Union Programme “Competitiveness of Enterprises and Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (COSME) (2014-2020) is attached to this Law.

LAW No. 06/L –080 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN

### THE UNION PROGRAMME “COMPETITIVENESS OF ENTERPRISES AND SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED ENTERPRISES (COSME) (2014-2020)”

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 080 23 July 2018 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-029-2018, dated 08.08.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
