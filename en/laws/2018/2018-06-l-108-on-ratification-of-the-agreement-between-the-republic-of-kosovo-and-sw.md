# LAW NO. 06/L-108 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND SWISS CONFEDERATION ON SOCIAL SECURITY

LAW No. 06/L –108 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND SWISS CONFEDERATION ON SOCIAL SECURITY

LAW No. 06/L –108

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### SWISS CONFEDERATION ON SOCIAL SECURITY

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND SWISS CONFEDERATION ON SOCIAL SECURITY

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour, and Social Welfare and the Swiss Confederation, represented by the Ambassador of Switzerland to Kosovo, signed on 08.06.2018 in Prishtina.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Social Security Agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare, and the Swiss Confederation, represented by the Ambassador of Switzerland to Kosovo, signed on 08.06.2018 in Prishtina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Agreement on Social Security signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare, and the Swiss Confederation, represented by the Ambassador of Switzerland to Kosovo, is attached to this Law.

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND SWISS CONFEDERATION ON SOCIAL SECURITY

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
