# LAW NO. 06/L-095 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND KOSOVA ON KOSOVA'S PARTICIPATION IN 'ERASMUS+', PROGRAM OF THE UNION FOR EDUCATION, TRAINING, YOUTH AND SPORT

LAW No. 06/L-095 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND KOSOVA ON KOSOVA’S

### PARTICIPATION IN ‘ERASMUS+’, PROGRAM OF THE UNION FOR EDUCATION, TRAINING, YOUTH AND SPORT

LAW No. 06/L-095

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND

### KOSOVA ON KOSOVA’S PARTICIPATION IN ‘ERASMUS+’, PROGRAM OF THE UNION

### FOR EDUCATION, TRAINING, YOUTH AND SPORT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND

### KOSOVA ON KOSOVA’S PARTICIPATION IN ‘ERASMUS+’, PROGRAM OF THE UNION

### FOR EDUCATION, TRAINING, YOUTH AND SPORT

#### Article 1

The purpose With this law is ratified the Agreement between the European Union and Kosova on Kosova’s participation in ‘Erasmus+’, Program of the Union for Education, Training, Youth and Sport.

#### Article 2

Scope of law This law is valid for ratification of the Agreement between the European Union and Kosova on Kosova’s participation in ‘Erasmus+’, Program of the Union for Education, Training, Youth and Sport.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official gazette of the Republic of Kosova Law No. 06/L-095 20 September 2018

Promulgated by Decree No.DL-044-2018, dated 09.10.2018, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
