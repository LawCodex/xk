# LAW NO. 06/L-022 ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES

LAW No. 06/L –022 ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES LAW No. 06/L –022

### ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law shall regulate the organisation, functioning, principles, manner of obtaining and forfeiting of the status of social enterprise, as well as it shall determine the terms and conditions a subject must meet in order to obtain the status of social enterprise.

#### Article 2

Scope This Law shall apply to all natural and legal persons who take part in the activities of social enterprises in accordance with this Law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. A Social Enterprise- is a legal person irrespective of the manner of its establishment, which contains social objectives in its charter, conducts economic activities, carries out production of goods and provides services in the general interest of society and integrates into the work the persons from vulnerable working groups;

1.2. Activities of social enterprise - shall be deemed activities that serve general social interests, in the form of delivery of goods and social services that enhance the quality of life, strengthen social co-operation and encourage integration of vulnerable groups;

1.3. Vulnerable groups – shall mean categories of persons that are defined by this Law to have difficulties integrating into the labour market for various reasons;

1.4. Members of social enterprise – are persons, founders or co-founders, employees who manage social enterprises with equal rights;

1.5. Employee of social enterprises- shall mean a person employed based on a contractual relationship or any other legal form according to the legislation in force;

1.6. Beneficiary of social enterprise- is a person using the goods and services of the social enterprise;

1.7. Volunteer- is a person engaged in the implementation of the activities of the social enterprises and who provides services of public interest free of charge;

1.8. Legal Form – shall mean any of the forms of establishment of legal persons, which apply for the status of social enterprise;

1.9. Legal person- shall mean the type of business organization which will be established as a social enterprise, non-governmental organizations, foundations, associations and cooperatives that are registered with the public authorities according to the applicable legislation;

1.10. Public contract – is a contract concluded in accordance with the legal provisions into force on Public Procurement;

1.11. Minister- is the Minister of Labour and Social Welfare;

1.12. Ministry- shall mean the respective Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare;

1.13. Ministries – shall mean Ministries of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.14. Municipalities – shall mean Municipalities of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 4

General principles

\1. A social enterprise shall be established and operates according to the following principles:

1.1. free initiative;

1.2. restriction of profit distribution;

1.3. engagement in social or other activities aimed at employment of vulnerable groups serving general public interest;

1.4. autonomous management;

1.5. volunteerism;

1.6. adherence to market rules;

1.7. non-discrimination in management and decision-making;

1.8. participation of employees and beneficiaries;

1.9. transparency of control.

#### Article 5

Social Enterprise

\1. Social enterprises shall be divided into two (2) categories:

1.1. Category A social enterprises;

1.2. Category B social enterprises.

\2. Category A social enterprises shall provide services that guarantee the inclusion of marginalized persons in improving their social welfare and shall develop activities in the following fields:

2.1. social assistance and support for sheltering;

2.2. family assistance and family care;

2.3. health services and home nursery care;

2.4. provision of daily services and home care for persons with mental, sensory and physical disabilities;

2.5. support and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities;

2.6. daily care services and residential care for disabled children;

2.7. day-care home services and residential services for elderly persons;

2.8. services provided for re-integration of persons raped during the war;

2.9. services provided for re-integration of victims of trafficking, abuse and domestic violence and re-integration of delinquent youth;

2.10. psycho-social services and treatment of substance abuse cases causing addictions;

2.11. education and vocational training and transport services for persons with disabilities;

2.12. employment, training and self-employment services for vulnerable groups.

\3. Category B social enterprises shall be the enterprises that employ at least thirty percent (30%) employees from the vulnerable groups and shall be engaged in providing services useful for the society, or in growing, manufacturing, producing, processing of products for sale, as well as develop activities in the following fields:

3.1. cultivation and growth of spices, aromatic and medical herbs;

3.2. conservation of fruits and vegetables from households that produce or cultivate these products in rural or urban areas;

3.3. production and preparation of the handmade products such as knitting work from textiles, work clothes and other crocheted clothes;

3.4. production and preparation of both hand and machine-made products;

3.5. preparation or production, wrapping and packaging of honey, fruits, vegetables and other products of herbal or animal origin;

3.6. production, maintenance of bicycles, chairs and other assisting equipment for persons with disabilities;

3.7. collection, processing, disposal of non-hazardous waste and their sorting for recycling purposes;

3.8. maintenance and care for public spaces, cultural heritage monuments and memorials;

3.10. services and activities of tourism, cultural heritage and HOREC sector (Hotel, Restaurant, Cafe) with the participation of different social categories;

3.11. services and activities in the treatment of animals which are deemed a threat to public health and human life.

\4. Category B social enterprise may conduct activities of Category A social enterprises, provided that they employ at least thirty percent (30%) of persons from vulnerable groups.

\5. Vulnerable persons employed in the social enterprise must be actively involved in the work of delivering services, or in growing, manufacturing, producing, or processing products for sale.

\6. Changing and supplementation of the fields or activities for Category A and B social enterprises shall be regulated by a sub-legal act proposed by the Ministry and issued by the Government of Kosovo.

#### Article 6

Disadvantaged groups

\1. Disadvantaged groups are categorized the persons who have many difficulties in finding jobs, and include:

1.1. long-term unemployed persons and that are registered with the Employment Office for more than twenty-four (24) months;

1.3. unemployed people with disabilities, who may be part of the labour market in compliance with the applicable legislation;

1.4. poor families and beneficiaries of social assistance;

1.5. repatriated persons, who are granted such status according to the legislation into force;

\2. The Ministry may change, supplement or propose other categories of persons with difficulties in integration in the labour market, as decided by sub-legal act issued by the Government of Kosovo.

#### Article 7

Prohibitions and restrictions

\1. A social enterprise shall not have, in the course of its activity, the right to give support to political parties as well as to the candidates running for public office.

\2. Legal persons or for-profit and non-profit entities cannot change their status into a social enterprise if subject to bankruptcy or liquidation proceedings.

\3. Social enterprises shall have the right to use up to thirty percent (30%) of their profit on wages and salaries for the staff of the social enterprise.

#### Article 8

Associations of social enterprises

\1. Social enterprises may establish associations of social enterprises with the purpose of protection of interests and promotion of their activities.

\2. Associations of social enterprises shall be established and registered according to the legal provisions into force.

#### Article 9

Legal status of social enterprises

\1. A social enterprise may be established under one of the legal forms foreseen by legal provisions into force.

\2. A social enterprise may be established as a legal person which shall, in its charter, specify activities carrying social goals and objectives.

\3. Any legal entity that fulfils the requirements according to this Law is entitled to use the “Social Enterprise” designation.

\4. There is prohibited the use of the “Social Enterprise” designation by any entity that is not registered according to this Law.

\5. Registration procedures of social enterprises shall be regulated by a sub-legal act which is proposed by the Ministry and issued by the Government of Kosovo.

#### Article 10

Social Enterprises Charters

\1. In addition to legal person charters, which may be of the form chosen by the founders, in case of exercise of activity of a social enterprise, the charters shall also stipulate the following:

1.1. the obligation to not distribute the profit;

1.2. category of the scope of activity according to Article 5 of this Law;

1.3. the manner of management that guarantees non-discrimination in participation;

1.4. persons responsible for the management of social activities;

1.5. supervisory body, which exercises control over meeting the requirements determined in this Law by the legal person;

1.6. the manner of participation of employees in strategic decisions of the enterprise;

1.7. book-keeping and financial reporting rules.

#### Article 11

Obtaining the status of social enterprise

\1. In order to obtain the status of social enterprise, the legal entity shall submit the request and the social business plan to the respective department of the Ministry. The social business plan shall consist of a brief outline of the social problem or problems that the social enterprise is intended to address, and the means proposed of addressing it.

\2. The Ministry shall review the request and the social business plan, along with requirements LAW No. 06/L –022 ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES foreseen in this Law, and shall permit the exercise of the activity.

\3. Award of social enterprise status shall be done with a decision by the relevant Commission of the Ministry.

\4. Following approval, the respective department of the Ministry shall issue a certificate of status to the social enterprise.

\5. Certificates of social enterprise status shall include a registration number, the name of the social enterprise, and the category of activity of the social enterprise.

\6. Procedures and documentation required for obtaining, rejection and forfeiture of social enterprise status, as well as the composition of the relevant commission of the Ministry, shall be regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Government of Kosovo.

#### Article 12

Rejection of the status of social enterprise

\1. Status of social enterprise shall be rejected in cases when a legal person:

1.1. fails to submit all documentation under the terms of this law and procedure provided by the sub-legal act for the status of social enterprise;

1.2. fails to meet the principles and criteria set forth by this Law;

1.3. the applicant presents false information or documentation;

1.4. if subject to restrictions set forth in Article 7 of this Law.

\2. Any rejection of the status of social enterprise shall be made by a decision of the relevant Commission of the Ministry, within sixty (60) days upon the application.

#### Article 13

Forfeiture of the status of social enterprise

\1. Social enterprise status shall be forfeit if:

1.1. the social enterprise submits a justified request to the Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare for revocation of the status;

1.2. it carries out operations in violation of the conditions foreseen by Articles 5 and 6 of this Law;

1.3. it uses this status for unfair competition in contradiction with activities aimed at general social/public interest;

1.4. it bankrupts according to applicable legislation or if it terminates its activities.

\2. Forfeiture of status shall enter into force through a decision of the relevant commission of the Ministry, which shall be implemented in the course of ten (10) days, thus causing deletion or deregistration of the social enterprise from the registry of social enterprises.

#### Article 14

The right to appeal

\1. Any party dissatisfied with the decision of the competent authority of the Ministry may submit an appeal in writing to the second instance authority.

\2. The second instance authority shall review the complaint and notify the appellant of its decision in writing not later than thirty (30) days of receipt.

\3. The party dissatisfied with the decision of the second instance body may file a claim for opening an administrative dispute in the Competent Court, within thirty (30) days of receipt of the decision.

#### Article 15

Registry of social enterprises

\1. The Ministry shall establish a Registry of social enterprises, which shall contain the following data:

1.1. the name, the office of registration, and the address of the legal person that will develop the activity of the social enterprise;

1.2. the full names, personal numbers, and permanent or temporary addresses of authorized persons;

1.3. the registration number of the social enterprise;

1.4. the legal form of social enterprises;

1.5. the number and date of charters;

1.6. the scope of activity of the social enterprise pursuant to Article 5 of this Law;

1.7. the amount of initial capital or potential amount for commencement of activities, enterprise real estate and other assets values;

1.8. the number and date of the act of closing as well as the act of liquidation;

1.9. data registration in case of restructuring.

\2. The Registry of social enterprises shall be managed and maintained by the Ministry.

\3. The Registry of social enterprises shall be maintained upon electronic database.

\4. Data held in the registry of social enterprises shall be public, in compliance with provisions of the Law on protection of personal data.

#### Article 16

Participation of employees and persons who benefit from social activities

\1. Any citizen of the Republic of Kosovo or foreign citizen shall, according to the legislation into force, have the right to be a member or employee of a social enterprise or benefit from the activity of a social enterprise.

\2. Irrespective of the chosen legal form, a social enterprise shall set out in its documentation the manner of participation in which employees or persons will benefit from social activities of the enterprise and their participation in the strategic decisions of the enterprise.

\3. Employees and beneficiaries shall be entitled to request information which allows them to participate in strategic decision making, especially on issues related to working conditions as well as the quality of goods and services delivered by the enterprise.

#### Article 17

Volunteers

\1. Depending on the chosen legal form, a social enterprise can engage people that contribute voluntarily.

\2. Inclusion and the contribution of volunteers in social enterprises shall be regulated according to legislation into force on volunteering.

#### Article 18

Restriction of profit distribution

\1. A social enterprise may use its proceeds to expand, invest and develop its activity as well as for contingency funds, which shall not be distributed.

\2. Distribution of any kind of profit is not allowed, in favour of members, employees or beneficiaries of the social enterprise.

#### Article 19

Reporting requirements

\1. A social enterprise shall maintain accounting books, prepare financial statements and publish internal and external audit reports in accordance with the legislation in force at least once a year.

\2. A social enterprise shall reflect its financial situation, including assets, and disclose activities conducted in pursuit of social objectives.

\3. Reporting procedures on achievement of social objectives and financial reports shall be regulated by a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

#### Article 20

Obligations of social enterprises

\1. Depending on the chosen legal form of a social enterprise, obligations assumed upon award of status shall be covered through its property.

\2. In case of the liquidation of a social enterprise, its assets and remaining funds shall either be used for the public interest or shall be transferred to other social enterprises.

\3. In case of forfeiting the status of social enterprise, the legal entity shall be retroactively liable for and subject to payment of taxes and fees for the last twelve (12) months, for which it enjoyed fiscal incentives during the period of development of social enterprise activity.

#### Article 21

Control bodies

\1. Control of social enterprises shall be conducted in accordance with the rules set forth in the applicable legislation, depending on the chosen legal form of the social enterprise.

\2. Notwithstanding the rules established according to the legislation in force, the charters shall set out control of social enterprises by one (1) or two (2) experts whenever so requested by one-fifth (1/5) of its members or steering bodies, though not more than once a year.

\3. The experts shall monitor the realization of the social purpose of social enterprises, particularly with regard to implementation of Articles 5 and 6 of this Law.

\4. The experts may carry out controls and inspections at any time, and may request information from administrators on the conduct of specific actions.

#### Article 22

Mergers and demergers of social enterprises

\1. Social enterprises may merge or de-merge with any other social enterprise, in accordance with rules of the legislation in force for each of the forms of organization, as well as based on conditions provided by this Law.

\2. Mergers and de-mergers of social enterprises are carried out upon decision of the relevant Commission of the Ministry.

\3. In any case, mergers and de-mergers are reflected in the registry of social enterprises.

#### Article 23

Planning and promotion of social enterprise

\1. The Ministry shall cooperate with all relevant ministries on the areas and activities of social enterprise for development and drafting of a set of measures to promote social entrepreneurship.

\2. Ministries and municipalities may participate in and contribute to the development and LAW No. 06/L –022 ON SOCIAL ENTERPRISES promotion of regulated social enterprises.

\3. Municipalities and other public authorities should provide adequate spaces for the social enterprises where the social incubators are established, or should support the opening of existing incubators for the initiatives of social enterprises.

#### Article 24

Providing the funds

\1. Funds for development and promotion of social enterprises may be provided from the Budget of Kosovo and other local and international sources, contribution of local and foreign citizens, private companies and international organizations.

\2. The Ministry may plan funding for the implementation of measures in the field of employment and social welfare services.

\3. Ministries and municipalities may plan funding for the implementation of measures, as part of their own programmes.

#### Article 25

Promoting and employment of vulnerable groups

\1. A social enterprise which employs people from vulnerable groups provided in Article 6 of this Law, shall be eligible for support under subsidy programmes for salaries of employees that fall under this category.

\2. A social enterprise shall be eligible for full support provided for in paragraph 1 of this Article if it enters into employment contracts with persons from categories of persons provided in Article 6 of this Law for an indefinite period or for a period of at least six (6) months.

#### Article 26

Public contract

\1. Public authorities in the implementation of this Law shall be permitted to enter into contracts with social enterprises through the procedure according to the Law on public procurement.

\2. Ministries and municipalities may provide subsidies and grants for social enterprises.

\3. In any case, procedures for the award of these contracts, grants and subsidies shall observe the general principles of transparency, competitiveness and control based on legislation into force.

#### Article 27

Fiscal incentives

\1. Regardless of the forms of the organization and the activity they exercise, social enterprises shall be exempt from tax on profit they generate in the course of discharge of such activities.

#### Article 28

Monitoring and control

\1. The Ministry shall exercise supervision and control of implementation of the provisions of this law by social enterprises through its relevant structures.

\2. Depending on the nature of the enterprise and the social activities it exercises, the ministry shall exercise supervision and control in coordination with relevant institutions.

#### Article 29

Sanctions, complaints and fines procedure

\1. Social enterprises that are found to have acted in violation of this Law shall be fined according to Law on Minor Offenses.

\2. Social enterprises that are found to have acted in violation of Articles 4 and 7 of this Law shall be fined in the amount of one thousand (1,000) Euro to two thousand (2,000) Euro.

\3. Social enterprises that are found to have acted in violation of Articles 5 and 6 of this Law shall be fined in the amount of one thousand (1,000) Euro to three thousand (3,000) Euro.

\4. Social enterprises that are found to have acted in violation of Articles 18 and 19 of this Law shall be fined in the amount of three thousand (3,000) Euro to five thousand (5,000) Euro.

\5. Revenues from fines are recorded in the Kosovo Budget as revenues from MLSW, which are subject to the legal rules for their use.

\6. Deadlines for payment of fines, procedures and appeals are regulated by sub-legal act proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government, based on the provisions of this Law and the Law on Minor Offenses into force.
