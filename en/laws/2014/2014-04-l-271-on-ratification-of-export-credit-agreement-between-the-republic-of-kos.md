# LAW NO. 04/L-271 ON RATIFICATION OF EXPORT CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG TO FINANCE THE PROJECT "MODERNIZATION OF EDUCATION SYSTEM IN KOSOVO THROUGH E- EDUCATION"

### LAW NO. 04/L-271 ON RATIFICATION OF EXPORT CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG

### TO FINANCE THE PROJECT “MODERNIZATION OF EDUCATION SYSTEM IN KOSOVO THROUGH E-EDUCATION”

### LAW NO. 04/L-271

### ON RATIFICATION OF EXPORT CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG TO FINANCE THE PROJECT “MODERNIZATION OF

### EDUCATION SYSTEM IN KOSOVO THROUGH E-EDUCATION”

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF EXPORT CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG TO FINANCE THE PROJECT

### “MODERNIZATION OF EDUCATION SYSTEM IN KOSOVO THROUGH E-EDUCATION”

#### Article 1

Aim of Law This Law aims to ratify the Export Credit Agreement to finance the project “Modernization of Education System in Kosovo through e- Education”, made between the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement Export Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG regarding the financing of the project “Modernization of Education System in Kosovo through e - Education” signed on February 27, 2014 in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In Annex No. 1 of this Law is attached the Export Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG regarding the financing of the project “Modernization of Education System in Kosovo through E - Education”

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 04/L-271 17 April 2014

Promulgated by Decree No.DL-021-2014, dated 05.05.2014, President of the Republic of Kosovo Atifete Jahjaga.
