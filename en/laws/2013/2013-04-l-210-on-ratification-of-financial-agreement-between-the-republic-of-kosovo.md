# LAW NO. 04/L-210 ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION – SECOND ADDITIONAL FUNDING FOR ENERGY SECTOR CLEAN-UP...

### LAW NO. 04/L-210

### ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION – SECOND ADDITIONAL

### FUNDING FOR ENERGY SECTOR CLEAN-UP AND LAND RECLAMATION PROJECT- CLRP

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION – SECOND

### ADDITIONAL FUNDING FOR ENERGY SECTOR CLEAN-UP AND LAND RECLAMATION

### PROJECT- CLRP

#### Article 1

The purpose of the law This law aims to ratify the financial agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association on Second Additional Financing for Cleanup Energy Sector and Land Reclamation Project - CLRP.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement Financial Agreement (Second Additional Financing for Clean-up Energy Sector and Land Reclamation Project) signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association on May 15, 2013 in Prishtina is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents

\1. In Annex No. 1 of this law is attached the "Financial Agreement (Second Additional Financing for Clean-up Energy Sector and Land Reclamation Project), made between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association

\2. In Annex No. 2 is attached "General Conditions for Credits and Grants" of the International Development Association.

\3. In Annex No. 3 is attached "Guidelines on procurement of goods, works and services, aside from the services of consultancy, loans received from IBRD as well as credits and grants received from IDA, by World Bank borrowers.”

\4. In Annex No. 4 is attached "Guidelines on selection and employment of consultants under IBRD loans and IDA credits and grants by World Bank borrowers "

\5. In Annex No. 5 is attached "Guidelines on preventing and combating fraud and corruption in projects financed by IBRD loans and IDA credits and grants"

\6. In Annex No. 6 is attached "The agreement for project" made between the International Development Association and the Kosovo Energy Corporation, signed on May 15, 2013 in Prishtina.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 04/L-210 31 July 2013

Promulgated by Decree No.DL-038-2013, dated 16.08.2013, President of the Republic of Kosovo Atifete Jahjaga
