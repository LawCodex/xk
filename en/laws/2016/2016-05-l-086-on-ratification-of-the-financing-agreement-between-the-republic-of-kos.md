# LAW NO. 05/L-086 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON PROJECT FOR IMPROVING EDUCATION SYSTEM

LAW No. 05/L-086 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON PROJECT FOR IMPROVING EDUCATION SYSTEM

LAW No. 05/L-086

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON PROJECT FOR

### IMPROVING EDUCATION SYSTEM

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON PROJECT

### FOR IMPROVING EDUCATION SYSTEM

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association on the Project for Improving the Education System.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financing Agreement is ratified between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association on the Project for Improving the Education System.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In Annex 1 of this law is attached the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association on the Project for Improving the Education System.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 05/L-086 24 February 2016 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-003-2016, dated 10.03.2016, President of the Republic of Kosovo Atifete Jahjaga
