# LAW NO. 05/L-038 ON KOSOVO ACADEMY OF SCIENCES AND ARTS

LAW No. 05/L-038 ON KOSOVO ACADEMY OF SCIENCES AND ARTS

LAW No. 05/L-038

### ON KOSOVO ACADEMY OF SCIENCES AND ARTS

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON KOSOVO ACADEMY OF SCIENCES AND ARTS

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

The purpose The purpose of this law is to regulate the organization and of the other aspects of overall functioning of the Kosovo Academy of Sciences and Arts (hereinafter: the Academy).

#### Article 2

The establishment and status

\1. The Academy is the highest institution of science and arts, established by the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The Republic of Kosovo provides conditions for work and development of the Academy.

\3. The Academy is an independent institution in the field of science and art.

#### Article 3

The activity of the Academy

\1. The activity of the Academy is activity of special public interest in the Republic of Kosova.

\2. The special public interest of the Academy is expressed in exercising its activity in the field of science and art, which includes promotion, cultivation, stimulation and development of scientific opinion and of artistic creativity.

#### Article 4

The residency and emblem of the Academy

\1. The residency of the Academy is in Prishtina.

\2. The Academy has its own emblem.

#### Article 5

The legal subjectivity The academy is public legal entity with rights, duties and responsibilities set with this law and internal acts of the Academy.

#### Article 6

The statute

\1. The statute of the Academy defines, in compliance with this law, its organization, manner of electing members and working and governance bodies of the Academy.

\2. The statute of the Academy is adopted by the Assembly of the Kosovo Academy of Sciences and Arts with 2/3 of votes of all members.

\3. The Academy has other general acts, by which, in harmony with the statute of the Academy, shall regulate important issues for work.

#### Article 7

The funding

\1. The work of the Academy is funded by:

1.1. the budget of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. the funds earned by implementation of scientific and artistic projects;

1.3. other legitimate donations and resources.

\1. The purpose of the Academy is development, stimulation, promotion and advancement of science and arts in Kosovo, through:

1.1. the implementation of scientific projects with interest on social, economic and cultural development of the country;

1.2. the care for creation and advancement of scientific staff, especially of new generation;

1.3. gathering, systematization, preservation and study of materials with special scientific interest, especially of those related to the cultural inheritance and the past of Kosovo;

1.5. assessment of current situation and proposal of measures for advancement of scientific opinion and quality increase of the artistic creativity;

1.6. reviewing general problems from the fields of scientific and artistic creativity;

Working bodies and cooperation

\1. In order to advance the scientific-research and artistic work with overall interest for science and art in Kosovo, the Academy shall establish its sections, research institutions and study centres.

\2. The Academy in implementation of its functions cooperates with scientific and academic institutions of higher education, scientific institutions, economic entities, state agencies and with employees and scientific researchers from different fields of science and art.

### CHAPTER III

### ORGANIZATION AND LEADERSHIP OF THE ACADEMY

#### Article 11

Organization and leadership

\1. The scientific and artistic activity in the Academy is developed within sections, research institution and other study centers.

\2. The sections are holders of rights, duties and responsibilities of the Academy for scientific and artistic fields, for which they are established.

\3. In the Academy sections act regular and part-time members of the relevant scientific and artistic fields.

\4. Regular and part-time member of the Academy may be part of only one (1) section.

\5. The work of the section is led by the Secretary of the section.

\6. The work research institutions and other study centers is led by director or the relevant leading body.

\7. Organization and work of sections, research institutions and of other study centres of the Academy, is determined with the Statute of the Academy and with special normative acts.

#### Article 12

The bodies of the Academy

\1. The bodies of the Academy are:

1.1. Assembly;

1.2. Chairmanship; and

1.3. Chairman.

#### Article 13

Assembly of the Academy

\1. Assembly is the highest decision making body of the Academy.

\2. The Assembly consists of all regular and part-time members of the Academy.

\3. In the work of Assembly can participate also its external members of the Academy, without the voting right.

\4. Session of the Assembly of the Academy is convened by:

4.1. Chairmanship of the Academy;

4.2. at least two (2) sections of the Academy; and

4.3. ten (10) regular and part-time members of the Academy.

#### Article 14

Duties and responsibilities of the Assembly of the Academy

\1. Duties and responsibilities of the Assembly of the Academy include:

1.1. approval of the Statute of the Academy;

1.2. approval of instructions, regulations and other normative acts of the Academy;

1.3. establishment of sections, research institutions and study centers;

1.4. approval of the Academy’s work program;

1.5. approval of the annual budget, financial plan and of final accounts of the Academy;

1.6. approval of the work report of the Academy;

1.7. election of regular and part-time members, external and honorary members of the Academy;

1.8. approval of the election of secretaries of the Academy sections;

1.9. other obligations defined by this law and with the Statute of the Academy.

#### Article 15

The decision making

\1. The Assembly of the Academy can be gathered and decide by the presence of the majority of regular and part-time members of the Academy in it.

\2. For election of Chairman, Deputy-Chairman and of the Academy’s Scientific Secretary, for selection approval of secretaries of the Academy sections, and for election of part-time members, external members and honorary members of the Academy, it is necessary the majority of votes of all regular and part-time members of the Academy.

#### Article 16

The Academy’s chairmanship

\1. The Academy’s chairmanship comprise of: Chairman, Deputy chairman, scientific Secretary and secretaries of the Academy sections.

\2. The Academy’s chairmanship coordinates general works from the area of its responsibilities.

\3. The Academy’s chairmanship for its work responds to the Assembly of the Academy.

\4. The chairmanship implements decisions and attitudes of the Assembly.

\5. The Academy members over seventy five (75) years of age cannot exercise leading functions.

\6. The Academy members exercising political and state functions cannot exercise leading functions at the Academy.

\7. The Academy’s chairmanship is elected for a four-year (4) period with the right of being reelected for another mandate.

### CHAPTER IV

### THE ACADEMY MEMBERS

#### Article 18

Members

\1. The Academy has regular members, part-time members, external members and honorary members.

\2. Being member of the Academy is eternal.

#### Article 19

The regular member

\1. The regular member of the Academy can be elected scientific or art employee, for special achievements in the field of science and art.

\2. Only the regular members of the Academy can carry the title Academic.

\3. The regular member of the Academy can be elected a citizen of Kosova who is distinguished science employee and of art that has scientific and artistic achievements, whose works represent culminant realizations in the field of science or art and are very valuable inside and outside the country.

#### Article 20

Part-time member Part-time member of the Academy can be elected employee of science or art, citizen of Kosovo, who has created scientific and artistic works of special significance.

#### Article 21

External member

\1. External member of the Academy can be elected employee of science or art living and acting

outside the country, who has created significant work for Kosovo, or with general value for science or art.

\2. The statute of regular and part-time member is accepted for external member of the Academy in accordance with the provisions of the Statute of the Academy.

#### Article 22

Honorary member Honorary member of the Academy can be elected distinguished creators of science or of art, who is not citizen of Kosovo, who with its work has become generally known to science or art and who has contributed to Kosovo.

#### Article 23

The election procedure

\1. The procedure of proposal, voting and election of the Academy members is defined with statute and other normative acts of the Academy.

\2. The elections for members of the Academy are conducted every four (4) years.

\3. The Academy members can be elected candidates, who have not passed the age of sixty five (65).

#### Article 24

The rights and duties The regular and part-time member of the Academy takes duties and responsibilities and enjoys the rights defined with this law and with the Academy statute.

#### Article 25

Rewards

\1. The regular and part-time member of the Academy with permanent residence in the country is entitled to permanent monthly reward.

\2. The height of reward for regular and part-time member of the Academy is based on decision of the Government of Kosovo no. 02/211 of August 16, 2006 and will change depending on decisions for salary height of regular and associate university professors.

\3. The chairmanship members are entitled to monthly allowances for the performed functions.

\4. The criteria and height of members’ monthly allowance of the Academy for the functions they perform at the Academy, are set according to the legislation in force.

### CHAPTER V

### ADMINISTRATIVE, PROFESSIONAL AND TECHNICAL SERVICES

#### Article 26

The services of the Academy

\1. Administrative, professional and technical services are performed by the administration of the Academy.

\2. Secretary of the Academy leads the administration of the Academy.

\3. Secretary of the Academy is elected by the Academy Chairmanship with public competition.

\4. For its work, the Secretary of the Academy responds to Chairman and Chairmanship of the Academy.

\5. The employees in administrative, professional and technical services have the status of civil servants, which is adjusted with legislation on civil service and with the Statute of the Academy.

### CHAPTER VI

### FINANCING AND MEANS OF THE ACADEMY

#### Article 27

Financial sources

\1. Financial sources for the work of the Academy are provided by the Kosovo budget.

\2. With means from the Kosovo budget, work of sections is supported, research institutions, other study centres of the Academy, work of governing bodies of the Academy, international cooperation of the Academy, regular and periodical publishing activity of the Academy, enrichment of the library fund of the Academy, permanent monthly remuneration of the regular and part time members, allowances for performance of certain functions at the Academy, permanent monthly salaries for employees in administrative, professional, technical services and maintenance of the Academy.

\3. The Academy can provide means also from donations and sponsorships and such means can be used for its supplementary activity.

\4. The Academy can provide other supplementary means for scientific research and artistic work of its regular and part time members through its activities, scientific research and artistic mutual agreements and through donations of gifts according to the legislation in force.

#### Article 28

The ownership of the Academy

\1. The Academy is owner of the movable and immovable property which is given to it to perform its activity.
