# LAW NO. 05/L-106 ON THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR FINANCING THE MODERNIZATION PROJECT OF INVASIVE CARDIOLOGY SERVICES AT THE UNIVERSITY CLINICAL CENTRE OF KOSOVO

### LAW NO. 05/L-106

### ON THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR FINANCING THE MODERNIZATION PROJECT OF INVASIVE

### CARDIOLOGY SERVICES AT THE UNIVERSITY CLINICAL CENTRE OF KOSOVO

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR FINANCING THE MODERNIZATION PROJECT OF INVASIVE

### CARDIOLOGY SERVICES AT THE UNIVERSITY CLINICAL CENTRE OF KOSOVO

#### Article 1

The purpose of the law This law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement, signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for Financing Modernization Project of Invasive Cardiology Services in the University Clinical Centre of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The loan agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for financing the Modernization Project of Invasive Cardiology Services of the University Clinical Centre of Kosovo, signed on 29 October 2015 in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In Annex. 1 of this Law is attached the loan agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for financing the Project for Modernization Service of Invasive Cardiology at the University Clinical Center of Kosovo, signed on 29 October 2015, in Pristina.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 05/L-106 4 August 2016 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-032-2016, dated 23.08.2016, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
