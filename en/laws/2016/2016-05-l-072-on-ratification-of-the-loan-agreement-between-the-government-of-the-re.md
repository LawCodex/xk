# LAW NO. 05/L -072 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ON RAILWAY ROUTE 10 REHABILITATION PROJECT

LAW No. 05/L-072 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT ON RAILWAY ROUTE 10 REHABILITATION PROJECT

LAW No. 05/L -072

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND

### DEVELOPMENT ON RAILWAY ROUTE 10 REHABILITATION PROJECT

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT

### OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND

### DEVELOPMENT ON RAILWAY ROUTE 10 REHABILITATION PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development of Railway Route 10 Rehabilitation Project.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The loan agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development is ratified on railway route 10 rehabilitation project, entered into 04 September 2015 in London.

#### Article 3

Documents attached

\1. In Annex 1 of this Law there is attached to the loan agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development on railway route 10 rehabilitation project.

\2. In Annex. 2 of this Law there are attached the standard terms and conditions of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, dated December 1, 2012.

LAW No. 05/L-072 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
