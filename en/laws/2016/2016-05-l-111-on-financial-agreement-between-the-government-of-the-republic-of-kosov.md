# LAW NO. 05/L-111 ON FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK ON THE PROJECT FOR REHABILITATION OF THE RAILWAY LINE 10

### LAW NO. 05/L-111 ON FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK ON THE PROJECT FOR REHABILITATION OF THE RAILWAY LINE 10

### LAW NO. 05/L-111

### ON FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK ON THE PROJECT FOR REHABILITATION OF THE RAILWAY

### LINE 10

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Pursuant to Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK ON THE PROJECT FOR REHABILITATION OF THE

### RAILWAY LINE 10

#### Article 1

The purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financial Agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank for the Rehabilitation Project of the Railway Line 10, concluded on 8 December 2015.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement The Financial loan agreement is ratified between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank on the Project for the Rehabilitation of the Railway Line 10, concluded on 8 December 2015, in Pristina.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In Annex of this Law is attached The Financial Agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank on the Project for Rehabilitation of the railway line 10, concluded on 8 December 2015, in Pristina.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 05/L-111 4 August 2016 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-030-2016, dated 23.08.2016, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
