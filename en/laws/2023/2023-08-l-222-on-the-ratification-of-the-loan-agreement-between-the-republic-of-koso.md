# LAW NO. 08/L-222 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE “PRISTINA SOLAR HEATING PLANT” PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-222 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR

### RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR THE “PRISTINA SOLAR HEATING PLANT” PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-222

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR

### THE “PRISTINA SOLAR HEATING PLANT” PROJECT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT FOR

### THE “PRISTINA SOLAR HEATING PLANT” PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement for the Pristina Solar Heating Plant Project, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Loan Agreement for the Solar Heating Plant Project of Pristina, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, is attached to the Appendix of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-222 23 November 2023 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-192/2023 dated 06.12.2023 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
