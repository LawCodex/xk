# LAW NO. 08/L-194 ON CENTRAL CRIMINAL RECORDS SYSTEM OF KOSOVO

### LAW NO. 08/L-194 ON CENTRAL CRIMINAL RECORDS SYSTEM OF KOSOVO

### LAW NO. 08/L-194

### ON CENTRAL CRIMINAL RECORDS SYSTEM OF KOSOVO

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON CENTRAL CRIMINAL RECORDS SYSTEM OF KOSOVO

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law regulates the central criminal records system of the Republic of Kosovo, the establishment of structural institutional mechanisms of the system, maintenance and release of data, as well as the procedure for rehabilitation of convicted persons and access to this information, including the relevant deadlines.

2.This Law is in accordance with:

2.1. Regulation (EU) 2019/816 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 establishing a centralised system for the identification of Member States holding conviction information on third-country nationals and stateless persons (ECRIS-TCN) to supplement the European Criminal Records Information System and amending Regulation (EU) 2018/1726;

2.2. Council Decision 2009/316/JHA of 6 April 2009 on the establishment of the European Criminal Records Information System (ECRIS) in application of Article 11 of Council Framework Decision 2009/315/JHA of 26 February 2009 on the organization and content of the exchange of information extracted from the criminal record between the Member States.

#### Article 2

Scope This Law shall apply to all institutions of the Republic of Kosovo, the judicial system of the Republic of Kosovo which shall enable the connection of the data system as well as the respective authorizations of the institutions in this data and it shall determine the manner of keeping, processing, exchanging and distributing this data from central criminal records for the subjects of the law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

1.Terms used in this Law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Criminal Records System (CRS) - a central unified institutional hierarchical structure, which manages the database of convicted persons in the Republic of Kosovo, as defined by this Law and relevant legislation in force;

1.2. Criminal Records- evidence from a national register that registers a final decision of a criminal court regarding punishments of natural and legal persons in connection with a criminal offense;

1.3. Certificate on Criminal Punishments- a document issued by CRS for natural and legal persons, based on their request or based on a request of another competent body which verifies the data on the punishments of a certain person;

1.4. Extract E1- a document issued by CRS for the purpose of criminal proceedings, which contains complete records of convictions, including expunged criminal records of a person’s punishment;

1.5. Extract E2 - the document issued for the purpose of administrative proceedings, which contains certain data on the conviction of a person that has not been expunged. Exceptionally, in this extract there shall be stated even the data on expunged punishments, as defined in Article 27 of this Law;

1.6. Punishment- any final criminal punishment imposed in criminal proceedings by the competent court against natural and legal persons;

1.7. Criminal file- personal data on the perpetrator of the criminal offense, data on the punishment, court reprimand, measure of compulsory treatment or release of the perpetrator from the punishment imposed; changes in the data on the punishments that have been registered in the criminal file and the data on the punishments served as well as on the expungement of the punishment;

1.8. Foreign person- any natural person and legal person who is not a citizen of Kosovo;

1.9. KJC- Judicial Council of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.10. Stateless person- a person who is not considered a citizen of any state as defined in the respective law on citizenship.

1.11. OCE- Office of Criminal Evidence functions as a unit of the court of first instance;

1.12. Criminal Procedure –as defined in the Criminal Procedure Code.

### CHAPTER II

### ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND COMPETENCES OF CRS

#### Article 4

Criminal Records System

\1. The Central Criminal Records System of the Republic of Kosovo functions as a department within the KJC, and is responsible for all issues related to criminal records in the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The organizational structure, duties and responsibilities of the respective department shall be determined with a sub-legal act issued by the KJC.

#### Article 5

Functions of CRS

\1. CRS has the following functions:

1.1. develops, promotes and maintains the database containing the records of punishments

of natural and legal persons in accordance with the requirements of this law and other laws in force;

1.2. manages the database, in accordance with the provisions of this law and other laws in force;

1.3. determines procedures for recording punishments in the database;

1.4. registers in the database all existing criminal records from the first instance courts of Kosovo;

1.5. registers and maintains the punishments of foreigners by the Courts of Kosovo;

1.6. registers the punishments of natural and legal persons for Kosovo citizens by foreign courts in accordance with the respective legislation into force;

1.7. issues criminal records certificates to all natural and legal persons requesting this information for themselves as defined by this law and the laws in force;

1.8. provides information on criminal records, at the request of the courts, the State Prosecutor’s Office, the Police and other bodies authorized by law in relation to any natural or legal person in accordance with the laws in force;

1.9. expungement of all criminal punishments from the database in accordance with the decisions in the judicial and legal rehabilitation procedure in accordance with the relevant legislation;

1.10. exchanges information through the relevant unit in the Ministry of Justice with foreign countries, regarding the registers of punishment of natural and legal persons, in accordance with the relevant legislation in force;

1.11. implements other rules and procedures necessary for the implementation of this law.

Relevant procedures will be regulated with a sub-legal act issued by the KJC.

### CHAPTER III

### DATA MANAGEMENT IN CRS

#### Article 6

Keeping of criminal records Criminal records are kept in the form of an electronic file, and if necessary, also in physical form.

#### Article 7

The role of the Ministry of Justice in CRS

\1. The Ministry of Justice has full access in the CRS.

\2. The access from paragraph 1 of this Article is related to the duties and responsibilities within the scope of the Ministry of Justice and is exercised through the respective units.

\3. The access from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined with sub-legal act issued by the Ministry of Justice.

\4. The Ministry of Justice has no right to interfere or intervene in the CRS.

#### Article 8

Information security within the CRS

\1. The criminal records register is kept in the form of an electronic file and is maintained with a high level of security against any data damage or loss, in accordance with the relevant legislation on prevention and combat of cyber crimes, as well as the relevant legislation on personal data protection.

\2. Only the authorized personnel of the relevant department (CRS) shall have access to register, modify and update data in CRS in accordance with this Law and relevant legislation in force.

#### Article 9

Decisions and acts entered in the CRS

\1. The CRS shall ensure that all punishments from Kosovo courts and foreign courts for citizens of Kosovo are entered in the database, which are managed, used and served in the proper manner, as provided by this law and relevant legislation in force.

\2. Data related to the punishment of persons are recorded based on the following decisions:

2.1. final decision in a criminal case by a Kosovo court;

2.2. final foreign court decision in a criminal case against a natural or legal person that has a residence permit in Kosovo, if a court of Kosovo has recognized the judgment on the punishment, or it is recognized directly in accordance with binding agreements for the Republic of Kosovo;

2.3. a final court decision ordering an alternative punishment or accessory punishment has been imposed;

2.4. final court decision on the application of the sanction in relation to a person younger than eighteen (18) years of age;

2.5. decisions resulting from extraordinary remedies and affecting penalties already entered in the database;

2.6. annulment of an unreasonable punishment;

2.7. notification by the court execution department of the execution of a punishment in all its elements;

2.8. decision of the panel approving the request for conditional release;

2.9. presidential act of pardon of the convicted person;

2.10. amnesty act valid for the convicted person;

2.11. administrative decision on legal rehabilitation;

2.12. court decision on judicial rehabilitation.

\3. Measures and punishments for minors, as determined by the provisions of the Juvenile Justice Code after the passage of a period of one (1) year from the date of termination of the measure if during this period, no new punishment or measure has been imposed.

\4. Decisions regarding the measures imposed on persons with mental disorders, when the treatment

#### Article 10

Type of data in the CRS

\1. The type of data that is entered in the CRS is as follows:

1.1. personal data of the convicted person;

1.2. details on the punishment;

1.3. data on criminal sanctions as well as measures and punishments imposed on juveniles;

1.4. data on the execution of sanctions;

1.5. rehabilitation data;

1.6. data on pardon, amnesty and similar acts after the punishment that affect the legal status of the convicted persons as well as all other data proceeded according Article 9 of this Law;

1.7. as well as any additional data related to the case.

2.6. citizenship;

2.7. personal identification number (PIN);

2.8. previous name and PIN, in case of any change;

2.9. any additional data related to the subject of the law.

\3. The optional data to be entered in the database are as follows:

3.1. name and surname of the parents;

3.3. nickname;

\4. In case of a foreign natural person the following data shall be entered in the database:

4.4. citizenship;

4.6. date and validity of the temporary residence permit, as defined by the applicable Law on Foreigners;

#### Article 12

\1. The data of convicted legal persons that is recorded in the database is as follows:

1.1. name of company;

1.2. business registration number;

1.3. the seat of the legal person

1.5. details of the responsible person;

1.6. any other data, as provided by the relevant Law on Liability of Legal Persons for Criminal Offenses;

1.7. any additional information related to the legal person.

\2. Every change of the data of legal persons must be updated in the database.

#### Article 13

The punishment data

\1. The database contains the following data on punishments, including the execution of the decision:

1.1. data on punishment;

1.4. data on the expungement of the imposed punishment;

1.5. any additional information related to the punishment.

\2. The data related to the conviction of a person contains the following elements:

\1. Data on sanctions contains the following elements:

2.4. date of completion of serving the imprisonment;

2.5. date of commencement and completion of serving accessory punishments;

2.6. basis for imposing and end date of probationary period;

2.7. date of commencement and termination of treatment with compulsory psychiatric measures or other similar measures;

2.8. the date of pardon, amnesty, conditional release or any other similar act for the convicted person;

2.9. any additional information related to the sanction.

### CHAPTER IV

### REHABILITATION AND EXPUNGEMENT OF PUNISHMENTS

#### Article 16

Legal Rehabilitation

\1. The CRS is the authority that shall take the administrative decision on legal rehabilitation, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Code of Kosovo. The CRS shall register in the database the legal basis for expungement and technically expunge the punishment.

\2. The CRS may establish an automatic expungement process for certain categories of punishments, in accordance with the requirements of the Criminal Code and the Criminal Procedure Code of Kosovo.

\3. Expungement of punishments from the database shall be handled in accordance with the legal provisions of the Criminal Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure and shall include a two-stage process, as follows:

3.1. Description of the legal basis for expungement in the database;

3.2. Changing the status of the punishment in the database, to a “expunged” punishment.

\4. The administrative decision in cases of legal rehabilitation of criminal punishments is taken by the director of the relevant department after reaching the relevant legal deadline and the notification of the relevant CRS official in the Basic Court or in the Branch.

\5. The notification from paragraph 4 of this article, must be properly explained and referring to the completion of all elements and legal requirements set out by law and supported by relevant documents.

\6. In any case of the rehabilitation procedure, the relevant department shall change the status of

the punishment in the database to the expunged punishment, within forty-eight (48) hours upon receiving the information on the final decision which constitutes the legal basis for the expungement.

\7. The change of the status of the punishment to the expunged punishment must be double-check verified, respectively by an official who changes the status and by an official who approves it.

\8. The CRS shall undertake all necessary actions for the expungement of punishments from the register of punishments which are not considered as criminal offences by the legislation in force.

#### Article 17

Judicial rehabilitation

\1. After taking the decision for judicial rehabilitation, as provided by the legislation in force, the CRS shall take the necessary administrative action on judicial rehabilitation, as defined by the provisions of the Criminal Code of Kosovo. The CRS shall register the relevant court expungement decision in the database and technically expunge the punishment.

\2. The provisions of Article 16 of this Law regarding the registration shall be applied appropriately in the case of judicial rehabilitation, unless otherwise provided by the relevant legislation in force.

#### Article 18

Rehabilitation data

\1. The data on the expungement of punishments is as follows:

1.1. date of the final ruling on legal rehabilitation;

1.2. date of the final court ruling on judicial rehabilitation.

#### Article 19

The consequences of the punishment expungement

1.The expunged punishments are not reflected in the criminal records certificates issued by the

### CRS.

\2. The expunged punishments shall be kept in the database, and can be used only for the purpose of criminal proceedings (Extract E1) and other purposes as defined by law (Extract E2).

\3. In cases where a punishment has been expunged, the data on such punishment shall be disclosed only to the court, state prosecutor and police.

\4. KJC shall keep a public register for the persons from paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article, where the data include the name and surname of the convicted person, year of birth and residence, hereby these data are accessible to public on the KJC webpage.

#### Article 21

Indirect access to data in the CRS

\1. The following institutions shall have the right to receive data from the CRS:

1.1. the authorized competent institutions responsible for the execution of criminal sanctions;

1.2. the competent institutions involved in the procedure of pardon, conditional release, early release or judicial rehabilitation;

1.3. relevant public institutions as provided by law, if accessory punishments or measures of mandatory treatment are still in force at the time of the request;

1.4. foreign and international institutions within the framework and in accordance with the procedures provided by the mechanisms and international cooperation agreements that are binding to the Republic of Kosovo;

1.5. other institutions established by law;

1.6. as well as legal representative or the authorized representative of the juvenile.

#### Article 22

Restrictions on the use of data issued by the CRS

\1. The classified data included in the register is treated in accordance with the relevant Law on classification of information and security verification.

\2. The data from paragraph 1 of this Article for the criminal punishment can be used only for the purpose for which it was issued.

#### Article 23

The right of access to personal information is possession of public authorities for the purposes of Extracts E1 and E2

\1. The subject of the data or his authorized person shall have the right to access the content of Extracts E1 and E2 in the presence of the relevant registry official.

\2. The content of the extracts may not be photographed, copied or otherwise reproduced.

\3. The copy shall be destroyed by the relevant official in the presence of the subject of the data or his authorized person. The relevant official shall keep a note of the destroyed extract and shall register it in the system.

### CHAPTER VI

### LEVELS OF EXTRACTS FROM THE CRIMINAL EVIDENCE REGISTER

#### Article 24

Levels of Extracts

\1. At the request of the authorized public body, the relevant department shall issue information on the criminal status of the person in the form of a criminal record extract, in accordance with the Criminal Code of Kosovo and relevant legislation in force.

\2. In addition to the Certificate, the relevant department issues two types of extracts of criminal punishments, namely Extract E1 for the purposes of criminal proceedings and Extract E2 for the purposes of administrative proceedings.

#### Article 25

Certificate on Criminal Punishments

\1. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment is issued at the request of the natural or legal person. The certificate is obtained online or in hard copy at any first instance court or branch of the court in the entire territory of the Republic of Kosovo, regardless of the place of residence or stay of the person requesting such certificate.

2.The certificate from paragraph 1 of this article can be obtained by the person personally or by his/ her legal representative or authorized person with power of attorney or authorization.

\3. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment serves for the needs of natural and legal persons, to prove the criminal past in relation to public or private authorities or for any other needs of the person in question.

\4. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment states only the final status punishments which have not been expunged.

\5. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment does not contain any information about a criminal case or procedure that is ongoing in any court in Kosovo.

\6. Any information that contains the protected personal data is not included in the certificate.

\7. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment is issued in Albanian and Serbian language.

\8. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment is delivered to the person at the time of application, as soon as possible and without any delay, based only on the data that exists in the criminal database.

\9. The Certificate on Criminal Punishment is valid for three (3) months from the date of issuance.

\10. The content and design of the certificate and Extract E1 and E2 are regulated with a sub-legal act issued by the KJC.

#### Article 26

Extract E1 The CRS shall, upon the requests of relevant justice and security institutions, issue Extract E1, which contains information on the criminal past of the convicted persons, including even the data on the expunged punishments in conformity with the rules and limitations determined in the Criminal Code of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 27

Extract E2

\1. The CRS shall issue Extract E2 at the requests of State Institutions. This extract contains only the data on punishments that have not been expunged.

### CHAPTER VII

### PROCEDURE FOR ISSUANCE OF CERTIFICATES ON CRIMINAL PUNISHMENTS

#### Article 28

Request for data from the CRS

\1. The request of natural persons for a certificate shall contain the following data:

1.1. name and surname;

1.2. date of birth and personal number of the person for whom the data is requested;

1.3. identification document.

\2. The request of legal entities for a certificate shall contain the following data:

2.1. name of business and business registration number of the legal entity for which the information is requested;

2.2. name and personal number of the applicant;

2.3. authorization of the responsible person of the legal person.

\3. The request for information made by the competent authorities regarding the criminal procedure or in other cases as regulated by the relevant laws, is made through the form determined by the KJC. The request must contain all the information as regulated under paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article. The requests must contain the reasoning that reflects the legal basis for the right to receive information, as regulated in the relevant law in force.

\4. The form of the request from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article is determined with a sub-legal act issued by the KJC.

\2. The Appeal shall be submitted to the body responsible for the CRS and then it shall be forwarded to the Agency for Personal Data Protection, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant Law on Personal Data Protection.

#### Article 33

Procedure for expungement from the register of criminal evidence

\1. The party whose punishment has not been expunged in accordance with the legal deadlines, shall have the right to request the expungement of the punishment from the database.

\2. The request for expungement of the punishment according to paragraph 1 of this Article shall be addressed to the KJC, through the official of criminal evidence of the respective first instance court.

\3. The relevant department in KJC must, within five (5) days from the submission of the request, issue a ruling on approval or rejection of the request from paragraph 2 of this Article.

\4. The CRS shall inform the party immediately on the measures taken.

\5. The dissatisfied party with the final decision of the CRS shall have the right to initiate an administrative dispute in the competent court as defined by law.

#### Article 34

Data correction and notification

\1. The inaccurate data shall be corrected based on a procedure which is determined with a sublegal act issued by the KJC. Supporting documents for correction shall be recorded and kept in the file or in any other form in the court archives.

\2. The interested parties shall submit a request to the CRS if their data needs to be corrected. After the correction of the criminal data, the CRS shall notify the party within thirty (30) days.

\3. CRS records in the system every intervention or update of data in the registers.

### CHAPTER X

Cooperation of the Civil Registration Agency with the Department of Criminal Records System

\2. The personal identification number shall be considered mandatory information for natural persons for all criminal cases in the Kosovo courts.

#### Article 36

The CRS and the Kosovo Agency of Statistics The CRS exchanges statistical data with the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, for the needs of use in accordance with the relevant legislation in force.

#### Article 37

The CRS and the Business Registration Agency

\1. With the purpose of exchange of the data for the convicted legal persons, the CRS Department and Business Registration Agency shall regularly ensure the exchange of data for legal persons.

### CHAPTER XI

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 39

Sub-legal acts

\1. Sub-legal acts determined in this Law shall be issued within six (6) months after the entry into force of this Law.

\2. KJC shall, within six (6) months after the entry into force of this Law, functionalize and begin the maintenance of public registers determined in Article 20 of this Law.

#### Article 40

Transitional provisions

\1. Upon entry into force of this law, the CRS shall conduct the legal rehabilitation altogether of all convicted persons, who are registered in the register and who according to the law should have been rehabilitated.

\2. The CRS, notwithstanding paragraph 1 of this Article, shall ensure the expungement of all punishments which have met the conditions for legal rehabilitation.

\3. Interaction between CRS and CIMS shall be established in such a way that as soon as the CIMS system is fully functional in the courts, the registration of criminal data is automatically uploaded to the CRS database.

\4. The CRS shall update the data in the register ensuring that all convicted persons who according to the legislation have the right to rehabilitation are expunged ex-officio from the register.

#### Article 41

Repeal

\1. Upon entry into force of this Law, the Regulation No.11/2022 on the Criminal Evidence in the Republic of Kosovo shall be repealed.

\2. Upon entry into force of this Law, there shall be repealed every other legal provision that determined the provision or issuance of certificates related to the criminal records.

#### Article 42

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
