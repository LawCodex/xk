# LAW NO. 08/L-153 ON EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-153 ON EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-153

### ON EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION

#### Article 1

The purpose The purpose of this Law is to provide comprehensive quality education in all forms of organization of care and education in early childhood for children from birth to the age of six (6) as well as determines the norms for the organization, functioning, supervision, institutional and professional responsibilities of all stakeholders on early childhood education.

#### Article 2

Scope

\1. This law shall be implemented by all institutions of Early Childhood Education, for children from birth to the age of six (6), ensuring:

1.1. the right of all children to inclusive quality education based on their individual needs;

1.2. children’s health, wellbeing and protection;

1.3. establishment of mechanisms for ensuring the provision of integrated services;

1.4. determination of the duties and responsibilities of service providers in Early Childhood Education;

1.5. mentoring, monitoring, inspection, financing and sanctioning of the service provider;

1.6. determination of mechanisms for establishing, monitoring and implementation of quality standards in all levels.

\2. Cross-sectoral cooperation and coordination between institutions at central and local level, and relevant actors in the field of Early Childhood Education.

#### Article 3

Guiding Principles for early childhood education

\1. Guiding principles for early childhood education, are:

1.1. the universal right to education for all children;

1.2. the child`s best interest as dominant consideration;

1.3. the child’s right to participate;

1.4. play-based education in accordance with the child’s development;

1.5. overall development of the child while respecting the individuality of each child;

1.6. equal opportunities for children and parents/legal guardians and continued involvement of parents/legal guardians in Early Childhood Education;

1.7. equality and non-discrimination without any distinction, regardless of race, color, gender, language, religion, national, ethnic or social origin, wealth, disability, family background or any other condition of the child or his/her parents/legal guardians;

1.8. inclusion, diversity and values of democracy, love, respect, cooperation, responsibility, tolerance, solidarity and empathy;

1.9. child’s freedom of expression;

1.10. prevention of all types of violence;

1.11. responsibility and accountability.

#### Article 4

Definitions

\1. Expressions used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Early Childhood Education: refers to the education of children to stimulate them to reach their full potential from birth until children enter the first grade of primary school.

1.2. Early Childhood Education in the family: means the education, care and development of children in the family environment carried out by parents/legal guardians with or without support provided by institutions responsible for the development of children at an early age.

1.3. Violence: means all physical and/or emotional forms of abuse, sexual abuse, neglect or negligent treatment, commercial exploitation or any kind of exploitation that results in potential or actual damage to health, survival, development or dignity of the child in context of the report of responsibility, trust, or force. Violence includes but is not limited to acts or intentional acts committed by one person against another, such as: the use of physical force, psychological pressure, any action that causes or threatens physical and mental pain; causing feelings of fear, personal danger, violation of dignity; physical attack regardless of the consequences;

insulting, cursing, calling offensive names and other ways of harassing; constant repetition of behaviours in order to humiliate the other person; putting the other person in a position to fear for their physical, emotional and economic condition.

1.4. Inclusion: is a concept of quality education based on rights that emphasizes equality or equal opportunities in access and participation and responds positively to the individual needs and competencies of all children without distinction. This means that program contents, methodologies, strategies and infrastructure are in line with individual needs of children so that each child develops his/her potentials to the maximum.

1.5. Early Childhood Identification: refers to the process by which developmental risks or difficulties are identified, including disabilities and developmental delays in the early childhood years.

1.6. The Individual Education Plan (IEP) is an official pedagogical document which is drafted for children with special educational needs, for whom the assessment and support team of various developmental fields has made the decision for education with special needs.

1.7. International Standard Classification of Education - ISCED: approved by UNESCO at the

General Conference in its 29th session in November 1997 and amended in 2011. ISCED is an instrument for collecting, compiling and presenting education statistics within countries as well as internationally.

1.8. Level zero (0): According to ISCED 2011, level 0 includes children from nine (9) months until the moment they start level 1, primary education. Level 0 includes preschool education (from nine (9) months to five (5) years old) organized in preschool institutions; and preprimary education (age five (5) until starting primary education, level 1) organized in preschool institutions and primary schools.

1.9. Early childhood education service providers: means all providers including early childhood education institutions, nannies, parents/legal guardians who provide early childhood education.

1.11. Preschool institution: offers education in early childhood, for children from nine (9)

1.11.1. Public Early Childhood Education Institutions: means the institution financed from the budget of the Republic of Kosova and managed by the municipal directorates of education (MED);

1.11.2. Private Early Childhood Education Institutions: means the institution that is financed and managed by legal entities and natural persons;

1.11.3. Early Childhood Education Institutions with public-private partnership: means institutions which are established with mixed capital determined by agreement between the public authority and the legal entity according to the legislation in force.

1.11.4. Early Childhood Education Institutions, organized within central, local institutions, various corporations: It is an alternative form which means the provision of early childhood education services, which are established/licensed and managed by central, local institutions or various corporations, depending on the founder. They are adapted within the above mentioned facilities or even separately, within the space where they operate, which meet the conditions provided by the legislation in force and as such serve for the inclusion of children and staff employed in these institutions and beyond.

1.11.5. Community-based Early Childhood Education Institutions: means the provision of early childhood education services organized and managed by the community in agreement with the municipality and in accordance with the standards approved by the competent institutions. These can be established inside and outside school facilities.

1.12. Primary schools: provide pre-university education regulated by the relevant law on pre-university education, part of which includes pre-primary education, and in cases where schools have adapted conditions for organizing pre-school education for children from three

(3) years of age.

1.13. Pre-primary education: is an educational program for children aged five (5) which is offered within the primary school and in preschool institutions.

1.14. Professional Nanny: certified person engaged by families for the care, development and education of the child at an early age at house conditions.

1.15. Parent: means a person(s) who have given birth to or adopted a child, both together or alone, married or unmarried, or who have recognized the maternity or paternity of a child born outside marriage.

1.16. Legal guardian: means the person who has the same responsibilities to care for the child, as a parent who has full legal and physical custody of the child.

1.17. Professional associate: means the educational/training service provided by professional associates including, but not limited to: pedagogue, psychologist and speech therapist, in order to improve and advance the work of the early childhood education institution.

1.18. Municipal pedagogical evaluation team: is an evaluation and support team established and organized by Municipalities to respond to the requests and needs of educational institutions to provide inclusive education.

1.19. Assistant: means a qualified person who assists and cares for children with disabilities during their stay in early childhood education services.

1.20. Educational staff: means the qualified person employed in the Early Childhood Education institutions in the organization of the educational process:

1.20.1. Educator: means a person qualified under the legislation in force employed to provide services in early childhood education institutions.

1.20.2. Support educator: the support educator provides professional support for educational staff, children with disabilities and their parents/legal guardians.

1.21. Steering Council: is the body responsible for the governance of the educational or training institution. Equivalent structure of the Steering Council refers to bodies that have the same competencies and responsibilities as the Steering Council.

1.22. Managerial staff: means the staff managing the Early Childhood Education institutions at the leader’s level.

1.23. Administrative staff: means staff engaged in administrative work in Early Childhood Education institutions.

1.24. Technical staff: are kitchen cooks, kitchen cook assistants and maintenance staff.

1.25. The development plan of the institution: determines the priorities of the educational institution based on the possibilities, circumstances and specifics of the educational institution and is drafted by the leaders of the preschool institution for their leadership mandate.

1.26. Annual work plan and program: means the definition of educational content and the manner of its implementation drafted by the educator in cooperation with professional associates based on the curriculum and development plan of the institution approved by the steering council of the Early Childhood Education Institution before the beginning of the new educational year.

1.27. Volunteer in Early Childhood Education: means individuals who provide voluntary work for Early Childhood Education institutions with the approval of the management of the institution and work without payment but their work experience is recognized under legislation in force.

#### Article 5

The Purposes of Early Childhood Education

1.The Purposes of Early Childhood Education are to:

1.1. promote comprehensive development, including the education, health and well-being of every child;

1.2. stimulate physical, cognitive, linguistic, social, emotional and personality development;

1.3. promote value-based education that ensures the development of interpersonal relationships, interaction skills, awareness and emotional self-regulation of the child;

1.4. provide a safe and supportive environment for active child learning through play and promote lifelong learning;

1.5. ensure the involvement of the child’s perspective and participation in decision-making, the development of responsibility for oneself, others and the environment;

1.6. create conditions for identification and referral of the needs and potentials of each child.

#### Article 6

Protection and rights of children

\1. All types of violence are prohibited to all service providers.

\2. All children within the territory of Kosova have the right to protection.

\3. All children within the territory of Kosova have the right to quality education in early childhood.

#### Article 7

Competencies and responsibilities of the competent Ministry of Education

\1. Competencies and responsibilities of the competent Ministry of Education are:

1.1. policy development and drafting of legislation for early childhood education;

1.2. core curriculum development for early childhood education;

1.3. drafting policies for continuous professional development of educational staff;

1.4. development and monitoring of the implementation of policies defining the norms and standards of the premises of educational institutions in early childhood;

1.5. providing technical assistance to municipalities in planning and designing the facilities of Early Childhood Education institutions;

1.6. establishment of mechanisms for early identification and referral of children;

1.7. licensing non-public institutions;

1.8. development of standards for monitoring and evaluating the quality of performance of the preschool institution;

1.9. development and monitoring of national standards of professions involved in early childhood education in cooperation with line ministries;

1.10. development of a code of ethics for educational professions which are regulated by a sub-legal act;

1.11. licensing of educational staff and professional associates for Early Childhood Education in accordance with legislation in force.

#### Article 8

Competences and responsibilities of the municipality

1.Competences and responsibilities of the municipalities are:

1.1. establishment of public Early Childhood Education institutions with the consent of the relevant Ministry of Education;

1.2. establishment of mechanisms for the implementation of policies for early childhood education developed by the relevant Ministry of Education;

1.3. implementation of standards for monitoring and evaluation of the performance of the Institution of Early Childhood Education in their municipalities;

1.4. cooperation with early childhood education institutions, family medicine centres for systematic visits of children and centres for social work.

#### Article 9

Competencies and responsibilities of educational institutions in early childhood

\1. Competencies and responsibilities of educational institutions in early childhood education, are:

1.1. implementation of all policies and legislation approved by the relevant ministry of education and those drafted in cooperation with other institutions for early childhood education;

1.2. creating a safe, stimulating, friendly and supportive environment for quality education and learning through play and interaction with other children;

1.3. ensuring that the food provided is safe and healthy;

1.4. drafting and approving the development and educational plan of the institutions for early childhood education;

1.5. ensuring active participation and cooperation with parents/legal guardians in the design and implementation of the educational and development plan;

1.6. identification and support children with disabilities based on their individual needs;

1.7. referral to the municipal assessment team to assess the child’s needs and further services;

1.8. approval of annual work plans and programs by the steering council before the beginning of the new educational year;

1.9. establishment of the steering council or an equivalent structure;

1.10. creating appropriate conditions and equal opportunities for continuous professional development for educational staff;

1.11. prevention, reporting and referral of all forms of violence to relevant institutions and

referral of violence and elements that affect the development of children, based on the legislation in force.

#### Article 10

Competences and responsibilities of the educational staff

\1. Competences and responsibilities of the educational staff, are:

1.1. planning, implementation and evaluation of the educational plan based on all official documents in accordance with the areas of development, interests and needs of children;

1.2. creating a safe and stimulating environment based on game, exploration and research;

1.3. implementing the decision of the municipal assessment team for children with disabilities;

1.4. creating a portfolio for ongoing assessment to shape the teaching and learning experiences for each child. Portfolio creation is done in collaboration with children and highlights progress, encourages educators to respond to the individual needs of children and is shared with parents/legal guardian;

1.5. creating reports and professional and cooperative communication with parents/legal guardians;

1.6. cooperation with colleagues inside and outside the institution, and with external associates;

1.7. identification, reporting and management of all issues to the competent authorities that may arise during activities related to the health and well-being of the child.

1.8. providing professional contribution to the participation/completion of evaluation, research instruments sent by the relevant ministry of education, municipal directorate of education, or scientific staff.

#### Article 11

Competencies and responsibilities of professional associates and managerial, administrative and technical staff

\1. Early childhood education institutions are run by managerial staff and supported by professional and technical associates.

\2. The competencies and responsibilities of the managerial, professional and administrative staff and technical associates are defined in a sub-legal act.

#### Article 12

Preschool institutions

\1. The forms of organization of preschool institutions are:

1.1. Public preschool institutions;

1.2. Private preschool institutions;

1.3. Preschool institutions in public-private partnership.

#### Article 13

Alternative organization form

\1. Alternative forms are forms of organizing preschool institutions which are organized in order to increase the involvement of children in early childhood education. These forms are regulated in this law and sub-legal acts.

1.1. organization within central, local institutions or various corporations;

1.2. community-based institutions;

1.3. other alternative forms organized in cooperation with the municipality.

#### Article 14

Organization of the program in institutions of Early Childhood Education

\1. Programs in early childhood education institutions are full-time programs and part-time programs;

\2. The programs, their schedules, the educator-child ratio according to the age groups and the regulation of the participation of the children in the Early Childhood Education institutions are determined by a sub-legal act.

#### Article 15

Curricula and programs in Early Childhood Education

\1. The curriculum supports the goals of early childhood education and aims at the learning and comprehensive development of the child.

\2. Educational programs in early childhood education institutions derive from the core curriculum for early childhood education.

#### Article 16

Provision of didactic tools

\1. The Institutions of Early Childhood Education provide educational materials, equipment and educational toys for children, which enable the realization of the educational work plan and program based on the curriculum.

\2. Toys must be certified according to ISO standards.

\3. Educational materials, toys and equipment should not contain any form of discrimination or stereotypes.

\4. Standards for educational materials, books, toys and equipment are regulated with sub-legal act.

#### Article 17

Early childhood education in the family

\1. Early childhood education is also supported in the family for children from birth until the children enter the first grade of primary school.

1.1. the relevant Ministry of Education in cooperation with relevant institutions and service providers develops forms of support for Early Childhood Education in the family, on the importance of development and investment at an early age;

1.2. family education is also supported for children who cannot go to preschool due to illness;

1.3. forms of support for Early Childhood Education in the family are regulated by a sub-legal act.

#### Article 18

Pre-primary education

\1. Pre-primary education is organized for children aged five (5) and operate within primary schools and preschool institutions.

\2. Pre-primary education becomes compulsory as provided in the transitional provisions.

#### Article 19

Pedagogical documentation

\1. Early Childhood Education Institutions use and maintain the pedagogical documentation defined by a sub-legal act by the relevant Ministry of Education.

\2. Pedagogical documentation is provided in physical or electronic copies.

#### Article 20

Collection, management and type of data

\1. Early Childhood Education Institutions collect, process, store, monitor and use personal data in registers, in accordance with legislation in force.

\2. Institutions that provide Early Childhood Education retain the following data:

2.1. data of children who have applied and those who have been accepted;

2.2. data on child’s payments;

2.3. data required by the Education Information Management System including attendance at the early childhood education institutions;

2.4. pedagogical documentation.

\3. The data defined in paragraph 2. of this Article, are used for statistics, research, planning, programs, services, financing, professional development and various reports of state interest.

#### Article 21

Personal data protection

\1. Early Childhood Education Institutions and the municipality protect the personal data of children and parents/legal guardians based on the legislation in force.

\2. Personal data may be used and published only in such a way as not to disclose the identity of an individual respecting the ethical principles governing Early Childhood Education.

\3. Personal data of children in Early Childhood Education institutions can be published only with the consent of the parent/legal guardian of the child, unless otherwise provided by special law.

Education institutions based on the needs identified in the community.

\2. The relevant Ministry of Education licenses non-public Early Childhood Education institutions in accordance with the legislation in force.

\3. The criteria for the establishment, licensing and termination of the activity of the Institutions of Early Childhood Education are drafted by the respective Ministry of Education with a sub-legal act for each form of organization.

#### Article 23

Termination of the institutions activity that provide education in early childhood

\1. Termination of the activity of the Early Childhood Education Institution or the separate class can be initiated by the relevant Ministry of Education or the respective municipality, in cases of violation of the license conditions.

\2. The initiator establishes the professional commission for reviewing the initiation for the cessation of the activity of the institution in early childhood education.

\3. The initiative for the termination of the activity of the institution of Early Childhood Education, or separate class, contains a detailed argumentation for the reasons of the activity termination, referring to the license conditions, legal violations, respectively objective circumstances that justify such a step.

\4. The relevant Ministry of Education in cooperation with the competent bodies terminates the work of all institutions that have not applied for a license and have violated the well-being of children and will initiate legal proceedings against the responsible persons in the Institution of Early Childhood Education.

#### Article 24

Buildings, equipment and playgrounds

\1. Early childhood education institutions are accessible indoors and outdoors to all children without distinction.

\2. Buildings of early childhood education institutions are safe, healthy, friendly and designed with construction safety parameters.

\3. All spaces, specifically multipurpose spaces, operating equipment and play equipment must be in accordance with applicable norms and standards.

\4. Possess fire-fighting equipment and other hazards emergency stairs and spaces necessary for rapid and safe evacuation in case of danger.

\5. Early childhood education institutions for three to five (3-5) age group can also function in school buildings in functionally separate spaces.

#### Article 25

Cross-sectoral coordination

\1. Coordinating mechanisms are established for cross-sectoral coordination on development, education and care in early childhood.

\2. Cross-sectoral programs for integration of services in early childhood have the content of education, care and development of children according to institutional responsibilities.

#### Article 26

Establishment of professional bodies Relevant central and local level public and non-public institutions in Kosova that support early childhood education may establish professional bodies for early childhood education.

#### Article 27

Management of the institution for early childhood education

\1. The Institution of Early Childhood Education is led and managed by the head of the institution and steering council or the equivalent structures.

1.1. the criteria for the selection of the leader, the managerial organization and the competencies of the leader of an institution for Early Childhood Education or separate parallels are defined by a sub-legal act;

1.2. procedures for the selection of the director/leader and governing bodies of public Early Childhood Education institutions are developed by the municipality, while other non-public forms are developed by legal or natural person who manages the institution;

1.3. the establishment and functioning of the steering council or equivalent structure, the composition and competencies for each organizational form of the Early Childhood Education service provision are regulated by a sub-legal act.

#### Article 28

Weekly fund of educator and professional associates in Early Childhood Education

\1. The fund of working hours for educational staff and professional associates in Early Childhood Education institutions should include the preparation, planning, implementation of educational activities, cooperation with parents/legal guardians.

1.1. working hours for educational staff is eight (8) hours per day, including six (6) direct hours with children and two (2) hours for preparation and planning;

1.2. working hours for professional staff are determined based on the legislation in force.

#### Article 29

Funding

\1. The allocation of budget funds for Early Childhood Education Institutions is done according to funding sources:

1.1. Kosovo Budget;

1.2. Own source revenues;

1.3. Donations and other resources.

\2. Own source revenues shall be determined by sub-legal act.

#### Article 30

Management of budgetary funds in ECE institutions

\1. The founder of the Early Childhood Education institutions is obliged to provide the budget for establishment and work of the institution.

\2. Budgetary funds from all sources of funding are planned and managed by the director of the institution approved by the steering council or the body with similar competencies.

\3. Expenditure of budgetary funds is based on the development plan of the Early Childhood Education institution, which is approved by the steering council.

\4. The founder prepares transparent annual budgetary plans and reports according to the legislation in force.

#### Article 31

Audit The audit of the expenditure of financial means in the Early Childhood Education Institutions is done by the competent auditor.

#### Article 32

Payments from parents/legal guardians for early childhood education

\1. The fee for the payment of the child attendance in the Public Early Childhood Education institution is determined by the respective municipality, based on the municipal standard of living and in an escalated manner.

\2. Payments for children of non-public Early Childhood Education institutions are made in agreement between the parent/legal guardian and the founders.

\3. The planning and spending of financial means is done according to the relevant Law on Public Financial Management and Accountabilities and other sub-legal acts.

#### Article 33

Monitoring and inspection

\1. The monitoring and inspection of the implementation of this Law is done and supervised by the relevant Ministry of Education and other line ministries, municipal directorates and all other institutions and inspection mechanisms according to their responsibility in early childhood education.

\2. Inspection of educational institutions in early childhood is done according to the legislation in force.

\3. Early childhood education institutions go through the process of internal and external evaluation of the work performance of the institution based on the performance standards se t by the relevant Ministry of Education.

#### Article 34

Educational staff and professional associates of the Early Childhood Education

\1. Educational activities in Early Childhood Education institutions are carried out by educational staff, assistants and supported by professional associates.

\2. The profile for the qualification of educational staff, assistants and professional associates is regulated by a sub-legal act.

\3. Educational staff and professional associates are offered continuous professional development.

\4. Continuous professional development is regulated by a sub-legal act.

#### Article 35

Training of professional nannies

\1. The relevant Ministry of Education, in cooperation with line ministries and other collaborators, develops a vocational training program for persons providing Early Childhood Education services in the family.

\2. Training, engagement, criteria and selection of nannies is regulated by sub-legal act.

#### Article 36

Volunteers in Early Childhood Education

\1. Volunteers engaged in Early Childhood Education institutions are recognized for their volunteer work experience.

\2. Volunteers are not hired as a replacement for the professional staff.

\3. Voluntary work in all ECE institutions is regulated by a sub-legal act.

#### Article 37

Transitional provisions Pre-primary education becomes mandatory from the school year 2024-2025.

#### Article 38

Sub-legal Acts Sub-legal acts provided for by this law shall be issued within six (6) months from entry into force of this law.

#### Article 39

Repealing provisions With entry into force of this law, is repealed the law no. 02/L-52 on Preschool Education and all other provisions that are in contradiction to this law.

#### Article 40

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
