# LAW NO. 08/L-216 ON RATIFICATION OF THE WESTERN BALKANS AGREEMENT ON THE RECOGNITION OF HIGHER EDUCATION QUALIFICATIONS

### LAW NO. 08/L-216 ON RATIFICATION OF THE WESTERN BALKANS AGREEMENT ON THE RECOGNITION OF HIGHER EDUCATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-216

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE WESTERN BALKANS AGREEMENT ON THE RECOGNITION

### OF HIGHER EDUCATION QUALIFICATIONS

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE WESTERN BALKANS AGREEMENT ON THE

### RECOGNITION OF HIGHER EDUCATION QUALIFICATIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Western Balkans Agreement on the Recognition of Higher Education Qualifications.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Western Balkans Agreement on the Recognition of Higher Education Qualifications shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
