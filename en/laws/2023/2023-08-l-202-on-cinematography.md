# LAW NO. 08/L-202 ON CINEMATOGRAPHY

### LAW NO. 08/L-202 ON CINEMATOGRAPHY

### LAW NO. 08/L-202

### ON CINEMATOGRAPHY

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON CINEMATOGRAPHY

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose of the Law This law aims to regulate the activity, organization, financing, production, distribution, displaying of film works in the country and abroad, promotion and development of cinematography.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this law are mandatory for all natural and legal persons as well as other institutions in the Republic of Kosovo that have certain rights, responsibilities and authorizations in the field of cinematography.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. For the purpose of this law, the expressions used shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Film or film work - means a visual work with or without sound, such as (featured)

played, animated and documentary film of any length and genre;

1.2. Audio-visual work - means a work such as: film, experimental film, multi-media and trans-media film project, film series, video games and any other work with visuals and with or without sound, regardless of the technology with which it was created and the way of display;

1.3. Film or audio-visual production - means the general production process and the activities undertaken from the stage of concept development, project development, preproduction, production, post-production, display, distribution and promotion of the work;

1.4. Domestic film - means:

1.4.1. the film, where the director and/or producer is a citizen of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.4.2. the film, where the producer is a legal entity residing in the Republic of Kosovo and the participation of authors, actors and/or other artists who are citizens of Kosovo and/or domestic capital is of the greatest importance;

1.4.3. the film produced by one or more Kosovo citizen producers, with at least one domestic author and with significant participation of domestic film workers.

1.5. Film series - means the audio-visual work divided into episodes;

1.6. Co-production - means film production carried out by two or more producers;

1.7. Major co-production - means the film or audio-visual work where the Republic of Kosovo financially supports an international project with not less than ten percent (10%) and not more than ninety percent (90%) of the total value, taking into account the culture test;

1.8. Minor co-production - means the film or audio-visual work where the Republic of Kosovo financially supports an international project with no less than five percent (5%) and no more than twenty percent (20%) of the total value, taking into account the culture test;

1.9. Author - means the natural person who creates the work;

1.10. Producer - means the natural or legal person who organizes, leads, supervises and/or takes organizational and financial responsibility for the production of a film or an audio-visual work;

1.11. Rebate - means the scheme of financial reimbursements for foreign legal persons, who create film or audio-visual works in the Republic of Kosovo, through financial investments from abroad;

1.12. Ministry - means the relevant ministry for culture;

1.13. Minister - means the relevant minister of culture;

1.14. Distributor - means the natural or legal person, who performs actions or services, which coincide with the distribution of the film or audio-visual work;

1.19. The culture test - means the classification of the categories of the competition for financial support based on the criteria that are determined by the by-laws of the KCC;

1.20. Debutant director - means the natural person who directs a long length film for the first time;

1.21. Short length film - means a film work lasting up to thirty (30) minutes;

1.22. Medium length film - refers to the film work that lasts from thirty (30) to sixty (60)

minutes;

1.23. Long length film - means a film work that lasts more than sixty (60) minutes.

#### Article 4

The exercising of cinematographic activity

\1. The cinematographic activity in the Republic of Kosovo is considered a field of special public interest.

\2. The cinematographic activity in the Republic of Kosovo can be exercised by any domestic or foreign natural or legal person.

\3. The domestic or foreign legal person has the right to exercise the cinematographic activity provided that it is registered in the Republic of Kosovo in accordance with the legislation in force.

#### Article 5

The right to filming

\1. Film or audio-visual work can be filmed in the entire territory of the Republic of Kosovo, unless otherwise provided by other legal provisions.

\2. If the filming must be done in facilities or environments, where access to the public is limited, the producer must first request a filming permit from the competent body at least fifteen (15)

days prior to the beginning of filming.

\3. The competent body is obliged to respond to the applicant within fifteen (15) days.

#### Article 6

Responsible and Supporting Institutions for Cinematography

\1. Institutions responsible for cinematography are:

1.1. The Ministry of Culture, which determines the general policies for the development of cinematography in the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. The KCC as a public service institution within the relevant Ministry of Culture, responsible for the development of cinematography;

1.3. Other institutions of the central or local level, which help the activities of cinematography, within their fields of responsibility.

#### Article 7

Kosovafilm The Social Enterprise “Kosovafilm” founded in 1969 as a company for the production, displaying and distribution of the films, with all property and intellectual rights, is a special unit in the Kosovo Cinematographic Centre.

#### Article 8

Responsibilities of the Kosovo Cinematographic Centre

\1. The Kosovo Cinematographic Centre (hereinafter KCC) has the following authorizations and responsibilities:

1.1. it promotes and financially supports cinematographic activities, with the aim of implementation of cinematographic policies in the service of the public interest of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. it promotes Kosovo cinematography in festivals and film markets in the country and abroad, and organizes, supports and participates in other events that serve to the promotion and development of cinematography;

1.3. it has a specific budget code and manages its own finances;

1.4. it cooperates with institutions, public and private organizations, as well as local and foreign associations in all matters of interest to create a favourable environment for the development of cinematography, including education through film;

1.5. it cooperates with counterpart authorities of other countries, cooperates and adheres to regional and international mechanisms, initiates and signs agreements in the field of cinematography;

1.10. it ensures the implementation of measures for the collection, protection, preservation and processing of audio-visual heritage, respecting the standards set by the International Federation of Film Archives - FIAF;

1.11. it collects, protects, processes and preserves films produced by the Former “Kosovafilm” Social Enterprise, inheriting all rights and obligations;

1.12. it collects, protects, processes and preserves films financially supported by the KCC, films of independent producers of Kosovo, films of students, audio-visual recordings produced by cultural institutions in Kosovo and other audio-visual recordings of cultural interest, which are considered as the cultural heritage of Kosovo, produced by other cultural institutions within the Ministry or by any other institution or organization that operates with the budget of Kosovo;

1.13. it collects, protects, processes and preserves films and audio-visual materials, of cultural interest for the Republic of Kosovo, which it collects, by agreement, from regional and international archives;

1.14. it offers professional and legal protection, through the processing and storage of public or private/family audio-visual archives that are of interest to the cultural heritage of the Republic of Kosovo;

#### Article 9

Governing bodies

\1. The governing bodies of the KCC are:

1.1. Professional council;

1.2. The director.

#### Article 10

The professional council of the KCC

\1. The professional council of the KCC (hereinafter the Council) consists of five (5) members and has a four (4) year mandate, with the possibility of re-election.

\2. The Council of the KCC consists of prominent personalities from the field of cinematography and culture.

\3. The Council of the KCC is appointed and dismissed by the Minister.

\4. Two (2) members are elected by the minister, one (1) of them is a representative of the Ministry, one (1) from the field of cinematography.

\5. Three (3) other members are elected from the proposals of active associations and the community of filmmakers, according to the Minister’s invitation.

\6. The KCC Council reports to the Minister.

\7. The members elect among themselves the Chairman of the Council of the KCC.

\8. Members of the KCC Council cannot be in a regular employment relationship with the KCC.

\9. Members of the KCC Council cannot be members of the evaluation jury or commissions of the KCC.

#### Article 11

Duties and responsibilities of the KCC Council

\1. Advises the Minister on the development policies of the KCC.

\2. Supervises and evaluates the implementation of the development policies of the KCC. Drafts reports and recommendations on the effectiveness of the implementation of current policies and measures to be taken by the Ministry in order to advance the cinematography.

\3. Participates in the process of drafting legislation in the field of cinematography.

\4. Advises the Director in the selection of juries and commissions of the KCC.

\5. Offers professional advice for the smooth running of the work of the KCC.

\6. The exercise of duties and responsibilities of the Council as well as the method of compensation for the work of its members shall be regulated by a sub-legal act approved by the relevant Ministry.

#### Article 12

Election of the Director of the KCC

\1. The director of the KCC is elected through a public competition process.

\2. The election procedure of the Director of the KCC is organized by the Ministry in accordance with this law and the legislation in force.

#### Article 13

Criteria for Selection of the Director of the KCC

\1. The candidate for KCC Director must meet the following minimum criteria:

1.1. to have citizenship of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. to have professional work experience in the field of cinematography or culture, and work experience in management positions;

1.3. to have general knowledge in the field of culture, especially in the field of cinematography;

1.4. to have a program and vision for cinematography and the management and development of the KCC;

1.5. Other criteria for the Director are set out by competition, based on the legislation in force.

#### Article 14

Duties of the Director of the KCC

\1. The Director of KCC is the main managing and leading body and has the following duties:

1.1. leads, coordinates and organizes the general and organizational work of the institution in function of the successful implementation of the activity;

1.2. exercises the responsibility of the highest executive body within the institution;

1.3. presents and represents the institution in all cases, except when otherwise determined by law;

1.4. cooperates with the Council to coordinate the activities in order to fulfil the objectives of the KCC or program policies;

1.5. undertakes all legal actions in the name and on behalf of the institution, including the conclusion of contracts and agreements in accordance with the law;

1.6. oversees the budget revenues and expenditures;

1.8. consults with the Council for evaluation juries and financial commissions;

#### Article 15

KCC competitions

\1. KCC financially supports cinematography based on periodic competitions.

\2. Domestic film and audio-visual works have priority in the KCC competitions.

\3. The value of financial support of long length domestic film and audio-visual works can reach up to eighty (80%) percent of the total budget of the work, except for the works of debutant directors and short and medium length films, which can be supported up to one hundred (100%)

percent of the total budget of the work.

\4. The value of financial support of film and audio-visual works of major co-production is considered when the participation of funds of the Republic of Kosovo is from ten (10%) percent to ninety (90%) percent of the total budget of the work.

\5. The value of financial support of film and audio-visual works of minor co-production is considered when the participation of funds of the Republic of Kosovo is from five (5%) percent to twenty (20%) percent of the total budget of the work.

\6. Procedures and other criteria for competitions and financial support of film and audio-visual works, as well as complementary activities, are defined by a special bylaw issued by the Minister.

#### Article 17

Commission for Categorization

\1. The commission for categorization is the state authority, within the KCC, that grants permission for public display and categorization, according to the suitability for public display of film or audio-visual works produced in the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. Film or audio-visual works produced outside the Republic of Kosovo, where the displayer and/ or distributor has a prior categorization from the country of origin, do not need to be examined by the Commission for categorization.

\3. The Commission for categorization, grants permits according to the following categories:

3.1. category “G”, without limitation;

3.2. category (10+), unsuitable for ages under ten (10) years;

3.3. category (14+) unsuitable for ages under fourteen (14) years;

3.4. category (18+) unsuitable for ages under eighteen (18) years.

\4. The categorization committee may refuse to grant permission for public display if the film or audio-visual work is in conflict with this Article.

\5. The Commission for categorization is appointed by the Director of the KCC.

\6. The Commission for categorization consists of five (5) members, prominent personalities in the field of cinematography, culture, sociology, psychology, civil society.

#### Article 18

Rebate

\1. The Republic of Kosovo, through this law, enables subsidizing, through rebate, enabling the reimbursement of thirty (30%) percent of the eligible costs, spent directly by the foreign investor in Kosovo, for the production of the film or audio-visual work.

\2. The budget for the rebate is allocated with a special budget code according to the deadlines for planning the annual budget of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 19

The right to rebate

\1. Only producers of film or audio-visual works dedicated for public display are entitled to the rebate, in accordance with the conditions defined in paragraph 3. of this Article.

\2. Legal persons based in a foreign country through the producer registered in the Republic of Kosovo who have signed a cooperation agreement for the purpose of creating a film or audiovisual work and who meet the following criteria are entitled to the rebate:

2.1. to have the relevant copyrights for the project;

2.2. to have at least one film or audio-visual work displayed publicly in cinemas, TV or other platforms during the last three (3) years;

2.3. to have declared to the KCC the planned expenses in the Republic of Kosovo for the purposes of the project.

\3. In the evaluation of the reasons for the eligibility of the implementation of the rebate, one or several of the following criteria are applied:

3.1. the use of human resources of the Republic of Kosovo;

3.2. the use of production or post-production capacities of the Republic of Kosovo;

3.3. filming in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo;

3.4. the use of goods and services in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo for the needs of the creation of the film or audio-visual work.

\4. The procedures and conditions for the right to rebate are determined by a by-law proposed by the Ministry and approved by the Government.

\5. The budget dedicated to the realization of the rebate cannot be spent for other purposes by the KCC.

#### Article 20

Financing of the KCC

\1. The means for financing the KCC are provided by the budget of the Republic of Kosovo and other alternative financing in accordance with the legislation in force.

\2. KCC creates its own revenues from the following activities:

2.1. sale of films produced by Kosovafilm;

2.2. services as a distributor of archival fund copies;

2.3. archive services offered to third parties in the case of the generation of film copies and other audio-visual copies from the archival fund;

2.4. the displaying program of the cinematheque;

2.5. sale of specialized film publications;

2.6. sale of copies of photographs from films and other reproductions of special archival materials.

\3. The KCC has a specific budget code and manages its own finances.

\4. The procedures for collection, supervision and administration of other own revenues from the activity of the KCC are regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister as well as in accordance with the rules determined by the state treasury.

#### Article 21

Cinemas of special public interest and the establishment of other cinemas

\1. Cinemas that currently have the status of social and public property, in accordance with Article 4 of this law, are declared to be of special public interest.

\2. In relation to cinemas as in paragraph 1. of this Article, all institutions that exercise public authority in the Republic of Kosovo cannot undertake actions in the direction of:

2.1. change of destination;

2.2. privatization.

\3. Cinemas as public service institutions or units can be established in accordance with the provisions of the legislation in force for organization and operation of the state administration, with this law and other normative acts in force.

\4. Other cinemas with non-public legal entity status may be established in accordance with the legislation in force.

\5. Within the framework of the KCC, a special unit is established for the management of the network of public cinemas, with the aim of preserving cinemas, developing and managing cinemas.

\6. The establishment, the way of administration, the conditions and criteria for the operation of the Cinemas are determined by a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

#### Article 22

Annual Awards for Cinematography

\1. The Ministry gives annual awards for cinematography.

\2. The procedures, criteria and financial value of the awards are regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 23

Rewards for International Achievement in Cinematography

\1. The Ministry financially rewards citizens of the Republic of Kosovo for international successes in cinematography.

\2. The financial reward according to paragraph 1. of this Article is given to those who win prizes in the main competition in prestigious international festivals and academies.

\3. The criteria, financial rewards and categorizations of festivals and achievements in festivals and other important academies are regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 24

Conflict of interest The members of the Council of the KCC, the Director of the KCC, the members of the evaluation jury, the members of the commission for categorization and all other persons, elected or appointed in the bodies of the KCC, who evaluate and make decisions, must declare the potential conflict of interest, as defined in the legal provisions in force. Once they have made the conflict of interest declaration, they must withdraw from evaluation and decision-making.

#### Article 25

Protection and preservation of audio-visual heritage

\1. Producers, owners and other possessors of film or audio-visual works and other film and audio-visual materials that have historical, artistic, cultural and research importance for the development of cinematography shall undertake actions for their protection and preservation.

\2. Film or audio-visual works and other film and audio-visual materials are considered and protected as cultural heritage and the legal regulation for the protection and preservation of cultural heritage and archival materials is applied to them as well.

#### Article 26

Distributors, displayers and festivals

\1. The public display of film or audio-visual works can be done by legal persons, registered for this activity, if they have space and equipment in accordance with the legal provisions in force.

\2. Displayers and festivals, for the displaying of foreign film or audio-visual works, must respect the categorization by country of origin or distributor.

\3. Displayers and festivals must make the categorization public, as well as not allow access to the show for persons under the categorized age.

\4. The KCC has the right to ask distributors, performers or festivals to change the certain categorization if it is not in accordance with this law or the by-laws of the KCC.

\5. The distribution and public display of film or audio-visual works that promote or incite ethnic, racial, religious, gender and sexual orientation hatred, as well as other criminal activities, is prohibited.

#### Article 27

Protection for minors

\1. It is forbidden to distribute, sell and rent film or audio-visual works to minors, which are categorized as inappropriate for their age, according to Article 26 of this law and the legislation in force.

\2. The prohibition referred to in paragraph 1. of this Article also applies to sales through the mail, the internet or any other way.

#### Article 28

Reproduction and Trading A film or audio-visual work can be sold, displayed publicly, and put into use if the distributor, namely the legal or natural person who has a corresponding contract with the producer or the holder of the rights of use and when this coincides with an external film or audio-visual work, as well as if it was imported in accordance with the legal or by-law provisions for the import of goods.

#### Article 29

Copyright All rights and obligations related to the copyright of a film or audio-visual work are applied in accordance with the legal provisions of the law on copyright and this law.

#### Article 31

Imposition of fines Fines are imposed by the competent body based on the Law on Minor Offences.

#### Article 32

Changing the Ownership of “Kosovafilm”

Kosovafilm, according to Article 7 of this Law, shall be transferred to the ownership of KCC within one (1) year from entry into force of this Law.

#### Article 33

Bylaws By-laws for implementation of this law shall be drafted and approved no later than six (6) months from the date of entry into force of this law.

#### Article 34

Repeal After the entry into force of this Law, Law No. 2004/22 on Cinematography shall be repealed.
