# LAW NO. 08/L-226 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2022 PROGRAMME”

### LAW NO. 08/L-226 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

### ON IPA 2022 PROGRAMME”

### LAW NO. 08/L-226

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2022 PROGRAMME”

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2022 PROGRAMME”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on IPA 2022 Programme.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on IPA 2022 Programme shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-226 8 June 2023

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-113/2023 dated 22.06.2023 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
