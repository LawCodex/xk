# LAW NO. 08/L-162 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE LABOUR AND TRANSFERS AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE KOSOVO SOCIAL ASSISTANCE SYSTEM REFORM PROJECT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE LABOUR AND TRANSFERS

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE KOSOVO SOCIAL

### ASSISTANCE SYSTEM REFORM PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement for the Kosovo Social Assistance System Reform Project between Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the International Development Association.

#### Article 2

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law is attached the Financing Agreement for the Kosovo Social Assistance System Reform Project between Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the International Development Association.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
