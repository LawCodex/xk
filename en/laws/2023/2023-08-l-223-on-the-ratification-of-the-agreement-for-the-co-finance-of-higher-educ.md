### FRANCE IN PRISTINA

#### Article 1

The Purpose of the Law Through this law, the Agreement for the Cooperation between the Ministry of Education, Science, Technology and Innovation of the Republic of Kosova and the Embassy of France in Prishtina for the Co-finance of Higher Education Scholarships at the Master’s Level is ratified.

#### Article 2

Scope of the law The scope of this law is the Co-financing of Higher Education Scholarships at the Master’s Level.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to Annex 1 of this law is the Agreement for the Cooperation between the Ministry of Education, Science, Technology and Innovation of the Republic of Kosova and the Embassy of France in Prishtina for the Co-finance of Higher Education Scholarships at the Master’s Level.

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosova.
