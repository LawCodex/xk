# LAW NO. 08/L-204 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PROJECT “ENERGY EFFICIENCY IN PUBLIC BUILDINGS IN PRIZREN” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT

### LAW NO. 08/L-204 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PROJECT “ENERGY EFFICIENCY IN PUBLIC BUILDINGS IN

### PRIZREN” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT

### LAW NO. 08/L-204

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PROJECT “ENERGY

### EFFICIENCY IN PUBLIC BUILDINGS IN PRIZREN” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT FOR THE PROJECT “ENERGY

### EFFICIENCY IN PUBLIC BUILDINGS IN PRIZREN” BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the loan agreement for the project “energy efficiency in public buildings in Prizren” between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Loan Agreement for the project “Energy efficiency in public buildings in Prizren”

between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-204 23 March 2023 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-64/2023 dated 06.04.2023 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
