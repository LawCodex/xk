#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement for the project "Green Cities Framework 2 Window 2 - Energy efficiency in public buildings in Pristina" between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

#### Article 2

Attached Documents The text of the Loan Agreement for the project "Green Cities Framework 2 Window 2 - Energy efficiency in public buildings in Prishtina" between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
