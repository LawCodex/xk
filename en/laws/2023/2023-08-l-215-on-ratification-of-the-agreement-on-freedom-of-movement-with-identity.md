# LAW NO. 08/L-215 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT WITH IDENTITY CARDS IN THE WESTERN BALKANS

### LAW NO. 08/L-215 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT WITH IDENTITY CARDS IN THE WESTERN BALKANS

### LAW NO. 08/L-215

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT WITH IDENTITY

### CARDS IN THE WESTERN BALKANS

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT WITH

### IDENTITY CARDS IN THE WESTERN BALKANS

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the Agreement on Freedom of Movement with Identity Cards in the Western Balkans.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Agreement on Freedom of Movement with Identity Cards in the Western Balkans shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
