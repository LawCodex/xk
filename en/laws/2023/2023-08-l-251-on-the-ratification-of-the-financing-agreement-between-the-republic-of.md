The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOR AND TRANSFERS,

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PROJECT “REHABILITATION OF

### RAILWAY LINE 10 B”

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the European Investment Bank for the project “Rehabilitation of Railway Line 10 B”.

#### Article 2

Attached documents Attached to the Appendix of this Law is the text of the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the European Investment Bank for the project “Rehabilitation of Railway Line 10 B”.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-251 23 November 2023 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-188/2023 dated 06.12.2023 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu
