### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law aims to define rules for the prevention, control of infectious diseases and related special health issues.

\2. This law is partially in compliance with:

2.1. Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/945 of 22 June 2018 on the communicable diseases and related special health issues to be covered by epidemiological surveillance as well as relevant case definitions;

2.2. Decision No. 1082/2013 / EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2013 on serious cross-border threats to health and repealing Decision No 2119/98/EC;

2.3. Commission Decision of 30 April 2009 amending Decision 2002/253 / EC laying down case definitions for reporting communicable diseases to the Community network under Decision No. 2119/98 / EC of the European Parliament and of the Council.

#### Article 2

Scope The legal provisions of this Law shall be applied by the public and private, local and foreign, natural and legal person, health institution and other entities responsible for preventing and controlling the communicable diseases and special health issues.

#### Article 13

General measures for the prevention and control of infectious diseases

\1. General measures for the prevention and control of infectious diseases are:

1.11. provision and enhancing of human resources for the prevention and control of infectious diseases, and provision of personal protective equipment, which serve to protect health workers and other persons exposed to infectious agents from contamination, infection and exposure to infectious diseases;

### CHAPTER IV

### EPIDEMIOLOGICAL SURVEILLANCE, CONTROL, ALERTING AND REPORTING

#### Article 19

Epidemiological surveillance and control

\1. The National Institute of Public Health determines the criteria for epidemiological surveillance of infectious diseases or emergency events, which have an impact on public health and are approved with decision by the Minister of Health.

\2. The network of epidemiological and laboratory surveillance of infectious diseases and special health issues should contain the following main elements.

2.1. routine electronic indicator-based infectious disease surveillance system;

2.2. electronic event early warning and alerting system;

2.3. data exchange between the data source and the NIPHK;

2.4. integrated system for zoonoses of human, animal and environmental epidemiological surveillance which is determined by government decision.

\3. Infectious diseases in the framework of epidemiological surveillance are selected according to the following criteria:

3.1. diseases that cause or have the potential to cause high morbidity and / or mortality in and around the country and for which there should be a national, cross-border and global coordination;

3.2. diseases for which the exchange of data provides early notification of public health threats;

3.3. rare and serious diseases, not immediately distinguishable, at the national level, but for which data collection allows the development of hypotheses and the detection of epidemic outbreaks;

3.4. diseases for which there are effective prevention measures;

3.5. diseases that when compared to other countries, can contribute to the evaluation of national and international programs.

\4. In case of alert of any threat to health, NIPHK performs case immediate supervision.

#### Article 23

\1. All healthcare settings are obliged to provide measures for the prevention and control of healthcare-associated infections.

\4. The sale of antimicrobials without a doctor’s prescription is prohibited.

#### Article 24

Serious national and cross-border health threats

\1. NIPHK alerts through the early warning system the occurrence or development of a serious cross-border health threat if the following criteria are met:

1.1. unusual or unexpected event for the given place and time, or causes or may cause mass morbidity or mortality in humans, or increases rapidly and may escalate or exceed or may exceed national response capacity;

1.2. events that may constitute a public health emergency of international concern.

\2. During notice of an alerting, the NIPHK will promptly communicate through the EWS any available information available that may be useful for coordinating the response, while including:

2.1. type and the origin of the agent;

2.2. infectious diseases;

2.3. antimicrobial resistance and infections related to health care in the field of infectious diseases (specific health issues);

2.4. the date and place of the incident or outbreak;

2.5. route of spread;

2.6. toxicological data;

2.7. detection and confirmation methods;

2.8. public health risks;

2.9. public health measures implemented or intended to be taken at national level;

2.10. other measures under public health measures;

2.11. personal data necessary for the purpose of tracking contacts in accordance with Article 11 of this Law;

2.12. any other information related to the serious cross-border threat.

\3. In case of serious cross-border threat, NIPHK in cooperation with International Institutions (ECDC, WHO) assesses the risk.

\4. The Ministry is obliged to designate a contact point for reporting cross-border threats for infectious diseases according to the International Health Regulation (IHR).

#### Article 25

Preparedness and Response

\1. The risk assessment for epidemic outbreaks is made by the NIPHK.

\2. The Minister issues a decision to increase the level of preparedness for public health emergency response according to the recommendation of the NIPHK.

\3. The Minister notifies the Government of Kosovo of the emergency and requests the increase of the level of readiness at the state level.

\4. The government, after declaring the emergency situation, activates the National Response Plan.

\5. Exchange of best practices with other states and relevant institutions.

\6. The Readiness and Response Committee established by the Government has the task of coordinating and undertaking the necessary cross-sectoral actions according to the situation.

\7. The declaration of an epidemic of infectious diseases in Kosovo is made by the Minister of Health on the recommendation of the National Institute of Public Health.

\8. The Minister shall, by a decision, activate the Operational Centre in the Ministry of Health.

\9. Municipalities are obliged to implement Government Decisions in public health emergency management.

10 The official declaration of the end of the epidemic is made by the Minister on the recommendation of the NIPHK.

#### Article 26

Isolation

\1. Persons who are found to have communicable diseases with a risk of spreading and as per the doctor’s instructions need to be isolated in the infectious disease clinic, wards for treating communicable diseases and other institutions, where necessary:

1.1. exceptions from paragraph 1 of this Article, are sick persons whose health condition enables isolation at home, according to the doctor’s recommendation;

1.2. person isolated at home conditions, is supervised by a doctor in the municipality where he lives or is isolated;

1.3. the isolation period ends after the person recovers and does not pose a risk of spreading, after the doctor’s diagnosis.

#### Article 27

Quarantine

\1. Quarantine means separating and restricting the movement of people who have been exposed to infectious disease to monitor their health status.

### LAW NO. 08/L -200 ON PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF INFECTIOUS DISEASES

\2. Quarantine lasts depending on the incubation of the infectious disease.

\3. In case of an epidemiological indication, persons coming to the Republic of Kosovo as seekers of international protection, asylum seekers and persons under additional or temporary protection, in order to prevent the spread of infectious diseases are placed in quarantine.

\4. The Minister, upon the recommendation of NIPHK, shall issue a decision on the establishment of the quarantine regime and the duration of the quarantine regime for persons suspected of communicable diseases, including the relevant measures and instructions.

\5. The quarantine area is defined as the facilities and settlements designated by a decision issued by the Government proposed by the Ministry, based on the recommendations of NIPHK, including relevant measures and instructions.

\6. Persons with other diseases that require continuous treatment, after the doctor’s diagnosis are self-quarantined and released from quarantine.

\7. The medical team after the action it takes in relation to quarantined persons, reports to EOCNIPHK and EOC-MoH.

\8. It is forbidden for physical persons to leave the quarantine without the permission of the responsible doctor.

#### Article 28
