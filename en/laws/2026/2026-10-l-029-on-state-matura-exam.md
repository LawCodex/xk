# LAW NO. 10/L-029 ON STATE MATURA EXAM

### LAW NO. 10/L-029 ON STATE MATURA EXAM

### LAW NO. 10/L-029

### ON STATE MATURA EXAM

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo,

Approves:

### LAW ON STATE MATURA EXAM

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

The purpose

\1. This has for purpose:

1.1. determining the status of the State Matura Exam in the education system of the Republic of Kosova;

1.2. determining the content, conditions and way of organizing the State Matura Exam and the rights of the candidates;

1.3. determining responsible structures and their competences for the organization of the State Matura Exam.

#### Article 2

Definitions

\1. The definitions and abbreviations of this Law are as follows:

1.1. “State Matura” - is a standardized final state exam that can be taken by all candidates who successfully complete higher secondary education;

1.2. “Standardized test” - is the test where all questions/requirements are the same for all candidates, the time given to each candidate is the same and the way the test is evaluated is the same for all candidates;

1.3. “Candidate” - the student, the person who successfully completes the final class of higher secondary education (inside or abroad) and undergoes the Matura exam;

1.4. “Ministry” - The Ministry responsible for education;

1.6. “Unit” - The unit responsible for the Matura exam within the Ministry;

1.7. “ECC” - Examination Center Commission;

1.8. “Administrator” - person responsible for administration of Matura exam;

1.9. “Supervisor” - person responsible for supervision of Matura exam;

### CHAPTER II

### STATE MATURA EXAM

#### Article 3

The status of the State Matura Exam

\1. The State Matura Exam is an external assessment of the achievements, knowledge, skills and abilities acquired during pre-university education in accordance with the Curriculum and the Qualifications Framework of the Republic of Kosova.

\2. All higher secondary school candidates who have successfully completed the last year and are equipped with a diploma, earn the right to take the State Matura Exam.

\3. Except from paragraph 2. of this Article all vocational secondary school candidates who have successfully completed their final year and are awarded a diploma, are entitled to take the State Matura exam on the basis of additional conditions, if any such, defined in the relevant law on professional education in the Republic of Kosova.

\4. Successful completion of the Matura exam is a condition for the admission of candidates to study programs of higher education in the Republic of Kosova according to the relevant legislation.

#### Article 4

The content of the Matura exam

\1. State Matura exam is composed of:

1.1. compulsory subjects (mother language, math and english language); and

1.2. elective subjects.

\2. Elective subjects of the Matura exam are proposed by the Unit and approved by SMC.

\3. The candidate has the right to be determined for elective subjects according to his/her selection and in accordance with the selection rules defined in the Matura exam catalog.

\4. The State Matura exam is developed in writing.

#### Article 5

Rights of the candidate undergoing the State Matura exam

\1. The candidate is subject to the Matura exam in one of the official languages of the Republic of Kosova or in one the official languages in the municipal level in the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. Entering the Matura exam in another language may be done if so decided by a decision from the Minister.

\3. The candidate with special needs is subject to the State Matura exam according to the conditions and criteria that suit their needs and which are determined by a sub-legal act of the Ministry.

\4. Candidates from adult education are also subject to the Matura exam according to paragraph 1. of this Article.

\5. Candidates who have completed higher secondary education outside Kosova can also take the Matura exam according to paragraph 1. of this Article.

### CHAPTER III

### RESPONSIBLE AUTHORITIES

#### Article 6

Responsible authorities

\1. The authorities responsible for the State Matura are:

1.1. Ministry;

1.2. State Matura Commission;

1.3. Unit;

1.4. Municipal Education Directorates;

1.5. High Secondary Schools;

1.6. Examination Center Commission(ECC).

\2. The Ministry may establish other support mechanisms with the Minister’s decision.

#### Article 7

Ministry

\1. The Ministry is responsible for the organization and implementation of the Matura exam process, which includes:

1.1. approves the regulatory and normative basis;

1.2. ensures the financing of subject experts, responsible commissions and human and logistical infrastructure for the organization and holding of the Matura exam;

1.3. provides printing press for the printing of tests;

1.4. provides transportation for the parties involved;

1.5. maintains the database, publishes reports on student results and issues candidates' certificates;

1.6. supervises the process of implementing the Matura exam by the authorities defined in this law;

1.7. appoints the State Matura Commission, taking into account gender and ethnic representation, through a decision of the Minister;

1.8. appoints by decision the administrators and supervisors involved in the Matura process.

#### Article 8

State Matura Commission

\1. The State Matura Commission has eleven (11) members with a three (3) year mandate, in this composition:

1.1. five (5) members from the Ministry, of which at least one (1) comes from the Unit;

1.2. two (2) members from Higher Education Institutions;

1.3. one (1) member from the Kosova Pedagogical Institute;

1.4. one (1) member from the Municipal Education Directorates (MED);

1.5. two (2) members from higher secondary schools, from which one (1) from gymnasiums and one (1) from vocational schools;

\2. The chairperson of the SMC is one of the five (5) members of the Ministry, appointed by decision of the Minister.

\3. SMC, as needed, can invite different experts to discuss and solve professional issues or to provide expertise that preceded its decisions.

#### Article 9

Duties and responsibilities of the State Matura commission

\1. The competences of the State Matura Commission are:

1.1. approves the list of subject experts for the compilation of requirements for the Matura exam according to the Unit's proposal;

1.2. approves the work plan for the organization of the Matura exam drafted by the Unit for the following year, no later than September 30;

1.3. approves the Matura exam calendar drafted by the Unit for each exam period for the following school year;

1.4. approves changes in the Matura exam calendar due to emergency and unusual situations announced at the local/national level by the Unit in accordance with SMC;

1.5. approves the catalog and harmonizes the subjects for the Matura exam, in accordance with the curricula according to the directions and profiles respectively, with the proposal of the Unit;

1.6. approves the format and form of the tests designed by the Unit;

1.7. defines the criteria for administration of the Matura exam;

1.8. takes a decision on the permission or justification of the candidates who, due to health problems, death in the family, traffic accident and other reasons, were not present at the Matura exam;

1.9. approves the guide for the administration of the Matura exam prepared by the Unit.

1.1. designs standardized tests based on the Kosova Curriculum Framework;

1.2. engages subject matter experts through the competitive procedure and sends the list of selected experts to SMC for approval;

1.3. prepares and after approval by SMC, publishes and distributes the guide with the relevant information for the Matura exam to the responsible parties;

#### Article 13

Examination Center Commission (ECC)

\1. ECC shall consist of three (3) members. One (1) member is the principal of the respective school, one (1) member from the MED and one (1) member from the Ministry, who is also the president of ECC.

\2. The competences of the members of the ECC are regulated by a sub-legal act.

### CHAPTER IV

### THE PREPARATION OF THE STATE MATURA EXAM

#### Article 15

Submission of the Matura exam

\1. The candidate submits the Matura exam no later than fifteen (15) days before the day of the exam, according to the procedure or guideline of the SMC/Ministry.

\2. The information that must be presented in the candidate's file are:

2.1. grade certificates on completion of higher secondary school grades;

2.2. certificate from the birth register;

2.3. diploma of the higher vocational secondary schools.

#### Article 16

Place of holding the exam

\1. Places to hold the Matura exam are set by MED.

\2. The premises where the Matura exam is held must meet these criteria:

2.1. to have enough space to hold the exam without any obstacle;

2.2. to have sufficient lighting and ventilation;

2.3. to have informative and guiding materials for the smooth progress of the exam;

2.4. to be supervised and secured so that candidates can undergo the exam without being obstructed by others;

2.5. to have the possibility of placing devices that prohibit electronic communication inside and outside during the exam.

#### Article 17

Verification of the candidate's identity Verification of the candidate's identity during entrance to the Matura exam is done through the official identification document or the document issued by the school institution.

#### Article 18

The evaluation criteria of the Matura exam

\1. The evaluation of the candidate's answers is done with points, in an identifiable and quantifiable form.

\2. The passing threshold in the State Matura exam is forty percent (40%).

\3. The form and content of the Matura exam certificate is determined by SMC in a standardized way.

\4. Candidates dissatisfied with the results achieved in the Matura exam, have the right to reject the result in writing before receiving the certificate and to undergo the Matura exam in the following terms. Such rejection of the candidate is addressed to the Unit in writing within a period of two (2) days from the day of the result announcement.

#### Article 19

Obligations of higher education institutions

\1. The results of the Matura exam are an integral part of the criteria for the admission of candidates to programs of higher education institutions.

\2. The admission of students will be based on the results achieved:

2.1. thirty percent (30%) during higher secondary education;

2.2. thirty percent (30%) in the Matura exam;

2.3. forty percent (40%) in the internal assessment of academic units.

#### Article 20

Publication of exam results The unit is obliged to present the results of the Matura exam to the SMC for approval.

After approval by SMC, the Unit publishes the results.

#### Article 21

Right to appeal

\1. All candidates who dispute the evaluation process of their test for procedural or material reasons have the right to appeal.

\2. The candidate has the right to file a complaint:

2.1. in the first instance, at the Unit;

2.2. in the second instance, at the Commission for Submissions and Complaints in the Ministry.

\3. The deadline for submitting a complaint to the first-level body is three (3) days after the publication of the results, while in the second instance body it is five (5) days from the day the candidate received the response to the complaint.

### CHAPTER V

### SAFETY, STORAGE AND ARCHIVING THE MATERIAL/DOCUMENTATION

#### Article 22

Safety of State Matura Documentation

\1. The natural and legal person directly involved in the implementation of the Matura exam and who has access to the exam materials, sensitive data related to the exam and personal data for the identification of candidates, must:

1.1. to preserve the data secrecy and handle the data and materials in accordance with the rules for preserving the secrecy of the exams determined by the ministry, and

1.2. to sign the document prepared in accordance with the legal status by the ministry and approved by SMC.

\2. The natural and legal person who acts contrary to this law and the rules on the exam confidentiality, the provisions of the Criminal Code and other legal and sub-legal acts in force are applied to him/her.

#### Article 23

Storage and archiving the State Matura documentation

\1. Materials containing exam questions/requests, forms, sensitive exam-related data, candidate identification personal data, and test scores are classified as official confidential documents.

\2. The following rules apply to storage and security of exam documents and materials:

2.1. all exam documentation is stored on the computer, using security elements such as: multiple passwords, lack of electronic and network communication, and storage in physical form is subject to the same security standards;

2.2. each secret document is stored in the Unit's archive for six (6) months. After that period, such documents are destroyed according to the legislation in force on personal data;

2.3. the storage and archiving of Matura documentation is based on the legal provisions in force for the storage and archiving of official documentation and that of personal data.

### CHAPTER VI

### MISDEMEANORS AND DISCIPLINARY VIOLATIONS

#### Article 24

Misdemeanors

\1. In terms of this law, a misdemeanor is considered:

1.1. unauthorized access to documents and data classified as secret or enabling access to them in an unauthorized manner;

1.2. violation of the information confidentiality or official documents, including test materials and their unauthorized distribution;

1.3. making possible, from the part of the official person, copying or systematic misuse of activity in the premises where the State Matura exam is held;

1.4. non-notification, by the official person, to the higher bodies for copying or systematic misuse of activities in the premises where the Matura exam is held;

1.5. serious disobedience of the authorized person to the orders and instructions of the bodies that organize the Matura exam;

1.6. the organization of the activity of serious legislation violations by authorized persons and parties involved directly and indirectly.

\2. Misdemeanor violations will be sanctioned with a fine, according to the following definition:

2.1. in case the violation from sub-paragraph 1.1. and 1.2. of Article 24 of this Law is ascertained, the natural person is fined in the amount of one thousand (1,000)

up to two thousand (2,000) euros, while the legal person in the amount of five thousand (5,000) up to fifteen thousand (15,000) euros;

2.2. in case the violation from sub-paragraph 1.3. of Article 24 of this law is ascertained, the natural person is fined in the amount of one thousand (1,000) up to two thousand (2,000) euros, while the legal person in the amount of five thousand (5,000) up to twenty thousand (20,000) euros;

2.3. in case the violation from sub-paragraph 1.4., 1.5. and 1.6. of Article 24 of this law is ascertained, the natural person is fined in the amount of one thousand (1,000) up to one thousand five hundred (1,500) euros, while the legal person in the amount of five thousand (5,000) up to ten thousand (10,000) euros.

\3. The procedures related to the execution of the misdemeanor sanction, the appeal and other procedural principles are determined by the relevant law on misdemeanors of the Republic of Kosova.

\4. The competent authority to investigate, ascertain and impose the misdemeanor sanction is the Education Inspectorate of the Republic of Kosova, based on the official investigation or report by a competent body of the Matura process according to this Law or third parties.

#### Article 25

Disciplinary violations

\1. Disciplinary violation will be considered any violation of the duties and responsibilities of the person or persons authorized to organize and conduct the Matura exam according to this Law, which have not reached the degree of dangerousness to be considered a misdemeanor according to Article 24 of this Law.

\2. Disciplinary violations will be ascertained by the responsible bodies of the State Matura defined in Article 6 paragraph 1. of this Law, and for those, following disciplinary measures will be given to the offending entities:

2.1. termination of the commitment contract for the employee to serve as authorized person in the Matura exam process;

2.2. decision on non-compensation, or partial compensation, of the authorized person in the Matura exam process;

2.3. publication of the name in the list of transgressors of the Ministry's disciplinary rules;

2.4. taking off the right to serve as an authorized person in the Matura process.

\3. The disciplinary violations will be decided in an administrative procedure according to the relevant law for the general administrative procedure in the Republic of Kosova.

\4. The implementation of disciplinary procedures is supervised by the Education Inspectorate of the Republic of Kosova.
