### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### ALBANIA ON THE MEDITERRANEAN GAMES PRISHTINA 2030

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is the ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Albania on the Mediterranean Games Prishtina 2030.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Albania on the Mediterranean Games Prishtina 2030 shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosova.
