# LAW NO. 10/L-021 ON THE FINANCING OF WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT

### LAW NO. 10/L-021 ON THE FINANCING OF WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT

### LAW NO. 10/L-021

### ON THE FINANCING OF WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE FINANCING OF WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. The purpose of this law is to regulate the manner and the sources of financing the water resources management in the Republic of Kosovo, the financing principles and water payments for the use of water and the discharge of waste water, as well as the method of determining the amount of these payments and the method of payment of water payments.

\2. This law is partially in compliance with Directive 2000/60/EC of the Parliament and of the Council of Europe establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy

### (OJ L 327).

#### Article 2

Scope

\1. This law shall be applicable by all institutions and persons who use water, discharge waste water and those who produce or import mineral fertilizers.

\2. The provisions of this law do not apply to the fees for water supply services and sewerage services which are regulated by water services legislation.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The expressions used in this law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. Agency – Agency for Water Resources Management;

1.2. Aquaculture – the cultivation of aquatic organisms for the purposes of their economic use;

1.3. Alluvion – sand, gravel and stones in the bed and around the banks of streams;

1.4. Inspectorate - Central Inspectorate of Environment, Water, Nature, Spatial Planning and Construction, within the ministry responsible for environment and spatial planning;

1.5. Minister - the minister responsible for the water resources management;

1.6. Ministry - the ministry responsible for the water resources management;

1.7. Water abstraction - taking water from water resources of surface water or groundwater;

1.8. Water facilities - constructions together with their accompanying equipment which constitute a technical and functional entirety and serve for water regulation, protection from the harmful actions of waters, abstraction of waters for their use, and for water protection;

1.9. Water service provider - any provider of water supply services; services of waste water collection, treatment and discharge; and wholesale water supply services;

1.10. Person - any natural or legal person;

1.11. Water use – water use according to this law means the use of water by a natural or legal person according to the water right defined by the legislation in force;

1.12. Water meter - the measuring device with all its accompanying components (such as flow sensors, devices for automatic flow recording) whereby there is measured and recorded the amount of water flowing through the flow medium, whether pipe or channel.

#### Article 4

Principles of financing water resources management

\1. The financing of water resources management shall be based on the following principles:

1.1. the management of water resources is financed from the revenues realized from water payments which are paid by water users and water polluters, as well as from other sources defined by this law;

1.2. water has its own economic value, which is determined based on the economic analysis of the costs necessary for its use and protection, as well as for the stimulation of users for efficient use of water resources;

1.3. the water payments that are paid according to this law are determined based on the ‘user pays’ and ‘polluter pays’ principles.

#### Article 5

Financing sources for water resources management

\1. The financing sources for water resources management shall be as follows:

1.1. Water payments;

1.2. Budget appropriations;

1.3. Donations and other sources in compliance with the legislation in force.

### CHAPTER II

### WATER PAYMENTS

#### Article 6

Classification of water payments

\1. Water payments are:

1.1. Payments for water use;

1.2. Payments for water protection;

1.3. Payments for the use of alluvium.

#### Article 7

Payments for water use Payment for water use shall be paid for water abstraction and its use.

#### Article 8

Obligors of payment for water use

\1. Payment for water use shall be paid by natural and legal persons who abstract and use surface water and groundwater for:

1.1. Hydropower generation;

1.2. Personal needs or for the provision of water supply services;

1.3. Irrigation;

1.4. Balneological needs – curative spas;

1.5. Packaging and sales;

1.6. Technological process;

1.7. Aquaculture;

1.8. Swimming pools and aquaparks;

1.9. Other commercial purposes.

\2. Water payment shall not be paid for the use of water for fire extinguishing.

#### Article 9

Basis for calculating water payments for water use

\1. Water payment for water use according to paragraph 1., sub-paragraphs 1.2. to 1.9. of Article 8 of this law shall be calculated based on the amount of the abstracted water, expressed in cubic meters (m3).

\2. Water payment for water utilization for hydropower production according to paragraph 1., sub-paragraph 1.1. of Article 8 of this law shall be calculated based on the amount of electricity produced, expressed in kilowatts (kWh).

\3. Exceptionally from paragraph 1. of this Article, in case the amount of water for irrigation cannot be metered, the water payment shall be calculated based on the area/surface of the irrigated land, expressed in hectares (ha).

#### Article 10

Water payment for water protection Water payment for the protection of waters shall be paid for direct or indirect pollution of waters in proportion to the degree of pollution caused by the waste water discharge, respectively, by other substances that deteriorate the quality of waters.

#### Article 11

Obligors of payment for water protection

\1. Obligors of payment for water protection are natural and legal persons who:

1.1. Discharge waste water;

1.2. Produce or import mineral fertilizers;

1.3. Produce or import preparations for plant protection.

#### Article 12

Basis for calculating water payments for waste water

\1. Payment for the waste water discharge from sub-paragraph 1.1. of Article 11 of this law shall be calculated based on the quantity expressed in cubic meters (m3) of the waste water discharged.

\2. If the amount of discharged water cannot be metered for any reason, the payment for the discharge of waste water shall be calculated based on the amount of water extracted from the resource for persons discharging waste water who use water directly from the water resource, respectively, the amount of water supplied by the water service provider to the persons who discharge the waste water and who are supplied with water through public water supply systems.

\3. Payment for the production or import of fertilizers or preparations according to subparagraph 1.2. shall be calculated based on the quantity expressed in kilograms (kg) of mineral fertilizers produced or imported respectively.

\4. Payment for the production or import of preparations according to sub-paragraph 1.3.

shall be calculated based on the quantity expressed in kilograms (kg) or liters (l) of the plant protection preparations produced or imported, respectively.

\5. The amount of the payment for the waste water discharge from sub-paragraph 1.1. of Article 11 may not be less than the costs of the waste water treatment.

\6. Payment for the waste water discharge shall not be paid if the quality of the waste water discharged is in accordance with the legal requirements for the waste water treatment.

#### Article 13

Metering of the amount of the water used and the amount of the discharged waste water

\1. All obligors of payment for water use according to sub-paragraph 1.2. to 1.9. of Article 8 of this law shall be obliged to install the water meter for metering the amount of water used at all water abstraction points.

\2. All obligors of payment for the protection of waters that discharge waste water according to sub-paragraph 1.1. of Article 11 of this law shall be obliged to install water meters at all points of waste water discharge.

\3. The type of water meter and the location of the installation must be such as to enable accurate metering of the amount of water abstracted, respectively of the amount of waste water discharged, as well as verification by inspectors of the volume of water abstracted respectively of discharged waste water.

\4. The Ministry shall issue a sub-legal act for the metering of water by persons who use water and/or who discharge waste water according to the water right.

\5. The sub-legal act from paragraph 4. of this Article shall regulate issues related to the location of the water meter installation, verification of measurement accuracy, selection and installation conditions, the manner of keeping data of abstracted water and reporting of information.

#### Article 14

Water payments for the use of alluvium

1.The obligors of the payment for the extraction of alluvium are natural and legal persons who extract sand, gravel, stones and other material from the beds and banks of streams as well as from the beds and banks of accumulations and areas at risk of erosion.

\2. The water payment for the use of alluvium shall not be paid in cases where the alluvium is used for the construction and maintenance of regulatory or protective facilities in the sections of water bodies from which the alluvium is used, based in this case on the design documentation and water acts.

#### Article 15

Basis for calculating the payment for the use of alluvium The payment for the use of alluvium shall be calculated based on the quantity in cubic meters (m3) of the extracted alluvium reported by the person who performs the extraction of alluvium based on the right secured in compliance with the legislation in force, as well as confirmed by the authorized person who supervises the extraction of alluvium.

#### Article 16

Calculation of water payments

1.The Agency for Water Resources Management (hereinafter: the Agency) shall do the calculation of water payments.

\2. The calculation of water payment shall be done in the form of the financial decision issued by the Agency, to which is also attached the statement of the calculation according to the special elements of the calculation.

\3. The calculation of water payments shall be done by the 20th of the following month for the previous month based on the data reported for the previous month.

\4. In the event that, for any reason that is acceptable to the Agency, there is no data for the previous month, the decision shall be made based on the assessment of the water used, respectively the energy produced, and the amount of the waste water discharged.

\5. The assessment from paragraph 4. of this Article shall be based on the average consumption of the corresponding period of the previous year.

\6. The calculation according to paragraph 4. of this Article may be done for a period not longer than two (2) months.

#### Article 17

The amount of water payments The amount of payments for the water use, payments for water protection, and payments for the use of alluvium, as well as the method of calculating these payments, shall be defined by

#### Article 18

Collection of water payments The Agency shall be responsible for the distribution of financial decisions to the obligors of water payments as well as the collection of revenues from water payments.

#### Article 19

Reporting by the obligors of water payments

\1. Obligors of water payments from Article 8 of this law must report to the Agency on the amount of water abstracted (in m3), respectively on the amount of energy produced (in kWh)

in the case of hydropower production.

\2. Obligors of water payments from Article 11 of this law must report on the quantities of waste water discharged (in m3), respectively on the quantity of chemical fertilizers or plant protection preparations produced or imported (in kg or l), in the case of producers and importers of mineral fertilizers and/or preparations for the protection of plants which are introduced into the Kosovo market.

\3. Reportings on the quantities of water used and on the quantities of waste water discharged must be based on meterings with adequate water meters and the reported data must be verifiable.

\4. In the event that the obligors of water payment from paragraph 1. of this Article do not report on the quantities of water abstracted respectively on the amount of energy produced in accordance with paragraph 3. of this Article, their water right for water use shall be revoked, in accordance with the legislation in force.

\5. Reportings from paragraph 1. and 2. of this Article must be submitted by the 10th of the following month for the previous month.

\6. Kosovo Customs shall be obliged to inform the Agency about the importers as well as the type and quantity of mineral fertilizers and plant protection preparations which are imported and introduced into the market of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 20

Record of water payments

\1. Record for water payments shall be kept by the Agency.

\2. In case of administrative or judicial proceedings, this record shall have the power of a public administration document.

\3. The sub-legal act issued pursuant to Article 16 shall define the content of the record that the Agency must keep.

#### Article 21

Method of paying water payments Water payments shall be paid according to the financial decisions.

#### Article 22

Deadlines for the payment of water payments The deadline for payment of water payments shall be fifteen (15) days from the date of issuance of the financial decision.

#### Article 23

Collection and interest in water payments With regards to the issues related to the collection, interest, statutory limitation and control of water payments, there shall be applied the provisions of the relevant tax legislation.

#### Article 24

Appeals

\1. The procedure for submitting and reviewing appelas shall be applied in accordance with the legislation in force on general administrative procedure.

\2. The superior body that reviews appeals under this Article shall be the Ministry.

\3. Notwithstanding the appeal, the obligor of the water payment must pay the amount of the water payment within the specified period.

#### Article 25

Administrative supervision The administrative supervision on the implementation of this Law and the acts issued based on it, as well as the administrative supervision of the Agency in the implementation of public authorizations and responsibilities under this Law shall be applied by the Ministry.

#### Article 26

Inspective supervision Inspective supervision over the implementation of the provisions of this Law and the acts issued based on it, except for the implementation of public responsibilities according to Article 20 of this Law, shall be applied by the Inspectorate.

### CHAPTER III

### OTHER FINANCING SOURCES

#### Article 27

Financing from the Budget Financing of investments for the water resources management may also be done from the budget of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 28

Financing from donations and other funds The construction and maintenance of water facilities may also be financed by donations, credits and loans from financial institutions.

### CHAPTER IV

### PUNITIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

Minor-offence sanctions

\1. Legal entities that act in contradiction to the provisions of this law will be sanctioned with a fine of five hundred (500) to twenty thousand (20,000) EUR for the following minor offences:

1.1. they do not report according to Article 19 of this law;

1.2. they do not permit authorized persons access to the documentation and the works performed in accordance with Article 26 of this law.

\2. For the violations from paragraph 1. of this Article, the natural person and the responsible person of the legal entity shall be fined from one hundred (100) to two thousand (2,000)

### EUR.

\3. With the exception of paragraphs 1. and 2. of this Article, for minor offences committed with the purpose of obtaining personal gain with which the financial benefit was obtained, the perpetrator may be severely punished, and at most up to the double amount provided for with the minor offence sanction of a fine for that minor offence.

\4. Imposition of the minor-offence sanctions with a fine defined in this law shall be done in compliance with the relevant legislation in force on minor offence.

### CHAPTER V

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

Approval of sub-legal acts

\1. Within six (6) months from the entry into force of this law, the Ministry shall issue a sublegal act on water metering according to Article 13 of this law.

\2. Within twelve (12) months from the entry into force of this law, the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, at the recommendation of the Ministry, shall approve the sub-legal act on water payments according to Article 17 of this law.

#### Article 31

Installation of water meters

\1. Persons who currently use water based on a water permit or concession without a water meter for metering the amount of water abstracted, must install water meters at all points where they abstract water from the water resource within a period of twelve (12) months from the entry into force of this law.

\2. In case the obligors of water payment do not act according to paragraph 1. of this Article, their water permit shall be revoked.

\3. Calculation of the water payment for the use of water for the period until the water meter is installed according to paragraph 1. of this Article shall be based on the professional assessment carried out by a commission appointed by the Agency for this purpose.

\4. The assessment from paragraph 3. of this Article must take into account the request for water submitted by the holder of the water permit or concession when applying for a water permit, the production or operational capacity of the holder of the water permit and other information that can be assessed as relevant for this assessment.

#### Article 32

Agency Until the establishment and functioning of the Agency, its responsibilities shall be implemented by respective unit of the Ministry competent on water resources management.
