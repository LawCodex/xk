# LAW NO. 10/L-034 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM NO. 2 OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVA AND THE EUROPEAN UNION FOR THE IPA 2020 PROGRAMME”

### LAW NO. 10/L-034 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM NO. 2 OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVA AND THE EUROPEAN UNION FOR THE IPA 2020 PROGRAMME”

### LAW NO. 10/L-034

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM NO. 2 OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVA AND THE EUROPEAN UNION FOR THE IPA

### 2020 PROGRAMME”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM NO. 2 OF THE FINANCING

### AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVA AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

### FOR THE IPA 2020 PROGRAMME”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Addendum No. 2 of the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosova and the European Union for the IPA 2020 Programme”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Addendum No. 2 of the Financing Agreement for IPA 2020 between the Republic of Kosova and the European Union” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law.

The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosova.

Law No. 10/L-034 24 April 2026
