#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement “High Speed Road Pristina-Mitrovica Project”

(Additional Loan) between the Saudi Fund for Development and the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement

\1. The Loan Agreement “High Speed Road Pristina-Mitrovica Project” (Additional Loan) between the Saudi Fund for Development and the Republic of Kosovo is hereby ratified.

\2. The main objective of this project is to provide a loan for a road project that will have beneficial effects on the economic development of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. The Agreement includes the provision of a loan to assist in financing the Pristina-Mitrovica High Speed Road Project (Additional Loan).

\4. The Loan Agreement is totally amounted to EUR 13,020,804.42 or (53,000,000) Saudi Riyals.

This will enable the Government of Kosovo to finance the reasonable cost of goods, works and services required for the implementation of the Project.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to this Law there is the Loan Agreement “High Speed Road Pristina-Mitrovica Project”

(Additional Loan) between the Saudi Fund for Development and the Republic of Kosovo.
