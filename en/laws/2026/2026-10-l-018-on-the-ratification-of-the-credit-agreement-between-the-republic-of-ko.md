### DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE PROJECT “WESTERN BALKANS TRADE AND

### TRANSPORT FACILITATION 2.0”

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims at ratifying the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance and the International Development Association for the Project “Western Balkans Trade and Transport Facilitation 2.0”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. The Law on the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance and the International Development Association for the Project “Western Balkans Trade and Transport Facilitation 2.0” is hereby ratified.

\2. The main objective of the agreement is to increase the efficiency of trade and transport and to strengthen the quality infrastructure in the territory of Kosovo.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Attached to this Law there is the Credit Agreement for the Project “Western Balkans Trade and Transport Facilitation 2.0”.
