### LAW NO. 10/L-016

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE FRENCH DEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE POLICY-BASED CREDIT

### PROGRAM

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo,

Adopts:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE FRENCH DEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE POLICY-BASED

### CREDIT PROGRAM

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the French Development Agency for the Policy-Based Credit Program signed on March 18, 2026 in Prishtina.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. The Law on the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the French Development Agency for the Policy-Based Credit Program is hereby ratified.

\2. The main objective of this project is to advance the energy and climate sector in Kosovo through energy market reforms, promoting decarbonisation, increasing energy efficiency and developing renewable energy sources, as well as accelerating the transition to green energy through harmonizing the legal and policy framework with European Union standards and fulfilling the country's international commitments.

\3. The Agreement includes financial support for the implementation of reforms in the energy and climate sector, including the advancement of the energy market, the promotion of decarbonisation policies, the increase of energy efficiency and the development of renewable energy sources, as well as the harmonization of the legal and regulatory framework with the standards of the European Union and the Energy Community.

### LAW NO. 10/L-016 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### FRENCH DEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE POLICY-BASED CREDIT PROGRAM

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to this Law there is the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the French Development Agency for the Policy-Based Credit Program.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 10/L-016 10 April 2026
