### LAW ON SOCIAL AND AFFORDABLE HOUSING

### CHAPTER I

### GNERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims at establishing social and affordable housing programs, through defining the rules, procedures of planning, administration, implementation and delivery of the programs, as well as determining the eligibility criteria and opportunities to benefit from housing programs for families and individuals in need.

#### Article 2

Scope Provisions of this Law shall apply to the institutions responsible for planning, administration, monitoring, implementation and delivery of housing programs at the central and municipal level, as well as the beneficiaries and other parties.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this law have the following meanings:

1.1. Social housing - is housing for rent in a publicly owned housing unit, from the public stock of social housing, or of other entities, which are given to families or individuals who have not resolved the housing issue, whose income is insufficient to provide adequate market-rate housing. The payment of rent for social housing is the payment percentage not exceeding thirty percent (30%) of the family’s income;

1.2. Affordable housing- housing units, such as those for rental and those for profit ownership, that are affordable, from that part of society whose income is below the national average household incomes;

1.3. Housing conditions - minimum standards of living, ensuring unhindered access to basic services such as: water, hygiene, electricity, sufficient space, access to health, social, work and education services. Adequate housing is a fundamental human right, as defined in international human rights conventions, namely Article 25 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and Article 11 (1) of the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights;

1.4. Measures for realization of affordable housing - subsidy of loan, loan interest, immediate grants, or other support, such as technical assistance, facilitation of access to microcredit, partial loan guarantee or social support defined by Law;

1.5. Housing unit - a housing unit or other independent spaces in an individually owned building, the size of which is determined by its horizontal and vertical boundaries;

1.6. Residential building - are two (2) or more residential units, with or without auxiliary spaces that make up an entirety and have a common entrance;

1.7. Housing stock - is the total number of housing units, houses, jointly-owned housing (condominiums) in Kosovo, including those in public, rented, private ownership and other ways of acquiring ownership, such as properties confiscated from illegal activities;

1.8. Stock of social housing units - are all housing units intended for social housing in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.9. Target groups - are the groups that, due to economic, social factors, geographical location, or physical circumstances, are at high risk of falling into poverty, harm, or discrimination, which are set forth in Article 5 of this Law;

1.10. Beneficiary - is the family or the individual, who according to the provisions of this law and sublegal-acts, have won the right to benefit from social or affordable housing programs;

1.11. Special housing programs - are programs that provide suitable and affordable housing for families and individuals who are not in an economic position to afford the offers of the free housing market and need the state assistance;

1.12. Management and administration of buildings and housing units - are the activities, decisions and measures related to the maintenance of the building for social and affordable housing;

#### Article 4

General principles

1.The principles on which social and affordable housing policies are governed in the Republic of Kosovo are:

1.1. Environmental protection - housing shall be planned, built and used in a manner that reduces environmental impact and promotes environmental sustainability;

1.2. Economic effectiveness - housing shall be considered as a stable element for a high-level economy, also a sector that fulfils people’s needs;

1.3. Social inclusion and participation - policy and debate on housing shall be enhanced by emphasizing civic engagement, social inclusion, public health, transparency and ethical processes interest;

1.4. Cultural appropriateness - housing policies shall consider issues related to cultural identity, cultural value and emotional well-being, where residents have a sense of belonging and are able to participate in community life;

1.5. Non-discrimination and accessibility - any other principle that prohibits discrimination and inequality, disqualification or other forms of abuse in the treatment of beneficiaries and the fair and equitable delivery of housing programs;

1.6. Partnership - social and affordable housing shall be developed and maintained through partnerships between government bodies, municipalities, private developers and non-profit organisations.

#### Article 6

Affordability rate

\1. Social housing is considered affordable if the family or individual does not spend more than thirty percent (30%) of the net monthly income for housing.

\2. The degree of affordability is determined according to the income level of the family economy, divided into four (4) categories as set forth in Article 9 of this Law.

\3. Housing expenses do not include expenses for water supply services, electricity, and other basic services.

#### Article 7

Criteria of benefit

\1. The right to benefit from social housing programs is that family or individual that:

1.1. is a citizen of the Republic of Kosovo, or has a residence permit in the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. does not own or possess any residential unit, individual house or other, that meets the minimum conditions for living, based on the standards and norms of construction and housing determined by the legislation in force in the Republic of Kosovo;

1.3. the residence issue has remained or is at risk of remaining unresolved, as a result of one (1) or more of the following factors:

1.3.1. damage of the building to the point that it does not meet the conditions to be a residential unit;

1.3.2. from the risk of destruction due to its age;

1.3.3. displacement due to public investments, or due to the demolition of the building, which does not meet the conditions to be legalized;

1.3.4. the impossibility of paying the rent at market conditions;

1.3.5. family or individual without care;

1.3.6. natural disasters and man-made disasters;

1.3.7. damage of the individual house, residential units during the 1998/1999 war;

1.3.8. the income of the family or individual is insufficient to cover the cost of housing according to the criteria specified in Article 9 of this Law;

1.3.9. the family or individual meets the other criteria to benefit from any of the social housing programs, according to the public call and the criteria of this Law;

and

1.3.10. other circumstances which municipalities consider relevant in the local context.

The right of priority

1.1. family composition;

1.3. the social-economic situation assessed according to income categories, defined in Article 9 of this Law.

#### Article 9

Categorization according to income

\1. The family or individual is categorized according to the level of monthly incomes as follows:

1.1. First category (I), extreme poor;

1.2. Second category (II), poor;

1.3. Third category (III), with low incomes;

1.4. Fourth category (IV), with average incomes.

\2. The definition of the category from paragraph 1 of this Article, is done through the verification and application of the poverty test, based on the criteria of OECD.

\3. The Ministry shall, by sub-legal act, determine the category of the family or individual and the criteria they must fulfil in order to benefit from the housing programs.

#### Article 10

Application and selection procedures

\2. The municipality, through a public call, shall set the benefit criteria, the procedures and application method and the time limits for social housing programs.

\3. The application commences upon the submission of the application, according to the standard form provided by the municipality of the applicant’s residence.

\4. The application procedures shall be transparent, open and enable the application of any family or individual that meets the criteria provided for by this law.

\5. The municipality shall offer support for filling out the application, without administrative costs.

\6. The municipality shall publish on its website the information related to the application procedures.

\7. The municipality shall be obliged to publish in a complete and publicly accessible manner, for each public call:

7.1. the list of applicants;

7.2. the list of beneficiaries;

7.3. the scoring for each applicant according to the determined criteria; and

7.4. the reasoning for selection or rejection.

\8. The publication of data shall be made on the central electronic platform for housing and on the official website of the municipality, in accordance with the relevant legislation on the protection of personal data.

\9. Any selection decision that is not justified and published according to this Article shall be considered invalid.

\10. Non-compliance with this Article constitutes a serious administrative violation and entails disciplinary responsibility for the responsible officials.

### SOCIAL HOUSING PROGRAM

#### Article 12

General conditions of the Social Housing Program

\1. The Social Housing Program shall provide non-profit rental housing for families or individuals whose income is insufficient to cover the cost of housing according to the conditions and criteria of this law.

\2. Social Housing Program shall be realized through:

2.1. providing non-profit rental housing units owned by the municipality, or any other public entity which are managed by the municipality or through any other public authority; and

2.2. housing rent subsidy for beneficiary families or individuals in privately owned housing units.

#### Article 13

Stock of social housing units

\1. The municipality shall have under its ownership, shall create and expands the stock of social housing units through:

1.1. renovation of the existing social housing buildings and units;

1.2. conversion of old buildings or the existing structures into social housing buildings;

1.3. construction of new buildings for social housing by the municipality, the government or donors;

1.4. purchase of buildings or housing units with favourable conditions in the market for the purpose of use for social housing;

1.5. construction of social housing units by public or private entities or public-private partnerships, according to the provisions of the legislation in force; and

#### Article 14

Administration and maintenance of social housing buildings and units

\1. Social housing buildings built with financing from the budget of the Republic of Kosovo or other funds shall be transferred under the ownership of the municipality in whose territory they are built.

2.Social housing buildings shall be administered and maintained by the responsible body of the municipality.

\3. Municipalities have the right to transfer administration rights to other public or private entities, including public enterprises, commercial companies, NGOs and entities with social enterprise status.

Restriction and transfer of the right of social housing

### CHAPTER VI

### AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROGRAM

#### Article 26

Affordable Housing Program

\1. The affordable housing program is a program that facilitates the benefit of m housing units from the beneficiary selected on the basis of the criteria determined by this Law and sub-legal acts, through the following measures:

1.1. loan subsidy or loan interest;

1.2. offering immediate grants;

1.3. other social, technical or financial support, which is considered suitable for the realization of goals of this law and for which justification and approval has been given by the Ministry;

1.4. construction of housing units by the Municipality or the Government or in cooperation between the Municipality and the Government as part of an affordable housing program; and

1.5. other form approved by Decision of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. Beneficiaries of this program are young couples and families consisting of a single parent, based on the following criteria:

2.1. the issue of residence has not been resolved, according to the provisions of this Law;

2.2. the age of one of the young couples is under the age of thirty-seven (37);

2.3. they realize income according to categories defined in Article 9, paragraph 1, subparagraphs 1.3 and 1.4 of this Law.

3.The Government of the Republic of Kosovo, at the proposal of the Ministry, by a sub-legal act defines the procedures and criteria for benefiting from the affordable housing program.

\4. The measure defined in paragraph 1 sub-paragraph 1.4 of this Article is regulated by the call for application and agreement between the Municipality and the Ministry.

#### Article 27

Sale of the Housing Unit from the Affordable Housing Program

\1. This Article shall determine the conditions and the manner of selling a publicly-owned housing unit for which the residential right to affordable housing has been acquired in accordance with this Law.

\2. A housing unit financed and constructed within the framework of the Affordable Housing Program shall be sold to the beneficiary in accordance with this Law.

\3. The right to purchase belongs solely to the beneficiary selected on the basis of the criteria and categories determined by this Law and/or sub-legal acts.

\4. The beneficiary who has acquired the right to purchase the housing unit, in the event of a request for selling it, after fulfilling all contractual obligations, is obliged to return the ownership right to the Housing Agency/the body responsible for the administration of affordable housing units.

\5. The Housing Agency shall carry out the evaluation and financial compensation of the housing unit in accordance with the criteria determined by sub-legal act, for the purpose of reprocessing this unit for future applicants.

\6. The evaluation of the Housing Agency shall, under no circumstances, result in an amount lower than the total of all contractual obligations completed by the beneficiary.

\1. Housing units from the affordable housing program can be financed from the budget of the Ministry, the budget of the municipality, the budget of other public institutions, public-private partnership, donations and loans.

\2. Funding from the budget of the Republic of Kosovo or municipalities is offered in the form of a grant or loan subsidy, or loan interest, or the construction of units, with the purpose of transferring ownership for categories determined in Article 9, paragraph 1, sub-paragraphs 1.3 and 1.4 of this the Law, according to criteria determined in Article 26, paragraph 2 of this Law.

\3. Additional conditions and criteria can be established in cases where the financing of affordable housing programs is done through the financing of International Financial Institutions, as part of a ratified international agreement.

### LAW NO. 10/L-022 ON SOCIAL AND AFFORDABLE HOUSING

#### Article 29

Limitation and transfer of the right to the affordable housing benefit

\1. The beneficiary family in the affordable housing program cannot alienate or rent out the housing unit during the loan repayment period.

\2. The beneficiary family in the affordable housing program who has made the loan payment, by becoming its owner, in case of alienation of the housing unit, loses the right to benefit from other housing programs for a period of ten (10) years.

### CHAPTER VIII

### INSPECTIVE SUPERVISION

#### Article 41

Inspective supervision

\1. The inspective supervision for the implementation of this Law and the relevant sub-legal acts shall be performed by the construction/housing inspectors in the Municipality, in compliance with the relevant legislation into force on Inspections.

\2. The relevant inspector of the Municipality is obliged to carry out a regular inspection every six

(6) months in the housing units owned by the Municipality and used by the beneficiaries.

\3. If during the inspection it is found that the provisions of this Law have been violated, the inspector is obliged to present this to the responsible body.

### CHAPTER IX

### APPEAL

#### Article 42

The right to appeal

\1. Against the decision of the Commission for evaluation and selection, an appeal is allowed in accordance with the legislation in force on the General Administrative Procedure.

\2. Against the decisions issued in the inspection procedure according to this Law, an appeal is allowed in accordance with the legislation in force on the General Administrative Procedure and the legislation in force on Inspections.

\3. The superior body that reviews the appeals according to this Article is the Appeals Commission, established by decision of the Mayor of the Municipality. The work and procedure of the appeals commission shall be based on the rules defined for collegial bodies in the legislation in force on the General Administrative Procedure and that on Inspections.

\4. Against the decision of the Commission for reviewing the appeals of the Municipality, an administrative dispute may be initiated in the responsible court for administrative matters.

### CHAPTER X

### PUNITIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 43

Minor offense sanction by fine

\1. For violation of provisions of this Law or the sub-legal acts issued according to it, the following fines shall be imposed:

1.1. for violation of Article 18, paragraph 2, sub-paragraph 2.1 of this Law, the lessee shall be fined in the amount of one hundred (100) to two hundred (200) Euro;

1.2. for violation of Article 18, paragraph 2, sub-paragraph 2.2 of this Law, the lessee shall be fined in the amount of two hundred (200) to four hundred (400) Euro;

1.3. for violation of Article 18, paragraph 2 sub-paragraphs 2.3, 2.4, 2.5 and 2.6 of this Law, the lessee shall be fined in the amount of four hundred (400) to six hundred (600)

Euro;

1.4. for violation of Article 29, paragraph 1 of this Law, the beneficiary shall be fined in the amount of one thousand (1000) to two thousand (2000) Euro.

\2. Falsification and any other fraud related to the housing rental subsidy as well as the benefit of the affordable housing unit shall be classified as criminal offenses and punishable under the Criminal Code in force.

\3. The fine shall be issued by the construction/housing inspector of the Municipality.
