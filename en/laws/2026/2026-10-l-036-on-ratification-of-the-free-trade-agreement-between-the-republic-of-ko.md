# LAW NO. 10/L-036 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EFTA STATES

### LAW NO. 10/L-036 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### EFTA STATES

### LAW NO. 10/L-036

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND EFTA STATES

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND EFTA STATES

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is Ratification of Free Trade Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the EFTA countries.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement Hereby shall be ratified the Law on Free Trade Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the EFTA countries adopted on 22 of January 2025.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Attached to this Law is The Free Trade Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the EFTA countries.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
