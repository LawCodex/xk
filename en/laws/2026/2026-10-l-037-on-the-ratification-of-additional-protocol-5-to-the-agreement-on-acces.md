# LAW NO. 10/L-037 ON THE RATIFICATION OF ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL 5 TO THE AGREEMENT ON ACCESSION AND AMENDMENT OF THE CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE AGREEMENT

### LAW NO. 10/L-037 ON THE RATIFICATION OF ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL 5 TO THE AGREEMENT ON ACCESSION AND

### AMENDMENT OF THE CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE AGREEMENT

### LAW NO. 10/L-037

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL 5 TO THE AGREEMENT ON

### ACCESSION AND AMENDMENT OF THE CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE

### AGREEMENT

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL 5 TO THE AGREEMENT ON

### ACCESSION AND AMENDMENT OF THE CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE

### AGREEMENT

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is the Ratification of Additional Protocol 5 to the Agreement on Accession and Amendment to the Central European Free Trade Agreement.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement Hereby shall be ratified the Additional Protocol 5 to the Agreement on Accession and Amendment to the Central European Free Trade Agreement adopted on 26 May 2017.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Attached to this Law is Additional Protocol 5 to the Agreement on Accession and Amendment to the Central European Free Trade Agreement.

### LAW NO. 10/L-037 ON THE RATIFICATION OF ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL 5 TO THE AGREEMENT ON ACCESSION AND

### AMENDMENT OF THE CENTRAL EUROPEAN FREE TRADE AGREEMENT

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 10/L-037 27 April 2026

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-64/2026 dated 13.05.2026, Acting President of the Republic of Kosovo Albulena Haxhiu
