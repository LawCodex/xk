# LAW NO. 10/L-011 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR ENERGY PROJECT IN KEK – GLOBAL GATEWAY

### LAW NO. 10/L-011 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR ENERGY PROJECT IN KEK – GLOBAL GATEWAY

### LAW NO. 10/L-011

### ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR

### ENERGY PROJECT IN KEK – GLOBAL GATEWAY

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR

### ENERGY PROJECT IN KEK – GLOBAL GATEWAY

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank for the Photovoltaic Solar Energy Project in KEK – Global Gateway.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. The Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank for the Photovoltaic Solar Energy Project in KEK – Global Gateway is ratified.

\2. The main objective of this agreement is to borrow 33 million Euros from the European Investment Bank to invest in the project.

\3. The project envisions the construction of a photovoltaic solar plant with an installed capacity of up to 100 MWac, which will be installed on the former ash landfill of the Kosovo A power plant.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Attached to this Law there is the text of the Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank for the Photovoltaic Solar Energy Project in KEK – Global Gateway.

### LAW NO. 10/L-011 ON THE RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE PHOTOVOLTAIC SOLAR ENERGY PROJECT IN KEK – GLOBAL GATEWAY

#### Article 4

Entry into Force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 10/L-011 2 April 2026
