# LAW NO. 10/L-010 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 2 TO THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017 PROGRAMME, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

LAW NO. 10/L-010 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 2 TO THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2017

### PROGRAMME, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION”

### LAW NO. 10/L-010

ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 2 TO THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR

### IPA 2017 PROGRAMME, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION”

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “ADDENDUM No. 2 TO THE FINANCING AGREEMENT

### FOR IPA 2017 PROGRAMME, PART ONE, BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### THE EUROPEAN UNION”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to ratify the “Addendum No. 2 to the Financing Agreement for IPA 2017 Programme, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Addendum No. 2 to the Financing Agreement for IPA 2017 Programme, part one, between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this Law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 10/L-010 2 April 2026

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-09/2026 dated 17.04.2026, Acting President of the Republic of Kosovo Albulena Haxhiu
