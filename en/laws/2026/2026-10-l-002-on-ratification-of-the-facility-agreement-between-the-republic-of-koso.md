The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “FACILITY AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVA AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON SPECIFIC ARRANGEMENTS FOR

### IMPLEMENTATION OF UNION SUPPORT TO KOSOVA UNDER THE REFORM AND

### GROWTH FACILITY”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Facility Agreement between the Republic of Kosova and the European Union on specific arrangements for implementation of Union support to Kosova under the Reform and Growth Facility”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Facility Agreement between the Republic of Kosova and the European Union on specific arrangements for implementation of Union support to Kosova under the Reform and Growth Facility” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Offical Gazette of the Republic of Kosova.
