# LAW NO. 06/L-136 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR THE PROJECT OF WASTEWATER DISPOSAL SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF PODUJEVA

### LAW NO. 06/L –136 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG

### FOR THE PROJECT OF WASTEWATER DISPOSAL SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF PODUJEVA

### LAW NO. 06/L –136

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR THE PROJECT OF WASTEWATER

### DISPOSAL SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF PODUJEVA

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG FOR THE PROJECT OF WASTEWATER

### DISPOSAL SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF PODUJEVA

#### Article 1

Scope This Law aims to ratify the credit agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for the project of wastewater disposal system for the municipality of Podujeva.

#### Article 2

Rectification of Agreement This Law shall ratify the credit Agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for the project of wastewater disposal system for the municipality of Podujeva signed on 24 September 2018.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The credit Agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG for the project of wastewater disposal system for the municipality of Podujeva is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after being published in the Official Gazette of Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 136 30 January 2019 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-49-2019, dated 12.02.2019 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
