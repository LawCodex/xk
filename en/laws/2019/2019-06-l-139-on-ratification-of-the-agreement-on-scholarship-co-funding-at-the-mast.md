# LAW NO. 06/L-139 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SCHOLARSHIP CO-FUNDING AT THE MASTER STUDY LEVEL BETWEEN THE FRENCH EMBASSY IN PRISHTINA AND THE MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

### LAW NO. 06/L –139 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SCHOLARSHIP CO-FUNDING AT THE MASTER STUDY LEVEL BETWEEN THE

### FRENCH EMBASSY IN PRISHTINA AND THE MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

### LAW NO. 06/L –139

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SCHOLARSHIP CO-FUNDING AT THE

### MASTER STUDY LEVEL BETWEEN THE FRENCH EMBASSY IN PRISHTINA AND THE

### MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SCHOLARSHIP CO-FUNDING AT THE

### MASTER STUDY LEVEL BETWEEN THE FRENCH EMBASSY IN PRISHTINA AND THE

### MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

#### Article 1

Purpose of the law By this law, there is ratified the agreement on scholarship co-funding at the Master study level between the French Embassy in Prishtina and the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

#### Article 2

Scope of law This law is valid for ratification of agreement between the French Embassy in Prishtina and the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after being published in the official gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 139 30 January 2019 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-58-2019, dated 14.02.2019 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
