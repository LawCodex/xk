# LAW NO. 06/L-142 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF SEWERAGE SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF SHTIME

### LAW NO. 06/L –142 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN

### RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF SEWERAGE SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF SHTIME

### LAW NO. 06/L –142

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE

### PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF SEWERAGE SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF

### SHTIME

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of Constitution of Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING

### OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF SEWERAGE SYSTEM FOR THE

### MUNICIPALITY OF SHTIME

#### Article 1

Scope This Law aims to ratify the credit Agreement between Republic of Kosovo and UniCredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of sewerage system for the municipality of Shtime.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies the credit agreement between Republic of Kosovo and UniCredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of sewerage system for the municipality of Shtime.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The Agreement between Republic of Kosovo and UniCredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of sewerage system for the municipality of Shtime is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after being published at the Official Gazette of Republic of Kosovo Law No.06/L - 142 30 January 2019 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-62-2019, dated 14.02.2019 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
