The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE STATUS OF ALBANIAN EDUCATION EMPLOYEES OF THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO FROM ACADEMIC YEAR 1990/91 UP TO THE ACADEMIC YEAR 1998/99

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this Law is to recognize the work experience and to determine the rights to additional compensation over the pension that the beneficiary realizes, for the Albanian education employees of the Republic of Kosovo who worked from the academic year 1990/91 up to the academic year 1998/99.

#### Article 2

Scope

\1. The scope of this Law is to determine the rights on additional compensation for Albanian education employees of the Republic of Kosovo who worked from the academic year 1990/91up to the academic year 1998/99.

\2. This law shall not be applicable to all employees who have contributed during this period and were financed outside the system of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Teacher - preschool teacher - educator, class teacher (grades I-IV of primary school), primary school teacher subject based (grades V-VIII of primary school), teacher-professor of the secondary education, professors of higher school and university professor;

\2. Professional associates of educational institution - school - pedagogue, psychologist, librarian, physician, technician - medical nurse and social worker;

\3. Administrative employees of educational institution – secretary, cashier, chief accountant, referent and administrator-typist;

\4. Technical staff of the educational institution - the housekeeper, servant, guard;

\5. Preschool institution – kindergartens;

\6. Primary school – educational institution for mandatory eight year education (grade I-VIII).

\7. Secondary school – educational institution for general, art and vocational secondary education;

\8. Advanced School – educational institution for higher education, mainly two years of education;

\9. University - educational-scientific institution of higher education and scientific research within which operate faculties in which education lasts from four (4) to six (6) years per generation;

\10. Director and deputy/assistant director of the school – heads and managers of the school;

\11. Education inspector – a person with special authorization who supervises application of legality and sub-legal acts in educational institutions;

\12. Municipal Pedagogical Department – an educational institution providing professional guidelines and overseeing qualitative and quantitative implementation of educational curricula.

As of September 1, 1995, the municipal pedagogical departments are reformed into sectors for professional oversee of the Municipal Education Council.

\13. Pedagogical – educational Advisor – a person who attends in classes and deals with instruction and oversees implementation of curricula for certain educational levels, respectively for certain educational subjects;

\14. Municipal Education Council – as of 10 July 1995, the municipal educational secretariats were converted into the Municipal Education Councils;

\15. Financing Council – a state authority for financing education activities;

\16. Ministry of Education of the Republic of Kosovo – the highest state authority for educational policy in the Republic of Kosovo;

\17. Work experience – period of employment with educational and scientific institutions from academic year 1990/91 up to the academic year 1998/99;

\18. House transformed to school – a private house and other facilities in which were held education process and management of educational and scientific operations;

\19. Owner of the House transformed to School – the person who has provided the house/ facility for organization of education process;

\20. Commission - the Governmental Commission for recognition and verification of status of the Albanian Education Employees of the Republic of Kosovo who have worked from academic year 1990/91 up to the academic year 1998/99.

#### Article 4

Beneficiaries of the Additional Compensation

\1. The retired person who worked in Albanian education of the Republic of Kosovo from the academic year 1990/91 up to the academic year 1998/99, enjoys an additional compensation for the given contribution.

\2. Beneficiaries of the additional compensation are:

2.1. Teachers;

2.2. Professional Associates;

2.3. Director and deputy director of the school;

2.4. Administrative staff;

2.5. Technical staff of schools;

2.6. Higher education employees within the Albanian language education system.

#### Article 5

Categories of beneficiaries for additional compensation

\1. Beneficiaries of additional compensation are categorized based on their working years from the academic year 1990/91 until the academic year 1998/99, where for each working year, over the pension that they receive, they shall benefit the percentage amount of the basic salary of the primary education of the Republic of Kosovo, such as following:

1.1. one (1) working year 3.46%;

1.2. two (2) working years 5.19%;

1.3. three (3) working years 6.91%;

1.4. four (4) working years 8.64%;

1.5. five (5) working years 10.37%;

1.6. six (6) working years 12.10%;

1.7. seven (7) working years 13.83%;

1.8. eight (8) working years 15.56%;

1.9. nine (9) working years 17.28%.

#### Article 6

Houses transformed into Schools MEST by sub-legal act shall define procedures for recognition and assessment of the contribution for the owners of houses transformed into schools and other objects.

#### Article 7

Submission of application for realization of the right to additional compensation MEST in cooperation with MLSW and MoF shall draft a sub-legal act by which defines the terms and conditions for realization of the right to additional compensation.

#### Article 8

Deadline for realization of the right to additional compensation

\1. The right for realization of the additional compensation for categories defined by this law shall be effective from the retirement day.

\2. Deadline for accomplishment of the additional compensation for categories defined by this law shall be effective after the entry into force of this law.

#### Article 9

Submission of applications for additional compensation

\1. Application for additional compensation for categories defined by this law, may be filed by the beneficiary or any other person authorized by him/her or by the spouse after death of the beneficiary.

\2. For realization of the right to additional compensation, according to the provisions of this Law, the applicant shall submit an application to the Regional Office of the Pension Administration Department under MLSW.

\3. The spouse, after the death of beneficiary, is obliged to notify in written the pension administration of Kosovo on any change of circumstances that affect the right to receive the additional compensation.

\4. The Kosovo Pension Administration, as an administrative unit within the Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare, manages and administers payment of additional compensation according to this Law.

#### Article 10

Sub-legal acts Ministry of Education, Science and Technology within six (6) months from entry into force of this Law shall issue sub-legal acts for its implementation.

#### Article 11
