# LAW NO. 06/L-111 ON SALARIES IN PUBLIC SECTOR (Declared Invalid with Judgement no. KO219/19, Published: 13.07.2020, find at Judgements, Constitutional Court)

### LAW NO. 06/L –111 ON SALARIES IN PUBLIC SECTOR

### LAW NO. 06/L –111

### ON SALARIES IN PUBLIC SECTOR

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON SALARIES IN PUBLIC SECTOR

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Scope and Purpose

\1. This Law defines:

1.1. the system of salaries and remunerations for Public Officials and Functionaries who are paid from the state budget, excluding KIA and KSF;

1.2. rules for determining salaries for employees of publicly owned enterprises in Kosovo; and

1.3. criteria for transitional salary and other benefits after the end of the function of public functionary and public functionary with special status, as well as for former high official that is realizing the rights according to the relevant Law.

#### Article 2

Definitions

\1. Expressions/words in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Basic salary - is the main or only component of the salary, which rewards the work according to the level of complexity of function, position or grade and level of required qualification for that function, position or grade. Complexity is a combined consequence of responsibility, importance and decision making discretion, difficulty and risk in performing duties for that function, position or grade and limitations of that work;

1.2. Salary allowance - is a component of salary, added to the basic salary and which aims to rewards the work in specific conditions, sectors or peculiarities that are not rewarded with basic salary. Salary allowance may be regular when it is received every month or not-regular when it is given randomly according to this Law;

1.3. Regular allowance - is the allowance for market conditions, special allowance, performance allowance in the system of pre-university education and for difficult/ harmful working conditions;

1.4. Non-regular allowance - is the allowance for overtime work/outside normal working hours and performance allowance;

1.5. Reward - is the payment that rewards the work performed by a part-time member of a collegial body;

1.6. Compensation - is the payment in the form of reimbursement of expenses incurred or that will incur by the official, for travel, stay or representation in or while performing official duty;

1.7. Salary class - is a division corresponding with a specific coefficient and which consists of a group of functions, positions or grades of the same level or almost the same complexity and who receive the same basic salary;

\2. Expressions: “public functionary”, “public functionary with special status”, “public official”, “civil servant”, “civil servant with special status”, “public service employee”, “cabinet employee”, “administrative and supporting staff” in this law have the same meaning as defined in the Law on Public Officials in the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 3

Principles of salary and rewards system

\1. Salaries are based on the principles provided in this law, as follows:

1.1. principle of lawfulness - means that salaries of recipients are determined only in accordance with this Law and sub-legal acts authorized and issued for its implementation;

1.2.principle of fairness - means that the salary level should be a fair reward for the complexity of work and contribution of individual in the performance of the organisation;

1.3.principle of equal pay - means that every salary recipient shall receive equal pay for the work in the same or comparable function, position or grade;

1.4.principle of transparency - means that the procedure for determined the salary, its level, and administration of salary system should be transparent and open to public, while not falling in contradiction with the protection of personal data according to applicable legislation;

1.5. principle of non-discrimination - means that no salary recipient shall be discriminated in salary, as defined in the law on the protection from discrimination;

1.6. principle of predictability - salary level determined under this law cannot be lowered, unless in an extraordinary situation of financial difficulties and only according to the law.

### SALARY OF CIVIL SERVANT

#### Article 4

Salary of civil servant

\1. Salary of civil servants consists of basic salary and allowances, as the case may be.

\2. Allowances that are received by civil servant, accordingly, are:

2.1. allowance for market conditions;

2.2. performance allowance;

2.3. special allowance for positions or group of positions according to Article 8 of this Law.

2.4. allowance for overtime work.

\3. Salary of the employee of the Administration of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo shall be regulated by this Law, according to Annex 1 of this Law.

\4. Allowance and compensation of the employee of the Administration of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo shall be regulated by this Law and by special act adopted by the Presidency of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

\5. Regulation by special act, according to paragraph 4 of this Article, shall be done based on the nature and specific conditions of the work of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 5

Basic salary of civil servant

\1. The basic salary of civil servant is that part of salary that civil servant receives for the work performed in a certain position or in a certain grade.

\2. Basic salary of civil servant is defined by the Salary Class to which the respective job position belongs according to Annex No. 1 attached and integral part of this Law.

\3. Classification of a specific position of civil service according to paragraph 2 of this Article is done based on the rules for evaluation and classification of job positions, according to the provisions for classification of positions of civil service in accordance with the legislation on public official. The class to which a certain position belongs is determined explicitly in the regulation on internal organisation of the institution adopted according to the Law on Organisation and Functioning of State Administration and Independent Agencies.

\4. In the case of civil servants with special status, for whom according to the Law on Public Officials applies the system of personal grades, basic salary of public official is determined by the Salary Class to which the grade belongs, according to Annex No. 1 attached and integral part of this Law.

\5. In the case of civil servants with special status, according to paragraph 4 of this Article, Government of Kosovo, upon proposal of minister responsible for public administration and minister responsible for category of employees where the personal grades system applies, adopts with a sub-legal act, equivalence of the position with grade.

#### Article 6

Allowance for market conditions

\1. Allowance for market conditions is received for specific required professions in specific key professional positions of civil service, for which the recruitment or retention of employees is objectively impossible because private market offers at least fifty percent (50%) higher salaries than the public sector.

\2. Value of allowance for market conditions cannot be higher than fifty percent (50%) of basic salary.

\3. Types of Positions and relevant professions for which the allowance for market conditions is received, value and procedures for receiving it are approved with a sub-legal act of Government

\4. Following the adoption of sub-legal act by the Government according to paragraph 3 of this Article, benefit of allowance for market conditions is approved by the ministry responsible for public administration and ministry responsible for finance, upon justified proposal of respective institution.

#### Article 7

Performance allowance

\1. Civil servant receives an annual performance allowance based on annual results of performance appraisal.

\2. Performance allowance shall be received by the civil servant who is appraised “Excellent” in the respective year, in a fair proportion, in compliance with the provisions of the Law on Public Officials with regards to the civil servant by comparing it with the overall performance of the institution.

\3. Performance allowance is received at the end of each year, where the total value cannot exceed fifty percent (50%) of the basic monthly salary.

\4. The fund used in any institution for performance allowance cannot be higher than zero point five percent (0.5 %) of total funds used for basic salary in the same financial year.

\5. Government of Kosovo, upon proposal of the responsible ministry for public administration and ministry responsible for finances, adopts with a sub-legal act the measure and procedure for receiving performance allowance.

#### Article 8

Special allowances of civil servants

\1. Special allowance over the basic salary shall be received by:

1.1. officials of Tax Administration and investigation inspectors of the Kosovo Competition Authority;

1.2. guardians at the correction service;

1.3. fire-fighters in dangerous operations.

\2. Special allowances, as per paragraph 1 of this Article cannot be higher than fourty percent (40%) of the basic salary according to Annex 1 of this Law.

\3. The list of positions or position group benefiting special allowances, rules for receiving and value of the allowance shall be determined with a sub-legal act of Government, at the proposal of the ministry responsible for public administration and ministry responsible for finances.

\4. Civil servants in the Anti-Corruption Agency, who exercise functions of the Agency, shall receive allowance up to twenty percent (20%) on the basic salary.

#### Article 9

Overtime allowance or compensation for overtime work

\1. Civil servant benefits salary allowance and/or compensatory time off, for overtime work, work on duty, work at weekends, work on official holidays and work at night.

### CHAPTER III

### SALARIES OF PUBLIC FUNCTIONARIES

#### Article 10

Salary of public functionary

\1. Public Functionary benefits a fixed salary equal to basic salary for the function.

\2. Basic salary of public functionary is defined by the Salary Class to which the respective function belongs according to Annex No.1 of this Law attached and integral part of this Law.

\3. Public functionary does not benefit other allowances over the basic salary, with the exception of special allowance defined in line with Article 12 of the Law.

#### Article 11

Reward of part-time functionaries

\1. Members of collegial-type managing bodies (councils, boards, committees), constitutional institutions, independent agencies and justice system institutions, established by law, who do not exercise a full-time mandate and are not explicitly referred to in Annex 1 of this Law, are rewarded with twenty percent (20%) of the salary of full-time chairperson of the collegial body.

\2. In case when the chairperson of managing body is also part time, then his/her reward, and reward of members of managing body shall be respectively thirty percent (30%) of the salary of general secretary of the institution for the Chairperson and twenty percent (20%) for members.

\3. Reward stipulated in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article are not received every month, subject to participation in meetings of collegial body, but not less than once a month, and for every meeting of collegial body, subject to participation in the meeting, in case when such body convenes less frequently than once a month.

\4. Chairpersons, members and other full-time employees of the institution do not receive the rewards stipulated in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article for participation in meetings of collegial body.

#### Article 12

Special allowances for public functionaries

\1. Deputies may receive special allowance over the basic salary.

\2. Special allowance, from paragraph 1 of this Article, shall be given to the deputy for parliamentary function: President of the Assembly, Vice President of the Assembly, Chairperson of the parliamentary committee, deputy chairperson of the parliamentary committee and head of the parliamentary group.

\3. The Deputy has the right on the compensation for participation in the plenary session, for the participation in the meeting of the parliamentary committee as well as for monthly expenditures.

\4. Criteria and procedures for allowances and compensation from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall, upon the proposal of the relevant parliamentary Committee on budget and finance, defined with a regulation by Presidency of Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

\5. Allowance from paragraph 2 of this Article shall be paid only for one function and can not exceed thirty percent (30%) of the basic salary.

\6. Allowance and compensation for the political staff of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo shall be regulated by a special act adopted by the Presidency of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

### CHAPTER IV

### SALARIES OF PUBLIC FUNCTIONARIES WITH SPECIAL STATUS

#### Article 13

Salary of public functionary with special status

\1. Public functionary with special status receives a fixed salary equal to basic salary for the respective function or grade.

\2. Basic salary of the functionary is defined by the Salary Class to which the respective function or grade belongs, in accordance with Annex No. 1 of this Law attached and integral part of this Law.

\3. Functionary with special status does not benefit other allowances over the basic salary, excluding Article 14 of this Law.

#### Article 14

Special allowance for the public functionary with special status

\1. Police officers shall benefit a special allowance for those tasks they perform in the sectors or special operations with a risk for the life.

\2. Special allowance, according to paragraph 1 of this Article, can not be higher than forty percent (40%) of the basic salary, according to Annex 1 of this Law.

\3. Police inspectorate, customs officers and officials of the Tax Administration shall benefit an allowance up to twenty percent (20%) of the basic salary.

\4. List of positions of police officers, police inspectorate, customs officers and officials of the Tax Administration that benefit a special allowance, rules for the benefit and value of the allowance shall be determined by sub-legal act of the Government, upon the proposal of the Ministry responsible for internal affairs and Ministry responsible for finances.

### CHAPTER V

### SALARIES OF THE CABINET EMPLOYEE

#### Article 15

Salary of the cabinet employee

\1. Cabinet employee receives a fixed salary equal to the basic salary.

\2. For every title of a cabinet employee will be defined a Salary Class such title belongs, in accordance with Annex No.1 of this Law attached and integral part of this Law.

\3. In addition to basic salary, cabinet employee, with the exception of political advisor, may benefit a special allowance which cannot exceed twenty percent (20%) of the basic salary.

\4. Criteria and procedures for benefiting the allowance according to paragraph 3 of this Article are regulated with a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

The structure of salary of public service employee and administrative and support staff

\1. Salary of public service employee and administrative and support staff consists of basic salary and allowances.

2.1. allowance for difficult/harmful working conditions;

2.2. performance allowance for employee of pre-university education system

2.3. special allowance for positions or group of positions according to Articles 19 and 20 of this Law.

2.4. allowance for overtime work.

\3. Basic salary for public service employee and administrative and support staff is determined according to the Salary Class to which the position belongs, in accordance with Annex No. 1 attached and integral part of this Law.

\4. The provisions of Article 9 of this Law shall apply for overtime work.

### CHAPTER VII

### SALARY CALCULATION AND PAYMENT

#### Article 21

Calculation of basic salary

\1. Basic salary is that part of salary received for the full-time work performed in a specific function or position.

\2. Full-time work is considered the work performed for forty (40) hours a week. In order to calculate the salary, one (1) month is considered to have one hundred and seventy-three (173)

working hours.

\3. The value of basic salary for the full-time work is calculated by multiplying the salary coefficient of the Salary Class with monetary value of the coefficient.

\4. The ratio of coefficient is one to ten (1:10) distributed in sixty-nine (69) coefficient levels, attached and integral part as Annex 2 of this Law.

\5. Public functionary, public functionary with special status, public official and university academic staff receiving the basic salary according to Annex 1 of this Law, if they get engaged to work in another public institution, when allowed under the special law, shall receive a payment, as a salary up to twenty percent (20%) for the engagement in that institution.

\6. Government of Republic of Kosovo upon proposal of the responsible ministry for public administration and ministry responsible for finance shall, by a sub-legal act, adopt payment terms, amount and procedure for additional engagement according to paragraph 5 of this Article.

\7. The basic salary increases based on the working experience for zero point five percent

(0.5%) for every full year of work.

\8. Government of Republic of Kosovo upon proposal of the ministry responsible for public administration and ministry responsible for finance shall, by a sub-legal act, adopt rules for calculation of work experience under paragraph 7 of this Article.

#### Article 22

Calculation and payment

\1. Salary (basic salary and allowances) is calculated every month by the relevant institution, against the effective working time and rules of this Law.

\2. Salary is paid on monthly basis.

\3. Salary is paid through the payroll by transferring the money to personal/ individual bank accounts by the end of the respective month.

\4. Salaries are paid through the salary system managed by the Ministry of Finance which communicates with human resources management system.

\5. Government of Republic of Kosovo shall, by a sub-legal act, upon the proposal of the minister responsible for public administration and ministry responsible for finance, adopt detailed rules for implementation of this Article.

#### Article 23

Setting the coefficient value and the fund for allowance

\1. Monetary value of coefficient for the first year of application of this Law will be two hundred and thirty-nine (239) Euro.

\2. For other budget years, monetary value of the coefficient is determined with annual budget Law.

\3. Monetary value of the coefficient shall be determined in accordance with the legislation on management of public finances.

\4. The general fund for all allowance according to this Law may not exceed the eight percent (8%) of the general budget of the basic salary determined according to the Law on budgetary appropriations.

\5. The Fund according to paragraph 4 of this Article shall be allocated by the responsible ministry of finance to the budget organizations only in accordance with this Law and sub-legal acts adopted based on this Law.

#### Article 24

Lowering of salary level Salary level determined according to this Law, in extraordinary situations of financial difficulties, can be lowered by Law.

Compensation for travels and representation costs

\1. Public officials and functionaries are entitled to compensations for expenses incurred for official travel and stay abroad.

\3. Conditions, method, value of compensation and procedure for benefiting the compensation under paragraph 1 and 2 of this Article is adopted by a sub-legal act of the Government of Kosovo, upon proposal of responsible minister for public administration and responsible ministry for finances.

### CHAPTER IX

### PROVISIONS ON SALARIES OF PUBLICLY OWNED ENTERPRISES

#### Article 27

Competence for determining salaries in publicly owned enterprises

\1. Collegial managing bodies of publicly owned enterprises with over fifty percent (50%) of state capital, adopt:

1.1. salary level of their employees set by the Government, within the minimal coefficient 1 and maximal coefficient 7, according to the value of the coefficient determined by Article 23 paragraph 1 of this Law;

1.2. amount of performance allowance, but not more than one annual salary a month, after the publication of positive annual financial result, and upon adoption by the interministerial committee for publicly owned enterprises.

\2. For publicly owned enterprises operating with losses, or which are subsidized by the state, Government of Kosovo upon proposal of inter-ministerial committee for publicly owned enterprises, adopts, by special acts, the salary level and performance allowances for their employees, according to paragraph 1 sub-paragraph 1.1 of this Article.

\3. Municipal Committee of Shareholders shall, for publicly-owned enterprises, with over fifty percent (50%) of capital owned by municipalities, set the level of salaries of their employees, within the minimal coefficient 1 and maximal coefficient 5, according to the value of the coefficient determined by Article 23 paragraph 1 of this Law.

### CHAPTER X

Transitional provisions

\1. Provisions of this Law for the system of salaries, allowances and remunerations and Annex No.1 of this Law shall not be applied for the public functionaries with special status: judge of the Constitutional Court, judge, prosecutor, chair of the Judicial Council and chair of the Prosecutorial Council until 31 December 2022.

\2. Public functionary from paragraph 1 of this Article shall not benefit any other allowance on the salary and shall not benefit any salary increase for the period defined in paragraph 1 of this Article.

Provisions of this Law for the system of salaries, allowances, remunerations and Annex 1 of this Law shall not apply to Privatization Agency of Kosovo until 31 December 2022.

#### Article 30

Transitional payment after the end of function

\1. Public functionary and public functionary with special status: President of the Assembly, Prime Minister, President of the Constitutional Court, Chief State Prosecutor, President of the Supreme Court, who after his/her mandate ends does not receive any income from the state budget, by the institution where he/she has performed the function, shall be entitled to:

1.1. the transitional compensation for five (5) year in the value of seventy percent (70%)

of the last basic salary in the institution where he has performed the function;

1.2. an assistant, a driver, that are selected by the functionary as well as an official vehicle in use, that shall be paid in compliance with the relevant Law on salaries; and

1.3. a furnished office.

\2. The right determined in paragraph 1 of this Article is applied if the public functionary has served at least one (1) year of his mandate.

\3. Deputy who, after the end of the regular mandate or early one, does not receive any income from the state budget, shall be entitled to the compensation of transitional salary for one (1) year in the value of one hundred (100%) of the last basic salary.

\4. Public functionary: Deputy Prime Minister, Minister and Mayor who after the end of the mandate does not receive any income from the state budget, shall be entitled to the transitional compensation for one (1) year in the value of seventy percent (70%) of the last basic salary, in the institution where he has performed the function.

\5. Public functionary: Deputy Minister who after the end of the mandate does not receive any income from the state budget, shall be entitled to the transitional compensation for six (6)

months in the value of seventy percent (70%) of the last basic salary, in the institution where he has performed the function.

\6. The right to the compensation of the salary determined in paragraph 4 and 5 of this Article shall be applied only he has exercised his regular mandate for at least six (6) months.

\7. The benefit foreseen according to this Article shall not be paid if the functionary:

7.1. resigns from the function;

7.2. is dismissed from the function for legal violations;

7.3. after the end of the mandate, exercises any function for which he is paid by the Budget of the Republic of Kosovo;

7.4. with a final decision of the court has been announced guilty for a criminal offence committed during the time he/she has performed in the institution, or during the period of receiving the benefit foreseen by this Article.

\8. Functionary, defined in this Article, after the end of the mandate, shall have the right to seek protection in compliance with the Law.
