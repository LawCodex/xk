# LAW NO. 06/L-155 ON THE PROHIBITION OF GAMES OF CHANCE

### LAW NO. 06/L-155 ON THE PROHIBITION OF GAMES OF CHANCE

### LAW NO. 06/L-155

### ON THE PROHIBITION OF GAMES OF CHANCE

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE PROHIBITION OF GAMES OF CHANCE

#### Article 1

Purpose By this Law shall be prohibited and closed all games of chance in the entire territory of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Abrogation By this law, the Law no. 04/L-080 on Games of Chance and sub-legal acts issued for its implementation shall be abrogated.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 06/L-155 28 March 2019 Pursuant to the article 80, paragraph 5 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
