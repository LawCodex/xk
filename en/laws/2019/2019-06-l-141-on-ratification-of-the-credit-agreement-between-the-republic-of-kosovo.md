# LAW NO. 06/L-141 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND RAIFFEISEN BANK INTERNATIONAL AG

### LAW NO. 06/L –141ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND RAIFFEISEN BANK

### INTERNATIONAL AG

### LAW NO. 06/L –141

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND RAIFFEISEN BANK INTERNATIONAL AG

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND RAIFFEISEN BANK INTERNATIONAL AG

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the credit agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and Raiffeisen Bank International AG for the project for construction of the drinking water supply system for the Municipality of Ferizaj.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement This Law ratifies the Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and Raiffeisen Bank International AG for the project for construction of the drinking water supply system for the Municipality of Ferizaj.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Credit Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and Raiffeisen Bank International AG for the construction of the drinking water supply system for the Municipality of Ferizaj is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 4

This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 141 30 January 2019 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-60-2019, dated 14.02.2019 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
