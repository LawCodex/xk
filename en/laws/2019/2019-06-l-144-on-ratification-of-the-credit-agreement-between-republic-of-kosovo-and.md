# LAW NO. 06/L-144 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A WATER SUPPLY SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF ISTOG

### AW NO. 06/L –144 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN

### RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A WATER SUPPLY SYSTEM FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF ISTOG

### LAW NO. 06/L –144

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of Constitution of Republic of Kosovo,

Adopts

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE CREDIT AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND UNICREDIT BANK AUSTRIA AG IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING

### OF THE PROJECT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A WATER SUPPLY SYSTEM FOR THE

### MUNICIPALITY OF ISTOG

#### Article 1

Scope This Law aims to ratify of the credit agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of a water supply system for the Municipality of Istog.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies the credit agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of a water supply system for the Municipality of Istog.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The credit agreement between Republic of Kosovo and Unicredit Bank Austria AG in relation to the financing of the project for construction of a water supply system for the Municipality of Istog is attached to the Annex of this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.06/L - 144 30 January 2019 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-63-2019, dated 14.02.2019 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
