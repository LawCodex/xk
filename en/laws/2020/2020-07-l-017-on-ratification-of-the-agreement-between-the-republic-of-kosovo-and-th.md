# LAW NO. 07/L-017 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION FOR DEVELOPMENT IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE EMERGENCY PROJECT COVID 19 FOR KOSOVO

### LAW NO. 07/L-017 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL

### ASSOCIATION FOR DEVELOPMENT IN RELATION TO THE FINANCING OF THE EMERGENCY PROJECT COVID 19 FOR KOSOVO

### LAW NO. 07/L-017

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION FOR DEVELOPMENT IN RELATION TO THE

### FINANCING OF THE EMERGENCY PROJECT COVID 19 FOR KOSOVO

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION FOR DEVELOPMENT IN RELATION TO THE

### FINANCING OF THE EMERGENCY PROJECT COVID 19 FOR KOSOVO

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law intends to ratify the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Association for Development in relation to the Financing of the Emergency Project Covid-19 for Kosovo, signed on 29 June 2020.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Association for Development in relation to the Financing of the Emergency Project Covid-19 for Kosovo, signed on 29 June 2020.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex of this law is attached the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Association for Development in relation to the Financing of the Emergency Project Covid-19 for Kosovo, signed on 29 June 2020.

#### Article 4

Entry into Force This Law enters into force fifteen (15) days after its publishing in the Official Gazette of Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 07/L-017 14 August 2020

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-114/2020 dated 20.08.2020 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
