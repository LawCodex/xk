The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON FUNDING THE PROJECT ON REAL

### ESTATE CADASTER AND GEOSPATIAL INFRASTRUCTURE BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law intends ratification of the Agreement on Funding the Project on Real Estate Cadastre and Geospatial Infrastructure between Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association, signed on 7 February 2019.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies the Agreement on Funding the Project on Real Estate Cadastre and Geospatial Infrastructure between Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association, signed on 7 February 2019.

#### Article 3

Attached documents

\1. In the Annex of this law are attached the following documents:

1.1. The Agreement on Funding the Project on Real Estate Cadastre and Geospatial Infrastructure between Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association, signed on 7 February 2019; and

1.2. The general funding conditions of the International Development Association.
