# LAW NO. 07/L-009 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO ON THE PARTICIAPTION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION’S PROGRAM “FISCALIS 2020”

### LAW NO. 07/L-009 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO ON THE

### PARTICIAPTION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION’S PROGRAM “FISCALIS 2020”

### LAW NO. 07/L-009

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO ON THE PARTICIAPTION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION’S

### PROGRAM “FISCALIS 2020”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE EUROPEAN UNION AND

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO ON THE PARTICIAPTION OF KOSOVO IN THE UNION’S

### PROGRAM “FISCALIS 2020”

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the agreement between the European Union and Kosovo on the participation of Kosovo in the Union’s Program “Fiscalis 2020”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement This Law ratifies the agreement between the European Union and Kosovo on the participation of Kosovo in the Union’s Program “Fiscalis 2020” that has entered into force on the 1st of August 2017.

#### Article 3

Attached documents In the Annex of this law is attached the Agreement between the European Union and Kosovo on the participation of Kosovo in the Union’s Program “Fiscalis 2020”.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 07/L-009 23 July 2020

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-106/2020 dated 06.08.2020 President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi.
