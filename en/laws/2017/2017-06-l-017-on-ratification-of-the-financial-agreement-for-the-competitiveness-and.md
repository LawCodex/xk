# LAW NO. 06/L-017 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR THE COMPETITIVENESS AND EXPORT READINESS PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

LAW No. 06/L-017 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR THE COMPETITIVENESS AND EXPORT READINESS PROJECT

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

### LAW NO. 06/L-017

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR THE COMPETITIVENESS

### AND EXPORT READINESS PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE

### INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR THE

### COMPETITIVENESS AND EXPORT READINESS PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

#### Article 1

The purpose of the law This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement for the Competitiveness and Export Readiness Project, between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance and the Association for International Development.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financing Agreement for the Competitiveness and Export Readiness Project is ratified, between the Republic of Kosovo and the Association for International Development, concluded on 02.08.2017 in Pristina.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to this Law is the Financing Agreement.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 06/L-017 06 December 2017 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-029-2017, dated 22.12.2017, President of the Republic of Kosovo Hashim Thaçi ________________________ Annex of the Law No. 06/L-017, The Financing Agreement for the Competitiveness and Export Readiness Project is ratified, between the Republic of Kosovo and the Association for International Development, attached to the law is published in the website of the Official Gazette (gzk.rks-gov.net)
