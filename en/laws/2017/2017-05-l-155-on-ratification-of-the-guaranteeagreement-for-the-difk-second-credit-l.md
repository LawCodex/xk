#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the Guarantee Agreement for DIFK - Second Credit Line to Deposit Insurance Fund of Kosovo, signed between the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Minister of Finance, and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement Guarantee Agreement is ratified for DIFK - Second Credit Line to the Deposit Insurance Fund of Kosovo between the Republic of Kosovo (represented by the Minister of Finance) and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, signed on 22 December 2016 in Pristina.

#### Article 3

Documents attached

\1. In the Annex of this Law there are attached the following documents:

1.1. Guarantee Agreement for DIFK - Second Credit Line to the Deposit Insurance Fund of Kosovo between the Republic of Kosovo (represented by the Minister of Finance)

and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development;

1.2. Loan Agreement for DIFK - Second Line of Credit between the Deposit Insurance Fund of Kosovo and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development; and

1.3. Standard terms and conditions of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
