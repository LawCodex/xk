Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCIAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE

### GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF

### FINANCE, AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF AUSTRIA, REPRESENTED

### BY THE FEDERAL MINISTRY OF FINANCE

#### Article 1

Purpose of the Law This Law aims to ratify the Agreement on Financial Cooperation between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance, and the Government of the Republic of Austria, represented by the Federal Ministry of Finance.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement It is ratified the Agreement on Financial Cooperation between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance and the Government of the Republic of Austria, represented by the Federal Ministry of Finance.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In the Annex of this Law there is attached Financial Cooperation Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance, and the Government of the Republic of Austria, represented by the Federal Ministry of Finance.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
