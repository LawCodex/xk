# LAW NO. 06/L-018 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR ADDITIONAL FINANCING FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

LAW No. 06/L-018 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR ADDITIONAL FINANCING FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL

### DEVELOPMENT PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

LAW No. 06/L-018

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR ADDITIONAL FINANCING

### FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR ADDITIONAL

### FINANCING FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

### ASSOCIATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Financial Agreement for Additional Financing for the Agriculture and Rural Development Project between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financial Agreement for Additional Financing for the Agriculture and Rural Development Project between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association, signed on 02.08.2017 in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Along with this draft law is the Financing Agreement for additional funding of the Agriculture and Rural Development Project between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association and the General Conditions for Grants and Loans of the International Development Association.

LAW No. 06/L-018 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCIAL AGREEMENT FOR ADDITIONAL FINANCING FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL

### DEVELOPMENT PROJECT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

#### Article 4

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
