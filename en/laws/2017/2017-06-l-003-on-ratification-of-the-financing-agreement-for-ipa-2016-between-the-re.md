# LAW NO. 06/L-003 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2016 BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

LAW No. 06/L-003 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR

### IPA 2016 BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

### LAW NO. 06/L-003

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2016 BETWEEN THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCING AGREEMENT FOR IPA 2016 BETWEEN

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the Financing Agreement for IPA 2016 between the Republic of Kosovo and European Union.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Financing Agreement for IPA 2016 between the Republic of Kosovo and European Union shall be considered ratified by 2/3 of the deputies of the Assembly of Kosovo and shall enter into force in accordance with the deadlines determined by the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo
