LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THe AGReeMeNT BeTWeeN THe GOVeRNMeNT OF THe RePuBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THe GOVeRNMeNT OF THe uNITeD STATeS OF AMeRICA TO IMPROVe COMPLIANCe OF TAX ReGuLATIONS AT THe INTeRNATIONAL LeVeL AND IMPLeMeNTING OF FATCA ReGuLATIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose of the Law This law aims to ratify the agreement signed between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the United States of America to improve the tax compliance at international level and implementation of FATCA regulations

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement The Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the United States of America to improve compliance of tax regulations atinternational level and implementation of FATCA regulations concluded on February 26, 2015 in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Documents attached In Annex No. 1 of this Law is attached Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the United States of America to improve compliance of tax regulations at international level and implementation of FATCA regulations.
