#### Article 1

Purpose The aim of this law is to ratify the Bilateral Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg on the Development Cooperation Project: “KSV/018: Institutional and Technical Support for the Water Supply System in the Mitrovica Region”

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement The Bilateral Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg on the Development Cooperation Project:

KSV/018: Institutional and Technical Support for the Water Supply System in the Mitrovica Region”, concluded on 7 April 2015, in Prishtina is hereby ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to this Law is the Bilateral Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg on the Development Cooperation Project: “KSV/018: Institutional and Technical Support for the Water Supply System in the Mitrovica Region”
