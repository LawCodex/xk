Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION FOR THE HEALTH

### REFORM PROJECT

#### Article 1

The purpose of the law This Law aims to ratify the Financing Agreement entered into between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association to finance the Health Reform Project in the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement The Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Association for Health Development Project, concluded on 15 January 2015, in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached Documents Attached to this Law is the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for Health Project and the General Conditions for Grants and Loans of International Development Association.
