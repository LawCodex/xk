# LAW NO. 05/L-011 ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION REGARDING THE PROJECT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND RENEWA BLE ENERGY

Law No. 05/L-011 ON RaTIFICaTION OF FINaNCING aGREEMENT BETwEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO aND THE INTERNaTIONaL DEVELOPMENT aSSOCIaTION REGaRDING THE PROJECT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY aND RENEwaBLE ENERGY Law No. 05/L-011

### ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION REGARDING THE PROJECT ON

### ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND RENEWABLE ENERGY

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION REGARDING THE

### PROJECT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND RENEWABLE ENERGY

#### Article 1

The purpose of the law This Law aims to ratify Financing Agreement entered into between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for Financing the Project on Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement The Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for the Project on Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy, concluded on 15 January 2015, in Pristina, is ratified.

#### Article 3

Documents attached Attached to this Law is the Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association for the Project on Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy and General Conditions for Grants and Loans of International Development Association and Explanatory Letter for timetable 3 - Amortization Plan.

Law No. 05/L-011 ON RaTIFICaTION OF FINaNCING aGREEMENT BETwEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO aND THE INTERNaTIONaL DEVELOPMENT aSSOCIaTION REGaRDING THE PROJECT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY aND RENEwaBLE ENERGY

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No.05/L-011 26 March 2015 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-005-2015, dated 10.04.2015, President of the Republic of Kosovo Atifete Jahjaga.
