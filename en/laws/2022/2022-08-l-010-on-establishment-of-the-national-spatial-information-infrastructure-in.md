# LAW NO. 08/L-010 ON ESTABLISHMENT OF THE NATIONAL SPATIAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE IN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON ESTABLISHMENT OF THE NATIONAL SPATIAL INFORMATION

### INFRASTRUCTURE IN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law defines the general rules for establishment of the National Spatial Information Infrastructure (hereinafter: NSII) in the Republic of Kosovo, for the implementation of spatial, environmental policies and activities that may have an impact on them.

\2. This Law is partly compatible with the European Union Directive 2007/02 EU of the European Parliament dated 14.03.2007, which defines the Spatial Information Infrastructure in the European Community.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this law are mandatory for all public and private authorities in order to increase efficiency for sustainable collection, maintenance, distribution and use of spatial information and better exchange through the implementation of effective interactive information and services standards which will be used by public and private authorities within the territory of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. Infrastructure for Spatial Information - metadata, entirety and spatial information services, network services and technologies, arrangements for distribution, access, use, coordination and monitoring mechanisms, processes and procedures that are in compliance with this Law;

1.2. National Spatial Information Infrastructure - the spatial information infrastructure applicable in the national territory;

1.3. Spatial data - any data with a direct or indirect reference to a specific location or geographical location;

1.4. Spatial information entirety - a set of spatial information which can be identified;

### LAW NO. 08/L-010 ON ESTABLISHMENT OF THE NATIONAL SPATIAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE IN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

1.5. Spatial information services - operations which can be performed by means of a computer program on spatial data which are found in the entirety of spatial data or in the relevant metadata;

1.6. Spatial object - an abstract presentation of real-world phenomena related to a specific location or geographical location;

1.7. Metadata - is information that describes the entirety of spatial data and spatial data services, which enable them to be discovered, inventoried and used;

1.8. Interaction - the ability to combine the spatial information set and the services to interact, without repeated manual interventions, in such a way that the result is coherent, while the added value of the set of information and services is expanded;

1.9. Geoportal - a website which provides access to the services defined in Article 17, paragraph 1. of this Law;

1.10. The Third Party - any natural or legal person other than a public authority;

1.11. Ministry – respective Ministry on Environment and Spatial Planning;

1.12. Minister – minister of the Ministry on Environment and Spatial Planning.

#### Article 4

Principles

\1. The establishment of the NSII in the Republic of Kosovo is guided by the principles of:

1.1. legality;

1.2. protection of the public interest;

1.3. informing the public with official information;

1.4. protection of personal data;

1.5. protection of intellectual property;

1.6. transparency;

1.7. sustainable development, and

1.8. providing quality services versus effective costs.

### CHAPTER II

### NATIONAL AUTHORITIES OF THE SPATIAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE

#### Article 5

National coordinating structure of the spatial information infrastructure

\1. The authorities responsible for coordinating the spatial information infrastructure are:

1.1. Kosovo Cadastral Agency (KCA);

1.2. Inter-Ministerial Committee for Land Administration and National Infrastructure for Spatial Information of Kosovo (Committee) and

1.3. Public authorities.

#### Article 6

Kosovo Cadastral Agency

\1. KCA is the State Spatial Information Authority in the Republic of Kosovo.

2.KCA is a decision-making, executive and coordinating institution that supervises the implementation of this law and the sub-legal acts issued in its implementation.

\3. KCA is responsible to:

3.1. implement strategy on national spatial information infrastructure;

3.1.1. strategy of NSII shall b based on the principles of this law;

3.1.2. the NSII strategy is drafted by the Kosovo Cadastral Agency, in cooperation with the committee and other competent authorities, while it shall be approved by the Government on proposal of the Ministry for a period of five (5) years;

3.1.3. the NSII strategy, as needed can be amended and supplemented after at least three (3) years after its approval.

3.2. propose the issuance of administrative instructions for setting state standards for the unification of the creation of spatial information for certain areas, in compliance with the European standards as well as the rules for update, exchange, access and use of spatial information and related services.

3.3. administer spatial information collected, processed and updated by public authorities, according to relevant fields;

3.4. ensure coordination of work during activities and taking initiatives related to spatial information in the public and private sector;

3.5. establish and administer the NSII Geoportal, guarantee the access of the public and interested subjects, in accordance with the provisions of this law and sub-legal acts;

3.6. represent the country in the European and international organizations, membership in which serves the functioning and modernization of the national spatial information infrastructure;

3.7. represent the country on any other issues related to the spatial information infrastructure.

\4. KCA ensures that the metadata created for the entirety of spatial information and services are in compatibility with the topics listed in Annexes 1, 2 and 3, and that these metadata are updated.

\5. KCA takes the necessary measures to ensure that the metadata are complete and of sufficient quality to meet the purposes set out in this Law.

#### Article 7

Committee for Land Administration and National Infrastructure of Spatial Information of Kosovo

\1. The Committee is an advisory body to the KCA regarding the development of the national spatial information infrastructure.

\2. The Committee carries out its activity whenever necessary for the advice of the authority responsible for the NSII.

\3. The Government of the Republic of Kosovo upon the proposal of the Minister determines the composition and responsibilities of the Committee for Land Administration and National Infrastructure for Spatial Information of the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 8

Public Authority

\1. For the purpose of this Law, the Public authority means:

1.1. all institutions of government or public administration, including public advisory bodies at the local and central level;

1.2. natural and legal persons who perform public administrative functions according to state laws, including specific tasks, activities or services related to the environment; and

1.3. natural and legal persons who are responsible for, and perform public functions, or perform public services related to the environment, under the control of a body or person from sub-paragraph 1.1. or 1.2. of this Article, if the bodies or institutions act in a judicial or legislative capacity, then they will not be considered as public authorities for the purposes of this Law.

\2. The Public Authority defined in paragraph 1. of this Article, is responsible:

2.1. for the preparation of spatial information according to standards;

2.2. for updating the spatial information in the NSII Geoportal; and

2.3. for coordinating activities with the KCA for specific areas.

#### Article 9

Working Groups

\1. In order to coordinate activities among the authorities responsible for the NSII, working groups are established for certain areas:

1.1. Working Group for Institutional and Legal Capacity Building;

1.2. Working Group on technical issues, standards, metadata and other services;

1.3. Working Group on Public Relations and Communication; and

1.4. Working Group on Economic Affairs.

\2. The working groups present to the KCA the proposals from their field of activity.

\3. The Ministry with a sub-legal act determines the composition, field of activity and the way of functioning of the working groups.

### CHAPTER III

### SPATIAL INFORMATION

#### Article 10

Collection, processing and updating of spatial information

\1. Collection, processing and updating of spatial information for each of the topics defined in Annex 1, 2 and 3 of this law, is performed by public authorities in accordance with the standards set by a sublegal act.

### LAW NO. 08/L-010 ON ESTABLISHMENT OF THE NATIONAL SPATIAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE IN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

\2. Public authorities collect, process and update spatial information, in compliance with applicable law and publish it on the NSII Geoportal.

\3. KCA, depending on the quality of information, approves or rejects its inclusion in the NSII Geoportal.

\4. If the spatial information is not according to the conditions determined by the sub-legal act, KCA gives the recommendation for the necessary corrections.

#### Article 11

Metadata

\1. Public authorities responsible for the creation or maintenance of spatial information, provided in Annex 1, 2 and 3 of this law, in cooperation with the KCA, create, collect and update metadata for this data, in accordance with this law and administrative instructions.

\2. Metadata contains information about:

2.1. compliance of the entirety of spatial information with the rules of interoperability of the entirety of spatial information;

2.2. conditions relating to the access to and use of the entirety of spatial information and spatial information services and, where applicable, the corresponding tariffs;

2.3. the quality and validity of spatial information entireties;

2.4. public authorities responsible for the creation, administration, maintenance and dissemination of spatial information entireties and spatial information services;

2.5. restrictions on public access and the reasons for these restrictions, in accordance with the rules set out in Article 19 of this law.

\3. The public authorities responsible for the creation of metadata ensure that the metadata are complete and in such a quality as to meet the conditions under paragraph 1. of Article 18 of this law.

\4. The Ministry with a sub-legal act determines the standards for the creation, storage, updating and structure of metadata.

#### Article 12

Interaction of spatial information sources

\1. KCA, in cooperation with the relevant public authorities, takes the necessary measures to ensure the interoperability of all spatial information services, for existing spatial information and for those created following the entry into force of this law, as well as for the corresponding metadata.

\2. In order to ensure the interoperability of the entirety and spatial information services, the KCA, in cooperation with the relevant public authorities, determines the definition and classification of the entirety of spatial information, related to the topics in Annex 1, 2 and 3 of this Law.

\3. The Ministry with a sub-legal act determines detailed rules for the realization of interoperability of spatial information resources.

\4. To ensure compliance of spatial information that lie between the border of Kosovo and another country, description and location of these common spatial information, when deemed necessary, can be done with the consent of both countries.

### CHAPTER IV

### INTER-INSTITUTIONAL COOPERATION AND COORDINATION

#### Article 13

Inter-institutional cooperation and coordination

\1. KCA, in accordance with its responsibilities under this law, cooperates and coordinates the actions and initiatives of public authorities in the field of spatial information.

\2. KCA provides inter-institutional cooperation and coordination on:

2.1. collecting, processing and updating spatial information;

2.2. setting the state standards and the uniform rules;

2.3. investments in the spatial information sector.

#### Article 14

Cooperation with other actors

\1. KCA, in addition to public authorities, cooperates closely with the private sector, in order to create an as comprehensive as possible spatial database.

\2. Public authorities cooperate with each other, but also with the private sector, for the implementation of joint initiatives, the exchange of spatial information produced by them, the development of information technology and the enrichment of the spatial information base.

\3. To ensure cooperation, according to paragraph 1. and 2. of this Article, KCA and public authorities inform private and academic entities about the initiatives undertaken by them, in implementation of this law, and invite them to participate and express their opinion through participation in the meetings of the Inter-Ministerial Committee for Land Administration and National Infrastructure on Spatial Information of Kosovo, organized for this purpose.

#### Article 15

Exchange of spatial information

\1. Public authorities exchange the entirety and services of spatial information, guaranteeing each other access to them and their use for the purposes of exercising their public functions.

\2. The Ministry with a sub-legal act determines the rules for the exchange of groups and services of geospatial data among public authorities.

### CHAPTER V

### ACCESS TO SPATIAL INFORMATION, LIMITATIONS AND CONDITIONS

#### Article 16

Network services

\1. KCA makes available to the public spatial information, for which metadata have been created, according to this law and the sub-legal acts in its implementation, through a network that provides services:

1.1. search, by which it becomes possible to search the entirety and services of spatial information, based on the corresponding metadata and display the content of these metadata;

1.2. maneuvering, by which it is possible to minimally display, navigate, zoom in or out,

stratify the entirety of the spatial information that appears, and display the information legend and any relevant metadata content;

1.3. downloading, which enables the downloading of copies or parts of the entirety of spatial information and, where appropriate, direct access to it;

1.4. transformation, through which the transformation of the entirety of spatial information is enabled, in order to achieve interoperability;

1.5. that allow the collection of spatial information services.

\2. The services provided for in paragraph 1. of this Article shall be constructed taking into account user-related requirements, which shall be user friendly, available to the public and accessible via Internet or any other appropriate means.

\3. The Ministry, by a sub-legal act, determines the technical standards for the provision of services by the NSII Geoportal.

#### Article 17

Establishment and maintenance of the NSII Geoportal

\1. In order to provide services for the use of spatial information, KCA establishes, develops and maintains the Geoportal for NSII.

\2. The NSII Geoportal links services to each of the topics set out in Annexes 1, 2 and 3 of this Law.

\3. Through the NSII Geoportal, public authorities are enabled:

3.1. metadata management;

3.2. the right to search and see;

3.3. the right to downloads and

3.4. insertion of spatial information as well as other information related to the National Spatial Information Infrastructure.

#### Article 18

Spatial information in the Geoportal on NSII

\1. In the Geoportal on NSII are published the complete sets of spatial information which meet the following conditions:

1.1. they deal with an area where the Republic of Kosovo exercises the rights of jurisdiction;

1.2. they are in electronic format;

1.3. they are maintained by the public authorities as follows:

1.3.1. produced, managed or updated by that authority within the scope of its public duties;

1.3.2. a third party who has been given the right to supplement or amend;

1.3.3. they relate to one or more of the topics set out in Annex 1, 2 or 3 of this Law.

\2. In case the spatial information sets which are in accordance with the conditions set out in

\3. The description of existing data topics set out in Annexes 1, 2 and 3 of this Law may be reviewed and adopted by the relevant public authority in order to take into account new spatial data needs in support of Kosovo policies which have an impact on the environment.

#### Article 19

Restrictions on the access

\1. KCA and the relevant public authorities restrict public access to the complexes and spatial information for search services, if they consider that such an approach will have a negative impact on one of the following cases:

1.1. confidentiality of procedures of public authorities, when such confidentiality is provided by law;

1.2. international relations and public security or National defense;

1.3. justice, the right of every person to a fair trial or the right of a public authority to conduct criminal or disciplinary investigations;

1.4. confidentiality of commercial or industrial information, where such confidentiality is provided by laws for the protection of economic interests, including the public interest in maintaining statistical and tax confidentiality;

1.5. intellectual property rights;

1.6. confidentiality of personal data and/or files relating to a natural person, where that person has not agreed to the disclosure of information to the public, when this confidentiality is provided by law;

1.7. the interests of protection of any person who has provided information on a voluntary basis, without making it legally obligated to do so, unless that person has given consent to the publication of the information in question;

1.8. information related to environmental protection, for the location of rare species;

1.9. is in contradiction to the applicable laws in Kosovo.

\2. Restriction of access, provided for in paragraph 1. of this Article, shall be construed in a limited way, in special cases, the interest of the public served by offering such access.

\3. In any particular case, the interest of the public served by disclosing the information will be measured in relation to the interest served by restricting or conditioning access.

\4. Public authorities of the Republic of Kosovo, on the grounds of sub-paragraphs 1.1., 1.4.,

1.6., 1.7. and 1.8. of paragraph 1. of this Article, may not restrict access to information related to emissions into the environment.

#### Article 20

Applicability The implementing rules, which are referred to in this law, are based on international standards.

### CHAPTER VI

### PUNITIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 21

Punitive measures

\1. The responsible persons of the national spatial information infrastructure authorities defined in Article 5 of this Law shall be sanctioned if they do not adhere to the responsibilities defined by this law or misuse them:

1.1. with a fine from two hundred (200) Euros up to two thousand (2.000) Euros for natural person;

1.2. with a fine from three hundred (300) euros up to two thousand (2.000) euros for the responsible person of the legal person;

1.3. with a fine from five hundred (500) euros up to five thousand (5.000) euros for a natural person conducting individual business;

1.4. with a fine from two thousand (2.000) euro up to twenty thousand (20.000) euro for legal person.

\2. Initiation of a procedure for minor offenses against the responsible persons of the national authorities of spatial information infrastructure, defined in Article 5 of this Law, is conducted by the authority that ascertains that the responsible persons of that authority have not complied with the responsibilities defined by this law or misuse them, at the competent bodies in accordance with the legislation in force.

### CHAPTER VII

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 22

Transitional provisions

\1. Spatial information collected, stored and updated by public authorities until the entry into force of this law, must be standardized according to the rules set out in a sub-legal act issued by the ministry within the time limit specified in paragraph 2. of this Article.

\2. Information on the topics set out in Annexes 1, 2 and 3 of this Law shall be prepared by the public authorities according to the deadline:

2.1. not later than two (2) years, in the case of spatial information sets corresponding to the topics specified in Annex 1 of this law; and

2.2. not later than four (4) years in the case of sets of spatial information corresponding to the topics specified in Annex 2 or 3 of this law.

\3. The deadlines set out in sub-paragraphs 2.1 and 2.2 of this Article shall begin to apply after the issuance by the ministry of administrative instructions for the implementation of this law.

#### Article 23

Sub-legal acts For the implementation of this law, The Ministry shall approve the sub-legal acts within six (6)

months from the entry into force of this Law.
