#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Co-Financing Agreement for the Prizren Innovation and Training Park Project (PIT) between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the Federal Republic of Germany represented by GIZ.

#### Article 2

Attached documents Attached to the Appendix of this Law is the text of the Co-Financing Agreement for the Prizren Innovation and Training Park Project (PIT) between the Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labor and Transfers and the Federal Republic of Germany Represented by GIZ.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
