# LAW NO. 08/L-114 ON POPULATION AND HOUSING CENSUS

### LAW NO. 08/L-114 ON POPULATION AND HOUSING CENSUS

### LAW NO. 08/L-114

### ON POPULATION AND HOUSING CENSUS

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON POPULATION AND HOUSING CENSUS

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law determines the manner of organizing and developing the process of the Population and Housing Census in Kosovo (hereinafter: “Census”); and determines competencies and responsibilities of the bodies that organize, assist and conduct the Census, documentation, processing, storage, publication of data and administrative measures.

\2. This law is partially in line with Regulation (EC) No. 763/2008 of the European parliament and of the council of 9 July 2008 on population and housing censuses, and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/543 of 22 March 2017 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EC) No 763/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council on population and housing censuses as regards the technical specifications of the topics and of their breakdowns.

#### Article 2

Scope

\1. The provisions of this law are applicable to the entire territory of Kosovo.

\2. The implementation of this law is done by the individuals and bodies defined according to this law.

\3. The census shall include: collection, processing and publishing of data on the population, households, and dwellings in Kosovo according to international standards, based on decision of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo on the date and duration of the Census.

\4. The data of the Census are necessary for state institutions of the Republic of Kosovo, economic organizations, academic institutions, for civil society and other interested parties for the purpose of planning and implementation of overall development policies, scientific research, and other analyzes necessary for various areas of society.

\5. The census shall provide information on:

5.1. number and geographical distribution of the population throughout the country, demographic structure and other socio-economic characteristics of the population, number and location of settlements, residential buildings, buildings used for residential purposes or other destination, assessment of living conditions of the population, number of households, and other individual characteristics.

5.2. foreign persons working within the country for businesses purpose or international organizations, and their families, provided that they meet criteria for usual residence within the country.

5.3 Kosovo’s diplomatic and military personnel are registered as resident population.

\6. International staff of diplomatic and liaison offices, and KFOR military staff are not included in the Census.

#### Article 3

Definitions

1.Terms used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. Resident population – is the person who has lived in his/her usual residence for a continuous period of time for more than twelve (12) months before the reference date of the census, or who have arrived in that place during the twelve (12) months before the reference date of the Census with the intention of staying there for over a year. Person who have been temporarily absent for a shorter period from his/her place of usual residence for a period of less than twelve (12) months for various reasons such as:

work, study, travel, medical treatment or any other reason with the purpose of returning should be included in the census, respectively in the resident population;

1.2. Non-resident population - are persons living outside Kosovo for more than twelve

(12) months, for whom the person or family member provides data on the occasion of the census. Also, it includes persons who have come to stay in Kosovo for less than twelve (12) months and do not intend to stay longer than twelve (12) months from the reference date;

1.3. Household – is each person, each family or other community of persons, who declare that they are living together and jointly distribute their income to meet basic living needs;

1.4. Dwelling/House – are one or more spaces, intended for housing, having an independent exit, and that at the census moment is inhabited or is intended for housing to a person or group of persons;

1.5. Kosovo Agency of Statistics - the competent institution for official statistics, determined according to the relevant Law on Official Statistics of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.6. Census - the population and housing census;

1.7.CCC - Central Census Commission;

1.8.MCC - Municipal Census Commission.

### CHAPTER II

### METHOD OF THE CENSUS

#### Article 4

Obligation to respond the census

\1. Every resident of the Republic of Kosovo is obliged that during the census to provide to the census officer all accurate data for himself/herself and his/her relatives.

\2. Every resident is free to show more than one ethnicity in cases of naturalization, or parents of different ethnicities.

\3. Every person is free not to declare his/her ethnic and religious affiliation.

\4. For the person under fifteen (15) years, the data is offered to the registering official by the parent, other adult person, or guardian.

#### Article 5

The authority and method of the census

1.The census shall be organized and conducted by Kosovo Agency of Statistics through the relevant bodies established by the provisions of this Law.

\2. The Kosovo Agency of Statistics by decision shall determine the method of data collection in the Census.

#### Article 6

Time and term of the census

\1. The Date of Census and duration are proposed to the Government by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, while the Decision on the Date of Census is taken by the Government, no later than nine (9) months before the Date of the Census.

\2. Post-Enumeration Survey (PES) is conducted no later than one (1) month after the end of the Census in order to assess the coverage and quality of the data collected in the census.

#### Article 7

The census objective

\1. The census objectives are:

1.1. Population;

1.2. households; and

1.3. dwellings/houses.

#### Article 8

Data collected from the census

\1. The data collected during the census shall be categorized in:

1.1. data on resident: address-residence, demographic data, data on migration, level of education, employment, disability, and other social and economic issues;

1.2. data on households: composition and ratio of members in the household, housing status and other modalities, according to other methodological guidelines;

1.3. data on dwellings/houses: key characteristics of the dwelling/house, including dwelling/house status, ownership, number of rooms and other dwelling/house infrastructure equipment.

#### Article 9

Data protection and their confidentiality

\1. Institutions and responsible persons under this Law, during and after the census process, are obliged to:

1.1. maintain the complete confidentiality of individual data of the household and of the enumerated person, pursuant to the provisions of this Law and the respective Law

on Official Statistics of the Republic of Kosovo, as well as other relevant legislation on Personal Data Protection;

1.2. prevent the unauthorized access to the database and/or center, where the storage of group and individual data of the Census is done;

1.3. treat as anonymous data of all personal data on the ones enumerated, during and after the census, technical inspection and certification of their accuracy.

\2. The collected data, whether grouped or individual, are anonymous to third parties, and are used for statistical purposes of the census only. Anyone who uses the statistical data of the census is obliged to maintain professional data confidentiality in accordance with the legislation in force for the protection of personal data.

\3. The personnel, engaged in the census process shall safeguard the data from the Census as confidential information, protected by the legislation in force.

#### Article 10

The census results

\1. Kosovo Agency of Statistics shall publish:

1.1. preliminary results of the Population Census not later than three (3) months after the end of the census data collection.

\2. Results include at least:

2.1. number of resident population by gender;

2.2. number of households; and

2.3. number of dwellings.

\3. The first final results of the census shall be published no later than fifteen (15) months after the completion of the Census data collection. Other reports shall be published within three (3)

years after the census.

#### Article 11

Census questionnaire and documentation

\1. The Census Documents, prepared by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, are:

1.1. Individual questionnaire;

1.2. Household questionnaire;

1.3. Dwelling questionnaire;

1.4. Questionnaire for non-resident population;

1.5. Questionnaire for collective dwellings; and

1.6. Questionnaire for institutional households.

\2. With the proposal of the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, the Statistical Council reviews the Census questionnaires, without interfering in the essential issues and proceeds it to the Chief Executive Officer of KAS for approval. Questionnaires are approved six (6) months before Census.

### CHAPTER III

### ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 12

Kosovo Agency of Statistics

\1. Duties and responsibilities:

1.1. plans, leads and implements tasks and responsibilities, including human and financial resources within the Census project;

1.2. compiles the work programme, determines the methodology, selects the relevant technical tools and software programs, drafts questionnaires, issues necessary instructions and decisions, as well as independently leads the preparations for the implementation of the census on a professional and scientific basis;

1.3. organizes the work for:

1.3.1. designing questionnaires and applications as defined by the provisions of the Law on using the official languages in the Republic of Kosovo;

1.3.2. methodological guidelines;

1.3.3. maps of enumeration areas;

1.3.4. essential equipment; and

1.4. census branding;

1.5. receives the data and/or documentation completed during and after the census;

1.6. sets criteria for the selection of coordinators, monitors, supervisors and enumerators;

appoints coordinators, monitors and supervisors to the Municipal Census Commissions;

as well as compiles the short list for enumerators and participates in their selection;

1.7. selects and appoints the necessary staff including scientific and professional experts;

1.8. organizes and supervises the professional and methodological training of the members of the municipal commissions and the census supervisors;

1.9. provides professional and methodological support to municipal commissions in preparing and implementing the census;

1.10. informs and request from authorities and responsible bodies of the Government on the steps to be taken;

1.11. conducts outreach campaign and informs the public;

1.12. submits to the Municipal Census Commission (MCC) all enumeration/ census material for that municipality;

1.13. presents and publishes preliminary and final results of the census.

#### Article 14

Competencies of the Central Census Commission

1.The Central Census Commission:

1.1. approves rules of procedure for the Central Census Commission, without affecting the professional and organizational competencies in the Census.

1.2. supervises stages of preparation and progress of the census;

1.3. establishes and approves members of the MCCs, and informs municipalities for their duties and responsibilities;

#### Article 15

Municipal Census Commission

\1. Municipal Census Commission (MCC) shall be established in each municipality. The MCC represents the ethnic structure of population, taking into consideration legal provision for representation of non-majority community.

\2. MCC shall be established at least six (6) months before the date of the census.

\3. The MCC is obliged to take all necessary measures for the timely, quality, accuracy and reliability of all activities, including the protection of data confidentiality.

\4. The number of MCC members is determined depending on the number of inhabitants in that municipality. The basis for the number of inhabitants is the latest Population Estimate in that municipality, published by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics.

4.1. the municipality with over eighty thousand (80 000) inhabitants shall have eleven

(11) members;

4.2. the municipality with up to eighty thousand (80 000) inhabitants shall have nine (9) members;

4.3. the municipality with up to thirty thousand (30 000) inhabitants shall have seven (7) members;

4.4. the municipality with up to ten thousand (10 000) inhabitants shall have five (5) members.

\5. MCC consists of:

5.1. the mayor of the municipality;

5.2. one (1) representative from the Kosovo Agency of Statistics;

5.3. one (1) representative from civil society;

5.4. the other members, in accordance with paragraph 4. of this Article, shall be appointed by the Mayor of the Municipality, taking as a basis the representation of communities living in that municipality.

\6. In case the Mayor of the municipality does not appoint the other members within the deadline set in this Law for the establishment of the MCC, they shall be appointed by the Central Census Commission.

\7. Duties of the MCC:

7.1. provides spaces for training and other activities three (three (3) months before the beginning date of the census); provides at least one office for a period of two (2) months before and during the census; and a two (2) week period after the census;

7.3. implements recommendations of the Kosovo Agency of Statistics;

#### Article 16

Compensation Compensation of engaged personnel in the Population Census project, internal and external staff of the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, is done according to the Census Project-Plan, which is prepared by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics.

#### Article 17

Information

\1. Kosovo Agency of Statistics is responsible for creating, developing and maintaining the reputation of Population Census at the public opinion in the country. Makes public through the media and awareness campaign, the goals, procedures, institutional and civic obligations according to this law.

\2. Communication Campaign shall be implemented accordingly to Communication and Outreach Strategies prepared by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics.

#### Article 18

Maps Kosovo Agency of Statistics, at least six (6) months before the date of the Census, prepares all the necessary updated maps for every enumeration area and other accompanying lists.

#### Article 19

Data processing

\1. Kosovo Agency of Statistics prepares as follows:

1.1. The Data Centre (Database) for sending, or transmitting, data from the field, as well as ensures that the database meets the criteria for confidential data storage, storage by cyber security and unauthorized access;

1.2. The working space for census such as: offices for additional work, for discussion, training, storage, support rooms for field work, as well as other necessary rooms, shall be provided at least nine (9) months before the census.

#### Article 20

Census staff

1.Tasks and responsibilities of the census staff shall be as following:

1.1. the personnel of the Kosovo Agency of Statistics, together with experts, prepares infrastructure and instruments of the census, planning, training, supervision, processing, analysis, tabulation of grouped data, production and publication of the results of the census;

1.2. the personnel of Kosovo Agency of Statistics organizes and prepares documentation, conducts awareness and communication campaign, logistics and field census administration;

1.3. coordinator and monitor - coordinate between the MCC and the Kosovo Agency of Statistics for official statistics and supervises the census process in his/her municipality;

1.4. supervisor - participates in training, is provided with an official Authorization for Enumeration, assists and controls the work of enumerators;

1.5. reserve supervisor - participates in training, is provided with an official Authorization for Enumeration and is activated at work on the proposal of the KAS when required;

1.6. enumerator - participates in the training, is provided with an official Authorization for Enumeration, goes door to door to conduct the interview, by correctly completing the questions in the enumeration modules/ questionnaires;

1.7. reserve enumerator - participates in training, is provided with an official Authorization for Enumeration and is activated at work on the proposal of the supervisor when required.

#### Article 21

Census funding

1.The Census shall be funded by two (2) sources:

1.1. Kosovo Budget; and

1.2. Donors.

\2. The draft budget shall be prepared by the Kosovo Agency of Statistics.

3.. Financial agreements for funds raised by donors shall be specified through memorandum of understanding (MoU) or agreements.

### CHAPTER IV

### PUNITIVE PROVISIONS

#### Article 22

Prohibition of similar data collection

\1. Except for bodies and/or institutions that are expressly authorized by this law, all individuals and local and international organizations are prohibited from collecting similar data within the territory of the Republic of Kosovo during a period of three (3) months before realization of the Census, during the time of data collection, as well as three (3) months after the census.

\2. During the periods specified in paragraph 1. of this Article, no other statistical activity is conducted at central or local level that may affect the census.

\3. Population Census is not conducted in special situations such as: central, local elections, social unrest and external factors in a period less than six (6) months before the Census. In exceptional cases, the Census date is reviewed.

#### Article 23

Minor offense sanctions with fines

1.The staff engaged for the census, for any breach of the rules provided under Article 9 of this Law shall be punished with a fine in the amount from thirty (30) euro to two thousand (2,000)

Euros.

\2. Except for the data pursuant to Article 4, paragraph 3. of this Law, a person who refuses to give the data required by the enumerator or other authorized person for the census, stipulated under Article 4 of this Law, or knowingly provides incomplete and inaccurate information, shall be fined as follows:

2.1. the natural person and the responsible person of the legal person shall be fined in the amount from thirty (30) Euros up to two thousand (2,000) Euros;

2.2. the natural person who carries out individual business, shall be punished with a fine in the amount from two hundred (200) euros up to five thousand (5,000) Euros;

2.3. a legal person shall be fined in the amount from five hundred (500) Euros up to twenty thousand (20,000) Euros.

### CHAPTER V

### APPELLATE PROVISIONS

#### Article 24

Appeal

\1. Against actions related to the decisions taken in the registration process, the dissatisfied parties have the right to appeal within thirty (30) days to Kosovo Agency of Statistics.

\2. The appeal procedure is conducted pursuant to the provisions of the respective Law on General Administrative Procedure.

### FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 25

Repealing provisions With the entry into force of this law, Law no. 03/L-237 on the Population and Housing Census in Kosovo shall be repealed.

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
