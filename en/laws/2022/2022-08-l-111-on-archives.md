# LAW NO. 08/L-111 ON ARCHIVES

### LAW NO. 08/L-111 ON ARCHIVES

### LAW NO. 08/L-111

### ON ARCHIVES

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON ARCHIVES

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. The purpose of this Law is to define the basic rules for the organization, administration, functioning of the archival service in the Republic of Kosovo as well as powers of the competent bodies for the creation, receipt, protection, systematization, processing and use of archival material.

\2. This Law is entirely in line with Directive 2012/28/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 and Directive 2001/29/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2001.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this Law shall be mandatory for public institutions of central and local level, other legal and natural persons, including those who exercise public authority under the laws of the Republic of Kosovo and have legal obligations to establish, maintain, protect and use of archival property.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. State Agency of Kosovo Archives (hereinafter Agency) - is the only executive institution of state administration that organizes, directs and controls the archival activity in the country;

1.2. Archives - are specialized state or non-state institutions that collect, process, administer, store and put into service archival property which is protected and secured by the state;

1.3. Central Archive - administers and manages the archival materials of central level public institutions;

1.4. Inter-Municipal Archive - administers and manages the archival materials of public institutions at the local level;

1.5. Special archives - are archives formed by special laws by public and non-public institutions which exercise activities of social interest;

1.6. Private archives - are entities that administer, process, store and put into service documents of non-state origin;

1.7. Archival fund - constitutes the entirety of archival material, regardless of its form or bearer, which has been created by a legal or natural person during its activity;

1.8. Documents - are all acts created by public authorities during the exercise of their activity in administrative functions and acts created by other entities, respectively by legal and natural entities;

1.9. Archival material - all source material and reproduced by public institutions, legal and natural persons during their activity such as: manuscripts, printed data, drawings, photographs, audio-visual materials, electronic and digitized materials and all other aids, which enable a clear understanding of the content of the information and which have permanent value;

1.10. Registration/documentary material - the whole of documents or data created during the activity and work of public institutions, legal and natural persons, regardless of the form and format of the note;

1.11. Fundcreators - institutions defined in the scope of this Law, which during the performance of work tasks create the archival material which is thereof sent for archiving to the competent archive after the expiration of the term determined by law;

1.12. Fundholders - are institutions and subjects defined in the scope of this Law which possess the material with archival value created by someone else;

1.13. Public archival register - is the set of data on the archival material found in the fundcreator of the fund holder, including what, for various reasons, is found in natural persons;

1.14. Private archival material - is archival material created by the activity of natural and legal persons who perform activities or activities of general interest, but is not created within the exercise of public power or public services and is not publicly owned.

### CHAPTER II

### STATE AGENCY OF KOSOVO ARCHIVES

#### Article 4

Bodies responsible for archival activity The Agency is the only executive institution of the state administration that organizes, directs and controls the archival activity in the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 5

Organization of the Agency

\1. The State Agency of Kosovo Archives is an executive agency that operates within the relevant Ministry of Culture.

\2. The Agency performs the duties and responsibilities defined by this Law and with relevant legislation in force that regulates functioning and organization of executive agencies.

\3. The Agency is headed by the Executive Director, who is responsible for the administration, operation and management of the Agency.

\4. The appointment, dismissal, functions and responsibilities of the executive director are

regulated by the relevant provisions in force.

\5. In the exercise of leadership duties, the Executive Director shall be supported by a Deputy Director who exercises his duties and responsibilities in accordance with the provisions and rules set out in the relevant normative act for the classification of work positions in the civil service.

\6. The structure and internal organization of the Agency are regulated by a sub-legal act in accordance with provisions in force on the organization and functioning of the state administration and independent agencies as well as with provisions of this law, which is prepared by the relevant Ministry on Culture and approved by the Government.

\7. The Agency is organized in two levels: central archives and inter-municipal archives.

#### Article 6

Duties and responsibilities of the Agency

\1. The Agency is responsible for the implementation of international standards for archival activity.

\2. The Agency shall ensure the implementation of policies and strategic orientations for the advancement and development of archival activity through the application of technicalprofessional and methodological norms for the identification, acceptance, preservation, protection, regulation, processing, conservation, restauration, digitalization, use and publication of archival material.

\3. Advises, instructs and supervises implementation of legislation on archival material by public institutions, natural and legal persons and other entities.

\4. Provides advice, guidance, recommendations and information to the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and relevant ministries during the process of developing public policies and sectoral and cross-sectoral strategies, which in their structure may include issues that fall within the mandate of the Agency.

\5. Agency in cooperation with Institute for public administration shall prepare and organize professional training for archival officials.

5.1. training program content, manner and procedure of training organization shall be determined by sub-legal act proposed by the Agency in cooperation with Kosovo Institute for Public Administration, which is approved by the Government of the Republic of Kosovo.

\6. Performs functions and other responsibilities prescribed by law.

#### Article 7

Network of archival institutions

\1. For the purpose of development of archival activity, the archival network of the Republic of Kosovo shall be created.

\2. The archival network of Kosovo is organized on the principle of functional interconnection of all archival institutions of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. The archival network of the Republic of Kosovo consists of:

3.1. The State Agency of Kosovo Archives with its branches, respectively with the respective units, which include:

\4. No other units responsible for the archive can be established or functionalized, except those defined by this Law.

#### Article 8

Central archive and inter-municipal archives

\1. The central archive shall administer and manage the archival materials of public institutions of the central level, except for those institutions which, by special law and with the consent of the Agency, create their own archives, which administer and manage their own archival materials.

\2. Inter-municipal archives shall administer and manage the archival materials of the respective local public institutions:

2.1. The inter-municipal archives are:

2.1.1. Inter-municipal Archive of Prishtina which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: Prishtina, Podujeva, Kastriot, Fushë Kosovë, Lipjan, Drenas and Gracanica;

2.1.2. Inter-municipal Archive of Mitrovica which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: South Mitrovica, North Mitrovica, Leposavic, Zubin Potok, Zvecan, Vushtrri and Skenderaj;

2.1.3. Inter-municipal Archive of Peja which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: Peja, Deçan, Junik, Istog and Klina;

2.1.4. Inter-municipal Archive of Gjakova which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: Gjakova, Rahovec and Malisheva;

2.1.5. Inter-municipal Archive of Prizren which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: Prizren, Suhareka, Dragash and Mamusha;

2.1.6. Inter-municipal Archive of Gjilan which includes the fundcreating archchives in the following municipalities: Gjilan, Kamenica, Novobërda, Vitia, Ranillug, Partesh and Kllokot;

2.1.7. Inter-municipal Archive of Ferizaj which includes the fundcreating archives in the following municipalities: Ferizaj, Shtime, Kaçanik, Hani i Elezit and Shtërpce.

#### Article 9

Duties and responsibilities of the fundcreator

\1. Every fund creator shall be obliged to have the unit responsible for the archive known as the fundcreator archive.

\2. Fundcreator is obliged to maintain the material he possesses and creates it according to the

criteria provided in this Law and other legal acts until it submits it to the competent archive for permanent storage.

\3. The fundcreator is obliged that in accordance with the legal acts in force, to select the material with permanent value and the destruction of the material without permanent value.

\4. Fundcreators shall submit the archival material to the competent archive within the legal maturity date defined by this law.

\5. Fundcreators shall inform the Agency for the register, storage and any other changes regarding the archival material as a whole.

#### Article 10

Special archives

\1. Special archives are created by public and non-public institutions which carry out social activities of social and public interest.

\2. Public institutions which, within their scope of activity can create special archives shall be:

2.1. Kosovo Ministry of Defense;

2.2. Ministry of Internal Affairs;

2.3. Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Diaspora;

2.4. Kosovo Intelligence Agency;

2.5. Public and private institutions of higher education;

2.6. Clinical and University Hospital Service of Kosovo;

\3. Special archives shall store archival material in accordance with the provisions of the legislation in force.

\4. In case of cessation of activity of any of the subjects defined in paragraph 2. of this Article, their archival material shall be submitted to the competent archive.

#### Article 11

Private archives

\1. Private archives shall administer, process, store and provide archive material for service.

\2. Private archives may be established by natural and legal persons with the consent of the Agency in accordance with the legislation in force.

\3. After the establishment the private archives, shall become part of the archival network of Kosovo.

\4. Procedures for granting consent by the Agency for the establishment of private archives are determined by a sub-legal act, which is drafted by the Agency and approved by the relevant Ministry for Culture.

#### Article 12

Public archival register

\1. The Agency creates, administers and updates the archival register of archival material that falls within the scope of this Law.

\2. The public archival register contains data records for all public institutions, legal and natural persons in the Republic of Kosovo that possess archival material.

\3. All public institutions, including legal and natural persons are obliged to make available to the Agency all the data and information necessary for the updating of the public archival register.

\4. Refusal to cooperate from the subjects defined in paragraph 3. of this Article constitute a violation of the obligations defined by the legislation in force.

#### Article 13

Handover-receipt of archival material

\1. Public institutions within the scope of this Law are obliged to handover all archival material to the Agency within the legal deadline.

\2. The fundcreators and fundpossessers are obliged to handover to the Agency the archival material older than thirty (30) years from the year of creation.

\3. In special cases, respectively when the archival material is endangered as well as in cases when the activity of the fund creator is extinguished, the handover of the archival material can be done before the deadline defined in paragraph 2. of this Article.

\4. The archival material is handed over by the creator or possessor registered and evidenced in accordance with the rules and technical-professional and methodological norms for evidencing, handing over and receiving the archival material.

\5. If the archival material is not arranged according to the methodology and professional criteria, then, the Agency shall determine the deadline within which the possessor is obliged to arrange the amount of archival material in accordance with the professional instructions of the Agency.

If the possessor, for various reasons, does not follow the professional instructions for arranging the material, the Agency may undertake to do so, but only at the expense of the possessor.

\6. Expenses from paragraph 5. of this Article are calculated according to the fee provided by internal regulations, which is drafted by the Agency and approved by the and approved by the relevant Ministry of Culture.

\7. The procedure of handover-receipt of archival material is regulated by a sub-legal act, which is drafted by the Agency and approved by the relevant Ministry for Culture.

#### Article 14

Supervision and inspection

\1. Institution responsible for the implementation of this Law is the Agency, unless otherwise provided by this Law.

\2. In exercising the mandate and its responsibilities, the Agency shall inspect all entities that fall within the scope of this Law, in order to supervise and ensure the implementation of the provisions of this Law, laws and other bylaws as well as technical-professional of the archival field.

\3. Within the organizational structure, the Agency shall establish and functionalize the Inspectorate of the Agency, which will exercise supervisory, inspection, information and advisory powers, for all entities and other institutions that are legally given duties or responsibilities for the preservation, protection and administration of archival materials.

\4. Public institutions are obliged to provide access to the Agency officials to inspect the environment, conditions and method of administration of the registered/documented material.

5.1. regular and scheduled inspection;

5.2. inspection in exceptional cases related to, but not limited to, the risk of archival materials found with the fund creator and which may be initiated upon notice or request to the Agency;

#### Article 15

Administration and protection of archival material

\1. The archival material is stored as a whole and cannot be alienated, damaged or destroyed.

\2. The archival material is processed based on the criteria and standards for archiving and is preserved and protected from unauthorized use and misuse.

\3. The release of archival material that is in the possession of the Agency is subject to the conditions, criteria and procedures determined by a sub-legal act drafted by the Agency and approved by the relevant Ministry for Culture.

\4. Technical standards, criteria, rules and responsibilities for the care, preservation and protection of archival material as well as the disposal procedure of the invalid material or documents, shall be regulated by sub-legal act, drafted by the Agency and approved by the respective Ministry for Culture.

#### Article 16

Utilization of archival material

\1. Archival material is kept, stored and is used for educational, scientific, cultural, research, information needs, professional work and the needs of citizens.

\2. This Law shall guarantee the right to everyone, whether natural or legal person, without discrimination, on any grounds, to have access to the material archived in the Agency, with the exception of the restrictions provided by this Law and/or other legislation in force.

\3. The right to research and utilization of archival material in the Agency is part of the fundamental rights of the citizens for free and unhindered information and as such, in principle, can not be limited except in cases expressly provided by law.

\4. Access to archival material in the Agency is free of charge.

\5. The services required in the framework of the use of archival material are subject to the fees determined by a sub-legal act drafted by the Agency and approved by the relevant Ministry for Culture.

\6. Use of archival material may be limited, in whole or in part, in the following cases:

6.1. when there is doubt that the interest of the Republic of Kosovo, or the well-being of its citizens will be endangered;

6.2. when national security, protection and international relations in which the Republic of Kosovo is involved are violated;

6.3. when the private life of the individual is violated;

6.4. when the fundamental rights of third parties guaranteed by the Constitution, including the rights protected by the relevant legislation on personal data protection are violated;

6.5. when the financial, monetary or tax policy of the Republic of Kosovo is violated.

\7. Competent staff in the Agency, in case of using the archival material or creating a copy, shall issue the document which contains the facts contained in the archival material.

#### Article 17

Timelines for the use of archival material

\1. Archival material is available for use from the moment of receipt by the Agency, unless otherwise provided by law and other legal acts.

\2. If by giving in use the material, as provided in paragraph 1. of this Article, the protection of the state interests of the Republic of Kosovo is violated, then the material can be given for use only after the possibilities of negative impact have ceased.

\3. Archival material, which based on the purpose and essential content is related to the personal data of natural persons, shall not be given for use before the expiration of the following terms:

3.1. ten (10) years after the decease of the natural person;

3.2. one hundred (100) years from the year of birth, in case the year of death of the concrete person is not known;

3.3. sixty (60) years from the moment of creation of the archival material, in case the year of birth and that of death is unknown.

\4. Timelines set out in paragraph 3. of this Article do not apply to documents which at the time of creation were public and dedicated to the public.

\5. The spouse, children or parents of the person whose personal data are in the Agency shall have access to and use the material archived in the Agency even before the expiration of the terms specified in paragraph 3. of this Article, except in cases when the concerned person before his/her death has denied access to the relevant material for his/her family members.

\6. The use of archival material according to the timelines set out in paragraph 3. of this Article may be allowed on the basis of the submission of the request even before the expiration of this term only in the following cases:

6.1. when the person concerned has approved use of archival material;

6.2. in the event of the death of the person concerned, this right is exercised by his lawful descendants, unless the person concerned, while alive, has expressly prohibited access to his/her personal data.

#### Article 18

Timelines for storage of documents The Agency shall give its consent for the approval of the list of unique classification marks and timelines for storage of documents that according to the legislation in force, every fundcreator must prepare.

### CHAPTER III

### MOVEMENT AND TRANSPORT OF ARCHIVAL MATERIAL

#### Article 19

\1. Archival material preserved in the Agency, or other archives, part of the archival network of Kosovo, may be removed from the territory of the Republic of Kosovo, for various purposes, only when the agreement guarantees the preservation of their integrity and physical security during their movement and the activity.

\2. Submission of archival materials outside the territory of the Republic of Kosovo is done with the permission of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. Extraction of archival materials outside the territory of Kosovo may not be done without determining the necessary insurance in cases of damage, loss or eventual destruction, in part or in full, of archival material.

\4. The value of insurance of archival material that is taken out of the territory of the Republic of Kosovo is determined by agreement according to international standards.

\5. Prior to taking out of the territory of Kosovo, the archival material must be reproduced.

#### Article 20

Return of archival material to the Republic of Kosovo The Republic of Kosovo, in support of international acts and instruments regulating the archival field, has the obligation to commit the request for return of archival material to the country of origin that have been illegally released outside the state territory of Kosovo.

### CHAPTER IV

### ENRICHMENT OF ARCHIVES

#### Article 21

Ways of enriching the Agency with archival material In addition to internal archival material, the Agency shall also enrich with external archival material, by ensuring it through research, giveaway, donation, deposit and purchase.

#### Article 22

Purchase, restrictions on the sale or donation of archival material abroad and the priority of purchase

1.The Agency has the right to purchase documents which have archival and historical value.

\2. Documents of historical, national and state importance owned by private persons cannot be donated or sold abroad.

\3. For the documents listed in paragraph 2. of this Article, the Agency enjoys the right of priority for their purchase.

\4. Natural or legal persons, registered in the Republic of Kosovo, are obliged to cooperate with the Agency when they possess documents or materials of national, state or social importance, which materials are donated or sold to the Agency. In cases when the rightful owners or possessors of archival material refuse to donate or sell it, they are obliged to submit copies and all reproduced data to the Agency and are responsible for the preservation and security of the material in the original version.

### CHAPTER V

### PENALTY PROVISIONS

#### Article 23

Liability for minor offenses

\1. Any natural or legal person who, in any capacity, is in possession, care or administration of archival material, in addition to criminal liability defined by the Criminal Code of the Republic of Kosovo, is also subject to minor liability in accordance with applicable law on minor offenses and this Law.

\2. Natural and/or legal person for matters that present an offense is punished by a fine.

#### Article 24

Offenses

\1. For the purposes of this Law, misdemeanor offenses, for which legal entities and natural persons are subject to liability and sanctions for minor offenses, as follows:

1.1. misuse of archival material;

1.2. failure to comply with mandatory conditions, criteria and norms for the protection, preservation and adequate use of archival material;

1.3. failure of the fund creator or the fund holder in implementing the organizational and technical conditions for the protection and preservation of archival material;

1.4. non-compliance with the legal deadline for timely submission, in the form and procedure regulated by law or bylaws in force, of archival material by the fund creator/ fund holder;

1.5. refusal of cooperation or obstruction, in any form, by the person responsible to the officials of the Agency;

1.6. damage or loss as a result of negligence or failure to take preventive measures by the responsible person.

\2. Mandatory conditions, criteria, norms, including technical organizational conditions for the preservation, protection and adequate use of archival material are determined by a sub-legal act, drafted by the Agency and approved by the relevant Ministry for Culture.
