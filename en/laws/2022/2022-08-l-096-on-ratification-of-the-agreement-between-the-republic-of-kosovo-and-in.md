# LAW NO. 08/L -096 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON FINANCING THE FOSTERING AND LEVERAGING OPPORTUNITIES FOR WATER SECURITY PROGRAM

### LAW NO. 08/L -096 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

### ASSOCIATION ON FINANCING THE FOSTERING AND LEVERAGING OPPORTUNITIES FOR WATER SECURITY PROGRAM

### LAW NO. 08/L -096

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND

### INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON FINANCING THE FOSTERING AND

### LEVERAGING OPPORTUNITIES FOR WATER SECURITY PROGRAM

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION ON FINANCING THE FOSTERING

### AND LEVERAGING OPPORTUNITIES FOR WATER SECURITY PROGRAM

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law intends to ratify the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association on Financing the Fostering and Leveraging Opportunities for Water Security Program, signed on 13 July 2020.

#### Article 2

Ratification of Agreement This Law ratifies the Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association on Financing the Fostering and Leveraging Opportunities for Water Security Program, signed on 13 July 2020.

#### Article 3

Attached documents Attached to the annex of this Law there is the Financial Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and International Development Association on Financing the Fostering and Leveraging Opportunities for Water Security Program, signed on 13 July 2020.

#### Article 4

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
