# LAW NO. 08/L-169 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN ‘FISCALIS’, THE UNION PROGRAMME FOR COOPERATION IN THE FIELD OF TAXATION

### COOPERATION IN THE FIELD OF TAXATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-169

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO,

### REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN ‘FISCALIS’, THE UNION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO,

### REPRESENTED BY MINISTRY OF FINANCE, LABOUR AND TRANSFERS AND THE

### EUROPEAN UNION ON THE PARTICIPATION OF KOSOVO IN ‘FISCALIS’, THE UNION

### PROGRAMME FOR COOPERATION IN THE FIELD OF TAXATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This law aims to ratify the agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and transfers and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in ‘Fiscalis’, the Union programme for cooperation in the field of taxation.

#### Article 2

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law is attached the agreement between the Republic of Kosovo, represented by the Ministry of Finance, Labour and transfers and the European Union on the participation of Kosovo in ‘Fiscalis’, the Union programme for cooperation in the field of taxation.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 08/L-169 14 October 2022
