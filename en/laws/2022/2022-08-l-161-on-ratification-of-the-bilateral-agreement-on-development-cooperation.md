# LAW NO. 08/L-161 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON DEVELOPMENT COOPERATION BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GRAND DUCHY OF LUXEMBOURG”

### LAW NO. 08/L-161 ON RATIFICATION OF THE “BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON DEVELOPMENT COOPERATION BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE GRAND DUCHY OF LUXEMBOURG”

### LAW NO. 08/L-161

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE “BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON DEVELOPMENT

### COOPERATION BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GRAND DUCHY OF

### LUXEMBOURG”

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE “BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON DEVELOPMENT

### COOPERATION BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GRAND DUCHY OF

### LUXEMBOURG”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Bilateral agreement on development cooperation between the Republic of Kosovo and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Bilateral agreement on development cooperation between the Republic of Kosovo and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law.

The agreement is an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Offical Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-161 14 October 2022
