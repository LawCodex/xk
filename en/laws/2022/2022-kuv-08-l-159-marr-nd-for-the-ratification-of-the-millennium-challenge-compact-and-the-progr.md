# LAW NO. 08/L-159 FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE COMPACT AND THE PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA ACTING THROUGH THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE CORPORATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-159 FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE COMPACT AND THE PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA ACTING THROUGH THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE

### CORPORATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-159

### FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE COMPACT AND THE

### PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA ACTING THROUGH THE MILLENNIUM

### CHALLENGE CORPORATION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW FOR THE RATIFICATION OF THE MILLENNIUM CHALLENGE COMPACT AND THE

### PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA ACTING THROUGH THE MILLENNIUM

### CHALLENGE CORPORATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Millennium Challenge Compact and the Program Implementation Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the United States of America acting through the Millennium Challenge Corporation.

#### Article 2

Attached documents The text of the Millennium Challenge Compact and the Program Implementation Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the United States of America acting through the Millennium Challenge Corporation, is attached to the Appendix of this Law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
