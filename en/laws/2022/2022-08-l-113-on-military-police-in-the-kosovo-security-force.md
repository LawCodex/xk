# LAW NO. 08/L-113 ON MILITARY POLICE IN THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L-113 ON MILITARY POLICE IN THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L-113

### ON MILITARY POLICE IN THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON MILITARY POLICE IN THE KOSOVO SECURITY FORCE

#### Article 1

Purpose This law defines the mission, organization, authorizations and duties of the Military Police in the Kosovo Security Force.

#### Article 2

The scope This law applies to all military and civil entities, local and foreign, with or without citizenship, which with their actions violate the actitivity of the Kosovo Security Force inside and outside the country.

#### Article 3

Jurisdiction Military Police exercises its jurisdiction in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo and when deployed in mission outside of the country in support to the Kosovo Security Force.

#### Article 4

Definitions

\1. In this Law the terms below shall have the following meanings:

1.1. Military Police (MP) – is the Police of the Kosovo Security Force;

1.2. Member of Military Police – the KSF member who is selected and appointed to perform duties of the MP;

1.3. Information - is any knowledge that can be communicated or documented, regardless of the form;

1.4. Use of force - is the direct action of a member of the Military Police through physical force, equipment or other means, given in use, on the basis of and for the implementation of the law, in reasonable and proportional way;

1.5. Escort - is sending the person to the premises of the Military Police or other places designated by the competent bodies, with or without his/her will, for reasons provided in this law;

1.6. Information Activity – is activity of Military Police member performed in accordance with legislation into force for searching, finding, assembling, registration, administration,

verification, analyzing of data and information for illegal activity in the Kosovo Security Force;

\2. Other used definitions have the meaning according to the applicable legislation.

#### Article 5

Mission The mission of the Military Police is to maintain the military order and to guarantee implementation of the law in the Kosovo Security Force, protection of the life of personnel, protection of the state property in use or administration of the Security Force, inside and outside the country.

#### Article 6

Principles

\1. The Military Police conducts its activity on principles of:

1.1. lawfulness;

1.2. respecting fundamental human rights and freedom;

1.3. proportionality;

1.4. independence of investigation and operations;

1.5. professionalism;

1.6. impartiality;

1.7. objectivity;

1.8. integrity;

1.9. non-discrimination;

1.10. transparency;

1.11. control; and

1.12. accountability.

#### Article 7

Status The Military Police is a specialized structure within the Kosovo Security Force under the Commander of the Kosovo Security Force.

#### Article 8

Authorizations

\1. MP exercises its own authorizations in barracks or operational zones where the KSF operates, in MoD premises, out of the barracks against the KSF Personnel, MoD, and persons who violate MP legal orders.

\2. The Military Police for fulfillment of its mission relies on the provisions of this law, the Law on the Status, Immunities and Privileges of the Diplomatic, Consular and Personnel Missions in the Republic of Kosovo and the International and Military Presence as well as its Personnel, and other legislation in force, and has the following authorizations:

2.1. to perform security check including but not limited to: control of persons, documents, transportation means, luggage, buildings and spaces;

2.2. to regulate traffic and impose traffic fines according to the legislation in force, to KSF members and MoD personnel in official vehicles;

2.3. to regulate the traffic and impose traffic fines in line with existing legislation within KSF barracks;

2.4. to use emergency traffic signals for movement into traffic;

2.5. to restrict movements, stop, arrest and escort persons;

2.6. to use force and coercive means necessary and proportional in line with existing legislation;

2.7. to identify and verify persons, means and equipment;

2.8. to identify persons who use or wear uniforms, parts of uniform, insignia, symbols or similar means with those of the KSF;

2.9. to gather, assess, keep and proceed data and information concerning suspected violations;

2.10. to perform activity for prevention and investigation of illegal actions, according to the legal procedures in force;

2.11. to make temporary seizure of items, means and equipment in line with legislation in force;

2.12. to provide security of items, buildings, locations, zones, crime scene, facts and evidences;

2.13. to protect persons, buildings and zones of a particular importance;

2.14. to cooperate with the Kosovo Police and other Agencies of law enforcement and security;

2.15. to perform other duties within the KSF mission in accordance with orders received from chain of command;

2.16. to use property of the third party persons in emergency and extraordinary circumstances.

#### Article 9

Duties of the Military Police

\1. MP has the duty to:

1.1. maintain military order and discipline in KSF;

1.2. ensure the flow of military traffic;

1.3. prevent, investigate and detect crimes, which are investigated according to official position, committed by KSF members and other employees of KSF and Ministry of Defense, within the military installations or related to military activities in line with existing legislation;

1.4. protect personnel, delegations, and persons of special importance;

1.5. protect property, command and Military Units;

1.6. protect buildings or zones of special importance.

#### Article 10

Military Police Organization

\1. Military Police in Kosovo Security Force is organized in:

1.1. MP Command that ensures command and control in Military Police. The Command is comprised of commanding staff and functional sectors to support the operations of Military Police;

1.2. Close Protection Unit, which provides security and escort for important personalities during official activities;

1.3. Military rule and order unit ensures and controls the implementation of legal acts and bylaws on military order and discipline within the KSF activities;

1.4. Investigation Unit to prevent, detect, fight criminal activities, apprehending individuals that have committed or are about to commit criminal offenses within the area under KSF and MoD management and administration.

\2. The investigation unit from sub-paragraph 1.4. of this Article, while conducting its activities for violations which are foreseen by Disciplinary Code and other KSF norms, acts in cooperation with KSF units, while for infractions and criminal offenses, cooperates with Kosovo Police, Courts and Prosecution.

\3. Other specialized and operational units may be attached to Military Police, depending on KSF needs.

#### Article 11

Filing the Criminal Report Military Police Investigator files criminal report for deeds violating or that may violate Kosovo Security Force, in line with provisions of the legislation in force. The Investigator places the relevant documentation at the disposal of prosecutor in charge of criminal prosecution.

#### Article 12

Notification to report at the Military Police

\1. Military or civil personnel working in Kosovo Security Force and in Ministry of Defense is invited to report at MP Investigation unit office as a suspect, witness, or when in possession of information for criminal or disciplinary cases.

\2. Notification to report is made through notification letter, official email, telephone, by defining the reason to report, timing, place, as well as Military Police employee who has invited.

#### Article 13

Escorting to Military Police

\1. Members in uniform and civilians are escorted to offices of Military Police, at certain locations, by a Military serviceman in all cases when:

1.1 refusing to present Identification Document, or its authenticity is suspected;

1.2 opposing or obstructing the official performance of Military Police;

1.3. found at the site and suspected of committing criminal offense;

1.4. wanted by judicial bodies.

\2. The person, who is accompanied by the Military Police, has normal conditions of stay in line with legislation in force and after the clarification of the circumstances of the case for which he was accompanied, when he/she is not involved, the escort is terminated. In case the person is involved it is acted according to Article 14 of this Law.

\3. When the Military Police arrests a civilian in a structure or area of special importance, military area, at the scene suspected of a criminal offense, in repulsion of the attack on the team, persons or items and assets he has in defense, he will inform the Police of Kosovo.

\4. Against such a person, from paragraph 3. of this Article, the Military Police may use the necessary measures and coercive means, stopping him until the arrival of the Kosovo Police.

\5. In all cases, the escorted or detained person cannot be held for more than six (6) hours.

#### Article 14

Arrest and detainment Military Police shall arrest and detain a military member or civilian in accordance with the legal provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

#### Article 15

Use of force and firing weapons Military Police is authorized to use force, firing weapons, means and tools available, in line with principles of proportionality and necessity, in order to meet assigned authorizations and duties in line with legislation in force. In support of this Article, Minister of the Ministry of Defense shall issue sub-legal acts

#### Article 16

Uniforms, insignia and symbols of Military Police

\1. In addition to the official uniform, the military police may wear civilian clothes while performing their duties and authorizations.

\2. The Military Police member is equipped and carries Military Police Identification Document with identification number (the badge), and will identify himself/herself if required.

#### Article 17

Military Police Mobile vehicles/means While exercising its duties, the MP shall use military and civil means. MP may use vehicles that are granted traffic exemptions (alarm system and emergency lightning) and escorting means. Depending on the mission and duties, it may use transportation, battle vehicles, and MP-mission-tailored vehicles.

#### Article 18

Professional Training of Military Police

\1. MP is subject to professional training by KSF and other education institutions, which provide training in compliance with the law.

\2. In order to exercise duties and authorizations, the MP member shall be certified congruent to

internal standards set by the KSF and respective legislation in force.

\3. Military Police member is re-certified every five (5) years in a way defined by KSF and the respective existing legislation. Failing to re-certify implies the loss of status of MP, but not the service in KSF.

#### Article 19

Riskiness MP is entitled to hazardous remuneration in compliance with existing legislation.

#### Article 20

Exchange of information Military Police exchanges information with Kosovo Police and other law enforcements and security agencies, in line with existing legislation.

#### Article 21

Obligation to not give out information

\1. Military police member and other officers working in Military Police must not give out information, gained during or after work performance, except to individuals authorized by law.

\2. Obligation to not give out information is also applied by the member released or sacked from Military Police.

\3. As for information related to identity of witnesses and whistle blowers, the Military Police applies existing legislation.

#### Article 22

Sub-legal acts

\1. Pursuant to Articles 8, 9, 13 and 15 of this Law, the Minister of Defense shall issue special sub-legal acts, within twelve (12) months from entry into force of this law.

\2. Sub-legal acts in force shall continue to be implemented, until the issuance of new acts, provided that they are not in contradiction with this law

#### Article 23

Abrogation With the entry into force of this law, all bylaws that are in conflict with this law are repealed.

#### Article 24

Entry into force This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
