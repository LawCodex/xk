# LAW NO. 08/L-139 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE MITROVICA WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-139 ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK

### FOR THE MITROVICA WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

### LAW NO. 08/L-139

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE MITROVICA WASTEWATER

### TREATMENT PLANT DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN INVESTMENT BANK FOR THE MITROVICA

### WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Loan Agreement for the Mitrovica Wastewater Treatment Plant Development Project between Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank.

#### Article 2

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law there is attached the Loan Agreement for the Mitrovica Wastewater Treatment Plant Development Project between Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 08/L-139 11 May 2022

Promulgated by Decree No. DL-149/2022 dated 25.05.2022 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu __________________________________ *Annex of the Law No. 08/L-139, “On ratification on the Loan Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Investment Bank for the Mitrovica Wastewater Treatment Plant Development Project”
