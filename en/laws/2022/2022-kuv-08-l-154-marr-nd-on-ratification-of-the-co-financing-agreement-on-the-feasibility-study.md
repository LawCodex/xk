# LAW NO. 08/L-154 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT ON THE FEASIBILITY STUDY AND THE PROJECT-IDEA FOR THE RAILWAY CONNECTION DURRËS - PRISHTINA

### LAW NO. 08/L-154 ON RATIFICATION OF THE CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT ON THE FEASIBILITY STUDY AND THE PROJECT-IDEA FOR THE

### RAILWAY CONNECTION DURRËS - PRISHTINA

### LAW NO. 08/L-154

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT ON THE FEASIBILITY STUDY

### AND THE PROJECT-IDEA FOR THE RAILWAY CONNECTION DURRËS - PRISHTINA

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE CO-FINANCING AGREEMENT ON THE

### FEASIBILITY STUDY AND THE PROJECT-IDEA FOR THE RAILWAY CONNECTION

### DURRËS-PRISHTINA

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Co-Financing Agreement on the Feasibility Study and the Project-Idea for the Railway Connection Durrës-Prishtina”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement

\1. Co-Financing Agreement on the Feasibility Study and the Project-Idea for the Railway Connection Durrës-Prishtina, shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law.

\2. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
