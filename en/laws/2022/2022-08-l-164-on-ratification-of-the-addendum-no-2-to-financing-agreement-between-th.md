# LAW NO. 08/L-164 ON RATIFICATION OF THE ADDENDUM NO. 2 TO FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2018 PROGRAMME

### LAW NO. 08/L -164 ON RATIFICATION OF THE ADDENDUM NO. 2 TO FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO

### AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2018 PROGRAMME

### LAW NO. 08/L -164

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE ADDENDUM NO. 2 TO FINANCING AGREEMENT

### BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2018

### PROGRAMME

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE ADDENDUM NO. 2 TO FINANCING AGREEMENT BETWEEN

### THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION ON IPA 2018 PROGRAMME

#### Article 1

The purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the Addendum No.2 to Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on IPA 2018 Programme.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the agreement Addendum No.2 to Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on IPA 2018 Programme shall be considered ratified upon the entry in to force of this law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 08/L-100 21 January 2022 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-309/2022 dated 14.10.2022 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu __________________________________ *Annex of the Law No. 08/L-164, “On the Ratification of the Addendum No.2 to Financing Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Union on IPA 2018 Programme.” attached to the law is published in the website of the Official Gazette (gzk. rks-gov.net)
