### ACCESSION ASSISTANCE (IPA III)”

#### Article 1

Purpose The purpose of this law is to ratify the “Financial Framework Partnership Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Commission on specific arrangements for implementation of union financial assistance to Kosovo under the Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance (IPA

### III)”.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the Agreement “Financial Framework Partnership Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Commission on specific arrangements for implementation of union financial assistance to Kosovo under the Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance (IPA III)” shall be considered ratified upon the entry into force of this law. The agreement and its annexes are an integral part of this law.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo Law No. 08/L-148 28 July 2022 Promulgated by Decree No. DL-263/2022 dated 10.08.2022 President of the Republic of Kosovo Vjosa Osmani-Sadriu __________________________________ *Annex of the Law No. 08/L-148, “On Ratification of the Financial Framework Partnership Agreement between the Republic of Kosovo and the European Commission on specific arrangements for implementation of union financial assistance to Kosovo under the Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance (IPA III)” attached to the law is published in the website of the Official Gazette (gzk.rks-gov.net)
