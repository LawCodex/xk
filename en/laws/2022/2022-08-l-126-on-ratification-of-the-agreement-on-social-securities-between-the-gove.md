# LAW NO. 08/L -126 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITIES BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA

### LAW NO. 08/L -126 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITIES BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF

### KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA

### LAW NO. 08/L -126

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITIES BETWEEN THE

### GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITIES BETWEEN

### THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Agreement on Social Securities between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Republic of Albania.

#### Article 2

Attached documents In the Annex of this Law there is attached the Agreement on Social Securities between the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the Government of the Republic of Albania.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This Law shall enter into force on the day of the publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
