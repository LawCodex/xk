# LAW NO. 08/L-133 ON THE PARLIAMENTARY COMMISSIONER FOR THE SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L-133 ON THE PARLIAMENTARY COMMISSIONER FOR THE SECURITY FORCE

### LAW NO. 08/L-133

### ON THE PARLIAMENTARY COMMISSIONER FOR THE SECURITY FORCE

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON THE PARLIAMENTARY COMMISSIONER FOR THE SECURITY FORCE

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose

\1. This law aims to determine competencies, duties and responsibilities, the election, appointment and dismissal procedure of the Parliamentary Commissioner for the Security Force, work methodology, relevant issues on his/her work, administrative support, procedures for submitting the complaints and their review.

\2. The Parliamentary Commissioner for the Security Force of Kosovo has a mission to strengthen parliamentary control, rule of law and protection of human rights and freedoms of military personnel on Security Force of Kosovo.

#### Article 2

Scope of application

\1. This law is applied for the military personnel in the Security Force and the Ministry of Defense, inside and outside of the country.

\2. Except in cases when otherwise provided by this Law, the relevant Law on State Administration and Independent Agencies shall apply to all matters related to the work of the parliamentary commissioner for KSF.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The expressions and abbreviations used in this law shall have the following meaning:

1.1. KSF - Kosovo Security Force;

1.2. Minister - Minister of the Ministry of Defence;

1.3. MoD - Ministry of Defence;

1.4. Commissioner - Parliamentary Commissioner for the Security Force;

1.5. Functional Committee – respective parliamentary committee responsible for security and defence matters;

1.6. Military personnel of KSF – active members, reserve forces, cadets and recruits.

### CHAPTER II

### LEGAL STATUS AND WORK PRINCIPLES

#### Article 4

Legal status

\1. Parliamentary Commissioner for the Security Force is an independent body of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo whose competencies and responsibilities are defined by this law.

\2. The Assembly of Kosovo initiates and approves the Rules of Procedure of the Commissioner, which regulates procedures, work methodology and complaint procedure for the KSF members.

#### Article 5

Work Principles

1.The commissioner during the exercise of the mandate is guided by:

1.1. principle of lawfulness;

1.2. principle of non-discrimination;

1.3. principle of accountability;

1.4. principle of confidentiality; and

1.5. principle of professionalism.

### CHAPTER III

### COMPETENCIES AND AUTHORITY

#### Article 6

Competencies

1.The Commissioner, in performing supervisory activities, in the field of protection of the human rights and freedoms of military personnel of the KSF, has the following competencies:

1.1. to carry out administrative inquiries on different issues, at the request of the Assembly of Kosovo and of the Functional Committee. Such requests are addressed to the Commissioner, except in the event if one of the issues has not been discussed previously by the Functional Committee;

1.2. to carry out activities based on his/her assessment, after receiving information by the members of the Assembly of Kosovo, in any occasion, which indicates that the rights and freedoms of the military personnel of KSF are violated;

1.3. to consider submitted complaints by the military personnel of KSF, regarding the allegations on the violation of their rights.

#### Article 7

Authority

1.The commissioner, in the performance of duties, within his/her competences has the authority:

1.1. to handle complaint submitted according to this law;

1.2. to request information and access to the records of the MoD and KSF;

1.3. to request information and access in other institution regarding the KSF member;

1.4. to call for a hearing or to interview the complainant, witnesses of other parties involved in the complaint process;

1.5. to request expert analysis from an expert;

1.6. to proceed to the responsible authorities, the file of a criminal or disciplinary case in suspected cases for violation of the legislation in force;

1.7. to visit MoD, KSF, units of the KSF at any time with or without prior notice;

1.8. to request information from the Minister of Defense and the commander of KSF within their scope, regarding the rights and freedoms of military personnel of the KSF;

1.9. to give the responsible institutions the opportunity on resolving the submitted issue and in this regard to establish cooperation with the Ombudsperson;

1.10. to provide recommendations for the relevant institutions.

\2. Members of the KSF are obliged to respond to the Commissioner`s questions and invitations.

\3. MoD/COMFSK shall allow members of KSF to appear before the Commissioner at any time, except in case the mission of the KSF is violated and such permission must be granted immediately after the cessation of the reasons that postponed the appearance before the Commissioner. In such case, the Commissioner shall discuss this issue with the Inspector General, the Minister of Defense and COMFSK.

\4. Minister of Defense, COMFSK and other institutions are obliged to respond to the letters of the KSF Commissioner.

\5. Access to classified documents is done in accordance with the legislation on classified information.

\6. The KSF Commissioner may be present at the sessions of the Assembly of Kosovo and at the meetings of the functional committee.

### CHAPTER IV

### DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 8

Responsibilities

\1. The Commissioner shall submit a written report to the Assembly of Kosovo for each calendar year, until 31st of March.

\2. The Commissioner may submit a specific report at any time to the Assembly of Kosovo and the functional committee.

\3. When acting pursuant to the Article 6, sub-paragraph 1.2. of this Law, the Commissioner shall submit a specific report on the results of investigation to the applicant of appeals.

\4. The commissioner is obligated to be present in the sessions of the Assembly, or in the meetings of the Functional Committee to report for issues in his/her scope of work, when this is required from him/her.

#### Article 9

Confidentiality

\1. During the exercise of the mandate, the commissioner is obligated to keep confidentiality of information made to him/her available on official basis, in accordance with the legislation on classified information.

\2. The Commissioner even after the termination of the mandate is obliged to keep the confidentiality of the information made to him/her available during his/her mandate, in accordance with the legislation on classified information.

\3. This shall not apply to the official correspondence or issues generally known to the public or those issues which due to their significance clearly do not require to be classified by security level.

### CHAPTER V

### COOPERATION WITH OTHER INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 10

Cooperation with KSF and MoD

\1. In the performance of duties under his/her competencies, the Commissioner shall cooperate with:

1.1. Minister of Defence;

1.2. Inspector General;

1.3. FSK Commander.

\2. Institutions and bodies referred to in paragraph 1. of this Article shall be obliged to inform the Commissioner on initiated proceedings in accordance with his/her recommendations and the results of these proceedings.

#### Article 11

Cooperation with Ombudsperson Ombudsperson with the prior approval of the appellant shall submit the case to the Commissioner for further proceeding. The Commissioner shall be obliged to inform the Ombudsperson on the results of the proceedings.

#### Article 12

Cooperation with judicial and administrative bodies Judicial and administrative bodies shall be obliged to inform the Commissioner about the beginning of the proceedings, the lawsuit and the results of the initiated proceedings by his/her proposal.

### CHAPTER VI

### THE RIGHT TO APPEAL

#### Article 13

The right to appeal

\1. Military personnel of KSF shall have the right to contact directly the Commissioner and to file a complaint without mediation of the official bodies. A complainant shall not be subject to disciplinary procedures or discrimination due to a filed complaint.

\2. Anonymous appeals shall not be considered.

\3. When acting on a filed complaint, the Commissioner shall not reveal information related to the complaint publicly, if so requested by the complainant and if the request is not in conflict with the legal provisions.

\4. MoD and KSF shall be obliged to provide access on information, under the competences of the Commissioner for all military personnel of the KSF, on the procedure for submitting the appeal and the protection of appellant by any disciplinary measure or discrimination.

### CHAPTER VII

### ELECTION AND THE MANDATE

#### Article 14

Qualification for the election of the Commissioner

\1. The candidate applying for the position of the commissioner should fulfil the following conditions:

1.1. to be citizen of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. to have university degree;

1.3. to have at least eight (8) years of professional work experience, out of which at least five (5) years of experience in leadership positions;

1.4. to not have been sentenced by a final decision for a criminal offence;

1.5. to have high moral and professional integrity;

1.6. to not have been dismissed from work with disciplinary measure.

\2. Candidates running for KSF Commander shall be subject of the security control verification process before being sent to the Assembly for voting.

\3. Candidates who do not agree to undergo the security control verification process shall be excluded from the competition.

#### Article 15

Proposal procedure

\1. The proposal procedure starts with public vacancy announcement for the election of the Commissioner which shall be published in written and electronic media.

\2. In the competition shall be set the conditions for the election of the Commissioner, provided by this law. The deadline of the competition cannot be shorter than fifteen (15) days nor longer than twenty (20) days.

\3. After the expiration of the deadline provided for in paragraph 3. of this Article, the selection panel, appointed by the functional committee of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo, within a period of fifteen (15) days shall evaluate whether the candidates fulfil the conditions to be elected as a commissioner.

\4. The selection panel shall conduct the interview with each candidate, who fulfils the conditions to be elected commissioner and based on the data presented and on the results of the interview, prepares the short list of the qualified candidates for voting by the Assembly of Kosovo.

\5. The short list contains three (3) candidates, except in the case where within the number of three (3), there are more candidates with the equal evaluation points. The selection panel shall submit the list of candidates to the Committee, which proposes the short list to the Assembly of Kosovo. The proposal of the Committee contains reasoning why the panel gave priority to some of the candidates compared to other candidates.

\6. After the expiration of the mandate, the commissioner exercises his function until the election of a new commissioner.

\7. The election process of a commissioner begins six (6) months before the end of the mandate of a current commissioner.

#### Article 16

Election

\1. The commissioner shall be elected through a secret vote by the Assembly of Kosovo with the majority of the votes of deputies present at the Assembly and voting.

\2. If none of the candidates receive the necessary majority of votes in the first round, the second round is organised between two (2) candidates who have received the highest number of votes in the first round.

\3. If in the second round none of the candidates is elected, the Assembly within sixty (60) days shall announce a new competition for the Commissioner.

#### Article 17

Mandate

\1. The mandate of the Commissioner shall last for five (5) years without the right of re-election.

\2. The mandate of the Commissioner shall begin after taking an oath before the deputies of the Assembly of Kosovo.

\3. The text of the oath is: “I solemnly swear that I will perform faithfully and impartially the duties and functions entrusted to me by the Law, that I will protect and advance the respect for human rights and freedoms for recruits, cadets, and military personnel of the Security Force of Kosovo. I swear!”

#### Article 18

End of mandate

\1. The mandate of the Commissioner shall end:

1.1. at regular termination of the mandate;

1.2. resignation;

1.3. his/her death;

1.4. retirement;

1.5. in case of permanent loss of the ability to act, ascertained by the competent court;

1.6. in case of becoming incapable due to health reasons which makes it impossible to exercise the function for more than three (3) months;

1.7. in case is punished by a final court decision for a criminal offence which is punishable to more than six (6) months of imprisonment;

1.8. in case of dismissal.

#### Article 19

Dismissal procedure

1.The Assembly of Kosovo, with the proposal of the functional committee, parliamentary group or one-fourth (¼) of the members of Assembly with the majority votes of all MPs shall dismiss the Commissioner for the following reasons:

1.1. in case of violation of the provisions of this law;

1.2. in case of exercising his/her duties inconsistently with his/her function.

#### Article 20

Incompatibility of a function

1.Forbiden functions for the Commissioner are as follows:

1.1. cannot be member of any political party or exercise political, public or private professional activity;

1.2. cannot participate in governing bodies of civil, economic and trade organisations;

#### Article 21

Immunity The Commissioner enjoys immunity from prosecution and civil lawsuit for activities and decisions that are within the scope or responsibilities of his Institution.

### CHAPTER VIII

### ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT

#### Article 22

Seat The seat of the Commissioner shall be in the building of the Assembly of Kosovo.

#### Article 23

Staff

\1. For performing his duties, the Commissioner shall have at his/her disposal the supporting staff that will have the status of personnel of the Assembly of Kosovo and will be elected in accordance with provisions of the legislation on civil service.

\2. The supporting staff shall be organised within the office of the Commissioner.

\3. The organisation, duties and responsibilities of the commissioner`s office shall be regulated by the Rules of Procedure of the Commissioner.

#### Article 24

Salary

1.The salary of the Commissioner shall be set in accordance with the respective legislation for salaries in public sector and shall be at the same level as the salary of the member of parliament.

2.The Commissioner, whose regular mandate expires, has the right to remuneration for a period of twelve (12) months, if during this period he/she does not return to his/her position before election or to another position suitable for him/her.

#### Article 25

Budget The necessary financial means to perform the function of the Commissioner and his office are provided by the Budget of the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo.

### CHAPTER IX

### TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 26

Election of the first Commissioner The Procedure for election of the first Commissioner shall be initiated by the Functional Committee, no later than sixty (60) days after the entry into force of this law.

#### Article 27

By-laws No later than six (6) months from the entry into force of this law, the Assembly shall approve Rules of Procedure of the Commissioner.

#### Article 28

Entry into Force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
