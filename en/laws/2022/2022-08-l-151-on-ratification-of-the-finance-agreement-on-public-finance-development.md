# LAW NO. 08/L-151 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT ON PUBLIC FINANCE DEVELOPMENT POLICIES AND SUSTAINABLE GROWTH BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-151 ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT ON PUBLIC FINANCE DEVELOPMENT POLICIES AND SUSTAINABLE

### GROWTH BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

### LAW NO. 08/L-151

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT ON PUBLIC FINANCE

### DEVELOPMENT POLICIES AND SUSTAINABLE GROWTH BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

The Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE FINANCE AGREEMENT ON PUBLIC FINANCE

### DEVELOPMENT POLICIES AND SUSTAINABLE GROWTH BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO AND THE INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATION

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Finance Agreement for Public Finance Development Policies and Sustainable Growth between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association.

#### Article 2

Ratification of the agreement Through this Law the Agreement for Public Finance Development Policies and Sustainable Growth between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association is ratified.

#### Article 3

Attached documents The text of the Finance Agreement for Public Finance Development Policies and Sustainable Growth between the Republic of Kosovo and the International Development Association is attached to the Annex to this Law.

#### Article 4

Entry into Force This law enters into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
