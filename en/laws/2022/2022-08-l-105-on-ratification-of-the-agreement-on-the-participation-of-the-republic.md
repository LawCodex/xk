# LAW NO. 08/L -105 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN CREATIVE EUROPE PROGRAMME OF THE EUROPEAN UNION (2021-2027)

### LAW NO. 08/L -105 ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN CREATIVE EUROPE

### PROGRAMME OF THE EUROPEAN UNION (2021-2027)

### LAW NO. 08/L -105

### ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE REPUBLIC

### OF KOSOVO IN CREATIVE EUROPE PROGRAMME OF THE EUROPEAN UNION (2021-

2027)

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Articles 18 and 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF THE AGREEMENT ON THE PARTICIPATION OF THE

### REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO IN CREATIVE EUROPE PROGRAMME OF THE EUROPEAN

### UNION (2021-2027)

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law aims to ratify the Agreement on the participation of the Republic of Kosovo in the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union (2021-2027).

#### Article 2

Ratification of the agreement There is ratified the Agreement on the participation of the Republic of Kosovo in the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union (2021 - 2027).

#### Article 3

Attached documents

\1. The integral parts of this Law are:

1.1. Agreement between Kosovo and the European Union for participation of Kosovo in the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union (2021 - 2027);

1.2. Annex I - Rules governing the financial contribution of Kosovo to the Creative Europe Programme (2021– 2027);

1.3. Annex II - Sound Financial Management - Protection of financial interests and recovery.

#### Article 4

Competent authorities The competent authorities for the fulfilment of the tasks of this Agreement are the European Union and the Government of the Republic of Kosovo through the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports.

#### Article 5

Entry into force This Law shall enter into the force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
