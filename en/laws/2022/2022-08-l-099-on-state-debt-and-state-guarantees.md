# LAW NO. 08/L-099 ON STATE DEBT AND STATE GUARANTEES

### LAW NO. 08/L-099 ON STATE DEBT AND STATE GUARANTEES

### LAW NO. 08/L-099

### ON STATE DEBT AND STATE GUARANTEES

Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo;

Based on Article 65 (1) and Article 120 (3) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Adopts:

### LAW ON STATE DEBT AND STATE GUARANTEES

### CHAPTER I

### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

Purpose This Law regulates main principles and procedures for state borrowing, issuance of state guarantees, payment of principal, interest, fees and commissions related to the state debt, monitoring state guarantees and other costs related to them.

#### Article 2

Scope The provisions of this Law shall be applicable to all institutions and public entities in the Republic of Kosovo that are involved and assume obligations regarding state debt and state guarantees, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 3

Definitions

\1. The definitions used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

1.1. Fiscal Agent - shall mean the Central Bank of the Republic of Kosovo (hereinafter: the CBK), which acts as an agent of the relevant Ministry of Finance to carry out certain duties related to the fiscal affairs of the Republic of Kosovo;

1.2. Securities auction - shall mean the mechanism used to enable government securities to be issued in a full competition, using an authorized platform under the provisions of this Law;

1.3. Debt - any financial obligation to be paid or otherwise pay money deriving from the Financing Contracts, notes of treasury, bonds, overdrafts or other securities issued as consideration for the disbursement of funds, as well as the obligation to repay principal, interest, discount, fees, commissions or penalties of any nature;

1.4. Domestic Debt - shall mean a state debt issued or entered into with a lender within the country under this Law;

1.5. Municipal Debt - shall mean the total debt entered into by the municipalities of the Republic of Kosovo, borrowed through the Minister subject to the provisions and rules set out in this Law;

1.6. Government Debt - shall mean the total debt issued on behalf of the Government

1.7. State Debt - shall mean the total government net debt and municipal debt borrowed under the provisions of this Law;

1.8. External Debt -shall mean the state debt issued or entered into by means of international agreement and/or financial agreements with a lender incorporated abroad;

1.9. State Guarantee - a guarantee agreement issued for a loan of a public or private entity for which the Ministry has issued state guarantees under this Law;

1.10. Borrowing - shall mean the process of creating financial or credit obligations, namely state debt, by entering into financial agreements, international agreements or issuing securities;

1.11. Institutions and other public entities - shall mean all public institutions outside the Government and municipalities of the Republic of Kosovo, established under the Constitution and laws of Kosovo, exercising their activity in Kosovo at both levels of the organization, excluding the CBK;

1.18. State Debt Management - shall mean the process from negotiating a financing agreement or an international agreement, issuance of Government securities, servicing including pre-payment or buy-back, recording, validating, and reporting state debt;

1.19. Minister - shall mean the relevant minister of finance, as the sole authority to engage in state borrowing and issue state guarantees under this Law;

### CHAPTER II

### STATE DEBT

#### Article 4

State debt composition

\1. State debt consists of government debt and municipal debt.

2 Government debts consists of securities issued, international agreements and financial agreements subject to domestic or foreign legislation.

\3. Municipal debt includes debt entered through an international agreement or financial agreement that is subject to domestic or foreign legislation.

#### Article 5

Forms of state debt

\1. State debt is carried out as a domestic and external debt.

\2. Domestic debt may be carried out by issuing securities as well as through financial agreements concluded with creditors incorporated domestically.

\3. External debt may be carried out by issuing securities in international markets, by international agreements and by financial agreements with a creditor incorporated abroad.

#### Article 6

State debt restrictions The total state debt amount and state guarantee amount shall be projected and stipulated within the annual law on budget appropriations.

#### Article 7

Authorisation for state debt

\1. The Ministry has the sole authority to negotiate and borrow state debt on behalf of the Republic of Kosovo, through the relevant Minister of Finance, as the only authorized person, without the right to delegate such authorization.

\2. The Minister is authorized to negotiate and conclude necessary agreements which enables the issuance of securities in international markets.

\3. No other public institution in the Republic of Kosovo shall be entitled to incur state debt, on behalf of the Republic of Kosovo, according to the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 8

State Debt Terms

\1. State debt may be entered into or issued at a fixed or variable interest rate.

\2. Domestic debt may be issued only in the official currency of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. External debt may be issued in the official currency of the Republic of Kosovo, as well as other foreign currencies, having into account the medium-term priorities of currency risk in the state debt management strategy.

#### Article 9

State Debt Program

\1. The Ministry shall prepare and publish the State Debt Program (hereinafter referred as:

SDP), a three (3) year rolling program, reviewed at least once a year, which sets out the objectives, guidelines and measures for managing state debt and state guarantees under the best international standards for the purpose of securing adequate funding at the lowest cost and risk possible.

\2. The SDP is approved by the Government and submitted to the Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo (hereinafter: the Assembly) for their information no later than 30 April of the following year. After this date, SDP can be updated depending on the developments of the country’s macroeconomic framework.

\3. The SDP shall contain the elements including but not limited to the following:

3.1. objectives of state debt management;

3.2. characteristics of the state debt portfolio;

3.3. main macroeconomic developments and risks;

3.4. analysis of the major risks associated with the state debt structure;

3.5. identifying funding needs and resources based on the medium term fiscal framework;

3.6. medium-term debt strategic planning; and

3.7. other important/relevant information.

\4. SDP is the main document and serves as the basis for the borrowing process under the provisions of this Law, which includes main guidelines regarding the limitations and conditions of the state debt. No borrowing projections, or borrowing in itself, or any action that is inconsistent

with SDP shall not be undertaken.

\5. The Minister shall present annually to the Assembly the report on the level of state debt and state guarantees, by including the realization of the project financing, as well as the fulfilment of the SDP’s objectives, which shall be publicly available.

#### Article 10

State debt and state guarantees management

\1. The Ministry shall be responsible for managing the state debt and state guarantees.

\2. In accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article, the Ministry is authorized to enter into derivative financial arrangements in order to manage the cost and mitigate the risk exposure of the state debt portfolio.

\3. The Ministry may buy-back and prepay the state debt in order to manage the state debt portfolio more efficiently in accordance with the objectives set out in the SDP.

\4. State debt management procedures are regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 11

State debt service

\1. State debt service has the highest priority in terms of payment in relation to other public expenditures.

\2. The amount needed to meet the obligations to pay the principal, interest and other fees on behalf of the state debt service is provided under the annual law on budget appropriations.

\3. If the estimated amount is insufficient to fully meet the obligations for servicing the state debt, the Ministry may make the full payment or the remaining difference from the Treasury Single Account.

\4. In the event of payment from the Treasury Single Account as provided for in paragraph 3 of this Article, within forty-five (45) days from the date of payment, the Ministry shall prepare a report on the reasons for exceeding the budgetary projections and discloses it within the annual financial statements. The same information shall be included in the reporting according to paragraph 5 of Article 9 of this Law.

#### Article 12

Parity of state debt

\1. State debt shall have parity and equality of status, regardless of the period and format of debt acquisition.

\2. Claims regarding the payment of the principal, interest and other fees applicable to state debt charges constitute the first claim against the Treasury Single Account and shall not be dependent on any other claim.

#### Article 13

State budget ceiling

\1. The total state debt ceiling may not exceed the level as stipulated in the relevant law on public financial management.

### CHAPTER IV

### ENTERING AND ISSUANCE OF STATE DEBT

#### Article 15

Methods of entering into a state debt

\1. The entering into a state debt occurs through the following forms:

1.1. Financial Agreement;

1.2. International Agreement; and

1.3. Government Securities.

#### Article 16

Entering into a state debt through financial agreements

\1. State debt incurred under financing agreements, either domestic or external debt, is borrowed only through the Ministry.

\2. Execution and supervision of the implementation of projects financed through financial agreements are under full responsibility of the budget organization or project beneficiary institution. The budget organization and the beneficiary entity of the funding shall be involved in every stage of the financing securing process.

\3. The procedure for entering into and amending a financial agreement, shall be conducted through a competitive procedure as a service contract which is carried out according to the sub-legal act as issued by the Minister. The financial agreement reached under this paragraph is approved and signed by the Minister.

\4. The restructuring procedure of financial agreements and other related issues shall be subject to the same sub-legal act as stipulated under paragraph 3 of this Article.

#### Article 17

Entrance into state debt through international agreement

\1. Entrance into a state debt through international agreements shall be performed only through the Ministry.

\2. Entering into state debt through international agreements with other states, international organizations or international financial organizations or institutions shall be carried out in accordance with the procedures set forth in the legislation in force for international agreements.

\3. Notwithstanding paragraph 2 of this Article, international agreements with international organizations or international financial institutions, where the Republic of Kosovo is a member, shall not be subject to the procedures for approving the initiative for international agreements as stipulated in Article 4, paragraph 2 of Law No.04/L-052 on International Agreements or any other subsequent legislation that may have a similar regulation.

#### Article 18

Requests for funding through financial or international agreements

\1. Financing of investment projects or related public investments through borrowing is based on Government’s strategic documents and priorities, in line with the development strategy, as well as it should be economically and financially viable based on the feasibility study, as a sufficiently matured project and/or investment for implementation.

\2. Budget Organizations requesting to finance projects by means of borrowing through financial or international agreements shall, in advance, submit a documented request to the Ministry.

\3. The criteria and procedure for reviewing the request through an evaluation mechanism is defined by a sub-legal act issued by the Government.

#### Article 19

Amendments to the international agreement

\1. For any amendment to the terms of the international agreement, including the change of the loan sector purpose, increasing the loan amount, extension of the loan repayment term, the key interest rate, other applicable fees and other similar conditions, such agreement shall be subject to the amendment procedures of the agreement.

\2. The amendment of the terms of international agreement under paragraph 1 of this Article shall have the same decision-making procedure as that of ratification of international agreements.

#### Article 20

Restructuring of international agreement

\1. Restructuring of a project financed through an international agreement, including the restructuring of its components, reallocation of funds within the sector objectives, project implementation deadlines, or other project elements, which do not result in substantial changes under paragraph 1 of Article 19 of this Law shall not be perceived as an amendment to an international agreement, if expressly provided for in the ratified agreement. In such cases, the Minister is authorized to approve such restructuring in accordance with the agreement.

\2. The change in the loan amortization schedule and grace period of the ratified international agreement shall not constitute a substantial change to an international as long as this change does not result in an extension of the loan’s maturity period. In such cases, the Minister is authorized to approve such a change.

#### Article 21

Issuance of state debt through Government securities

\1. The Ministry has the sole authority to issue Government securities in domestic and international market.

\2. Issuance of securities in the domestic market is carried out through auction, placement or direct contracting, reserving the right to reject any or all of the bids. Issuance of government securities in the domestic market is done only in official currency of the Republic of Kosovo.

\3. The Ministry may decide to provide instruments aimed to develop the domestic securities market or involve the general public in person or electronically, negotiable or non-negotiable.

In such cases, government securities may be offered with the characteristics specified by the Minister.

\4. Issuance of securities in international markets is done according to the jurisdiction and rules in force of those markets and may also be made in other currencies.

\5. The process of issuance of securities in international markets, foreseen in the SDP and the annual Law on budget appropriations as specific item, is authorised, in advance, by the Government decision, which sets out at least the following elements:

5.1. total amount to be issued in the following year;

5.2. maturities of instruments being issued;

5.3. authorised currencies; and

5.4. other procedural aspects.

\6. The Minister approves the result and the issuance of the securities to the international markets as authorised by the paragraph 5 of this Article.

\7. The Ministry shall create an accounting system for registration, issuance, and account holder of the securities, service and maturing of Government securities.

\8. Procedures for issuing securities in the domestic and international market are set by sublegal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 22

Fiscal Agent of the Ministry

\1. The CBK is the fiscal agent of the Ministry. The role of the fiscal agent is determined in the bilateral agreement between the Ministry and CBK.

\2. The Ministry may, based on a bilateral agreement, authorize the CBK in the capacity of its fiscal agent, to act on behalf of the Ministry and with its approval, for the responsibilities as following:

2.1. to conduct auctions of Government securities;

2.2. to maintain an Electronic Registration System for Government securities;

2.3. to record the issuance, transactions and transfer of Government securities;

2.4. to facilitate the functioning of the secondary market for Government securities; and

2.5. to conduct other activities for which the parties have agreed.

### CHAPTER V

### GOVERNMENT DEBT

#### Article 23

Purpose of borrowing

\1. Borrowing on behalf of the Government is carried out only for the following purposes:

1.1. financing the projects and investments which are considered to be national objective reflected in governmental strategic documents;

1.2. financing the state budget deficit;

1.3. temporary financing of liquidity and maintenance of the bank balance as foreseen by the legislation in force on the public financial management;

1.4. re-financing or prepaying state debt;

1.5. honouring the obligations resulting from state guarantees, in whole or in part, in case the borrower fails to meet the obligations of the loan;

1.6. covering the costs associated with a state of emergency announced by the competent institution or a state of macroeconomic tumult, budgetary or financial shocks as set out in the relevant public financial management law; and

1.7. on-lending in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

\2. The criteria and the limitations for entering into borrowing shall be regulated with a sub-legal act approved by the Minister.

\3. Exceptionally, the restrictions and/or regulations applicable to the state debt shall be appropriately applied to the government debt.

### CHAPTER VI

### MUNICIPAL DEBT

#### Article 24

Purpose of municipal debt

\1. Borrowing on behalf of the Municipality is carried out for the following purposes only:

1.1. financing projects in accordance with the municipal development strategy or municipal development plans; and

1.2. for the co-financing of inter-municipal infrastructure projects.

#### Article 25

Authorization of municipal debt

\1. The project for which financing is requested has to be already part of the strategic municipal development documents, economically and financially viable based on the feasibility study as well as a sufficiently matured project for implementation.

\2. Municipal debt is initiated by the Mayor of the Municipality, who proposes the borrowing to the Municipal Assembly according to the purposes set out in Article 24 of this Law.

\3. Approval of the initiation of the procedures for short-term debt is done by a simple majority of

votes of the Municipal Assembly, while for long-term debt there is required the approval with 2/3 of the votes of all members of the Municipal Assembly.

\4. After preliminary approval by the Municipal Assembly, the Mayor of the Municipality shall send the documented request to the Minister to initiate the procedures for the international or financial agreement according to the provisions of this Law.

\5. The approval of the financial agreement or the ratification of the international agreement is subject to the procedure of entering into state debt according to Articles 16 and 17 of this Law.

\6. The conditions, criteria and procedure for reviewing the request through an evaluation mechanism is defined by a sub-legal act issued by the Government.

#### Article 26

Terms of municipal debt

\1. All municipalities of the Republic of Kosovo are entitled to take debt under the provisions of this Law.

\2. Municipal debt may be concluded at a fixed or variable rate of interest.

\3. General terms and procedures for municipal debt shall be determined by a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

#### Article 27

Municipal debt limits

\1. The Municipalities may take short-term and long-term debt.

\2. Short-term debt may be repayable up to twelve (12) months, for the purpose of the cash flow management in relation to own source revenues. Long-term debt is borrowed for the purposes stipulated in the Article 24 of this Law.

\3. The maximum amount allowed for all short-term debt of a municipality may not exceed five percent (5%) of the total appropriated municipal budget for the fiscal year in which the debt is borrowed.

\4. The allowed amount of long-term borrowing within a three (3) year fiscal period may not exceed fifty percent (50%) of the own source revenues of that municipality generated in the prior fiscal year at the time of signing the loan agreement.

\5. Notwithstanding paragraphs 3 and 4 of this Article, the amount of the total outstanding debt of a municipality within the fiscal year may not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the municipality’s own source revenues generated in the prior fiscal year.

\6. Entering into a new borrowing agreement from the Municipality is not allowed if there are less than six (6) remaining months until the end term of the Mayor.

\7. Long-term debt may not mature and be payable beyond the useful time of investment financed by the funds of this debt, limited to twenty (20) years.

\8. Municipalities cannot issue securities or issue municipal guarantees.

\9. Other procedures for borrowing by municipalities are defined by a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

#### Article 28

Municipal debt service

\1. The municipality is responsible for planning the municipal debt service from own source revenues in the annual law on budget appropriations, and in accordance with the Ministry instructions.

\2. The Ministry is authorized to service the debt of municipality on behalf and in the name of the municipality from own source revenues collected from the budget of the respective municipality, or carried forward from the previous year or any other source. This is specified in the loan agreement or accompanying documents.

\3. Own source revenues serve as a lien to secure the settlement of municipal debt obligations.

#### Article 29

Recording of municipal debt

\1. The Municipality shall register and update all its records in full and accurate manner for all liabilities that constitute the municipal debt under this Law.

\2. The registration form and content shall be determined by a decision of Government upon proposal of the Minister.

#### Article 30

Reporting of municipal debt

\1. The Municipality shall, through the Mayor, report to the Ministry within a period of seven (7)

days for all short-term and long-term municipal borrowing withdrawals.

\2. The Municipality shall report quarterly to the Ministry on the municipal debt portfolio and changes over the quarter, according to the format determined by the Minister’s decision at the latest fifteen (15) days after the end of the quarter. Following that, the Ministry shall consolidate them within the state debt report.

### CHAPTER VII

### STATE GUARANTEES

#### Article 31

Authorization to issue state guarantees

\1. The Ministry is the sole authority within the Republic of Kosovo to issue the terms and provide state guarantees for loan on behalf of the Republic of Kosovo.

\2. The Ministry shall provide state guarantees to a third party, financial institutions or private entities for the purpose of loan guarantee.

\3. Decisions for issuance of government guarantees shall be based on credit risk assessment of the project proposal. In cases the Ministry deems necessary, partial guarantees may be issued.

#### Article 32

Purpose and assessment of guarantees

\1. The state loan guarantee is issued to support projects or activities that are considered important for the public benefit or to achieve other purposes for the public benefit.

\2. The request from the potential obligor for issuing the state guarantee shall be assessed in advance by the Ministry according to the procedures and conditions set out in Article 18 of this

Law and approved by the Government.

\3. In order for state guarantees to be approved, the potential obligor must submit to the Ministry a proposal including the economic and financial analysis of the proposed project for funding, including a feasibility study on its reasonability.

\4. The decision to issue the guarantee is taken by the Government not later than ninety (90)

days after the completed request for a guarantee has been submitted to the Ministry.

\5. Guarantees are signed by the Minister, after the approval by the Government.

\6. State guarantee agreements for loan are only entered into the official currency of the Republic of Kosovo, are documented in writing and must contain an agreement with the obligor and another with the creditor.

\7. State guarantees for loan of any kind are subject to approval by 2/3 of all members of the Assembly.

\8. Guarantees shall be issued only after obtaining the approval under the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 33

Credit risk assessment and fees

\1. The Ministry sets out a procedure for assessing credit risk and possible losses from each state guarantee. This assessment is made prior to the issuance of the state guarantee and for each subsequent year after the issuance as long as it is in force.

\2. The expected amount of losses resulting from the assessment in paragraph 1 of this Article is foreseen each year as a reserve for state guarantees.

\3. The methodology for credit risk assessment and application of the guarantee fees shall be regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Government of Kosovo based on the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 34

Treatment of state guarantees at the state debt ceiling

\1. State guarantees issued and in force, for the purposes of calculating the state debt ceiling, are treated within the general ceiling according to Article 13 of this Law.

\2. The amount of the state guarantee that is taken as a basis in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article is the nominal amount of the issued guarantee, which is reduced by the repaid loan amount of the borrower for the loan amount for which the state guarantee has been issued.

\3. For the purposes of paragraph 2 of this Article, the budget organization and/or other entities implementing the project are obliged to report quarterly to the Ministry on the outstanding amount of guaranteed loan amount.

#### Article 35

Calling for state guarantee

\1. The obligor, to whom the state guarantee is issued, shall pay the required obligation for the guaranteed loan, in accordance with the terms set forth in the relevant agreement.

\2. In the event that the obligor expects to fail to pay the loan in the amount and within the time provided for in the relevant agreement, the obligor must inform the Ministry thirty (30) days before the due payment date.

\3. A payment under state loan guarantee may be converted into state debt only after it has been established that the payment or the expected loan payments for which the guarantee was issued were not made in accordance with the terms and conditions of the guaranteed loan.

\4. Even after payment of the amount of the security specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, the obligor shall remain liable to the Ministry for repayment of all payments made for such guarantee, including, but not limited to, interest, penalty and other expenses related to servicing guaranteed loan in accordance with the agreement between the obligor and the Ministry.

#### Article 36

Repayment of paid guarantee

\1. In the event of failure of the guarantee issued pursuant to the provisions of this Law, the guarantee agreement concluded in accordance with Article 32 of this Law shall include even the obligor’s obligation to pay the loan as a result of such failure.

\2. Based on the contract defined under paragraph 1 of this Article, the obligor shall agree that all funds in his accounts at the CBK and/or in all banking institutions are immediately debited for the repayment of the amount paid on behalf of the failed guarantee.

\3. In case of a guarantee for a loan or contract entered into by private entities, the guarantee agreement shall include provisions defining that in the event of a guarantee failure, all properties of the entity shall be subject to encumbrances and shall be held as collateral and/or mortgage and recorded as such in the respective registers by the competent institutions in the Republic of Kosovo.

#### Article 37

Termination of state guarantees

\1. The state guarantee shall terminate in the following situations:

1.1. the loan for which the guarantee is given, has been repaid in full by the obligor;

1.2. the loan contract for which a guarantee is issued has been terminated;

1.3. the terms of the guarantee have expired;

1.4. payment is done in full compliance with the terms of the guarantee; or

1.5. the creditor or the obligor has stated in writing the completion of the guarantee.

\2. Upon termination of the state guarantee under paragraph 1 of this Article, the Minister shall issue a decision for such termination.

### CHAPTER VIII

### ON-LENDING

#### Article 38

On-lending authorisation

\1. The Ministry may, upon request, through an agreement, on-lend at any public entity including the CBK, municipalities and public enterprises, from funds generated by the state debt.

\2. The Ministry may, through the on-lending agreement, on-lend funds, borrowed through financing, to public entities. The on-lending agreement shall be concluded in the following situations:

2.1. to borrowers in the public sector;

2.2. to non-public sector borrowers who provide basic public services in the fields of water, sanitation and waste, public hygiene, electricity generation and transmission, rural electrification and heating.

\3. Any on-lending agreement under paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be signed between the parties stipulating the rights and obligations.

\4. In the case of on-lending agreement according to paragraph 2 of this Article, the on-lending is done with similar financing terms from the Government, but not more favourable than the terms of the original loan.

\5. The process of assessing the on-lending request is subject to the same assessment procedure as any other funding provided for by this Law.

\6. On-lending should be projected in the annual Law on budget appropriations.

\7. The procedures, terms and conditions for such agreements as provided for in this Article shall be set out by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

#### Article 39

Risk assessment and fees

\1. The Ministry shall establish a procedure for assessing the risk of potential losses from each loan or on-lending, including credit risk, foreign currency risk and other risks that the agreement may be exposed to.

\2. The Ministry may apply a fee addition to the interest base rate in order to cover the risk. The revenues from this fee are recorded as budget revenues.

\3. The risk assessment for municipalities and public entities is done taking into account, inter alia, the financial statements and the Auditor General’s report. In the event that the Auditor General has issued an adverse opinion or a disclaimer of the audit opinion for the previous year, no financing may be allowed.

### CHAPTER IX

### RECORDING AND REPORTING

#### Article 40

State debt and state guarantees register

\1. The Ministry shall record and update all records in a complete and accurate manner for all liabilities that constitute net government debt and state guarantees under this Law.

\2. The Ministry consolidates and updates the register for all liabilities that constitute the municipal debt.

\3. The form of the register and the content shall be regulated by a sub-legal act on state debt management issued by the Minister.

#### Article 41

Reporting on State Debt and State Guarantees

\1. The Ministry shall prepare, consolidate and publish data on state debt and state guarantees at least every three (3) months, and an annual summary disclosing among others, the total state debt level.

\2. All budget organizations that have state debts or state guarantees under this Law are obliged to report the data required by the Ministry in the required format and deadline.

\3. The contents of the report and the level of disclosure of published data shall be determined by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

### CHAPTER X

### RESTRICTIONS OF THE INSTITUTIONS AND OTHER PUBLIC ENTITIES

#### Article 42

Borrowing of the institutions and other public entities

\1. Institutions and other public entities may borrow under the legislation into force on public financial management and the relevant legislation on public entities, only after obtaining a prior consent from the Minister.

\2. The debt as per paragraph 1 of this Article is not a state debt, neither shall be reported as state debt by the Ministry.

\3. Procedures, criteria including the audited financial statements, for the consent on financing by the public entities shall be determined by a sub-legal act approved by the Government.

### CHAPTER XI

### FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 43

Prohibited behaviour regarding financial market manipulation

\1. Participants in the Government securities market are not allowed to enter into secret agreements with one or more other participants with the aim to:

1.1. the influence on the market regarding the interest rate or coupon, price, amount of Government securities purchased in the auction or in the secondary market;

1.2. the impact on the composition of the portfolio held by any other market participant, the investment strategy of any other market participant or others, or vice versa; and

1.3. engaging in any transaction, practice or behaviour that would act as fraud or speculation on other market participants.

\2. Public officials who have a responsibility in the process of issuing or managing securities or external debt should implement the best practices to avoid the conflict of interest. The practice of the conflict of interest shall be regulated by a sub-legal act issued by the Minister.

\3. Violation of the provisions set forth in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be handled according to the criminal legislation into force or any other relevant legislation.

#### Article 44

Tax treatment The interest revenue or fees applied by issuing state debt or guarantees, as well as issuing Government securities or transferring their ownership shall be regulated in accordance with the tax legislation into force.

#### Article 45

Transitional provisions

\1. All procedures for borrowing, issuance of guarantees and securities initiated prior to the entry into force of this Law shall be addressed according to the provisions of Law No. 03/L-175 on Public Debt.

\2. Unless otherwise regulated by the Law on public financial management, the total debt ceiling including state guarantees may not be higher than forty percent (40%) of gross domestic product calculated according to the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 46

Prevalence of the Law In the event of a discrepancy between the provisions of this Law and other laws containing provisions for or affecting the status of state debt and state guarantees, this Law shall prevail for the matters regulated by this Law.
