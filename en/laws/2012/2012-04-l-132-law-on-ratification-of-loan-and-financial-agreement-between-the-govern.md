# LAW ON RATIFICATION OF LOAN AND FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE AND GERMAN BANK KfW IN RELATION TO THE WATER FACTORY IN SHKABAJ

### 1LAW NO. 04/L-132

### ON RATIFICATION OF LOAN AND FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE AND GERMAN BANK KfW IN

### RELATION TO THE WATER FACTORY IN SHKABAJ

Assembly of Republic of Kosovo, In support to Article 65 (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo, Approves

### LAW ON RATIFICATION OF LOAN AND FINANCIAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE

REPUBLIC OF KOSOVO, REPRESENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE AND GERMAN BANK KfW IN

### RELATION TO THE WATER FACTORY IN SHKABAJ

#### Article 1

Purpose of the Law This law aims the ratification of the Loan and Financial Agreement between the Government of Republic of Kosovo represented by the Ministry of Finances and the German Bank KfW, in relation to the project for water factory in Shkabaj, signed on 5th of June, 2012.

#### Article 2

Scope of work This law ratifies the loan and Financial Agreement signed between the Ministry of Finance on behalf of the Government of the Republic of Kosovo and the German Bank KfW, for water factory project in Shkabaj, signed on 5th of June, 2012.

#### Article 3

Entry into force This law shall enter into force fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.

Law No. 04/L-132 06 September 2012 Promulgated by Decree No.DL-043-2012, dated 28.09.2012, President of the Republic of Kosovo Atifete Jahjaga
